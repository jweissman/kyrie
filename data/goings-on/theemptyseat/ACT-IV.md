# Act IV: The Unspent Door

The first incursion is over, not the war. Begin with a kettle, boots drying and a
missing name on the kitchen list. No new army arrives during this interval. Then
the kitchen scales begin weighing a bowl differently on alternate beats.

The Stillhouse beneath the Tower of Doors contains an **Equivalence Engine**:
an intact Mathematical instrument joining a surveyed place in Imbar to a small
reach of the Plane of Earth. It can exchange a supported load between its two
ends. That is an experimentally demonstrated property, not a universal theory
of its powers. Whether the other rooms are inhabited, what the marked third
socket does, and what lies beyond the sealed lower door are initially unknown.
The final war act is still ahead.

### Three contrasting movements

1. **Quiet inventory.** Hest's kitchen is not a council. A meal is the immediate
   objective. The changed weight introduces a question, not a revelation.
   Mared's confidence is interrupted by the list of people missing from dinner.
2. **A machine and another household.** The company chooses its route and
   experiments. It can withdraw at every stage. No blind control must be pulled.
   The far ledge is genuinely in Earth. A seeded occupied/vacant record room
   changes the way information is obtained. A resident may care more about their
   missing accounts than either claimant's crown.
   Three optional episodes have their own payoffs: the household ledger, a meal
   exchanged for Earth salt, and a stopped delivery clock recovered by proof or
   physical measurement. The courier spends two actual meals on its return.
3. **One demonstrated use.** A surveyed counterweight makes a limited military
   strike possible. A civilian crossing has a different beneficiary and cost.
   The same engine cannot be used twice in this chapter: the operator disconnects
   its feed after the chosen exchange. Closing it is a complete, playable outcome.
   Nobody declares the Ferryman defeated. What the lower door holds belongs to
   the next expedition.

The enemy works independently: the counterweight is fixed eight hours after the
kitchen lead becomes available, whether the company investigates or not. Delay
retires the military strike, not civilian rescue or retreat. Kept records save a
survey; Tarian's bracing saves travel; an earlier rubbing saves military
calibration food; Cadi or Nerys can shorten the civilian preparation. These
advantages change costs, not the proof's scope.

## Variation and its denominator

Count **narrative delivery slots in the executable fourth-act source**: each
Observe/Event, Topic's Says and Action's Result counts once. Alternative branches
of one delivery count separately when authored separately. Count a slot as
procedural only when its entire delivery is supplied by a Deem-selected label.
Do not count clocks, gates, boilerplate, names or every word of a fixed paragraph
as generated content. The focused spec measures the ratio from the source and
requires at least 40%. This is an Act IV delivery-slot measure, **not** a claim
that 40% of all forty adventures or of the plot graph is generated.
The implemented source has **33 procedural deliveries out of 38 (86.8%)**,
including multiline observations. Repeated deliveries still count as slots,
not additional unique stories.

One installation draws a coherent occupancy profile, resident and discovery
report. Subsequent reads reuse that draw, including after saving. Other tables
vary the lull, experiment, empty-room evidence, negotiation, domestic detour and
aftermath. Actual occupancy and actions must agree; prose cannot invent a person
in an empty room. Rare Mathematical traces in generated works use stable
site-keyed Deem draws, yield real portable rubbings, and do not replace treasure.
The draw is four in twenty, only in generated works with a recognised inner way.
Halm, Fabian and Gammer Coller supply authored rubbings with distinct reasons;
Martin's circular clocks open the question that earns Coller's independent
measure. These are concrete earlier-adventure payoffs, not forty rewritten plots.

## Mathematical perception

Mathematician retains its earlier intelligence and Lockwork. It additionally
brings Mathematics and guaranteed mathematical checks. A guaranteed check is
taking twenty by proof, not guessing what an unseen room contains. Other
mathematical attempts roll against at least DC 30 when trained, DC 35 without
training; a natural twenty is the rare
breakthrough even when the total would be too low. This exception belongs only
to Mathematics, not persuasion, ordinary locks or combat.

Purple remains an engine-derived opportunity mark for true wonders.
Ordinary mathematical traces yield portable research without a wonder mark.
The Numbered House's counting glass earns a persistent structural sense, while
the mechanical star-tower orrery teaches ordinary astronomy. See
[Books, ruins and earned senses](MATHEMATICAL-WORLD.md) for the implemented
book leads, recurrent arcology family and the separate natural-philosophy
direction. No proof knows all properties of an artifact on sight.

## Breadth held apart from the proof

Further expeditions can recover the resident's names, inspect the lower door,
trace the third socket into Water, bargain with an Earth carrier, discover why a
royal survey omitted inhabited places, and return ordinary household property.
These are storyboard directions, not six shipped dungeons. The current
shared-metropolis placement blocker remains visible; this chapter is not landed
or represented as integrated kingdom-wide play.
