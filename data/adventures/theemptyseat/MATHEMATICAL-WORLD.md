# Books, ruins and earned senses

## The distinction

Mathematics is an art people practise. A wonder is something that changes what
can happen or what can be perceived. The first need not imply the second.

A household account, a mason's sector and a proof can matter without being
purple. Purple belongs to an actual wonder and the opportunity to meet it.
Finding a book does not certify that its destination is safe, vacant, intact or
understood. Nor does a successful proof certify safe operation.

Technical comprehension needs encountered evidence and sufficient familiarity
to recognise the question. Its floor is **DC 30 with training, DC 35 without**.
Higher authored difficulties remain higher. A natural twenty is a breakthrough;
Mathematician succeeds by proof. The engine chooses the best effective check,
including the different floors, rather than merely the highest raw bonus.

## What is implemented

### Ordinary books with grounded itineraries

Where a generated settlement has an actual taproom, its keeper has one antique
book to lend, provided the country has a suitable destination. This does not
invent an inn in the reference settlement. The book is a real owned, portable
item; borrowing grants permission, not ownership.

The destination is queried from actual unclaimed deep or great works, or the
site a placed arcology occupies. Another adventure's claimed ground is excluded.
A missing destination means no book is generated. A missing mapped seat is an
error, not a fictional address.

Five Deem profiles mix ordinary human purposes with recycled mathematical
material:

| Volume | The human material | The useful remnant |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A Widow's Reckoning | Household sums; a child's boots on the triangles | A traveller's bearings and an encoded old name |
| The Surveyor Who Would Not Come Home | Letters and angry corrections | A reliable itinerary and an invariant beneath a misspelling |
| Tables for an Unfinished Stair | Lodgings and onion soup | Maintenance bearings and paired load measures |
| Instructions for a Funeral That Did Not Happen | Mourning customs | A recycled survey sheet and its coordinate transformation |
| The Little Book of Bad Instruments | Apprentice complaints and failed calibrations | A master's journey and the instruments' common correction |

An **ARC** designation refers to an actual arcology placement; **SUR** refers to
another surveyed work. The country registry qualifies the site identity, keeping
the code stable across copies. A stamp is not a claim that
every source is an arcology.

Reading the itinerary needs no mathematical roll. It teaches the existing
location discovery, preserving its native landmark and passage chain. Reading
it supplies familiarity for the technical appendix. Resolving that appendix
restores the work's name, which existing named-work objectives read. Failure
does not withdraw the usable route. Neither action declares a marvel.

Every generated book profile is table-selected; the destination is a separate
site-keyed draw. This is procedural variation in deliveries and bindings, not
a claim that the plot graph or 100% of the world's prose is generated.

### Mathematical objects without false wonder

The ordinary trace kit now occurs on **four of twenty draws** in eligible
generated works with an inner way. Five profiles include fixed surveying
circles and hinged mason's sectors as well as incised patterns. They produce
actual portable rubbings, still useful in the Stillhouse calibration.

These objects are no longer declared `Marvel`. Their geometry can be difficult
without being supernatural. A Mathematician can recognise the question at once;
another character first examines the object. A failed trace attempt remains
bounded per company. This change does not place treasure caches.

### Arcology as a recurrent dungeon

The first country keeps its existing standing arcology. Subsequent countries
also install the arcology family, on available unclaimed ground, after the
ordinary draw. It does not disappear because a unique-adventure exclusion list
contains an earlier arcology. Separate regional installations retain separate
house state and seeded variation.

The intended density is a regular country-level dungeon slot, comparable to the
existing country-level deep-work slot, not a legendary one-off. Twelve cheap
placement cases prove recurrence on actual sites. They are **not** a census of
the assembled kingdom; terrain eligibility and the world gate still need CI
measurement. The recurrence currently uses the Numbered House archetype, not
four newly built architectural families.

Its Quiet Floor now always contains the **counting glass**. This is a true
wonder: the frame teaches the company to perceive load and strain without
keeping the frame. Understanding it uses the mathematical check and raises the
House's hum on success or failure. The Host offers a real explanatory question,
not a free upgrade or a promise that the instrument is harmless.

The earned structural sense persists in the saved company's grants without an
expiry. At the cliff chamber it reveals the actual maintenance branch beneath
the slab, opening the existing hidden way. This is an engine-owned observation
and consequence, not a decorative widget. It is company-level, not yet a
character-specific transferable faculty. Another counting glass does not stack
the same sense or invent a second UI component.

The Star-Readers' ordinary mechanical orrery now grants **Astronomy** when
correctly set. Its familiar twelve-sign count continues after temporary lore
expires. The existing sky instrument reads the current sign outdoors at night;
it is not yet a global indoor calendar widget. Its state is named `reckoned`
to avoid collision with the shared rules' undead `turned` status.

## The instrument portfolio: direction, not seven shipped upgrades

Each vault should contain one definite instrument. Its principle is legible
before its full use, its acquisition has an expedition cost, and the permanent
faculty makes earlier places worth revisiting. A duplicate principle supplies
information or another use, not another screen.

| Instrument | Faculty | What the engine can legitimately reveal | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Counting glass | Structural sense | Load-bearing versus skin; a real maintenance branch | Implemented, bounded Numbered House use |
| Ordinary mechanical orrery | Astronomy | Major birth signs and the current month-sign | Implemented through the existing outdoor sky reading |
| **The Mathematical Orrery** | Natural philosophy | A spatial stellar model, wandering bodies and the next actual eclipse | Designed, not implemented |
| Tide prism | Correspondence | Connected bodies of water and the conditions of a real passage between them | Direction |
| Vacant compass | Absence | A missing room or intentionally omitted interval, never a fictional generated room | Direction |
| Resonance comb | Harmonics | A particular material or mechanism answering through intervening structure | Direction |
| Interval clock | Causal timing | The phase of a known mechanism and the remaining safe interval | Direction |

These faculties compose: structural sense finds a chamber; absence distinguishes
its missing interval; harmonics identifies what answers there; correspondence
locates a passage; timing gives its window; natural philosophy explains why the
next eclipse will alter it. None automatically wins a battle or reads an unseen
occupant's intentions.

The Mathematical Orrery is not a renamed village armillary. Its wonder is the
permanent change in perception, not multiplication tables on brass.

### The stellar model must exist before its confident prediction

The current sky data holds twelve two-dimensional constellation drawings and
month/sign associations. It does not define orbital periods, nodes, inclinations,
angular sizes or an epoch from which an eclipse can be computed.

Natural philosophy needs a setting-owned celestial model with those quantities.
The engine should project positions, viewing coordinates and predicted events;
the page draws that projection in an optional three-dimensional instrument.
The terminal and runner receive the same event facts and a readable projection.
No client computes an eclipse or silently promotes a drawing into astronomy.
The ordinary month-sign reading can become a pinned global calendar instrument
without pretending to observe the sky through a roof. This belongs to the earned
instrument direction (RI-04) and exceptional responsive instruments (TO-05).

## Rich destinations to grow, not imaginary book addresses

| Dungeon form | Ordinary life in its remains | Singular instrument opportunity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strange pyramid | Quarries, burial accounts, later squatters | An absence compass beneath a deliberately missing chamber |
| Deepworks installation | Shift ledgers, kitchens, drains and active rival crews | A resonance comb in the load-bearing core |
| Levitating ruin | Anchor crews, hanging allotments and weather records | An interval clock beside the failing anchorage |
| Tundra vault | Storehouses and successive failed relief expeditions | The Mathematical Orrery inside a maintained, possibly occupied observatory |

These are four authoring directions, not four new locations already in the
world. A future book may point to one only after its actual site, entrances,
danger, inhabitants and instrument have been installed. A damaged itinerary
can omit a leg; it must not create a nonexistent destination.

For Act IV, the books and ordinary instruments widen the historical context of
the Stillhouse without turning every proof into a war asset. The dramatic
question remains who bears the cost of a real exchange. A better sense can
reveal a support or a limit; it must not silently remove the earlier betrayal,
the resident's claim, or the enemy's construction deadline.
