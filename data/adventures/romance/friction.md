# The Rose of Orlane: friction

This branch is a deliberately traditional adventure romance: visible treasure, captives in danger, a dragon fought in its lair, and magic learned from a recovered book. It belongs away from the starting settlement as a province to discover.

- A rescued group can leave its prison, but Kyr has no compact authored `Rescue` whose captivity, release and aftermath remain one semantic object.
- `Resolve teach` gives runnable spells to a caster and correctly gives none to a non-caster. The prose cannot say which named reader learned which spell without parallel state.
- A hoard is several ordinary items in a room. That needs no new system, but reach should recognize taking treasure as the point of the scene.
- Returning a heavy bell is holding one item, not a carrying problem shared by the company. Expedition logistics deserve a later proof slice, not a special case.

The inscription opens the prison, the prisoners identify the spellbook, the book teaches usable magic, and the returned bell changes Orlane. None is decorative talk leading nowhere.
