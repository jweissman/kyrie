# Caught Below

Someone went into old workings and has not come back. A fallen weight pins them, and the short way out will close.
The intended experience is in [doc/design/caught-below.md](../../../doc/design/caught-below.md); what it shows about
the language is in [doc/design/caught-below-report.md](../../../doc/design/caught-below-report.md).

## Roles a site must provide

| Role | Kind | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `party` | group | The player's company |
| `workings` | place (a site) | The place the missing person went into; named in prose |
| `captive_room` | place | Where the accident happened. The situation puts the trapped person and the fallen weight here |
| `tool_room` | place | Where a pry bar lies |
| `home` | place | Where the missing person lives |
| `lookout` | place | Where the person waiting for them stands and watches |
| `closing_way` | passage | A short way that will close; must connect two places (`connects(closing_way, ?a, ?b)`) |

The site supplies everything else: how the places connect, what guards or endangers the way, and who else lives
there. The situation never names a site place, person or passage.

## What the situation creates

- **The missing person** (`guest`): generated by DEEM from the `setting` context. They are pinned (`hobbled`), and
  freeing them sets their condition to *hurt* (can walk) or *lame* (cannot walk alone, depending on how they were
  freed). Their goals are to stay close while you lead them, otherwise to head home once you have left them or told
  them to go. They plan routes with the same `go` action as everyone else, so they avoid guarded, shut and dangerous
  ways.
- **The relative** (`relative`): waits at the lookout watching the workings, meets the missing person wherever they
  cross paths, then takes them home.
- **The fallen weight** (`rack`): pinning → propped / dug / toppled, each described in its own words.
- **The pry bar** (`bar`, an item of the catalog kind `crowbar`, from Orsino's gear list) at the tool room.
- **The closing** (`water`): a thing on `closing_way`, open until `closesAt(water)`, then closed (blocking).

Ways to free the guest:

- **Lever:** quick. It needs a crowbar in hand, either the situation's bar or one a companion carries.
- **Dig:** forty minutes with bare hands, or twenty for a company with the `digging` skill (the miner background).
- **Heave:** immediate, but it hurts the lifter and breaks the guest's leg.

A broken leg can be set by a company with the `mending` skill, from the medic background, the herbalist profession,
or a cleric's training.

## Installations

- [river.campaign.json](../../caught-below/river.campaign.json) binds the roles to [Reedwake and Censer
  Hill](../../sites/reedwake/site.kyr) with `setting: river`.
- [quarry.campaign.json](../../caught-below/quarry.campaign.json) binds them to [Skellfold and the high
  quarry](../../sites/skellfold/site.kyr) with `setting: quarry`.

`setting` selects names, the fallen weight, the tool, what closes the way, and when it closes
([tables.json](tables.json)): about 1:10–1:40 p.m. at the river and 12:10–12:40 p.m. in the quarry. Both
are reachable in time only by a company that goes fairly directly, because passages now take their real length.

The situation names no service, shop or inn. Those belong to the site, and the needs and services rules give
them meaning in every installation.
