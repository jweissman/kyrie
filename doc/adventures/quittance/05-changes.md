# Quittance — what changed between the design and the work

| # | Design said | Built | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Founder is Dame Honora **Quill** | Dame Honora **Hearn** | Surnames must be unique across all adventures (names lint); Quill, Vane, Ashby, Marl, Fisk, Quire, Lowe all collided with earlier adventures. Renames: Quill→Hearn, Vane→Pargeter, Ashby→Pettifer, Marl→Corbett, Fisk→Tench, Quire→Sprack, Lowe→Prest. |
| 2 | Days numbered from 0 | `day(clock)` is 1 on the first morning | Engine fact; every day-numbered rule shifted by one. |
| 3 | `regard` runs negative (−1, −2) | `regard` floors at 0; offences are recorded as a relation beside a `Decrease … or to nothing` | Measures cannot go below nothing. |
| 4 | Five Offices, each with a brigade and a house | Three Offices have places and people (Phoenix, Lamprey, Sovereign); Providence has a counter and an adjuster; the Residuary is one bald man with sandwiches | R10: depth over breadth. |
| 5 | The Fire is a lonely creditor | The Fire has an edge: it burned Cindermouth and is not sorry; it warns twice before it burns | R12. |
| 6 | The Hearing counts documents | It counts regard, arcs and witnesses; a document is one lever for Pettifer only | R4. |
| 7 | Prewitt moved by witness, kin, arithmetic | plus *grief* (a coal for Margery's lamp) and *hardened* (threat) | Four levers make three of four sufficient and the cost of the fourth (a scarce coal) a choice. |
| 8 | The Descent as a dungeon | A heat game, glass men that audit, a notarial lock, five readable clauses, a mood game with two warnings | R5. |
| 9 | Seven lamps in the Round | Four, each a different kind of problem, plus five ways to retire a man | R2. |
| 10 | The Great Tithe is a rule at midnight | A set piece with Tithe men, five ways round it, and an ending (*The Ward Without Its People*) for the evacuation | Second review C8. |
| 11 | Endings are static | Twelve "afterword" chapters tell the small threads over in the last minutes | C9. |
| 12 | Honora absent | Honora absent *and everywhere*: a pencil hand in six places | C1. |
| 13 | The hardened Prewitt has no teeth | The Phoenix's wardens come for the company | C8. |
| 14 | City alive "by clocks" | The Receipt, the paving, the plaster, the rhyme, the pageant, ambient sound/smell per district | R1, C2. |
| 15 | Twelve small adventures | Thirteen (the Arch counted) | — |
| 16 | A Hearing with the Chairmen as characters | The Chairmen are a group with a few lines | Not done: Hearing remains the weakest scene. |
