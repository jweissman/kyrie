# The Road the Star Came Down: the spine

*The design document for the five-act campaign that grew out of* Let One Remember. *It is written before the Kyr of
Acts II to V, and the Kyr is judged against it.*

## The one sentence

**In 412 the Lettings tried to forget the star in the Well, and forgot only its name; ever since, the name has been
gathering itself in the Margin, and the body has been lying in the Well without it. Ninety years ago the body woke
and went after the jewel. Now the name wants out, to go after its body. Whoever reaches the Hush first decides what
the Varakiiri is.**

This one fact is the strategic adversary, and it is the source's own: the Unsaid is a forgotten Varakiiri name
(*Sourcebook*, ground truth), the Nail followed Wenna north-north-east and up (the horn's bearing), and the Unsaid is
"very interested in where the Hush went". The campaign makes the connection explicit. A name without a body, and a
body without a name, are each a lesser thing. Reunited, they are a Varakiiri, awake and whole, in a world with eight
other jewels.

## The acts

The original *G-D-Q* run (*Against the Giants*, *Descent into the Depths*, *Queen of the Spiders*) goes down, from the
giants' halls to the Underdark and on to the Abyss. This campaign goes **up and out**, along the horn's bearing.
Each act has its own places, faces and kind of play. Each ends on a turn the company was present for, and each
carries what the company did forward as a resource or a wound.

### Act I, Let One Remember (built; reworked for drama)

*The urban investigation that opens the run, as the slaving ring opens* G-D-Q. The ring here is a ring of
belonging-thieves: Strays taking people's places, each carrying a syllable.

- **Hook.** The Rope and Lantern's board has seven jobs on it, each one of the sourcebook's expeditions with a
  payer and a price. The company arrives with a debt (the salvager's widow origin) and needs every one of them.
- **Inciting incident.** The first great gale fells a hoist tower at Hornwake and kills two. The company hears it
  told, or is there.
- **Midpoint reversal.** Link 143 falls; Hightown rises twenty metres. Everyone in Hornwake is outside looking up.
- **Crisis.** The Grounders gather on the rim to tell everyone to look down. It takes them two hours to gather.
- **Climax.** On the rim over the Socket, in the last gale: the company, if it is there and has the engineers'
  words, can turn three hundred people telling at once into one a day.
- **Arcs.** Teshen goes from wanting to be a stranger to finding the one place a Keld can be one (Act II). Linnet
  goes from asking the forbidden question to keeping the roster. Aron goes from a speech to a list. Corin Webb goes
  from a man in someone's chair to Corin of the Hollow, who once carried a syllable (Acts II and III).

### Act II, The Grey Country

*The Underdark of this run.*

- **Way in.** The anchoring rite. The prioress's sisters forget the company at the Seam, and the company walks into
  the lake onto a grey shore.
- **The clock.** The anchors above say the company's names, in relays, day and night. Every ordinary anchor holds
  for two days; a Keld anchor never tires, because a Keld cannot forget. The company's anchors are the people it
  helped in Act I: the prioress always; Judge Pennick if her deeds came back; Absalom Prew if he was given his name
  back; Bram Coyle if his lens came home; Mother Saska, and Teshen if she stayed above. When the voices run out,
  the company stays.
- **Places.**
  - *The drifts.* Lost things against forgotten fences, among them Sabeth Coyle's blue bucket.
  - *Keld Hollow.* Intact, smoke from its chimneys, nobody in the lanes. One house is truly empty if Corin was named.
  - *The untitled library.* Half the Calvessa's forgotten history, with the rite of 300 BB in it: the Forgetting of
    the Way Home, which made a war-band forget its way and wander the plateau until it died.
  - *The mountain's place.* A hole-shaped absence exactly the size of the Socket. A stone of that absence carried
    out is Hightown's place in a pocket.
  - *Iniry Calde.* The Calvessan mnemonist, forgotten here in the Short Grey of 2604. She can teach the old rite,
    and can come home if anyone above learns to remember her.
  - *The Unsaid's court.* The name, wearing forgotten things as a body, with three fragments of the road (the
    keepers' lapses) in its keeping.
- **The Unsaid's offer.** The fragments of the road, for its last syllables.
- **Resolutions.**
  - *Give it a name and a place in the Margin* (Corin's empty house). It stops taking, and stays. This needs the
    knowledge of what it is, a Keld to speak, and an empty house.
  - *Name it for what it is, and give it its body as its place.* It leaves for the Nail, and Act V wakes whole.
  - *Fight it.* Everything it took returns to Imbar at once, the mountain's place with it: Hightown regains its
    whole weight and falls.
  - *Bargain.* It crosses into Imbar in Act III, at the kin's head.
- **The Open Hand.** While the company is below, they hold their rite at the Seam if they have seven syllables.
  The company's Act I choices decide how many they have.

### Act III, The Kin Fall

*The giants' war of this run, but the giants come from the sky.*

- On the forty-second day the new star breaks over the Teskarn. The **Unfolded** come down in columns: deadiron
  bodies, as tall as a house, things that unfold out of the falling stars as the cave bear saw in the Root. They
  march the bearing of anything that knew the Hush: the horn first, then the Well, then the Seam and Wenna's band,
  the only record of where she went.
- **No spell touches them.** The Corrow has two arts that might:
  1. **The Forgetting of the Way Home**, the rite of 300 BB, from the untitled library or from Iniry Calde. It needs
     three hundred minds who can forget: the Letting sisters, the Open Hand if turned, Hornwake's Wake-moot if Lene
     and Ruth ask it, and Hightown's Unaskers, who have practised not knowing for two thousand six hundred years.
     The Keld cannot take part. Held on the Drag across the kin's road, it makes them forget the way. Its spill is a
     Grey Month, and the company chooses what the Corrow lets go to pay for it.
  2. **Iron.** Drevka's hammer, and Bede's last forty days: link 97's replacement split into blades.
- **Wenna's band.** If the kin reach the Seam they read her band and go up the road ahead of everyone. The company
  can break it first with Drevka's hammer, which breaks a laid band in one blow, and lose the only record of the
  way, keeping only the horn's bearing.

### Act IV, Under Way

*The descent of this run, inverted: an ascent.*

- **The vessel.** Hightown. A weightless mountain is steered by memory: one more Counterweight who holds its place
  sinks it; one the sisters let forget raises it. Drevka's hammer opens Drevka's own rivet, and the horn comes free
  of the Chain. Hung under the cradles, it is the only compass in Imbar that points at a Kiritarun.
  - If Hightown came down in Act I, the old rite can lift it again, as the Calvessa lifted it in 51 AB, at the
    rite's price.
  - If it was lost to the sky in Act I, there is no vessel, and the road closes.
- **The legs.** Over the Teskarn (it must rise), into the cloud sea, onto the Witness road (it must hold the bearing).
- **The Gate of Being.** An Arbiter of the Virina, who divided the planes, will open the door to one. The company
  has read Wenna's band, so every one of them knows the way. "Then there would be two." To pass, they must let the
  road go out of all of them but one.

### Act V, The Road the Star Came Down

*The 66th layer of this run.*

- Beyond the gates nothing is woven of the prima materium: it is all deadiron country, and no spell works anywhere.
- **Wenna.** She has been thinking "elsewhere" for ninety years to stay a thought ahead of the Nail. She is very
  old, and has forgotten everything but the keeping, her own name included. Bram's lens, which came home to the
  Coyle house in 2719 because she had forgotten it, gives her back herself.
- **The Nail.** It is a thought behind her. If the Unsaid went to it in Act II, it is whole and awake, and it speaks.
- **The endings.**
  - **One remembers.** A member of the company takes the charge. Wenna rests. The Nail, its quarry known to exactly
    one mind, falls as slowly as it did for two thousand years. The company comes home with the Hush, and Hightown
    can be set down in its own place.
  - **Never none.** The road is forgotten by every mind. The Hush slips into the Margin it binds, and the Margin
    comes unbound into the Corrow.
  - **Given back.** The Hush is given to the Varakiiri. The war beyond the gates of being begins again, with eight
    jewels left.
  - **Followed.** The company goes on with Wenna, a thought ahead, for ever. The Corrow is told nothing.

## Why it is not the other three adventures

- It does not descend; it ascends.
- Its plane of forgotten things is entered by being forgotten and left by being remembered, with the company's
  friends as the rope (*Thrume*'s Between is fallen into, and has a mouth).
- Its time runs at the ordinary rate everywhere (*The Patient Star*).
- Its law is that knowing has weight, not that watching fixes (*The House That Grows*).

## As built

All five acts are written and played (see the README). Where the build departs from this design:

- **Act III's iron route** (Bede splitting link 97's replacement into blades) is not built. Drevka's hammer is lent
  for the band and the needle only.
- **The Hartmark** appears only as Esk's men watching Hightown cast off. No harder envoy, no host.
- **Act IV's re-lift** of a fallen Hightown is by the Unaskers alone, at a cost of every Counterweight's knowing.
  The stone of the mountain's place is used only in Act V's homecoming.
- **The Open Hand's syllables** are counted at the rite: two that came through on Strays, Webb's one or two, the
  three Keld Strays if taken, and one if the company said syllables at the Seam. Saska's is never taken.
- **Wenna's age** is left as written: a hundred and fifty-nine, kept alive by nothing but the keeping.
