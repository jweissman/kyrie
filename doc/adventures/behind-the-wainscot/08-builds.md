# 08 — Builds: who can do what, and how differently

A single protagonist, or a company. Every gate has at least two ways round it, and at least one of them asks for no
roll at all but for something learned or carried. Checks named are the engine's (`check(actor, skill, dc)`), rolled by
whoever of the company is best at it: a recruited companion counts (Sorrel Haddow, a level-two bard, makes the
Moot's persuasion checks for a warrior in the Freehold journey).

## A company without charm

The warrior's journeys show that a protagonist with no persuasion can still do everything that matters: Sorrel
speaks at the Moot; the chart is written by her; every Talk gate has a knowledge or object alternative. What a
charmless company loses is speed and certainty: one failed urge at the Hearth is eleven dead.
