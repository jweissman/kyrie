# 04 — Adaptation: what Kyr and the engine say directly, and where they resisted

*Stage 3, written after reading the authoring guide (all of it), the Lull's whole module, Skeel's court, the shared
rules for people and inhabitants, `countries.ts`, the choice grouping and attention code of the browser, and the
Mission 18 one-universe design; and revised as the build met the engine.*

## Said directly, in the language as it is

| Ambition | How |
|---|---|
| A house twelve times your size | Ordinary containment: a building with three levels; every place a company stands is a Lodger-scale room (a shelf, a grate, a doll's house parlour). Scale lives entirely in prose. No new law. |
| The Lady's day, Mrs Pyecroft's day, the cat's night | Rules on `hour(clock)` asserting `at(…)` (`clock.kyr`); a day counter on the dawn, after Skeel's (`dayN`). |
| The Stillness, and being glimpsed | `nearLady`, a watch's grace (`nearSince`), an action `Time 0` to keep still; a rule that, a watch later, has her see you, frightening her (`fright`) and hastening the Smoking. |
| Contact by degrees | A measure `primed(lady)` raised by the chart, the letter and the concert; three versions of "step out into the light", each `Grave`, offered by how primed she is: certain, a hard roll, a formidable roll with a hand that sweeps. |
| Conversation only after she knows | `talks(lady)` retracted until `ladyKnows`; topics `with lady` thereafter. |
| A corrected chart, answered | A scenery Thing with four states (blank, corrected, questioned, answered) changed by night actions and by her own routine at nine and eleven. |
| The fire, the tap, the trap, the sulphur | Things with states (a `blocking` fire on the fender; a `dangerous` trap that `Hurts`); events `within house` so they are heard anywhere inside; Hazards for heat, jaws, a giant's hand, a cat's paw, sulphur smoke. |
| The cat | A local species *like behemoth*, territorial, so that it warns once and then comes; asleep, peaceable and tolerant by day; belled, tolerant at night too. Catmint sends it out to the garden. |
| The Moot | An `Occasion` with three `Matter`s; speech gated by a query (`vouched()`) on earlier deeds; lapse rules anchored at the parlour that say the default decision. |
| The will, the deed, the sale | Relations; Mr Saltash follows the company into the house (`acceptsEscort`, `following`); the deed is an item to be carried and lodged or sold (Grave). |
| Mrs Pyecroft's promise | `Promise … kept when … broken when …`. |
| Fennick's spring | A measure that runs down by the hour while he travels with the company; an action `Time 0` to wind him; a rule that stops him where he stands. |
| Recognition and persistence | `countries.json` (rumour, heard, far, skyline, notice, after); an optional imported `word` marked stale when she dies; chapters in `after.kyr` for return visits. |
| Experience | Milestones for deeds (forty to two hundred). |

## Worked round, and recorded in friction.md

- **Passage kinds and scale** (F1). A ladder lashed to a table leg does not change level; a sixty-yard walk across a
  giant's floor is indoors. The lint (rightly, for ordinary houses) warns on both. Written as `steps` and `passage`.
- **The browser makes anyone with an action a conversation** (F3). An action whose requirements name the Lady became
  "Talk to the Lady" in the browser before she could be talked to. Worked round with queries and rules so that
  "keep still" and "step out" are the company's own acts.
- **The glance shows one sentence** (F4). Hooks moved into the first sentence of a place (the notice; the colliers).
- **A Grave on a standard give** (F6). Selling the deed is written as an action, because `Wants` gives no place for a
  Grave.
- **Toll one way** (F5). A blocking thing blocks both ways; the Risers' ladders are two one-way passages.
- **Names across adventures** (F8). Six surnames were taken by other authors; renamed.

## Not attempted

- The protagonist cannot speak to the Lady except by the offered acts until she knows. A free-text "shout up at her"
  is not a Kyrie verb, and should not be.
- No combat at giant scale. The Lady is never fought; the cat is a monster to be avoided, not a boss.
- No map of furniture tops as a separate level. Considered (a level "up on the furniture") and rejected: it would
  have split the ground floor's map in two for a lint warning's sake.
