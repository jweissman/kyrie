# 05 — The map

## In the world

The Hollins country is a hill country in the west of the world's known lands, entered from the south over the march
of a mountain, forest or frost country (`countries.json`: side `north`, beside `mountain`, `forest`, `frost`). Its
entry is placed after every other authored country, on the north side, where Merrowdale always holds the first ring;
so it moves no other country in any world the tests draw (`test/hollins-placed.test.ts`, and `bin/kyrie countries`
before and after: identical but for Hollins). In the nine test worlds it lies two to four countries out from the first.

- **Heard of** in the country it is entered from: whoever keeps travellers there tells of "a village called Bootscrape,
  built on a doorstep", and a notice is nailed up there: WANTED, A RATCATCHER.
- **Further off** it is "where a giantess has come home to die in a house as big as a hill, and there are people
  living in its walls".
- **Seen** from high ground in the country next to it: "a grey hill with a roof on it, and windows in its side as tall
  as churches".
- **The road**: the moor road comes down over Scrape Moor into Bootscrape.
- **After**: once the Lady is dead, the notice is old news; whoever keeps travellers hears the company out and puts the
  notice in a drawer.

The Whitlocks are written as the last giant gentry west of the march, and Wendle as the county town a carrier's day
off. Neither is claimed for any other country (friction.md, F11).

## The house (Lodger scale: a giant is twelve times your height)

The house is one building with three levels. Every place a company stands in is a place its people stand in. Times
are walking times; climbs are knotted ropes, lashed ladders, a sash-cord, a ribbon.

The open floors (the Hall floor, the Carpet, the kitchen floor) are where the Lady walks by day and the Bishop
hunts by night. The Wainscot proper (the Skirting, the Button Market, the Nail, the Bell Walk, the wall-ways) runs
inside the walls, where neither can go; a company that keeps to it can cross the ground floor at any hour.
