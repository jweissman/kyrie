[Ask Nan Collier about Kit Lacey] Nan takes a scrap of paper out of her pocket and shows you: TWICE, in her own big pencil, and above it, in a careful schooled hand, a whole letter she has read so often it is coming apart at the folds. ‘Kit Lacey,’ she says. ‘Up the Stair. Dame Honor’s nephew, the heir of the Small House.’ She folds it up again. ‘He writes like a book. I write like a collier. He’s never seen me, and I’ve never been up the Stair.’ She looks at the burned grate. ‘He’ll be ashamed. When he sees. A collier, with a burned house.’
