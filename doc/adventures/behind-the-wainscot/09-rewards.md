# 09 — Rewards, progression, institutions, and what persists

honey; rope; a giant's pin for a spear (3, a fine weapon and a lever); three coin postage; six coin for all the
sulphur in Bootscrape.

## Experience

Combat (the rats, the pin-men, Dunstan Gall, the eel) gives the engine's experience. Deeds give milestones
(`endings.kyr`): the Hearth warned (100), the rats (50), Wat kept out of the trap (75), the cat belled (150), the First
Air (150), the chart written (100), the Wainscot made known (200), Dame Honor brought before her (100), the mother's
letter (100), the black-sealed letter read (75), the reply laid at the stone (75), the Clock kept going (75), the
Under-folk counted (75), the strongbox opened (100), the poison kept from her (150), the Smoking kept from the
Wainscot (150), the will lodged with the deed (200), Nan brought up the Stair (75), the Regiment's war ended (100).
The Freehold journey earns a level-one warrior some 1,100 experience in milestones, and the module's own thousand at its resolution: a second level, taken at the next sheltered rest (a bed at the Nail or the Mat).

## Companions

- **Sorrel Haddow** (bard 2): joins to write the chart; speaks for the company at the Moot; leaves it at the end, by
  her own choice, to be the Lady's scribe.
- **Lance-Corporal Fennick** (clockwork soldier): joins if asked; must be wound every day; stops where he stands if
  not. He would like to see the sea.
- Followers who come and go: Nan Collier, Dame Honor Lacey, Mr Saltash.

## Institutions that respond

| Institution | Responds to |
|---|---|
| The Little Court | Bars its door against a ratcatcher who told; opens the Small House to the colliers if spoken for, or if Kit stands up; makes itself known if urged; pays for the cat; lights the Small House for her |
| The Post | Enters you; keeps the letter; is shamed by a broken seal (DELIVERED, OPENED in red); finds its first page and enters the Under-folk |
| The Winders | Split by the strike; one stops the clock; a trap in their chapel; wind it on Sundays after, or stop it at the minute she died |
| The Keys | Play to the end; play under her deathbed |
| The Jarmen | Bar the Pantry; pay for rats; plot; are called off |
| The Tapwardens | Give the colliers water for a reason that holds; are flooded or cheer |
| The Under-folk | Counted, given water, and ferry you; or not |
| The Tin Regiment | Wound, run down, marched, or stood down |
| Bootscrape | Its innkeeper offers lofts and barns; its chandler refuses Pyecroft; its notary proves a will in the county court |
| The Hollins Stone Concern | Takes the house down, or goes home to Wendle on the carrier |

## What persists

- **In the house**, after her death in a world (chapters and observations in `after.kyr`): a Freehold Hollins with
  open shutters, the chart framed in the Hall with SO YOU DO in her hand, a green ladder at the step marked THE WAINSCOT.
  FREE, a letter to MY DEAR HONOR read aloud monthly in the Small House; a Hollins in scaffolding with the Skirting
  empty and GONE TO BOOTSCRAPE chalked on the flags; a Hollins dark with its letterbox nailed shut and a yellow
  tidemark along every skirting; a cold Hollins, people still going in and out of the letterbox.
- **In the world**: the notice in the country next to it goes stale; whoever keeps travellers there hears the company
  out and puts the notice in a drawer instead of the fire. The Freehold is "entered in the rolls of the county".
- **What is not yet persisted outward**: no other module reads `befell(hollins_freehold)`, because the canonical
  world events of Mission 18 (`data/rules/canon.kyr`) are not on main (friction.md, F10).
