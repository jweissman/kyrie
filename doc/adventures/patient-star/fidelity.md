# The Patient Star: creative fidelity specification

Written before any Kyr was written, as the reference the implementation is judged against. The source is *The
Patient Star* (eight tabs: Overview, The Land and Its Laws, Peoples and History, The Uplands in Year 380, The
Undersun, The Corbel and the Concord, The Keeping and the Masonry, Ithmar and the Deep Wonders), conceived without
reference to Kyr.

## What makes the campaign memorable

1. **One physical law, everywhere.** Direct light from the Late Sun carries *the stilling*. Silica it passes stops
   and becomes stillglass; water and flesh slow; iron sheds it as heat; reflection strips it. Everything else (the
   Clear Fields, the Holdfolk's mercy, Quell's windowless walls, the price of felt, the Seridane mirrors, the
   held king) follows from those four laws. The setting is a consequence engine, not a list of wonders.
2. **Being slow is the danger, not being hurt.** Dose does no harm. A slowed person cannot run, cannot follow a
   conversation, and is eaten by wolves who have learned to wait at the cord.
3. **The held are alive.** People laid in the light breathe once an hour or once a year. Visiting them is ordinary
   village life. Cutting the light kills them. The whole plateau's politics turns on that.
4. **History is physically present and mis-read.** The holding prayer is a granary label; the Waking Ward walls
   were written in the dark by the held who woke when the light was cut; the Annals' figures are lies. Truth is
   reached by putting two observations together, never by one inscription.
5. **The city is lit by mirrors whose aim is failing.** The lamp-chambers' cranks move light from one gallery to
   another; the gardens walk; the water in the Neck falls a hand a month. The geography changes with time.
6. **People with circumstances, not quest-givers.** Dagny wants Garret back and will forgive a debt for it; a
   crewmate wants the jars more than the man; Bren will not wake the held without their word; Ilis knows a truth
   she has not said; Saro never warms.

## The first slice

*Written first, for the opening (Hold One Season). The supermodule that followed covers all five tiers; its review
is at the end.*

The opening weeks of the *indebted cutter* origin (The Uplands in Year 380, "Player origins"): Quell, the West
Gallery and the Neck, the Shallows and the Granaries of Tamaun, the Outfall, and Ennick with its margin and the
holders' cord. About thirty rooms. Garret, the protagonist's uncle, has been held in an open granary vault for
eleven months.

### My additions (necessary, not in the source)

- **A stair between the Shallows and the Granaries.** The source says the levels connect but not where. I add a
  Seridane service stair by the Plate Hall.
- **The shutter cord.** The source puts the shutter's pulley in the vault ceiling. So that a shutter can be closed
  without standing in the beam, its cord is tied off on a cleat at the doorway. Whoever reads the room sees it.
- **Garret's crewmate.** The source says one crew member wants to leave him and take the jars. I name her Ness
  Oakum and give her a reason: the jars are dust, but intact umber-glazed jars sell for 3 crowns each to collectors.
- **The Mutual's yard dog**, Grit, as the ordinary test of a dog.
- **Which galleries the lamp-chamber serves.** The source says three or four. I make it three: the north stretch of
  the Mirror Road (a garden), the south stretch (a garden dying), and the Bunks.
- **A leak in the Mirror Road.** One cracked plaster seam throws a blade of direct light across the way to the
  stair. The source makes leaks "the commonest hazard in the city".

### Must not become

- A flag that says *dosed* with prose about slowness: dose must change what the slowed person can do and how long
  it takes.
- A dialogue menu that announces the prayer's meaning: the connection must be made from two things the player saw
  or heard.
- A "rescue Garret" button: Garret must be released by the laws (shutter, beam, felt, being led), in more than one
  way.
- A scripted betrayal: the crew's descent and Ness's choice must happen on the world's clock whether or not the
  player is there.

## Approaches the world must answer

| Approach | What should happen |
|---|---|
| **Canonical.** Take Dagny's offer, pass the chain-gate with a licence or a bribe, go down the West Gallery and the Neck, find Garret in the open vault, pull the shutter cord from the doorway | The beam goes off; Garret comes round over hours, hungry and confused, thinking it is last autumn; he can be walked out; Dagny halves the debt |
| **Into the beam.** Walk into the vault and drag him out | Whoever does it is dosed in the beam (much less in felt); Garret, out of the beam, recovers the same way; a rescuer who stays too long cannot leave unaided |
| **The Outfall.** Learn of it at Ennick, crawl up from the Weeping Wall | Closed by the flood at first; open once the flood falls in autumn; 8 hours in the wet; arrives at the Flood Line below the Granary Ring |
| **Open the Sluice** to drain the Flood Line early | The Outfall clears in time; Ennick's lower road washes out; Quell's north well drops; people in both places say so |
| **Saro.** Bring Saro, who does not slow, to fetch him | She walks into the light unaffected; she will do it if asked, but she wants to go further down |
| **Leave him held** and take the jars, as Ness wants | Garret stays held; Dagny does not forgive the debt; Ness sells the jars; the Bunks know |
| **Turn the lamp-chamber mirror** (not a solution, a mistake or a lever) | One garden darkens, another gallery lights; the Bunks crew notice their room has gone dark and say so |
| **Do nothing** | Dagny's crew goes down on its own; Ness and the others reach the vault and argue; within about two weeks of the start they bring Garret up, or Ness brings up the jars |

## After the supermodule

The whole campaign, Tiers I to V, was then written in the same module: 139 rooms on three planes. Measured against
the campaign's own list of what makes it memorable (above) and its wonders, puzzles and endings tabs:

### Preserved, and played

- **The four laws**, as two shared laws: dose by place, way and carried Splinter, felt and the Veil, recovery, half
  pace, hobbling, holding; and kept moments that a touch gives time.
- **History as evidence.** The hour fell (the Old Landing calendar in winter, or the Coverer debate plate); Selane
  was murdered (the Wall of the Stair, or the Keeping); the held feed the Masonry (the Keystone Gallery, or the
  Interlocutor); the prayer is a label; the Breach was the Split Ear and Bren. Each is inferred, or testified only to
  someone who has earned it, and each is a key: the Way's gates, the Ward's questions, the Concord's evidence.
- **The Undersun's five rules**: a room is a moment; touch gives time; speech is laid (whispering, the riot, the
  Interlocutor speaking with what was said); course-light doses; the name is the plan (Seridane Reach mapped by the
  Long Hour, the Listeners' Reach by Teodar's count, the Crown by the Keeping's new moons or Lirem's astrolabe).
- **Puzzles from the compendium**: sounding locks (three misses jam), the Steam Stair's cycle, Grammar doors (right
  syllable, trowel, or a wrong one that drops you into the floor's moment), the Burning Wood, the Fall, the Quarried
  name, the Fair door, the Keystone Gallery, the Silent Reach (rope), the Lintel (syllable or stance), the upright
  sea, the Long Hail, the rail-courses, the Way's Door, the Stair That Counts, the Gallery, the Ward, lost in time
  without the Tally, air-reckoning, holding on.
- **Wonders**: Ithmar (the Keeping's door, memory struck per step, exits anywhere), the Splinter, the trowel (traded
  for a memory), Lirem's astrolabe and its gong, the Veil, the Tally (lent for a true reason), Amaru's seal, the
  angle-books, quarried moments carried back.
- **Creatures**: the Laid, Masons, the Delver, Lintels, gleaners, rime-bells, archivores.
- **The long clock**: the Corbel descends, docks, the Tip drills, the Keeping comes unbound; Quell's 370s quarried;
  a Second Covering if the Concord votes it.
- **Endings**: seat, rebind (the binder set in the jewel), exile (held an hour and fall with it, stepped away with the
  jewel at three memories, or Saro holding on), bargain at the Corbel or at the Heart, covered, laid, set in the
  present.

### Compromised

- **Slowness** is half pace or hobbled, announced as overloading; conversation with a king at a sixth of a man's
  speed takes no time at all.
- **The Keeping's weeks** are real in the world's clock and cost real seconds of the player's.
- **Light and air on the way** are paid on arrival, as a property of each way.
- **Saro holding the Heart, and the binder** leave the company by the burial resolver: there is no other way for a
  companion to go.
- **The Interlocutor** is a body in a room that counts conversations; the Offers' returned moments are descriptions,
  not people who could be brought home.
- **The Concord** is six seats, three motions, four kinds of evidence and one vote, not five weekly sessions.
- **Duration**: grinding the drum-maker's wife out of the glass (twenty days in the source) is three minutes.

### Not represented

- Faction clocks beyond the crew, Amaru, Aske's carrying, the quarry, Halu and the Corbel: the Office's raid on the
  Bunks, the Tovash leaving in Frost, the Counting, the blood-price, Pell, Maddo Fenn, Ilis's last carry.
- The Splinter speaking a syllable each new moon and laying courses; the Remembrancer's Bell; traded moments
  (lightning, gale, silence); kept water; the Plumb-weight; the Course-glass lantern; the Courses' 3 cm a day growth
  through Tamaun's passages; Saro's tenfold speed at the Tip.
- The Keeping's moments playing backward as you walk inward (described, not modelled).
- None of these was abandoned for want of Kyr; each is another thing with states, a person with a pursuit or a rule.
  They were left for time.
