# The small adventures, and how they touch

*Twenty-three of the revised design's twenty-four are built (the bell-ringers' wager was cut: it was a clock gag
the Kindling did not need). Nine owe nothing to the books' waking. Each has a person who wants something, a
complication, more than one way through, and something it leaves in the city that is visible later.*

## How they touch

The rule was: *not "secretly about books", but "the city is one place"*.

- **Material threads.** The sluice (#2) runs the mills that make the paper Doble needs on day 4 for the Pleas (#6),
  which go to press if the Ordinance (#19) fails, which turns on the ballad (#5) and the Court's ruling (#4). The
  reed-ash (#22) is how anyone gets the ink to read inward (#13).
- **People threads.** Coddy (#2) knows the dry way through the reeds (#22). The Almanack (#1) knows who takes the
  Provost's cheese (#24), which is the key to the Chained Room (#15), and it knows where Jonquil lives (#10). Gil
  (#4) is the witness who wins the case. Wat (#17) is how Corvell's mystery (#9) points at Crake.
- **Pieces of the Colophon** are scattered across adventures that are about other things: the skipping rhyme (bridge
  children), the Ninth Plea (the Court clerk, for a coin), the variant (#8), the codex (#13), the Almanack's waking,
  the Canon (#11). Two pieces bring the Archivist's whole account.
- **Levers on the Parliament** come out of the city: the ruling (#4) moves the Roll; Jonquil's letter (#10) moves the
  Housebook; the Effusions (#7) move the Canto; the cure (#18) moves the Physick; reading to the bridge children
  moves the Primer; Crake's green ledger (#9) moves the Catalogue.
- **Levers on Rhule** come out of the city too: the cry at a Thursday burning, the Almanack's kept secret, the
  Court's ruling, the cure, the Physick's face.
- **Nine stand alone**: the heron, the cheese, the disputation, the roof, the overdue fine, the ferry, the sluice,
  the Awls, the reed-ash. They have consequences, but none of them is the plot in disguise.

## What happens if you ignore them

The city moves without the company: Thursday burnings happen; the bestiary is burned on day 5 if nobody quiets it;
the Ordinance is voted at noon on day 5; the Awls come on the fourth night; the Kindling is lit at twenty hundred on
day 9 by whoever Rhule has become; and the Parliament, unaddressed, lets the Speaker's motion stand.
