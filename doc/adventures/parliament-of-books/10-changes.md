# The record of changes

*From the treatment ([00](00-treatment.md)) through the severe reading ([01](01-editorial.md)), the adaptation to Kyr
([02](02-adaptation.md)), the build, the play, and the final editorial pass. What changed, and why.*

## Treatment → revision (stage 2)

| Treatment | Revised | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 22 situations, 15 about books, 12 about *talking* books | 24, nine owing nothing to the books' waking | a city of paper still has millers who do not care whether an almanack has a soul |
| The Awls as thugs | Mott's apprentices, sent by his journeyman | a fight with a question in it |
| The Colophon told by Quarles | assembled from seven pieces scattered across unrelated errands; Quarles tells it to one who has found two | exposition spent in one conversation is wasted |
| Eleven places in the Commonplace | nine, each with a purpose; the Correctory folded into the Errata, the Draft-Fields into the Market | a list of eleven is a theme park |
| Members as positions | members with a contradiction each | a position is a mouthpiece until it cannot resolve itself |
| Six clauses × three options | four articles × three readings; positions discoverable; every move for a stated reason | a spreadsheet on the page is not a game |
| Three outcomes and "a new Colophon" | combinations, with named settlements, each changing something durable | real alternatives |
| Scholar-only gates (the Archivist's test, the codex, the variant) | rewritten so anyone can pass, scholars faster | builds should open ways, not shut them |
| The Lexicon's ninth page as a third way in | a temptation inside the Commonplace, paid for by the city | too strong as a door |
| The Seat article | folded into the Sleep | four articles are enough |

## Revision → implementation (stages 3–4)

- **The Parliament sits on Kindling eve only.** First built to sit whenever called; a company could settle the fate
  of every book in Calamy on day 2, before the city had taught it anything. The vote is now a deadline the city's
  nine days build to.
- **Rhule's arc** became a measure (`doubt`) raised by some nine things she can be shown or told, closed by a lie
  (`liedTo`), with her three ends decided at nineteen hundred on Kindling eve.
- **The Almanack's carried copy** became a page companion (`travelsWith` without enlisting): the voice goes where the
  copy goes.
- **Standing** for the stacks route: the treatment gave none. The Second Canto now sponsors a reader Crake brings,
  or one who has read aloud for an hour in the Market; a thief is caught and *corrected* into the Catalogue.
- **The bestiary's beasts** were first a fight a lone bard could not survive; now reading them home needs a lantern
  and knowing their pages, not a check, and the bond is cleared at dusk so a night there is a choice.
- **The Awls** first attacked by day (explicit temperament overrode peaceable); now peaceable until the night, with
  their own warnings.
- **Manuals** needed an engine extension (`Resolve endow`, `levelOf`; see [02](02-adaptation.md)); the ceiling was
  raised from twenty to twenty-five when a level-ten mage was refused Quillon.
- **Per-binding speech** (engine): a poll told one vote; now each member answers.
- **Placement**: first tried beside marsh country, which broke the frontier tests (only forest, marsh, mountain and
  frost generate, and marsh east was taken); now beyond the east march of a cold country.

## Stage 5–6: what playing changed

- Seen-as names for eight buildings (the Lamb, the Binders' Hall, the church, the Imprimatur, the tower, the Chained
  Room, the Provost's Lodging, Corvell House, Quennell's), each named by a reason to know it.
- New levers so the Open Door is reachable without a freed Lexicon: the Primer told of the Unwritten nobody reads;
  the Physick told of toll-gates by a traveller.
- A bound Lexicon no longer takes its seat again the next pass.
- The Censors' clause reworded after the Imprimatur's appearance leaked into it.
- The funeral anchored at the church after it cut a search across the city and closed a route.
- The back stacks' closed end described so it reads true from the Market side.
- The Wellhead's basin no longer described twice in one page.
- The Commonplace made `elsewhere` (engine): no weather, no night-voice on its roads.
- The Kindling crowd's arrival line follows the scene of the packed Ashpit instead of repeating it.
- Manuals too strong for their reader say so when first carried, instead of in a room the reader may never revisit.
- Journeys added for every settlement, every Manual, Rhule's three ends, nine builds, autoplay and placement.

## What the final pass did not change, and should

See [09-critique.md](09-critique.md): the thin middle errands, the safe Commonplace, the predictable session, the
straw readings, the Almanack's middle days, long arrival pages.

## The second pass

A harder reading ([11-second-pass.md](11-second-pass.md)) found a chamber piece without an adversary or an
underworld. Added: **the Ash** (`ash.kyr`): the Cinderfield under the Foxing, the burned in ranks, Pardoe's Index and
its Penknife; Rhule's register, her father's book among the burned, and her candle as the Index's only light; the
Lamp's taper; the Index's claim of a seat on Kindling eve, ruled in order, and four ways to answer it there; its
bargain (giving the straw *Censors' discretion* a patron); **the Year of Ash** ending. Threaded foreshadowing through
the city: the ash in lines at the Ashpit, the pepper verse, Pardoe's slab, Quarles on Pardoe, the Imprimatur's
sealed correspondence, the Catalogue on other cities' Commonplaces. The Almanack's middle scenes: where its burned
copies went; last year's almanack in the front rank. Removed the unreachable *Recall, After a Burning*. Trimmed 57
reflexive intensifiers. Tuned the Cinders after they killed a level-8 warrior.

## The third pass: the Stacks

At the commission's further request, the Library Plane made deep ([12-the-stacks.md](12-the-stacks.md)): the Stacks
behind the Reading Room (fourteen hexagons and galleries, four sealed ways onto stranger libraries); the law that
writing drifts (registers, poems, letters, names); nine books readable end to end in the Stacks and three in Calamy
that lead there (Fenwick's rejected report in the Provost's study, Hask's prospectus at Quennell's, the Stranger's
Companion at the Lamb); Dr. Fenwick, Tobias Hask, Signature XVII, the Two Books, the ladder, the lamp, the Concordance; the
murder; the spiral's added step, the Hall of Mirrors, the lending desk, the silverfish and the Bound Man; the doors to
Cthelll and the Mount of the Exalted. Threads into the Ash: Rhule can Sew the Concordance; the Concordance makes the
Index readable by anyone; the drifted register must be rewritten before the burned can be named.
