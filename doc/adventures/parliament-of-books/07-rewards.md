# Rewards

*The commission asked for high-level rewards, Manuals especially, using the game's real development mechanics. The
engine had no way to say "a book raises a quality outside a level's development", so the one engine extension in
this adventure is exactly that: `Resolve endow(member, quality, degree)`, with `levelOf(member)` for what a Manual
asks of its reader. The creature store accepts an endowed raise beyond the level's points; the ceiling for a gift is
twenty-five. Probes: `test/fixtures/probes/manual`; in Calamy: `test/parliament-of-books-rewards.test.ts`.*

## The Manuals: books that read their readers back

| Manual | Where, and what it asks | What it gives | Once? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Corday's Grammar of the Sword** | At the outer edge of the Fore-edge, past the Scholiasts (fight them, go round like a hunter, or out-cite them like a scholar), sulking. Tell him the glosses will not trouble him; he asks to go home. Studied for six hours by a reader of **level 4 or more** | **Strength +2** (the cut) *or* **Dexterity +2** (the parry), the reader's choice | once for the company; then "give me to somebody younger" |
| **Quillon's Constitution of the Mind** | The Parliament's gift, from a drawer in the chamber floor, to the reader who seals a Colophon (any Colophon). Read through a night by a reader of **level 5 or more** | **Intelligence +2** | once |
| **The Primer's last lesson** | Read Dame Tibbet's Primer aloud in the Foxing, A to Z and the Lamp prayer; then ask it to teach you. Any level | **Wisdom +1**: "to look at a thing until it is seen" | once |
| **The Lexicon's ninth page** | Strike the three chains that hold the Black Lexicon at the top of Vask's tower | **Finger of Death**, taught: the spell Oddny Vask wrote the Lexicon to teach | once; and it costs the city: the freed Lexicon votes for anything put, the Housebook will not sit with it, and Hester Rhule can no longer become the Advocate |
| **The Physick's gift** | Restore Mother Orme's burnt beds (her excised chapters back from the Imprimatur) and ask | **Cure Disease**, taught to an ally: a leaf of script on the tongue | once |

A Manual too strong for its reader says so when it is first carried ("Go and be beaten by a few people first"), and
waits; it is not lost. The Manuals are the only permanent quality gains in the adventure, and they are deliberately
expensive in the story: Corday costs an expedition into the Commonplace's wild; Quillon costs the whole Parliament.

## Other rewards

- **Coin**: Sallis pays eight for the bond quieted; Sir Gideon twenty for the truth about his library (and it costs
  Crake a raid, unless the company lies); the disputation three.
- **A shelfmark**: standing in the Commonplace, by right.
- **Companions**: the Almanack (a voice that knows every kitchen, and opens topics everywhere); Crake (a door); Coddy
  (the dry way through the reeds); Rhule, for a while, if she will come and stand before the Physick.
- **Services the city owes**: Jonah's night ferry; Quennell's subscription; the Chained Room's key; Doble's press
  running; Calam-ink.
- **Durable changes** (the real reward, mostly): a court where books plead, presses open, an Advocate of Books, a
  library with a queue, a dying man reading his family's book.

## What was cut from the treatment's list

The Lexicon's ninth page stayed, but not as a third way in: it is the one bargain in the Commonplace that pays the
company in power and charges the city for it. The Freedom of the Lyceum and the Licence of the Imprimatur became
story facts (Rhule's warrant; the Provost's key) rather than items.

## Balance, honestly

+2 to a quality at levels 4–5 is generous by the measure of a level's development (a level gives a point or two to
place); it is meant to be: a city's worth of work for one book. The ceiling of twenty-five is higher than the game's
other numbers expect; it was chosen because a level-ten mage already has twenty-one intelligence and a ceiling of
twenty would have made Quillon a refusal for the readers most likely to want it. A game master might prefer `by_one`
for Quillon in a long campaign.
