# Fair Weather — rewards, progression, institutions and what persists

## Experience

Kyrie gives experience for what is resolved, found out, made and decided, never for what is killed. Fair Weather's
milestones (`growth.kyr`), 5,550 in all:

| Milestone | Worth |
|---|---|
| learned the tide of the Reach (the board and the withy way) | 100 |
| kept a night on the wreck | 200 |
| won the Constancy's dog (fed, or freed from the pen) | 200 |
| found the boy on the Skerry | 250 |
| saw how the Lantern went dark (her eyes, and the oil) | 250 |
| gave Loveday Kitto her eyes | 200 |
| climbed the Pulpit | 200 |
| brought Piran Penhallow to the Pulpit, or home from it | 200 |
| sang at the Gannet Supper | 100 |
| gave Ewan Marrock his wife's letter (true, a lie, or unread) | 200 |
| learned the leading mark | 100 |
| wrote up the Pilots' Rutter | 500 |
| found out who hung the light | 400 |
| brought Loveday to ask him / sent him to her | 400 / 300 |
| found Matthew Ferrar for his sister | 300 |
| gave the drowned their names | 250 |
| showed the Court a living thing | 300 |
| kept Loveday Kitto her Lantern | 200 |
| set the pilots on the Reach again | 800 |

A level-1 protagonist on the Pilots road reaches level 2 by the first night and level 3 or 4 by the end.

## Things

- **The Pilots' Rutter of the Gannet Reach** (made with Malachy from the company's soundings): carried away, a book
  that says what the company did.
- **A lead-line** (Bartle's; carried as a rope is).
- **A Penhallow climbing rope** (lent; Ebrel's, and she expects it back).
- **Spectacles** in a tin case, FOR THE OLD (one is given away, by design).
- **Matthew Ferrar's ring and letter** (given away, by design).
- **A cask of southern wine** (sold to Mother Jago at a profit).
- **Coin**: the wreck share, the wheel, the ropes, the stranger's portion, the Reeve's purse.

## Institutions that respond

- **The Wreck Court** sits whatever the company does, and its three matters are decided by who is in the loft.
- **The Wreck-House and its book**: the sale, the pen, the share, the grain going out in carts, the keys handed over.
- **The Lantern**: kept by Loveday, or given to Jack Cawse (whose light goes out on the first black night).
- **The pilot station on Inish Gall**: reopened, with a contract in Constance Ferrar's hand, a board on the harbour
  wall, a beacon lit, and a second gig under construction that two people disagree about naming.
- **The charter board**: oiled every week by Hender; NOR BOY chalked under it if the boy pointed.
- **The Strangers' Acre**: ten stones with names, or ten that say A SAILOR; an eleventh, or none.
- **Ferrar and Daughters**: the grain taken, or left for pilotage, or one part in eight left as "a debt to the
  Strangers' Acre"; an information laid in the south, or not.

## What persists, and is seen on return

All of these are observations and positions the world keeps after the ending is told (the Reach's chapter closes;
the book does not, in a generated world):

- On Fore Street: the Constancy's figurehead on the Reeve's gable beside the Lady with the Lyre (wreck); chalk dogs on
  every door (no wreck); in the church, the figurehead left by the altar rail "where people will look at her".
- On the quay: PILOTS OF THE REACH, and Jinny pretending not to be pleased; or the packet gone, and Kerensa with it.
- In the Admiral: the room loud with wreck money, or the slate with nothing written on it; Loveday by the back fire
  facing the Holm window; the yellow bird ("no wreck; a lodger").
- On the Holm: Loveday in her lamp-room, or Jack Cawse with his boots on the table and the wicks wanting trimming.
- In the Reeve's parlour: Hender waiting, having slept; or the book on the table, squared to the edge, and nobody.
- On the Skerry: GONE SOUTH. MIND THE SEALS, scratched on the door, and the coracle left to you.
- In the country behind: the innkeeper's talk changes once the Court has risen, and a company back from the Reach is
  told "A town that prays for wrecks. And it got one, and then it got you."

## Fault, which persists too

Three acts are grave and remembered: drowning the dog for the Reeve's shilling; taking his purse to say nothing;
asking Kerensa in the loft when she asked you not to. The first two make the ending A Stranger's Share, in which a
drunk in the Admiral tells a stranger about a stranger. The third, if she refuses, sends her off in the blue gig the
night before the packet, and she will not join you.
