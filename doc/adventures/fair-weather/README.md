# Fair Weather

*An independent adventure for Kyrie: a wrecking town on a tidal sea, three days after a merchantman went onto the
sands in fair weather. Conceived against the collection's own critical survey, attacked by its own editor, built in
Kyr, played, and attacked again. Spoilers throughout.*

Skeel lives by wrecks. Its houses are built of ships and every gable carries a figurehead. Its church sings IF SHIPS
MUST FOUNDER, LORD, SEND THEM TO SKEEL. Twice a day the sea goes out of the Gannet Reach and leaves nine miles of sand
with islands standing in it, and twice a day it comes back faster than a man can walk. Three nights ago the
Constancy steered for a light high on the Craigs that her master took for the Holm Lantern, and struck. At four on
the third day the Wreck Court will call her wreck, two parts to Skeel, *provided that no living thing came ashore of
her, neither man nor dog nor cat*. A black dog called Admiral is running wild on the Strand. A boy is hidden on Seal
Skerry. The Lantern was dark because someone put the sea in its oil.

Module `skeel`, in [`data/adventures/skeel`](../../../data/adventures/skeel): 17 files, about 1,690 lines of Kyr.
Played standalone from the title page (*Fair Weather*, `data/campaigns/skeel.campaign.json`), or found in a generated
world north over the march of a fen, forest or frost country, two to five countries out, where innkeepers say of it
"They say there's a dog".

## Status

**Complete and playable**: three days and a dénouement, seven endings, about seventeen smaller stories, lint clean.
Every ending is reached by a journey played through the labels a player sees.

```sh
bin/kyrie lint data/campaigns/skeel.campaign.json
bun test test/skeel.test.ts test/skeel-placed.test.ts            # 16 journeys, about four minutes
KYRIE_BROWSER=1 bun test test/skeel-browser.test.ts              # the coast road to the Strand, in Chromium
bun scripts/skeel-transcripts.ts                                  # rewrites playtest/*.md
bin/kyrie play data/campaigns/skeel.campaign.json --seed s1 --steps <steps.json>
```
