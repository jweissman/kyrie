# Fair Weather — who can do what: a build matrix

The protagonist is one person (a party of one, by default; the generated world's company otherwise), so what matters
is which of their skills open which doors. Every difficult problem in the Reach has at least two ways in, and
most have one that needs no skill at all, only time, money, attention or nerve. The table lists every check the
module makes, and the alternatives a build without that skill has.

| Problem | Skill-led way | Other ways (no skill) | Build that shines |
|---|---|---|---|
| Win the dog | **woodcraft** (sit downwind; trust +2); **tumbling**, hard (run him down — he comes, but will not look at you) | Throw him food three times (rations or a gannet egg); free him from the Reeve's pen | Ranger, monk |
| Free the penned dog | **lockwork** (pick the padlock); **brawn** (cut the cords) | Ask the Reeve; he lets it go after the Court | Thief, warrior |
| The Cawse lads on the Strand | **intimidation** (warn off; shared rules), **persuasion** (ask to let by); **combat** (three level-1 warriors with a club) | Pay them two coin, twice the Reeve's shilling; already have the dog at heel and they go home | Warrior, bard |
| Ewan and the boy | **persuasion** (tell him you are not the Reeve's) | Talk to him (Skeel; the seals); bring the dog, who finds his boy; push past him and fight a level-3 sealer (grave) | Bard; or anyone with the dog |
| Read Jacob's chart | **lore** (learns the leading mark from the margin, and that someone has wiped the dust along it) | Loveday tells it; the climbers' song keeps it; the night watch | Mage, bard |
| See through Hender | **insight** (his hands close on the pen at *Lantern*) | The facts: Nye's account with Loveday's oil, Kerensa's night, Malachy's four names, Cuddy's moved withies with the hook | Cleric, monk |
| Lie to Hender about the dog | **deception** (he pays the shilling, relieved, ashamed, and doesn't ask) | Don't take his offer | Thief |
| The Pulpit | **climbing** (the face, from the ledges) | The cliff edge (no skill; the wind strikes as you go, dexterity save); a rope (or Ebrel's lent one) gets you to the ledges | Monk |
| Work the climbers' ropes | **climbing** (eggs and coin; or a gannet at your eyes and a fall) | — (it is a job) | Monk |
| Talk Piran down | **persuasion** | Tell his mother (he will not forgive you); take him up with you | Bard |
| Carry Piran up | **brawn** | Ebrel's crew bring him up in a sling, and Ebrel does not look at you | Warrior |
| Help Kerensa with the gig | **mending** | Turn Bartle's wheel and ask her about the bar; bring her notebook back from Malachy | Cleric |
| Work the wreck | **brawn**, easy (four coin, or two for the slow) | — | Anyone |
| Force Matthew's chest | **brawn** | — (Constance's own topics name her brother; Nye knows the crew) | Warrior |
| Sing at the Gannet Supper | **performance** (the climbers like you; a rope lent) | Fail kindly; Ebrel takes up the song for you either way | Bard |
| Ask Kerensa in the loft | **persuasion**, hard (grave: if she will not, she goes) | Nye's identification; Loveday's question; the Reeve's own confession | Bard |
| Ask Constance to leave grain | **persuasion** (fair if you gave her Matthew's letter or ring; hard if not) | The Pilots | Bard; or anyone who did right by her brother |
| The flood | **constitution** saves | Learn the tide (the board, Malachy); be on high ground in the fifth hour | Anyone |
| The sinking sand | **dexterity** save | Pay Cuddy; learn the withy way from him or Malachy | Anyone |
| The Gut at the Sill | **strength** save | Wade at slack; take the Patience; ask Malachy to row you | Anyone |

## Builds walked through (on paper; three were played)

- **A warrior** (strength, brawn, intimidation): works the wreck at full share, carries Piran, forces the chest,
  cuts the pen, faces down the Cawse lads. Has no easy way into Ewan's trust except the dog, and no way to read the
  chart. A warrior's Fair Weather is the salvage and the hunt, and its best ending comes through people met on the
  sand: the dog, the boy. *Played*: the default ranger (Tamar) with low strength made the brawn checks narrowly.
- **A thief** (lockwork, stealth, deception): frees the dog from the pen at night, lies to the Reeve about drowning it
  (and is paid), and can bring a dog the Reeve thinks is dead up the loft. The cruellest comedy in the module.
  *Written, not played.*
- **A bard** (persuasion, performance, lore): talks Ewan round, talks Piran down, reads the chart, sings at the Supper,
  and is the one build that can ask Kerensa in the loft with a fair chance. The social road to the Court. *Partly
  played* (persuasion through the default; the loft question succeeded on the seed used).
- **A monk** (climbing, tumbling, insight): the Craigs are theirs, the face to the Pulpit, the ropes for eggs, the dog
  run down on the sand, the Reeve's hands watched. *Written, not played.*
- **A ranger** (woodcraft): the dog in half an hour. Otherwise this build leans on time and coin. *Played* (the
  standalone campaign's default is a ranger; the journeys mostly use the no-skill alternatives on purpose).
- **A cleric** (mending, insight, rites): the gig, the Reeve's hands. *Written, not played.*
- **A mage** (lore): the chart. Lore is otherwise little used: the weakest-served build. See the critique.

## Companions

- **Admiral**, once the Court has risen, if the company won him and he has nobody else (the boy never found): willing
  to join, as an animal at heel.
- **Kerensa Brask**, once she trusts the company (three steps of trust): willing to join, and while she travels with
  the company she rows it anywhere Jinny would sail, at no fare. If she was asked in the loft and refused, she is not.
