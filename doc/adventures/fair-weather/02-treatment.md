# Fair Weather — Stage 1: the treatment (as first conceived)

*This is the design before the editor got at it. [03-critique.md](03-critique.md) attacks it; the revised design that
was built is at the end of that document. Spoilers throughout.*

## The place

**The Gannet Reach** is a shallow bight of the western sea, nine miles across, lying beyond the coast march of a
generated country. Twice a day the sea goes out of it almost entirely, and twice a day comes back in faster than a
man can walk. At low water it is a desert of ribbed sand, cut by one deep channel (the Gut), with islands standing up
out of it; at high water it is a sea with islands in it. The people of the Reach live by the difference.

- **Skeel**, a town of four hundred on the north headland: herring, salvage, rope, eggs. Built of wreck-timber, its
  gables carry figureheads — every house has one and names it, and they are called the Wooden Saints. The church of
  **Lir Who Gives** has the Wreck Psalm painted on a board by the door ("*If ships must founder, Lord, send them to
  Skeel*"), and under it the town's charter: that what the sea brings to the Reach with *no living thing aboard,
  neither man nor dog nor cat* is wreck, two parts to the town and one to the Lord Admiral. Its inn is the **Admiral**.
  Its harbour dries at low water, so the boats sit on the mud and you walk past them.
- **The Strand**: the sands between Skeel and the islands. Walkable for about four hours of each tide, by the line of
  withies (laurel branches) the Sands-Guide sets out; off the line, the sinking sand. When the flood comes, it comes
  as a bore, a low wave over the flat, and a walker caught in the middle drowns.
- **Carrow Bank**, out on the Strand, where the *Constancy* lies broken, her stern high, her ribs open.
- **Hobb's Holm**: a tidal island with the **Lantern**, a squat tower with a coal-and-oil light kept by Loveday Kitto
  for forty-one years; a ruined hermit's cell; a well.
- **The Craigs**: the cliffs of the south arm, with the bird-ledges and the egg-climbers' camp, and the high ledge
  called **the Pulpit**.
- **Seal Skerry**: a low rock beyond the Holm, by boat only, where the grey seals haul out and **Ewan Marrock** the
  sealer lives in a turf bothy.
- **The Gut**, the deep channel that never drains, and the **Swatch**, the one place it can be waded at dead low
  water. Something large lives in the Gut.
- **Inish Gall**, beyond the Gut: three stone houses, two empty, a beacon cairn. **Old Malachy Sennen**, the last
  pilot of the Reach, lives there.

## The history (what is not explained)

Forty years ago the Reach had a company of pilots who brought ships through the channels for a fee. The Lord Admiral
bought the Reach's wreck-right from the crown and found that pilots were bad for wrecks; he did not forbid piloting,
he simply let the Lantern be kept and the pilot station's lease lapse. In one generation Skeel went from bringing
ships in to stripping them. Nobody in Skeel describes it like that. Before the pilots, there was a hermit on the Holm
who kept a fire, and before the hermit, nobody knows.

## The situation

Three nights ago, in fair weather, the *Constancy* (a merchantman out of a southern port, with grain, wine, a crate of
spectacles and a mailbag) steered for the Holm channel and struck Carrow Bank. The Lantern was dark; a light was seen
on the Craigs. By morning Skeel was aboard her with carts. The cargo is in the Wreck-House. At noon on the third day
the Wreck-Reeve, **Hender Brask**, will hold the Wreck Court in the net-loft and declare her wreck.

- The herring failed two seasons running. Without the *Constancy*'s grain Skeel's children go hungry this winter.
- **Loveday Kitto** says she fell asleep and the lamp went out. In fact her eyes are going and she did not see it
  gutter. The Reeve means to have her replaced.
- **A dog came ashore alive**: Admiral, the *Constancy*'s black water-dog, running wild on the sands. The Reeve has
  set a shilling on it "for a sheep-worrier".
- **A boy came ashore alive**: Nye Gallow, the cabin boy, fourteen, washed onto Seal Skerry clinging to a hatch, and
  hidden there by Ewan Marrock.
- On the second day **Constance Arden** comes in on the packet: the shipowner's daughter, for whom the ship was named,
  whose brother Matthew was the mate.

## Who did it

Hender Brask. The Admiral's factor wrote in the summer that the Reach's rents were two years in arrears and the
wreck-right would be leased to a Lowland salt company at Martinmas unless they were paid. Hender planned it for
weeks. On the evening of the wreck he rowed Loveday's oil out to the Holm as he does every Saturday, watered with
seawater; then he walked the Craigs path and hung a horn lantern on the Pulpit, where a light reads to a ship from
the south as the Holm. When the ship struck he covered the lantern. He walked the Strand at low water with it after,
and saw a boy on a hatch, and walked away.

## The acts

1. **Fair Weather** (day one): arrival on the coast road; the town on the wreck at low water; the stranger's share;
   the Wreck Psalm and the charter; the dog on the sands.
2. **Living Things** (days one to three): the Reach explored; the dog, the boy, the light; Constance comes; the town
   hunts the dog.
3. **The Wreck Court** (day three, noon).
4. **After** (the days following; return visits).

## The smaller stories

1. *Admiral* — catch the dog before the town does.
2. *Loveday's eyes* — find out why the Lantern went dark.
3. *The Egg-Climb* — Piran Hale, fifteen, saw a ghost-light on the Pulpit and will climb it.
4. *The Sealer's Wife* — Ewan's wife Morva "went back to the sea".
5. *The Mailbag* — letters of the drowned.
6. *The Wreck Sale* — the salvage auction on the quay.
7. *The Gut* — the thing in the channel.
8. *The Rutter* — Malachy's half-burned pilot book.
9. *The Reeve's daughter* — Kerra Brask wants to leave.

## Endings

Wreck · No Wreck · The Light (Hender exposed) · The Confession · The Pilots · Away.
