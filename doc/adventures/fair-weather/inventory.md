# Fair Weather — the smaller stories

Each is listed with its person, its place or practice, what is unknown or disputed, the approaches the game offers,
its turn, its consequence, and how it touches the Court. Three of them do not touch the Court at all, on purpose
([03-critique.md](03-critique.md), charge 1). "Tested" means a journey in `test/skeel.test.ts` plays it; "played"
means I played it by hand at the CLI and read the page.

## Livelihoods

A lone protagonist starts with eight coin and three rations, and the Reach costs money: a guide (1), Jinny's day of
sailing (3), a lead-line (2), a bed (the inn's fee). It also pays: a share for working the wreck at low water (4, or 2
for the slow), Bartle's wheel for an afternoon (2), a shift on the climbers' ropes (2 and a dozen eggs), work for your
keep at the Admiral, the wine cask resold to Mother Jago (7), the stranger's portion of the Admiral's third if the
ship is called wreck and you worked her (6), and Hender's purse (20) for silence.
