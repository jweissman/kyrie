# The Weather Drove: friction

## Creative intent
A drove of weather. The Widow is a cloud the Shales have driven nine generations; this year she walked into a
glasshouse. The adventure is about carrying one family's silence between two houses, and a last choice about which way a
living thing goes. Desert country, a rain that is also a death, a rainmaker with a howitzer, a salt-keeper whose ruin is
the town's rescue. The strongest verbs are carry, ask, keep still and decide. Story first: story.md.

## What works
- Thea's letter, "Dear D": a deduction across three places (grave, camp, gallery), with a roll-based fallback for each of
  the two stubborn people, which leaves a colder ending text.
- The call needs three people: the player stands at the back (`keep_still`) while they sing it.
- The sea is offered only to a player who has looked at her edge closely or been told. Ysolde's smoke closes the Gardens
  road, so neglecting the pans has a cost the player can read from the ridge.
- Salt: covering the heaps by hand, persuading, or kicking the fires out each leave a different fate.
- Seven journey tests: the warm road, whole rain, sea, gun fires, gun swung, the cup, smoke-blocked Gardens, kicked fires,
  persuasion without the letter.

## Passing tests versus satisfying play
Every ending but one is reached by test. No human has played it; no browser preview was run. The prose has been read
by its author only. `reach` ends in two endings and leaves the others to journeys.

## Incomplete
- `east_fires`, `fires_sniff`, `letter_obs`: reach never saw them (fires_sniff and east_fires are signs that need the
  ridge before the fires go out). Not a known bug, but unverified in play.
- No Deem table: variation is authored. The Widow's nightly mood by dice was cut; it would have been filler.
- No companion, no fight. A cloud-driving route (the Gardens road) is a single topic, not a journey.
- Turn choice with the Widow arriving by day 3 dusk: if called but never turned, the gun is disabled and the Cup does
  not fire; a company can stall there.
- The terrace sign for the grave says "Shale." too briefly; the cleric's voice carries the real reading.

## Authoring difficulties
- State and place names clash silently with shared rules (burning, shut, turned, camp); only the load error says so.
- Item kind names take an article ("a letter of Thea's"), so a possessive kind needs rewording.
- The 'heard everywhere' advisory counts every fate rule and chapter; endings cannot be anchored, so 21 advisories remain.
- A condition-teaching `Observe` and a Thing state duplicate text in one room unless one is cut by hand.

## Proposed Kyr/Deem improvements
- A `Ritual`/`Chorus` construct: several named people each contributing a part, with a single act to invoke it.
- Let fates be conditions of an ending, not a hand-chained rule ladder.
- A clock label for journeys ("dusk of day 3") and a `hold until` step.
- A Deem table of drove-songs, so a third part can differ by seed.
