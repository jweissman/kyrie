# Friction met writing The Light on Skerrow Holm

*New frictions only; those already in [friction.md](friction.md) and the other friction-*.md files are not repeated. 7 November 2026.*

1. **A worktree that starts from main lacks the branch** (as friction-wrongboat 1): `git reset --hard adventure/anthology` was the first step. *Could:* a fork flag for the base.
2. **The shell sandbox refuses compound commands** in a worktree-isolated agent (`for` loops, `printf "$x"`, `&&` chains with substitutions, heredocs fed to `python3`). Every script had to be written with the file tool and run alone. *Could:* say which constructs are refused, or allow a fixed list.
3. **Each `.kyr` file is its own `Module id "Name" { }` block** and the module lists them in `module.json`; a missing `canon` entry for the region's parent (`imbar`) fails at load with an error that names the missing place, not the missing line in the manifest. *Could:* a lint hint "add canon".
4. **`placed.json` and `module.json` differ** (the placed form imports `party` and declares `Entity party`; the standalone form owns it and a `Company`). Both had to be generated by a script (`mkmod.py`) and kept in step by hand. *Could:* one manifest with a `placed:` overlay.
5. **A reserved or canonical name is found only at load.** `arrivedAt` is already a canonical measure; redefining it failed the whole bundle. *Could:* a lint listing canonical names by prefix.
6. **`Retract at(X, ?p)` needs `?p` bound in the condition**, as friction-wrongboat 7 said for Topics; the same is true in a Rule. The error names the variable but not the Rule's condition it should have come from.
7. **A Rule that says something needs `at(party, X)` or an area anchor**; four of my rules (the walk, the stair, the lamp) failed lint for that alone. The message is accurate; it took a read of the guide to see that `at <room>` counts.
8. **`Observe … as sound when …` has its order fixed** (`as sound` before `when`); the other order parses as a condition with a stray word.
9. **A rule cannot `Assert knows`**; knowledge is `Observe … teaches`. I wrote three rules before finding that.
10. **`following` persons are not `at` anywhere.** When Seddon follows Hettie down the stair his `at` is gone, so every rule on `at(orrin, lamp_room)` goes quiet, including the one that keeps the lamp full. The night had to say that explicitly (the lamp runs out while he chases) and the spec had to pass through it.
11. **A scenery Thing at a place cannot be looked at** unless it has `Tries looking.`; the recommended lint says so for the lamp. I left it as advice (the lamp room's first sentence already says it).
12. **A fog or tide keyed to the hour needs the hour of *arrival*.** A passage takes an hour, so a party that sets off at eleven arrives at twelve; my first window (thin at twelve) meant that nobody could ever be lost. The Thing's state also updates on the tick, not on the step, so `Initially Assert state(fog, thin)` made the fog thin at 11:00 even though the rule said thick. I moved the window to one to two and the initial state to thick. *Could:* a `state at hour` form, or a way to ask the clock what hour the passage ends.
13. **A spec cannot read an ending's title from the page.** The epilogue is told but not the title (as friction-wrongboat 10), and the failing assertion printed twenty kilobytes of journey. `Endings.reached(j.campaign.world)?.title` works but is not documented in the guide. *Could:* `Journey.reached`.
14. **A warned creature's patience**: the bargain for the Caller (`answer_false`) must be `Time 0`; the note in the checklist was right and I followed it.
15. **A hazard struck on entry hits once** (`mud_lost`): there is no way to say "each watch you stay in it". I used a `lostTold` flag cleared by leaving.
16. **`Speaker piers` requires Piers to be in the room**; the action that hands Piers's notice to Seddon had to be `Speaker orrin`. Found when it would not appear.
17. **A value in a Measure with no upper bound**: `callN` rises without limit; it is read only at fixed counts (2, 3, 4), so nothing breaks, but there is no way to say "stop counting".
18. **Write-only flags are easy to leave.** Ten relations had writers and no readers after the first pass; they came from conversation answers that I then did not use at night. I removed them rather than invent readers. *Could:* the lint warns on a Relation asserted but never read.
19. **A long conversation tree is a lot of text for one flag.** Six topic trees with three answers each are eighteen `Topic`s; their effects are four. *Could:* a form for "a question and its answers" that sets one measure.
20. **Waiting is one atomic step** (friction-wrongboat 16) and the tide, the lamp and the calls all happen inside it. A spec that waits `=06:30` from 19:30 takes the whole night in one step; the route files wait in pieces where it matters.
21. **The explorer script became a test fixture.** I wrote a scratch script (`test/zz-skerrow.ts`) with `!holds`, `!at`, `!show`, `!roll`; it was how every route was found. It was deleted before the commit. *Could:* `bin/kyrie play --script routes.txt` in the tool.
22. **Voices drift when the same flag has three answers.** The tics grep found `very`, `quite`, `Aye` and `says nothing` in the first draft; "Aye" is on the anachronism list but is also the first thing a lighthouse keeper says. I cut it.

## Added after the hostile review (same day)

23. **A creature that cannot be fought has to be a Thing, and then has no species.** A `Person` with a `Species` is a combatant: a Level 4 caller was killed by one attack and the calls ran off a query that did not mention it. Moving it to a `Thing` made it untouchable but left `tide_caller` in the catalog with nothing to use it. *Could:* a non-combat `Person` that keeps a species and a lair but cannot be attacked, or a lint for a catalog species no module uses.
24. **A scenery Thing's states are a telegraph for free.** `Thing caller ... is scenery { State below/stair/here }` prints the state's text in whatever room the Thing is in; that was the whole of the "wet print on every tread" and the grey shape in the lamp room. Not documented; found by trying it.
25. **A Rule's `says` needs a literal `at(party, X)`**, so one "the thing takes someone" rule became four (cistern, stair, kitchen, lamp room). *Could:* `at(party, ?q) and at(caller, ?q)` counting as an anchor.
26. **A timer that resets whenever a condition flickers is wrong for hunts.** The caller's arrival time was reset each time the bell sent it to sea and it came back, so it never reached its delay. Timers for a hunt must be taken from the cause (`lowAt`, `outAt`: when the lamp changed), not from the hunter's arrival. *Could:* a documented `since(cond)` form that holds across flickers.
27. **`=until <condition>` is the only way to land in a window of two watches** (the interval in which the company may keep Seddon at his lamp). `=HH:MM` steps in hours; the wait is atomic enough that Hettie walks, Seddon follows and the sea decides inside one step. `=until` is in `Journey` and in no guide.
28. **A Topic's answers can set nothing, and nothing says so.** Seven of the eighteen answers had no effect when first written, the same fault the checklist names as write-only flags; the lint warned about the relations only, and only as advice.
29. **`Give X to actor` in a Topic's `On discuss`** works (book-of-faces uses it); the item must exist at a place first.
30. **A condition at one hour and an event an hour later interact with a bell on a ten-watch cycle.** A window of four watches in ten means a taking can be missed by a step. The delay had to be chosen so a silent stretch always falls inside it, and found by running the night.
31. **`j.at('caller')` is undefined while a Thing is between rooms** (Retract then Assert in one tick); a spec must `=until at(caller, X)` rather than read it after a wait.
32. **A party member "alone" is not a thing.** The company is one group at one place, so "the Caller takes whoever is alone, in the dark, off the lamp" can only be "takes one of the company in a dark room". *Could:* a split party.

## Added after the second review

33. **`Observe … near X as sound` hears only where X is.** `near` is "same place as X" (`LocalObservations.perceives`), so five calls and the knock, anchored near a Thing on the sands, were never perceived by anyone on the holm, and no probe of the lint or the lamp could show it: the page simply lacked the text. `within <area> as sound` is what an area-wide sound wants, and a spec must assert the text. *Could:* a lint for a sound whose anchor no `Person` can stand at, and a guide line on `near` vs `within`.
34. **A sound Observe is told on the step that ends while it holds, not when it begins.** A one-hour wait ends up to six watches after a call, so a window shorter than the longest step loses the call. The window must exceed the step (here seven watches). *Could:* tell an Observe once when its condition first becomes true.
35. **Quote typography in `j.page`.** A page asserts on curled quotes (`boy\u2019s`), so `toContain("boy's name")` fails with a page that plainly says it. Specs assert on a phrase without an apostrophe.
36. **A Rule with `at <area>` and `says` never speaks** when the party stands in a room inside the area; `at` means the room. (The first fix for the sounds was five such Rules, and they were silent.) Area anchoring is for `Observe … within`.
37. **A cost on a repeatable action needs a gate as well as a price.** `ring_bell` was free and repeatable; "weary an hour" was not expressible as a cost, only as `not bellRecent()` and `Time 2`.
38. **`Take` in a dark room is a bare label** (one `Take` for whatever is there); it found the holy water only after a torch was lit. The nudge is now a label on the shelf in the room's own text.
39. **`=until dark()`** is the way to land on dusk; `=20:30` is before it on some days.

## Added after the third review

40. **`capable(actor, flame)` is not what a lit torch gives; `capable(actor, light)` is.** The torch action was authored against a capability nothing grants and was never offered. Found only by playing it. *Could:* a lint for a `capable(…, X)` where no Item or Kind provides X.
41. **A torch lit before a long wait is burnt out after it.** The spec must light it when the thing is already in the room.
42. **A Query over `at(party, X)` disjunctions is how "the rooms it can reach" is written** (`exposed()`); with `Rule … when exposed() and at(party, ?q)` it hunts the party. It works, but a `says` rule still needs its own literal room, so nine takings.
43. **A `says` on a Rule is told wherever the party is.** Narration written as if the party stood in the room (a woman getting up from the table) is wrong from the lamp room. The fix is to write the told text as what carries (a voice, a door, a step) and put the sight in an `Observe at <room>`. *Could:* an optional room for a `says`, told only if the party is there.
44. **A measure cap can hide an action.** `fill_lamp` needs `oil < 10`; two fills in a row from low oil are fine, from nine are not, and the spec step was "not offered". Sensible, but nothing says why a button is gone.
45. **`=+N` and `=until`** are the two wait forms a spec has besides `=HH:MM`; `=+N` counts watches and `=until <condition>` steps one watch at a time.
