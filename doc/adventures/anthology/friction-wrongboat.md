# Friction met writing The Wrong Boat

*New frictions only; those already in [friction.md](friction.md) and [friction-muster.md](friction-muster.md) are not repeated. 7 November 2026.*

1. **A worktree that starts from main lacks the branch.** The fork's worktree began at main's tip and `adventure/anthology` had to be reset onto; the brief said "branch from current adventure/anthology" but the tool starts elsewhere. *Could:* a fork flag for the base ref.
2. **A pursuit that must start late needs a start time of its own.** There is no "wait N watches" in a `Pursuit`; each stage of Bess is a `when since(N)` clause on a measure the module has to set (`t0`), and the highest priority whose time has come wins, which means a late pursuit can *skip* a stage. *Could:* `Pursuit … after N` or a leg list.
3. **A pursuer's speed is the passages' times.** Bess crosses a five-hour road in one hop and is "at" the start until she arrives. A company cannot see her on the road, only learn of her. *Could:* a person in transit as a place the company can see from (a road room with sight-lines).
4. **A closed way's text appears every time the room is described** (circus friction 5 again): five gangplank passages with reasons made the deck read like a timetable; the fix was to give them none. *Could:* closed-way text only when asked.
5. **A scenery State needs `Initially Assert state(...)`** (friction 7 again): the mist read nothing until asserted.
6. **A territorial pair on a path meets the company wherever it crosses a room.** The lads, walking to the weir by the shortest road, ran into the company at the fen landing; the fix was a second, longer land way (`back_road`) that is shorter *for them*. *Could:* a person's route preference or a `Uses go avoiding …`.
7. **A rule in a Topic's `On discuss` cannot retract a variable-bound `at`** (`Retract at(bess, ?w)` is refused). Hence "she goes the wrong way" is expressed as a flag, not a move.
8. **Race names**: a dwarf cannot be a thief (the lint says so only after load); halflings are not playable; the scaffolded default party needed repairing.
9. **Name collisions** (Garrity, Teague, Quill, Thwaite, Hebden, Mudge) were found by the corpus-wide name lint one batch at a time.
10. **Endings say nothing of their titles in the page**: the epilogue is told, the title is not, so a spec must assert on a phrase of the epilogue. *Could:* `Journey.reached(title)` on a fresh checkpoint.
11. **`over` was needed** to say that `Without Shim` outranks `Down the Weir`; the journey warned of the contest (as the guide says).
12. **A satisfied pursuit falls through to the next lower one.** A chaser who reaches the room the company is in stops "seeking" it and the engine hands her to a lower-priority itinerary, so Bess oscillated quay and road. An arrival flag (`bessArrived`) did *not* fix it: it was tied to one landing, and the first doc claimed a fix that did not ship. The fix is `not together(bess, party)` on the itinerary pursuit, plus gating it to the one case it is for (the company aboard, with no path). *Could:* `Pursuit … holds` (stay while satisfied).
13. **A pursuit with no path does nothing and does not block.** Seeking a barge room while the barge is afloat falls through quietly; that is useful but not documented, and it makes "she cannot board on the water" an accident of the planks.
14. **`been(…)` is the party's, not a person's**; a person's history needs a Relation set by a Rule.
15. **`able(person)` is false for a territorial pair on a `Warns` encounter**, so a Rule conditioned on `able(lads)` never fires. Found only by bisecting a trace.
16. **A long wait is one atomic step**: `=+60` lets a chaser board, meet and take the box inside it, so specs must wait in pieces (`=+1`, `=+6`). *Could:* a wait that stops at the first arrival, or a trace of what fired.
17. **One pursuit per room** (30 for Bess, 30 for the lads) is the only way to say "go where the party is": `seeks at(bess, at(party))` is not a form. Generated by `genhunt.py` and patched by hand. *Could:* a pursuit that seeks another person's place.
18. **A Chapter text is told at the opening of the room** and cannot depend on what the player carries; ours first said "a hamper that is not yours" before the hamper was given.
19. **Pursuit decisions are made on arrival, and a long hop commits.** A chaser who sets off on a five-hour road is committed for thirty watches, so a company that moves meanwhile is already elsewhere; Bess lags a walker by design, and the test for the walk cannot say how far.
20. **A peaceable person cannot be given a fight and a pleading line.** The lads were `territorial` with `Warns` and `Pleads`, which made the engine stage a fight the company wins by existing, after which a rule still robbed it. They are now peaceable, and the menace, the meeting and the fight are rules and an action with a check. *Could:* a `Menace` form for a person who threatens without being hostile.
21. **`able(person)` is false for a person with `Warns`**, and `tolerates` is a built-in with no way to assert it for a custom reason; the module keeps its own `ladsPaid`.
22. **A pursuit that is not in the room cannot stop a company that walks on.** The lads' menace was a meeting plus five watches in one room, and a company that kept moving never gave it five. The fix was a *passage that is closed* while they hold it (`opening when not ladsBlock()`), not a better pursuit: the hunter's reach is the engine's weakest part. *Could:* a person who blocks, or a "following" pursuit that matches the target's speed.
23. **A hunt that is evaluated once a hop commits for the whole hop**, so Bess takes thirty watches to answer a move she could see.
24. **Hiding is a Relation, and what ends it is a rule that lists rooms.** A new hiding place that is not added to `hiding_ends` silently does nothing (the ferry, the mill yard and the quay bluff were all dead for a revision).
