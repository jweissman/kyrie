# Friction met deepening the Fourth Act

*Appended to [friction.md](friction.md) (not edited). 7 November 2026.*

1. **A follower is not always where the page says.** A person marked `away` and `following` the party can be put back by `put` in a test, and a question put to the player ("Ask Sir Gervase which kinds he will take") lost its answers when the asker was relocated between the two steps. Tests re-`put` the person between question and answer; whether a real follower can drift is unverified in the browser.
2. **A rule with no `at(party, …)` in its condition fires everywhere**, again (the voyage's `aboard` and `home_from_sarn`); all rules in voyage.kyr now carry the party's place.
3. **A lone character dies to the Colonel's trumpet** (4d6 against a level-1 body). The warning is on the page for ten minutes of game time, but a test or a player who waits through it loses the party. This is intended; recorded because it is the first death in the act that a wait alone can cause.
4. **No way to condition a Person's `State` on tolerance**, so a brooder, an owlbear or a herd keeps "blocking" text after it has been bargained with (friction 3 of the anthology still stands).
5. **Creatures cannot patrol.** The tyrant's dusk walk (plain_mid at 17, plain_south at 20, home at 22), the herd's day (lake, plain, plain) and the colony's launch are rules that move a `Person` by the hour with `Retract at` and `Assert at`; the sail-wings use `offstage`. It works, and it is one rule per leg.
6. **`Chapter` conditions are not ordered.** ch_crate, ch_voyage, ch_sarn, ch_after_sarn and ch_reckoning overlap by their conditions; which heading the page shows is the engine's choice.
7. **A region inside a standing scenario can hold two more regions** (the Isle and the brig), reached by ordinary passages; a ship is a region with three rooms and two gangways opened by a berth fact. No clock-driven travel exists, so the voyage is a berth rule and a wait of eleven days (`=+1600` watches in a spec).
8. **Question-and-answer topics** (`once` question, `answering` answers) work, but an answer is offered only while its speaker is present, and the question's `when` should not read the facts the answers assert (it stays asked, but is unreadable).
9. **Local Kind ids in a holding test** are bare in a Journey's `holds` (`preserve_key`) and prefixed in a rule; mixing the two is a silent false.
10. **An unlit outdoor room**: marking `wreck_camp` `unlit` (copied from Tallow's habit) hid its items by day. Outdoor rooms are not to be marked.
11. **Prose tics**: a grep for "the size of", "slowly", "a long time", "quiet" found 40 uses across 10 files in the Act IV text; about half were cut. The register listens to itself (circus friction 25).
12. **Time and rations**: eleven days at sea use the meal rule; the voyage adds twelve rations on boarding and twelve more on the way home as the brig's biscuit. A hunger system tied to a ship's stores would be better.

## The physical climax (direction change): what the engine could not do
13. **No tide clock.** A reef that shuts on a date is a measure (`reefDay`) set at landfall and compared with `day(clock)`; three actions (`run_reef`, `run_reef_with_crewe`, `run_reef_late`) differ by it. The sea itself, a passage that opens and closes, is a `Passage … opening when` fed by a rule, not a tide.
14. **No camp under attack.** The night on the quay is a rule that starts at a day and an hour, three actions that set refuge facts, and a rule that reads them six hours (36 watches) later and either kills the bosun (`Slay mudge`) or hurts the party. Nobody is hunted turn by turn; the hunter is never on the page; there is no one in the dark to fight, and the outcome is decided by what was done before dawn.
15. **No supplies as a system.** Water is a measure drunk down once a day at seven; thirst is a hazard; the spring is a room with a refill action. Rations are the shared meal rule plus a gift of twelve at each embarkation. A hold of casks, a carry weight, a hull that leaks as water, none exist.
16. **Fever** is a die at dawn in delta rooms, a hazard two days on, and two cures that spend an item; it is not a condition that tires, slows or spreads.
17. **Endings are facts read by observations near Ada and the Colonel.** Five endings are reachable (nothing; a loss; a fiasco; a hurt Colonel; the Colonel stays) and none is a civic settlement. The engine has no ending that is a state of a company rather than the book's closing: they cannot be recorded in a ledger or a chronicle except as the facts and the milestone.
18. **The Rest's reckoning (the Steward, the Dowager, the Warrant scoring) was removed.** Its rules and topics are gone from tent.kyr; the Revels' own facts are unread by Act IV.
19. **Voices.** A boisterous bosun (Mr Mudge, who shouts), a cook (Aggie Nunn, who gives no explanations), a boy who says four words at a time (Dab): `Manner` has no loud/laconic axis, so loudness is capitals and a verb; there is no per-person length limit and no way to lint that nobody explains significance.

22. **Leaving a person `away` is a leak.** Mudge and Aggie were parked away at the landing and never fetched; `crew_aboard_home` and `home_from_sarn` now return them, and Mudge only if he lives.
23. **Colonel decides:** the three leave topics were the player's. Now four topics guarded by what he has done (`colonelHumbled()` query) and held; Billie's resignation changes her troupe post (`billie_leaves`), unless she is a field-believer who stays to answer for it.

## Round three (hostile review of the third pass)
41. **The trumpet was a trap in the wrong place.** `trumpet_found` and `trumpet_blown` were rules `at lake_shore`, but a rule's `at` anchors only its `says`; its effects are global. A party that watched the herd safely from the ridge was struck by the herd (`herd_stride`) on the next tick, wherever it stood. Both rules now carry `at(party, lake_shore)` in their `when`, the blow waits two watches (`trumpetAt`, `watch(clock)`) so the player has a turn, `stop_colonel` costs a watch (so it can be tried about twice, not rerolled for free), and `trumpet_pocketed` puts the trumpet away if the party leaves the shore. FRICTION: an `at` clause on a Rule reads as a guard and is not one.
42. **Survival clocks ran only ashore.** Water, hunger and the night were `within(party, sarn)`, and the brig is in another region, so the party aboard the Sundial neither drank nor starved. They now read `(berthS(wapshotts) or within(party, sarn))`. A failed reef run costs two casks and two rations and three hours (all five run actions are `Time 3 hours` now, not 0); the hole in the hull on the way home also costs a cask a day.
43. **Warnings anywhere.** `thirst_bites` is no longer anchored to the landing; `water_low` and `rations_low` say in the morning when a cask or the biscuit is nearly gone; `fever_warns` says Mudge has the fever a day before it bites; each bite says so. Unanchored `says` on a rule is the only way to be heard off the quay.
44. **Free rerolls closed.** `drag_colonel_in` is `Time 1` and one try (`dragTried`); the long actions had `Time` in watches (a watch is ten minutes) where they meant hours; `fill_casks` is two hours, `hunt_meat` four, `follow_runners` three, `catch_specimens` four.
45. **Billie's lines only if Billie came.** The Colonel's four answers no longer name her; her reaction is an Observe near her gated on `billieAboard`; `billieResigned` and the regard bonus are rules gated on `billieAboard`; the coat missing from its peg at home is its own observation on `billieResigned`.
46. **The night, three ways.** Cages and fire together: clean. Cages alone: Mudge's arm, no death. Fire alone: crew hurt. Meat: four rations (scarce). Bare: Mudge dies. Two flasks of lamp oil lie in the Sundial's hold. The milestone is not awarded to a party that was away (`huntAbsent`).
47. **The pack comes every night from the fifth** (`packDay`), with print-marks in the sand on the mornings of the third and fourth nights (`pack_sign`) and clicking at dusk (`pack_dusk`). Each night resets the refuge, the fire and the meat; Mudge can die only once, and a bare night after that hurts the cook (`hunt_bare_later`). Departure is still gated on a night survived or three days ashore, so a party that leaves on day three never meets it.
48. **The Colonel's quay questions are one family.** All five answers are the same label ("Ask the Colonel what he means to do now") and exclusive by construction (`colonelWouldStay()`, `colonelHumbled()`, `colonelProud()`, egg held, specimens wanted and caught); the old second label and its dead "no" variant are gone.
49. **`end_plain` was unreachable** (the reef is gated on the Colonel having decided, and every decision sets a take flag or stay), so the observation is deleted rather than kept as authored text no play can reach.
50. **`top_lake` is one way** (cliff top to lake): the climb is the only way up. A black-channel oil-flask lies in the roots as a reason to risk the delta for the fire night.
51. **Continuity:** the Warden of the journal is Osric Orme (as on the graves); Dr Thorne came with two companions to a camp of three tents and three dead; Nebuchadnezzar the third is the lion at Dunmere and in the Inventory; the crate comes for whoever signed himself Keeper, whoever won the warrant; `ch_after_sarn` no longer claims what the Colonel brought home.
52. **Lint:** the 18 "to look at" were identical before round three. Eleven were mine and are fixed (two rules anchored at a place without saying so now carry the place in their condition; six write-only flags removed or read; two Roles that claimed a place); seven remain and are the old menagerie's tame beasts, not Sarn's.
53. **Played, not staged** (`test/circus-journeys.test.ts`): the Goose Fair through the Revels to the crate; the clutch coming home unreturned (the Duchess eats the ticket book); the young carried back and the Duchess let go (the board over the beast tent); the fire night to the hurt ending; the pack on a second night; the tar-pit rescue; the colony from the ledges (past the herd, by the lake); Harrow argued with and the calf bought. Stock (water, rations, coin) is still topped up by hand in these.

(c) `scopeAll`/`scopeSmall`, `billieNeither`, `billieCageSaid`, Mumchance's ending and Honoria's wedding are still thin.
