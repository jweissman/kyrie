# Friction met writing Three Nights at Sallowfield

*New frictions only; those already in [friction.md](friction.md), [friction-muster.md](friction-muster.md) and [friction-wrongboat.md](friction-wrongboat.md) are not repeated. 7 November 2026.*

## The form: a defence

1. **The adversary has no place to be.** Wyre is the most important person in the adventure and is nowhere until noon of the third day. Everything he "does" is a set of measures (`thrust` per post), rules keyed to what his scouts could see (`sawBarricade`, `sawCut`, `sawTower`) and a flag for what the boy carried. That works, but it is a ledger and not a person: the page cannot show him moving. *Could:* a `Pursuit` with a start time (Wrong Boat friction 2), or a "seen from" relation so that an Observe can say where he is without the party being there. I did not use `Pursuit`; the directive asked for "Person pursuits and rules keyed to party actions" and only the second was built.
2. **A battle is a comparison, not a sequence.** The engine has rules and measures; there is no turn structure, no "while", no loop. The four-movement night is four rules gated on `assaultAt + n` and a per-post comparison, and nothing the player does *during* it enters it. What the player chooses is where to stand and whom to post, once. *Could:* a Rule with a repeat clause, or a `Measure` that is an accumulator the player pushes on each action.
3. **Nothing narrates to a party that is elsewhere.** A clash at the bridge is invisible to a company at the ford. `Observe … near party when …` is the workaround; it is a global line that appears in every page while its condition holds, so each report needs a time window (`watch(clock) < assaultAt + 24`) to keep from being told for ever. *Could:* a `Rule … { Tell "…". }` that speaks once on firing.
4. **No lint for an exhaustive set of rules.** The third movement had `p3_fire`, `p3_fire_saved` and `p3_quiet`, each with a condition on `overrunCount` and on *which* posts had fallen. A case where only the nave fell matched none of them, so `p3done` was never asserted, so the picked men never came, so the best-defended run on the board skipped the climax. Found only by a spec that reached dawn. *Could:* a lint that finds a family of rules sharing a trigger whose guards do not cover each other.
5. **Dice are per entity and per tick, and not forced.** `Journey.roll('pass')` forces checks; it does not touch `dice(x)`, which the clash and the casualties use. The same route gave a different clash on different seeds, and a route that held on one seed lost on another. The balance was tuned by dividing the die (`dice(x) / 20`) until a prepared company's margin exceeded the spread, which is a design decision made to suit the test. *Could:* `Journey.dice(n)` that fixes the die, or the spec naming its seed in the fixture.
6. **A casualty roll has no memory.** `dice(?v) <= 15` is checked every tick the rule is true; `casualtyDone` is a hand-made latch. Every per-person lottery needs its own latch relation, and forgetting it is silent.

## Engine and language

7. **`Temperament` decides whether a person can be spoken to.** Wyre was first `Temperament proud` (a captain should be); a person who is not peaceable has no `Talk to` and no topics, only an encounter. The captain at the standing stone had to be `Peaceable` with `Manner proud`, and his menace moved to three veterans (`picks`) who carry the `Warns`/`Comes` lines. A single character who is both the man you negotiate with and the man who kills you is not expressible.
8. **A wait does not stop at a warning.** `=+N` and `=day N HH:MM` are one atomic step; the picked men's `Warns` ("will wait 7 minutes more, no longer") scrolls past and the fight is lost inside the same wait. The spec has to stop an hour short and wait "An hour" at a time (Wrong Boat friction 16, in a new place). *Could:* a wait that stops when anything with a patience arrives.
9. **The word "watch" is a unit and a reserved noun.** `Time N` and `=+N` are in watches (ten minutes), not hours; "an hour" is `=+6`. The first waits in the explorer were off by an order of magnitude. Stated in no document I found; read from the clock.
10. **A pre-chosen clock reads from the module's day 1, 08:00.** `=day 3 12:30` is correct and not obvious: watch 312 is noon of day 3, watch 366 is 21:00, dawn (414) is 05:00 of day 4. A table of watch → clock time would have saved an afternoon.
11. **Canonical words collide silently.** `captured` redefines a canonical term (renamed `heldScout`); `service`, `shut`, `closed`, `fallen`, `turned` and `watch` were all tried and refused, one at a time. A list of the reserved words for authors would be shorter than the errors.
12. **A Measure on a place read with the wrong entity returns 0, not an error.** `!measure defence clock` printed 0 for a run in which `defence(ford_stones)` was 9. Cost a round of probes.
13. **`holds` takes a relation, not a measure.** `!holds nerve ashworth` ends the explorer with "Unknown predicate"; the same line with `!measure nerve ashworth` works. The two are not interchangeable, but the error does not say.
14. **A group-addressed relation needs the group's id; a person-addressed one needs the person's.** The villagers' ids are their surnames (`ashworth`), not their given names; `nan` raised no error in `holds`, just `false`.
15. **`able(party)` is a legal ending condition and the way to write a party wipe.** The book closes on a lost fight with no ending unless one is authored; `Ending company_fell … when p4done(party) and not able(party)` works, and the epilogue is told. Not in the guide.
16. **`Observe … near party when …` is not in the grammar notes**; it appears in liberty-of-the-ice and was found by grep. It is the main way to say something about a place the company is not in.
17. **The country `marker` in `countries.json` is an entity id** (`sallowfield__wyre`), not a label. It must exist in the module and be unique across provinces. Not documented.
18. **`Topic … answering` returns to the question.** An answer topic is offered under the question's label while the question is open and afterwards in a "Ask … again" form; the label of the second differs from the first. A spec has to ask once and take one answer.
19. **A wait inside a room repeats its description every hour.** A six-hour wait produces six copies of the room, which the spec's trace then has to strip. (Wrong Boat friction 4, for waits.)
20. **A lint advice of "write-only" fires for a Relation that is only ever asserted from a Topic.** That is the right advice. The seven I had were cut (`bluffFailed`, `kerseyAsked`, `sawFord`) or given a reader at dawn (`craggKin`, `craggKilled`, `mowbrayArms`, `pinnSent`), as the checklist says.

## Tools

21. **A worktree-isolated agent cannot run a compound command.** Pipes with variables, heredocs and `cd` chains were refused ("too complex to verify that it stays inside the worktree"), including `sed` with a variable file name. All the exploring had to be done through a script file the agent wrote with the file tool, then run with a plain command. The refusal is correct and the workaround is cheap, but the message does not say which part of the command was the problem.
22. **No way to run one test by name that finds it.** `bun test -t "held at a cost"` reported `0 pass` for a test whose title began with those words (the `-t` filter and `describe` nest). Ran the whole file instead.
23. **A spec's wait loop must know about the climax.** The helper that waits to dawn had to learn "if the picked men are offered, answer them", because the only way to reach an ending is through a choice, and `play` of a wait is blind to it.

## Craft

24. **The sum of a prepared company is lethal to a level-one party in one round.** Three level-3 veterans killed all six of the company in a single hit each. That is the point (a genuinely dangerous fight with non-combat alternatives: argue, yield the grain, warn, let by), but it means *What the Steel Said* is the default outcome of `Attack`, and the spec for it is two lines.
25. **Hard-to-balance numbers are cheaper to balance by ending than by arithmetic.** The thrust, defence and bonus numbers were set by hand, probed with a score of explorer runs, and tuned (dice /10 → /20, bridge cut +6 → +10, flood +3 → +4) until: unprepared → *Ash*, walled and evacuated → *Half Saved*, fully prepared and the company at the right post → *Held, at a Cost*. A table of the numbers by route belongs in the treatment; I have not written it.

## Found by the hostile review

26. **A Thing's state can close a way.** Drawing the flood-gate floods the ford meadow, and the passage from the ford stones to the meadow goes with it; the route back to the village is then by the mill race. Found in the explorer by the choice that stopped being offered; not obvious from the module's text.
27. **A latch rule and the rules it guards run in definition order, in one tick.** `lowes_stamp` → `lowes_told` fired before the three "he saw the wall" rules defined after them, so the snapshot was blank until the guarded rules were written first. Nothing says rule order matters; it does. *Could:* simultaneous evaluation per tick, or a documented order.
28. **A hired companion is a party member: `dead(x)` and `able(x)` are true/false of them from the hour they join.** The dawn fates told a player that the woman beside them had died in the nave. Every "did this person die" test needs its own relation (`felled`), asserted beside each `Slay`; the engine gives no way to ask "was this person killed by the story".
29. **Endings that fire at the moment of dawn hide the dawn.** The epilogue closes the book before the fate Observes can be read; the endings wait an hour (`morning`) so the fates are told. *Could:* an ending that lists the scene's Observes first.
30. **An "answer" action cannot take a parameter chosen by the player**, so "what the boy carries" is two actions (truth, lie) rather than a free message; each new content option is a new action and a new fixture label.
31. **A topic can charge the purse** (`Decrease quantity(actor, gold)` in `On discuss`), which I had not tried; the peace now costs ten coin. Worth saying in the guide.
32. **A wait cannot be stopped from the module.** A `Chapter` turning, an Observe window and a `Warns` do not end a long wait; the picked men's patience ran out inside one. The only authoring fix was to make the dangerous visitors negotiators (`Peaceable`) who *take* something if ignored, which is a better design for the night anyway but not the engine fix asked for.
33. **A `Pursuit` works, but `Journey.at` cannot see a group moved by one** (`j.at('riders')` stayed `undefined` while the page said "Two of Wyre's riders takes the ford"); a spec must read the page. A `Pursuit` for a person with `Warns` also *walks them into the company and starts the fight* (`Temperament wary` riders killed a company that waited on the east road); scouts must be `Peaceable`.
34. **`Arrives` and `Uses go`.** A pursuit lint says "give them `Uses go`" only after the pursuit exists; `Arrives at X` plus pursuits is the way to start a patrol.
35. **No `Attack` against a person who is not hostile.** Once the picked men were negotiators the generic Attack choice disappeared; "draw on them" is an authored Action with a check, and a lost check is an authored `companyBroken` flag (the engine's own party defeat is not available to a module).
36. **Generic rules over places work.** One rule over `postsite(?p)` with `count(posted(?v, ?p) and able(?v))` replaced sixteen near-identical rules; the cost was a `here(?p)` flag with enter and leave rules because "the company is at ?p" cannot be asked of the nave's three rooms in one condition.
37. **Group moves and Journey.** After a patrol moves a group, the group's `at` reads `undefined` (friction 33), and `count(... and not travelsWith(?v, party))` is how a module asks "villagers who are not with the company".
38. **A topic cannot move another person to a place a rule will read, except by `Assert at`.** Walking Voll to the granary is `Assert at(voll, granary)` in a topic's `On discuss`; there is no escort that goes at the party's pace.
39. **Rule order is a design tool.** The snapshot only worked because the guarded rules were written before the latch; the same trick (a rule placed before another) was needed for the dawn guesses. It deserves a sentence in the guide.
40. **Unfixed.** The company's hour is still only five actions; the first-night watch is one posted villager at the ford or bridge (now an hour's cost); *The Road South, by Firelight* is unspecced.
41. **Weary is the engine's and a module can read it.** `weary(party)` is asserted by `data/rules/needs.kyr` after 108 watches awake; a module may read it and charge for it, and the sleep that clears it is the camp the company already has. (It was there all along and I had not looked.)
42. **A post that can be re-asked changes a person's post.** `post_villager` is exclusive, so asking Hob to hold the ford after the rally put him at the bridge moves him; the Nobody Left spec needed that.
43. **Numbering.** This file skipped 26 in an earlier revision; the items now run 1-43 without a gap.
44. **A hostile group that retreats ("Refuge") becomes a wall.** The raiders fled the farm to the south road and waited there for the company, who must pass; a company that walked south was killed with no ending. Anything that can end a fight must be somewhere the way past does not run through, or must not be hostile; a party wipe is an ending only if the module writes `not able(party)`.
45. **Two roads to the same state.** The foul-the-grain win and the full defence both end with the village standing; to make avoidance not dominate, the avoidance had to be made to require the defence (a wall, a second work) as well as a check. A module cannot say "no ending reached without N hours of work" except as a condition on the withdrawal rule.
46. **`Journey.latest` is the page of the last play only** and is the way to assert that something was *not* told in a given hour.
