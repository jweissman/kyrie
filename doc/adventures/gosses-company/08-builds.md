# Gosse's Company — who can do what, and what it is worth

*The character-build interaction matrix, as built and as proved in play, and the rewards and what they imply. Every
check in Kyrie is a d20 for the company member best placed, plus a quality's modifier, plus a proficiency where the
skill is theirs, against easy 10, fair 13, hard 16 or formidable 19; the odds are shown before it is tried, and it may
be tried again.*

## The principle

Nothing in the week is closed to any build. Every story that a skill opens has at least one other way through: a
thing found, a person asked, coin, or the timetable. What a build changes is **which way is easy, which is offered at
all, and what the night looks like as a result**. Five choices are offered only to someone of a trade or a gift, and
each of them is a line of dialogue or a moment, not a gate:

| Only for | Choice | Where |
|---|---|---|
| a player by trade (tragedian, comedian, playwright, juggler, town crier and fourteen more of Orsino's backgrounds: `craft_stage`) | "Tell Gosse you are of the profession yourself"; go on between the scenes on the green; tell Swale "as one player to another" that the laugh and the tears are the same breath; tell Dorcas what the Widow is; play Tapwell or the Ghost as one who knows how (a better scene, and Clay's regard) | the green, the loft, the night |
| one who keeps the faith (acolyte, priest: `craft_faith`) | tell Canon Mallory the boy will be safe among the players (Ned lent, no check) | the Canon's study |
| one of the water (boatman: `craft_water`) | tell Ferriby you are of the water yourself (the watermen row) | the Water Stairs |
| a mage with *Conjure Light* prepared | throw a conjured light up through the boards onto the Ghost as he rises | under the stage or in the wing, I.2 |
| a tallyman (anyone who signed on with Tunstall) | let the angel off; lay the stick down; answer to Tunstall for the book | the grey bench |

### Builds as played

| Build | Fixture | What the week was like |
|---|---|---|
| **A bard** (Wren, the city from the road) | `city.campaign.json`, `book.json` | Every persuasion check open, most at even chances; the lore check on the Ordinances "likely"; climbing failed (the lantern), and was not needed. The Book: licensed, nineteen lines restored, the author found, Tapwell played, the Patent by Clay, Gammage and Mallory. With one nod, the Author's Bow. |
| **A soldier** (Bran, from Wending Cross; charisma +1, wisdom −1) | `warrior.campaign.json`, `liberty.json`, `famous.json` | Not offered the player's lines; Dorcas won with the old notice, not words; the climb for the bees failed (Pudding brought Patience home anyway); the trap and the hampers by main strength; the Office's men faced down in the wing (a natural 20). The Liberty: whole, 21 crowns of fines, the Patent by Clay, Gammage and Carrow. Without Clay's *Dido*, two voices and the Liberty ending. |
| **A tragedian** (Fennick, a bard of the stage) | `player.campaign.json` | On the green, "Tell Gosse you are of the profession"; in the loft, "Tell Dorcas, as one player to another", which brings her round to the Widow without a roll. |
| **A cleric** (Dori, an acolyte) | `cleric.campaign.json` | The Canon hears "one who keeps the faith" and lends Ned without a roll. |
| **A mage** (Isolde, an arcanist) | `scholar.campaign.json` | The Ordinances "likely" on intelligence alone; under the stage on the night, a conjured light makes the Ghost stand in a column of moonlight (+2 temper, Clay). |
| **A thief** (Sly, a locksmith) | `thief.campaign.json` | The bear-pen's padlock picked without a roll. |
| **A stranger** (Wren, never hired) | `tallyman.json` | Signed on with the Office; let the angel off (the Canon looks away, pink); laid the stick down on the night (eight notches spared); could not save a company nobody helped: the Company Broken. |

Low charisma is the honest test of a social comedy, and the soldier is it: the week is harder in its middle (Dorcas,
Swale and the Canon are hard persuasion checks at +1) and is carried by things found and carried. It wins the Patent,
by a different coalition, with a louder and more expensive night.

## Rewards

| Reward | For | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| **Experience** | 28 milestones worth 5,650 in all (100 to 800 each: the parcel, the Widow, Swale and Pudding mended, the licence, the author found, the Fountain, the night played, the Wheel on its feet, the Patent, the Bow …), and 1,000 more when the adventure is seen through | a thorough week is two or three levels for a single protagonist |
| **A share of the house** | one of the company, on a night the Ghost was played | coin by the size of the yard: the fuller, the taller the pile |
| **A pallet in the loft** | one of the company, all week | lodging for nothing; "nobody will take your coin" |
| **A tallyman's pay** | a tallyman, a coin a night | the Office pays its own critics |
| **Gosse's crown of paste** | whoever stood on the boards as Tapwell or the Ghost | an item like an old glossary that grants **persuasion** to its wearer, with every king Gosse ever played and the player's name in the band: the company's honour, carried into the rest of the world |
| **The Master's licence** | the Author's Bow, asked of the Master afterward | an item that grants **deception**: LICENSED, WHATEVER THE BEARER SAYS, ON ANY STAGE, IN ANY CITY; "worthless outside Pentrice, and you must never tell anyone that" |
| **The city's regard** | everything | in a generated world the chapter closes and the world goes on: the Trial's playbill comes down off the taproom wall, and the innkeeper keeps it |

Both items are kinds of the module's own (`like old_glossary granting …`), so the skill they bring is the item's, and
leaves with it. Neither is a weapon; both are things to say with. In the standalone campaign the ending follows the
verdict by ten minutes, so the crown is the only one in reach; in a generated world, where the ending closes a
chapter and not the game, the Master can be found afterward.
