# Low Water: implementation and extension report

*The final report, for whoever integrates branch `adventure/low-water` (base `main` 6aedbe2, which `main` has not moved
past) and for authors after. The adventure is Kyr. Two small, general presentation changes were made to shared code,
each with its own regression test. Everything else in this branch is the adventure, its tests, and its documents.
The first-pass branch `claude/laughing-cerf-lwk6hb` is superseded by this one.*

## 2. Changes to shared code and data

Each is general, small, and tested on a fixture of its own. Neither names anything in this adventure.

### 2.1 A way under the ground is told without the hour or the sky

* **Where.** `src/narrative/journeys.ts` (`Journeys.below`, and the telling split into `voiced`), and three lines in
  `data/voice/journey.json` (`journey-depart.underground.1–3`, `when: { shelter: underground }`).
* **What.** A journey (a way of half an hour or more) between two underground places, or by a tunnel, culvert, crawl,
  shaft or drift, is told by the underground lines alone: no *shadows are long*, no dew, no light going.
* **Why.** Low Water's culvert from the Ninth Lock to the Undercroft is half an hour long, and the page told it as
  *The shadows are long when you leave the intake culvert*. The Patient Star's ledger reported the same fault (17,
  *no dew in a tunnel*).
* **Test.** `test/underground-journey.test.ts` on `test/fixtures/probes/underground-way`; it fails without the change.
* **Risk.** Any adventure with long underground ways now hears the underground lines on them. `narration-audit` and
  `voice` pass.

### 2.2 A thing's state is said as words on the page

* **Where.** `src/presentation/player-view.ts` (`PlayerView.condition`).
* **What.** A state name that is an identifier (`wound_up`) is shown as words (`wound up`) beside the thing.
* **Why.** State names share a module's namespace, so authors are pushed to names like `chain_cut`, and the page
  showed them raw.
* **Test.** `test/state-note.test.ts` on `test/fixtures/probes/state-note`; it fails without the change.
* **Risk.** A state whose identifier was meant to show with underscores. None does.

### 2.3 Data the shared world reads

* `data/adventures/countries.json`: one entry appended.
* `web/scenarios.ts`: one entry.
* `web/bundles.generated.json`: regenerated, not edited.

No other file outside the adventure, its tests, its script and its documents is touched.

## 3. What resisted

Twenty-seven entries, by severity and kind, with what I did and the remedy I suggest, are in
[friction.md](friction.md). These are reports for the builder. None of them was fixed in shared code on this branch.
The three that matter most for the next author:

* A way's `opening when` is checked before the module's own rules (1).
* A newly hostile person falls on the company in the same instant (2).
* Nothing compares an encounter's strength with the party's (27). Playing a failed check found a one-round defeat of
  the whole company that lint could not see.

Results on the branch head:

* **Lint.** `bin/kyrie lint data/campaigns/low-water.campaign.json`: 0 errors, 0 warnings. `bun run lint` and
  `bun run typecheck`: clean.
* **Full suite.** `bun test --parallel`, without the long Deem proof: 811 pass, 33 skip, 0 fail. That is 844 tests
  across 173 files, in 403 s. The browser tests are gated, and were run on their own.
* **Transcripts.** `bun scripts/low-water-transcripts.ts` rewrites six transcripts in
  [doc/playtests/low-water-2026-10-25](../../playtests/low-water-2026-10-25): the Accord, the Open Bowl, the End of
  the Song, the Census Heard, the Account Delivered and the False Measure.

**Not journeyed.** Paying the Assayers with the cup of still water; the Auditor's reply when the Council's verdict is
not Inestimable; the noon reading in the square. These are linted, and each uses relations the journeys above play.

**Performance.** The 36 journeys take 255 s run one after another. The heaviest is the Inestimable route at 22 s: the
whole first pass, then the Lector's bell, the Mere at night, Ammerlin in the water, the stair and the Auditor, then
back down for the Hearing. Most journeys take 3 to 13 s. Lint is about 5 s. Nothing in the module scans by the watch:
the reckoning runs every 48 watches by a measure, the clocks are measures compared with `watch(clock)`, and each grace
period is a single measure.

## 5. Integrating into `main`

1. Merge the branch. Expected conflicts: `web/bundles.generated.json` (regenerate with `bun run web:build`, never
   edit it), and `web/scenarios.ts` and `data/adventures/countries.json` (each an appended entry).
2. If countries are now placed differently, or a country's party is imported differently, `placed.json` follows
   The Long Man's: it imports `party` and exports `rim_road`, the entry.
3. Run:
   * `bin/kyrie lint data/campaigns/low-water.campaign.json`;
   * `bun test test/low-water*.test.ts test/merrow-to-ammerlin.test.ts test/underground-journey.test.ts test/state-note.test.ts`;
   * the browser suite with `KYRIE_BROWSER=1` (and `KYRIE_CHROMIUM` where Playwright's own Chromium is not
     installed).
4. Nothing in `src/` or the shared catalogs depends on the adventure. The two shared changes (§2) can be taken or
   left independently of it. Without §2.1 the culvert says the shadows are long. Without §2.2 the page shows
   `wound up` as `wound_up`.
5. In a generated world, `endings.kyr` is not installed. The Bowl goes on after any of its verdicts, and the
   Auditor's climax actions still change the world, but they close no book (friction 13).
