## 2. Changes to shared code and data

Each is general, small, and tested on a fixture of its own; neither names anything of this adventure's.

### 2.1 A way under the ground is told without the hour or the sky
* **Where**: `src/narrative/journeys.ts` (`Journeys.below`, and the telling split into `voiced`); three lines in
  `data/voice/journey.json` (`journey-depart.underground.1–3`, `when: { shelter: underground }`).
* **What**: a journey (a way of half an hour or more) between two underground places, or by a tunnel, culvert,
  crawl, shaft or drift, is told by the underground lines alone: no "shadows are long", no dew, no light going.
* **Why**: Low Water's culvert from the Ninth Lock to the Undercroft is half an hour long and was told "The shadows are
  long when you leave the intake culvert". The Patient Star's ledger reported the same fault (17, *no dew in a tunnel*).
* **Test**: `test/underground-journey.test.ts` on `test/fixtures/probes/underground-way` (fails without the change).
* **Risk**: any adventure with long underground ways now hears the underground lines on them. `narration-audit` and
  `voice` pass; the full suite passed (§4).

### 2.2 A thing's state is said as words on the page
* **Where**: `src/presentation/player-view.ts` (`PlayerView.condition`).
* **What**: a state's name that is an identifier (`wound_up`) is said as words (`wound up`) beside the thing.
* **Why**: state names share a module's namespace with its relations and the shared rules' (`shut` is taken; two
  things' `broken` collide), so authors are pushed to names like `chain_cut`, and the page showed them raw.
* **Test**: `test/state-note.test.ts` on `test/fixtures/probes/state-note` (fails without the change).
* **Risk**: a state whose identifier was meant to show with underscores. None does.

### 2.3 Data the shared world reads
* `data/adventures/countries.json`: one entry appended. A generated world whose first region is marsh, forest or frost
  now names *the Bowl of Ammerlin* beyond its south march (the Long Man keeps the north of mountain and forest).
* `web/scenarios.ts`: one entry. `web/bundles.generated.json`: regenerated, not edited.

## 3. What resisted, and what was done

Classified as the brief asks. Each was met in Low Water and is reproducible from its files.

| # | Finding | Kind | What I did | Remedy for Kyr |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | An `Observe … when not knows(f) … teaches f` stops holding as it teaches, and the page then shows the discovery's journal text instead of the scene | Authoring problem (silent) | Rules with `at room when at(party, room)` and `says … teaches` | Show a teaching observation's words in the moment it teaches; or lint the pattern |
| 2 | A place's observations are all told again on every arrival | Presentation limitation | `familiar(place)`, asserted on leaving, and a short `_again` observation for each transit room | A place's standing description told in full once and briefly after, by the narrator |
| 3 | State names share the module namespace (with relations and the shared rules) | Authoring problem | Prefixed names; then words that read as tags (`wound_up`), with §2.2 | Scope state names to their thing (the open finding *States share the entity namespace*) |
| 4 | `Buys` needs the buyer to have coin, and people have none unless given it | Authoring problem (silent: the choice simply is not offered) | `Initially Set quantity(halm, gold) to 120` | Lint a buyer with no purse; or give `Buys` a purse |
| 5 | `Sells` of a catalog item by a person who is not a trader did not appear | Authoring problem (silent) | An action that sells it and says the price | Document who may sell; lint a `Sells` that can never be offered |
| 6 | `carries(a, x) or carries(a, y)` in a requirement was never offered (both are the query `carries`, each binding `?m`) | Engine limitation | Two actions | Rename a query's internal variables apart when it is used twice in one predicate |
| 7 | A level must be inside a site or building; a site's name then labels every way into it ("Into the terraces of Ammerlin") | Authoring problem | No levels for terraces; *steps* between them | Let a settlement hold levels; or name the way by the room |
| 8 | Lint calls *the engine* in prose implementation talk | Authoring problem | The machine is the Reckoner | A per-module allowance, or a narrower pattern |
| 9 | A thing's own view shows its state, not the place's observations: a gate worked, the gauges unseen | Presentation limitation | Measures in each gate's state text (`{level(dyers)}`) | Show a thing's view the place lines that its change altered |
| 10 | No numbers in words | Authoring problem | One observation per value (nine wheels, nine counts) | `{ninths(reckoner) as words}` |
| 11 | No modulo or day-of-week; a nightly event needs a latch and a reset | Local mechanic | `sungTonight` set at seven, cleared at three | `Every day at 19` |
| 12 | A rule fires every watch its condition holds, and once per pass after a short action | Local mechanic | Counters measured against `watch(clock)`, not incremented per pass (the Equals' grace) | Already documented; worth a lint for counters incremented with no watch in the condition |
| 13 | A companion's topics are filed under them, and a thing's changes under the thing, on the page | Not a fault | Journeys in the browser go *the sump gate › Wind up* | — |
| 14 | An ending closes the book; a placed country cannot have one | Engine contract | Endings in `endings.kyr`, which only the standalone campaign installs; in a world the Bowl goes on | An ending a placed country can reach without closing the world (a chapter end) |
| 15 | Absolute watches shift when a module is installed late, measures compared with the clock too | Works as documented | Every timer is a measure set `Initially` or a literal compared with `watch(clock)` | — |
| 16 | A way's `opening when` is checked before the module's own rules, so a rule that makes it true in the same pass leaves the exit missing until the next pass | Engine limitation (silent) | Opened on the underlying fact (`carries`, `tried`) rather than on a relation a rule asserts | Evaluate openings after module rules, or once more at the end of a pass |
| 17 | `carries(group, x)` reads members' packs only; `Assert keeps(group, x)` is accepted and then not carried | Authoring problem (silent) | The heart is set down at the company's feet to be taken | Lint `keeps` on a group, or file it with a member |

## 5. Integrating into `main`

1. Merge the branch. Expected conflicts: `web/bundles.generated.json` (regenerate with `bun run web:build`, never edit),
   `web/scenarios.ts` and `data/adventures/countries.json` (each an appended entry).
2. If the main builder's Mission 16 has changed how countries are placed or how a country's party is imported,
   `placed.json` follows The Long Man's (imports `party`; exports `rim_road`, the entry).
3. Run `bin/kyrie lint data/campaigns/low-water.campaign.json`, `bun test test/low-water*.test.ts
   test/merrow-to-ammerlin.test.ts test/underground-journey.test.ts test/state-note.test.ts`, and the browser suite
   with `KYRIE_BROWSER=1` (and `KYRIE_CHROMIUM` where Playwright's own Chromium is not installed).
4. Nothing in `src/` or the shared catalogs depends on the adventure. The two shared changes (§2) can be taken or left
   independently of it; without §2.1 the culvert says the shadows are long, and without §2.2 the page says `wound up`
   as `wound_up`.
