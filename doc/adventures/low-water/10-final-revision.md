## The governing form: double entry

The theme of the work (accounting as a moral act) and its imagery (mirrors, doubles, the copied city) are now one
design. A ledger entered twice, once where a figure left and once where it arrived, is a mirror. The Interval is the
other side of the Bowl's book.

## Doubles, multiplied through the cast

* **Isaura and Dov.** The Office and the Ward, one on each side of the counter. Dov's grandmother turned the mirrors
  to the wall; Isaura turned them back, *unscientific*.
* **Orr and Aun.** The Cantor who cut the Fourth and the Lector who died keeping it. Orr learns it, at the empty
  bench with Aun's carved face (*I was so proud of it*).
* **Halm and Pell.** The Reader and the Second Reader. Halm learns he was *his postman, not his correspondent*, and
  offers to answer Pell with a false footnote. It is his own small double of Isaura's lie: *the most careful thing
  I've ever written. A lie has to be.*
* **The Assayers and the Equals.** Glass scales above, bronze balance below, both keeping the account level. The
  Office's lintel carries the same scales.
* **The company and its doubles.**

## The Auditor, rewritten

* No more capitals. Capitals in Low Water are for cut letters only: the motto, the arch, the dial, the dome.
* It speaks in the Office's register: *Received. That is reasonable. You asked, and it is the figure.*
* It explains less. The cosmology is shown once, in the model of the nine circles. Halm guesses at it once.
  The Auditor confirms it in passing.
* It **asks**: *What is the Ward worth?* The player can answer that the Ward has no figure. *That is a figure. It is
  the largest one there is… Inestimable.* The answer makes the account harder to close (approach −2).
* The villain's line about Pell is gone. In its place: *He will not like the answer. It is me. It is eight more of
  me.*
* Aun, through its voice: *She was not afraid of me… you can open anything that is afraid.*
* The False Measure's last line, *That is not what I counted*, is now said in the voice of her grandmother's Office.

## Foreshadowing

* **Mere-tears.** Green impact glass, a copper a handful on the north road.
* **Halm on the Print.** *Not fire from below.* The circles hall now ends: *The green glass in the Rim's dust is what
  splashed.*
* **The Ward's mirrors**, turned to the wall at night, *so the water can't learn your face*. Annet Kettle's mother
  stopped doing it.
* **At dusk your reflection in the Mere lifts its hand a moment late**, before anyone has seen the Reflected.
* **Frost on the fountain** on warm nights, once the approach has begun.
* **Broad flat prints on the lock steps** in the mornings. Piet sands them and writes them down.
* **The Lector's bench** in the real Sounding, with the face the Auditor copied. The choir touch it for luck. This
  also repairs a broken reference from the second pass.

## Danger, with ways around it

**The doubles** (`interval.kyr`). Linger among the Reflected in the Ward in the water, and copies of the company
walk up into the Bowl. After dark they stand on the Ward landing, and one watch after they notice you, they come:
four of them, your own shapes, hostile. There are five answers:

* fight them;
* sing the Seventh (a check);
* ring the Lector's bell;
* have Isaura measure them, calling every figure true until they hear that they are the wrong way round (*They can
  copy anything. They can't check it*);
* or take Dov's advice and stay off the landing after dark.

**The Assayers** (`citadel.kyr`). Three glass figures, level 4, who keep the account level. Break a jar in the hall of
receipts and they come, beams tipped, giving you two watches. There are four answers:

* fight them;
* set a weight-stone on the shelf where the jar stood;
* set the cup of still water there (*in a moment there is a face in it, looking down: yours*);
* or have Isaura enter the jar as a debit, honestly, on the right side of the book. *That's a real entry. I'll have
  to pay it.*

**Annet Kettle's jar.** If the diggers drowned, her jar is the one that can be broken. She stands up in the shape of
a girl, the right way round, and runs away down the stair. Afterwards a basket of river flowers waits on the
landing's lowest step.

**After the count closes.**

* By day, there is frost in the wet prints going down the landing.
* Isaura has counted them going: *one of them was walking the way you walk with a lamp in wind*.
* Dov saw the light, and tells you to go after it with the bell.
* A company without the bell can **sing the Seventh at the one with cupped hands** (a hard check; failure costs the
  cold).

## The Mere stands up, quietly

The giant woman of water is gone. In one night the Mere comes back to the fourth step of the Ward landing, where
Isaura learned to swim. In the reflection every lamp in the Ward is lit, and people are standing on the landing who
are not there. By morning it has gone back down. Isaura measures the tide-mark and underlines it.

The simile *the way you would practise a word in a language you meant to speak well* is now used once, for Aun's
image.

## Depth beyond the story

* **Shelves of years** in the hall of receipts: frosted jars from the winter the Mere froze; cracked jars from the Long
  Silence, faces looking up; a low shelf of very young faces.
* **The eight dark pins**, each drawn over the place its heart went in: a sea with a ring of boats, a range with a
  white scar, a wood with a road that goes round and round, a town of bells, a field cut short with one figure in it.
  Three are struck through like settled entries. Wherever the hearts went in, someone built something over them to
  count.
* **Pell's letters**: twenty years of them, wet and unrotted beside the glass chair. Every one is addressed to a blank
  space, because he never knew what to call it.

## Evidence

Three more journeys, making 28 in `test/low-water.test.ts`:

1. The doubles: loosed, noticed, the fight begun, and laid by Isaura's measuring.
2. Annet's jar: broken, the Assayers roused, and Isaura's debit.
3. The echoes in Act I: the Check, double entry, the Lector's bench.

Not journeyed (all linted): the weight-stone and cup payments, singing the doubles down, and singing for the heart.
They use the same relations as the journeyed routes.

## What I would still change

* **Act IV.** The Hearing still does not hear about the Auditor. A Council that votes on the Ward's figure, after the
  Auditor has asked for it, is the scene the work is missing.
* **The Collegium in Imbar.** Pell's other instruments (*MERROW NEXT*, the field cut short among the dark pins) wait
  for The Long Man to answer them.
* **Halm's false footnote has no consequence yet.** It should slow Pell's work in another country.
