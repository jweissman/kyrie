# A Winter in Carth

*A metropolitan region for Kyrie: a satire of bought penance, with a cold farm in the middle of it and a heresy trial
at the end. Spoilers throughout.*

On the Saturday before the Fast a beadle with a frozen foot offers a stranger two shillings to walk his prisoner the
last forty miles to Gaunt. Gaunt is a city where any penance may be done by proxy, by the day, at the Stand. The
prisoner is Ninian Hern, barefoot, ill and funny, for thirty years the best penitent-for-hire in the city. He is to be
tried on Ash Day for saying that nobody in Gaunt has ever been sorry for anyone. On the road he asks three questions.
On the fell the snow comes in, and the only roof is Carth: a farm keeping a forty-night cold vigil, for the rent, for
a sin somebody in the city paid to have kept. Inside are a coughing child, a dead man in the byre, and the Ash-reeve,
who comes at three without knocking and puts his bare hand in the ash. Then three days in Gaunt, and the Consistory
at noon on Ash Day.

The commission's two seeds are both in it. *A Winter in Carth* is the night at the farm. *The Road to Orm* is the walk
with Hern, escorting a heretic to his trial; the city is called Gaunt here.

## Compatibility

Gaunt reads nothing from another adventure, and no other adventure reads from it; no engine code or shared rule was
changed. It is appended last to `countries.json`, so every existing country keeps its place in a generated world, and
it is offered beside frost and forest countries, not mountains, so the frontier's placements are unchanged
(`test/frontier.test.ts`). Its people and places have names of their own (`test/adventure-names.test.ts`).
