# A Winter in Carth: the record of changes

*What changed between the revised design (03) and adaptation (04) and the module as built, and what Stage 6's
reading changed. Documents 01–04 are left as written; where they name something differently, this is the key.*

## Renamed

| In 01–04 | In the module | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Orm (the city, in the seed) | **Gaunt** | the city needed a name of its own; *Orm* belongs to the seed, and Hessel's walk already used the road to Orm |
| the North Gate | **the Fell Gate** | it is the gate the fell road comes in by, and the name says where the company has been |
| Canon Pellow | **Canon Hamo Laverock** (ids stay `pellow`) | surname taken elsewhere (`test/adventure-names.test.ts`) |
| Mother Tunstall | **Mother Sugden** (id `tunstall`) | likewise |
| the Schools; the Close; the Shambles | **the Divinity Schools; the Close of Saint Oswy; the Long Shambles** | proper place names must be unique across adventures |
| the Ash-reeve, unnamed | **Raistrick** | a name on the page, not "a Raistrick" (a Role read as a name) |
| Barefoot Hern at the window | a second person, `hern_window` | Kyr has no speaking through a window (friction 5) |
| the Lady masked | a second person, `masked` | a Person's name is told; the mask is the scene (friction 5) |

## Cut or changed from the design

- **Fighting Raistrick, bribing him, following him out** (design, Carth): cut. Following him adds nothing that his ten
  o'clock report does not; bribing him is refused by his character; fighting him in the dark made Carth a combat
  encounter, which is the one thing it must not be. What is left with him is talk and a lie.
- **A healing spell for the child**: the gate is *mending*, which a cleric or monk has; the spell itself is not read.
- **Returning to Carth.** Built (the vigil counts on, Raistrick comes at three every night, the old woman dies on the
  twenty-sixth morning if nobody stops her, Fly goes home, the fire is lit when released), but no journey plays a
  return; it is in the account, not on the page (12-critique).
- **The twelve strands** are regrouped as thirteen in 06: *shrift* apart from *the Fell Gate*, Fastens' Eve as *the
  masked Lady*, Ash Day as *the Consistory*. The three local stories are as designed.

## Changed by play (Stage 5)

- Joins line out of order: the first question moved to the Proxy's Stone, after Hern has joined.
- Morning came on the first night at six: Carth's morning is the first seven o'clock on a later day.
- Will lit the hearth silently: he says it now.
- Night: the module's own `nightfall()` everywhere (friction 1).
- Cold hazards were lethal and paralysing: all physical, smaller (friction 4).
- Raistrick's verdict decided as he rises, by the hearth as it then is, so relighting after he leaves keeps the vigil.
- Thackray could only be asked with Raistrick's report: the billet coin alone is enough.
- Hal's arrest told everywhere: only in Gaol Lane. Serjeants were too strong: two commoners.
- Gaunt was placed beside mountains and moved the frontier: beside frost or forest.
- The commoner build is not playable: the "nothing done" journey is a ranger's.

## Changed by reading (Stage 6)

From reading the eight transcripts, first alone and then by an outside reader asked only for faults:

- **Told to the wrong people.** Hern's noon appointment on Fastens' Eve was told to a company forty miles away; now told
  to whoever is with him, and silent otherwise.
- **Carth's continuity.** The old woman "came in from the byre" before she had gone there; a baby nobody had met
  appeared twice; Raistrick's in-the-dark arrival played with a fire blazing; the daylit room was described on a
  dusk arrival; a relit hearth was described the next morning as black and wet, and the chimney as smokeless; a
  household that slept in the byre woke in the house-place. Each has its own text now.
- **Payoffs that were placeholders.** Hern "tells back what you said on the fell, in your own words" became the
  company's actual answers, in his cell, through the bars and before the Consistory, one text for each answer (and
  one for a company that never walked with him).
- **The climax offstage.** A company not in the Chapter House learned the verdict only from an experience line; the
  recantation, the putting-out and the bearing now come down the Bearer's Steps as news.
- **The city's big night.** Fastens' Eve's masks and the Fast's midnight bell now reach a company anywhere in the
  city, not only in the Shambles and the Fat Lamb.
- **Small truths.** Hern does not "walk on" while kneeling; nobody shakes hands "for the third time" after one; Hern
  does not cite Kell's board to Bess unless he took it; Hal's line no longer prints both of its alternatives; the
  grey coat is seen in his cell before he wears it out of the window, and kept after; the Ladder's porch and its fates
  agree with who walked barefoot; the grandmother's death is told once, on the right morning; nights left at Carth
  are fourteen everywhere; the masked woman's milestone no longer names her before she is known; her farewell is a
  ladler's, not a cheerful one.
- **The engine's voice.** A frozen sky for the journey voice, so the Levels are no longer crossed "with the dew still on
  the grass" (friction 9); a ring picked up has a line.
- **A real bug.** A company owing fewer pains than a penance discharges could not do it at all (friction 2): fixed, and
  Gaunt gives no credit.
- **New builds and ways tested.** A mage's conjured light at Carth (a light with no warmth: the house notices); the
  monk's Barefoot soles; the back ways into the Close (the wall, the lodge).
- **A test lost and found.** A scripted edit had silently cut the tower and Prudence journeys from the small suite;
  restored, and passing against the revised module.
