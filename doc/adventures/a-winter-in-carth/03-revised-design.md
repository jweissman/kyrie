# A Winter in Carth — the revised design

*The design the build is held to, after the critique ([02-critique.md](02-critique.md)). Where the build later departed
from it, [13-changes.md](13-changes.md) says so.*

## 1. In a sentence

You are asked on the north road to bring a barefoot heretic to his trial in Gaunt, a city where the rich hire the poor
to do their penance; the snow shuts you for a night in a farmhouse keeping, for wages, a vigil of no fire that he
refused; and by Ash Day you have to decide what you owe him, the farm, and the city that will put him out into the
winter.

## 2. Form

| Field | This work |
|---|---|
| Protagonist | A stranger on the north road, paid to be a custodian; by the first morning, a party to what happened at Carth |
| Fault | Yes: whoever lights a fire at Carth takes the vigil's debt and the family's ruin; whoever does not, may let a child freeze |
| Central activity | Walking and arguing; enduring a night; working inside an absurd law (paying, bearing, forging, striking) |
| Minds change by | People: a child's breath, an old friend's hunger, a masked conversation, one cold night of one's own |
| Inheritance | A legend (Saint Oswy carried a sinner up the hill), with a second verse the adults do not quote and the children do |
| Mystery | One, human: who paid for the forty nights. Found through people; knowing it moves nobody by itself |
| Clock | Four days: the road; Shrove Monday; Fastens' Eve; Ash Day |
| Space | A road, a farmhouse, a walled city in three rings, a wood |
| Where it ends | In Gaunt, or at Carth |
| Endings | Five, narrowed by what has happened; Carth's fates beside them as an account; no coda of custom |
| Companion | A heretic in custody: funny, ill, wrong about some things, and nobody's clerk |
| Voice | Three registers: the city ironic, Carth bare, the road argued |
| Register reached | Anger, shame, terror, grief, hilarity, tenderness |

## 3. The calendar

The region keeps its own days from when the company comes in at the Penitent's Cross.

| | Day 1 (Saturday before the Fast) | Day 2 (Shrove Monday) | Day 3 (Fastens' Eve) | Day 4 (Ash Day) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Morning | The beadle at the Cross; the walk | The snow stops at seven; digging out; Thackray on the fell at nine | The city | Ashes at nine; the loser of the Ladder walks; the Barefoot out or in |
| Midday | The Proxy's Stone; the Drove Fork | The North Gate: the assessment | The Stand; the Schools dispute at two | **The Consistory at noon** |
| Afternoon | The snow comes when the company tops the fell | The city; Abel faints at the Stand at four | The Bishop's Wood | The sentence |
| Evening | Carth: the light put out | The tower watched; the gaol window | **Fastens' Eve** from seven: masks, the Lady in the Row, the widow, the Fat King burnt at midnight; Hal's ladder at eleven | **The Gate of Ash at dusk** |
| Night | The Ash-reeve at three | Carth again, for a company that goes back | The Fast rung in at midnight | The book closes |

## 4. The strands

### S1 The beadle's foot
At the **Penitent's Cross**, Beadle Clem Sutcliffe, frostbitten, with Hern sitting on the step of the Cross mending a
sandal he does not need. *Accept* (two shillings, eight more at the North Gate; Hern follows the company as its charge);
*refuse* (Hern walks in alone, a step ahead, and the company may meet him again at Carth or not at all); *carry the
beadle down too* (brawn: he cannot walk; with him the walk is slower and the beadle's talk is the city's orthodoxy at
its kindest). **Trace:** the custody is the city's business: the Gate asks who brought him.

### S2 The road to Gaunt
Three stations, three questions, each with three or four answers. The answers are facts the region reads.

- **The Cross**: *Can one man be sorry for another?* (Yes, that is what love is / No, sorrow is your own / It is a
  trade like any other / I do not know.)
- **The Proxy's Stone**, where Tobias Kell froze kneeling last winter with a board round his neck in another man's
  name, for another man's adultery, at a shilling a night: *What do we owe the dead?* (Their names / Nothing; they are
  past owing / What they were promised / To carry on what they did.) Hern takes the board off the dead man, if the
  company will help him, and reads the other man's name aloud.
- **The Drove Fork**: *Are you the one who promised the beadle this morning?* And he walks off down the drovers' road
  to see. *Stop him* (by word, or by hand); *let him go* (the flight, at once; the book will close without him);
  *go with him* (away from Gaunt; the city is not reached by this company, and Carth is never seen).

Later, Hern turns the answers. At Carth: "You said on the road that one man can be sorry for another. Are you sorry for
them? Then do something." In the gaol: "You said the dead are owed their names." At the trial, if the company speaks, he
quotes it back to the Consistory.

### S3 A Winter in Carth
**Arrival.** At the top of the fell the snow comes in; the road down is lost until morning. Carth is the only house.
Annis Scaife meets the company at the door with her hand out for its light: a flame is a fire, and the vigil forbids
fire. Inside, nothing can be seen; the company is in the dark until it leaves or lights something.

**The house.** The house-place (hearth cold twenty-three days), the box-bed where everyone sleeps, the loft, the byre
with the cow and the dead grandfather, Ralph Scaife, laid out on a hurdle in his Sunday coat, waiting for the ground to
soften. Old Mother Scaife sits up with him; she sets a place at the cold hearth for the Cold Guest, who comes to a house
that has let its fire go out for a debt. The dog will not go into the byre.

**The night.**

- At nine, Dowsie's cough. By midnight a fever. If nothing is done for her by morning, she does not wake.
- At eleven Old Mother Scaife goes to the byre and does not come back.
- At three the Ash-reeve. The latch lifts; a man crosses the dark room, kneels, and puts his bare hand into the
  hearth's ash; holds it there; stands, and goes. If the ash is warm, he says one word: "Broken."

**What can be done,** none of it required:

- *Light a fire on the hearth.* Warmth; Dowsie lives; Hern's cough eases. The vigil is broken, and the company takes
  its debt, seventeen nights. Will Scaife takes up his billhook; the household may put the company out into the snow.
- *Light it in the byre,* where Raistrick never puts his hand, and pack the hearth with snow before three: letter of
  the law (needs knowing how he checks: ask Annis, or watch).
- *Bring the cow and the calf into the house-place* (woodcraft): animal heat, no fire. Enough for the child.
- *Everyone into the box-bed, the company too*: body heat; the company bears the cold.
- *Tend the child* (mending: a poultice, a nursing night) or a healing spell.
- *Sit with the grandmother in the byre*, or lay the dead man out properly with the rites, which lets her come in.
- *Meet the Ash-reeve:* talk (he can be asked whose vigil it is; he will not say, but he will say where he reports);
  bribe (he refuses); trick (deception: "the warmth is the child's fever"); follow him out at three into the snow
  (stealth); fight him (he is armed, in the dark; and to kill him is a blood fault).
- *Confront the house:* tell Will that the vigil is Hern's, refused (Hern can say it himself, if the company asks him
  to). Annis has the last word on whether the company sleeps in the house or in the snow.

**Morning.** What happened is told plainly: who woke, and who did not; whether the hearth was warm; whether the dead
man has moved in the night, and why, if the company sat up to see.

**Returning.** Carth is three hours from the North Gate. A company that comes back finds what it left, a night or two
on: the grandmother's place at the hearth; Raistrick's footprints; what the city sent, or did not.

### S4 The North Gate
The **Assessor of the Gate** assesses every traveller for the faults of the road: lies told, blows struck, vigils
broken, the company kept. Keeping company with an accused heretic is itself one pain ("Association"). The company
leaves the gate with a **shrift** (pains owed) written on a tally-stick, which the whole city can read. Hern is
delivered to the Consistory gaol here, or walks to it himself.

Unshriven, the company cannot pass the Close Gate, cannot be heard at the Consistory, and pays a penny more for
everything in the Middle Town ("the unshriven's penny").

### S5 The Stand, and the Ladder
At **the Stand**, proxies in pens, with boards. The company may:

- **hire out**: a day's fast (no meal that day), a barefoot mile round the walls (cold hurt), a night's vigil on the
  Minster steps (cold hurt, a night lost), stripes (wounds); paid in coin;
- **hire** one to bear its own pains (coin per pain), and see who takes the work;
- **talk** to the proxies, who have opinions about everything.

**The Ladder** is the Stand's comedy. Mistress Grizel Dinsdale (brewster) and Master Pentecost Sowerby (draper) are
level on the second rung, and it is gilded at nine on Ash Day. Beats: Dinsdale hires the company; telling Sowerby
makes him hire twice as much; telling Dinsdale makes her hire the whole Stand for Ash Day; with no proxies left in
Gaunt, the Ladder becomes a public question. On Ash Day morning the loser, if the company has brought it to that, walks
barefoot to the Minster in person, the first burgess in eleven years to do so, and the city comes out to look. **Trace:**
a burgess who has walked will stand up at the trial.

### S6 The widow's thoughts
Abel Lumb faints in the Stand at four on Shrove Monday: he has fasted eleven days for Mistress Joan Pickersgill's
impure thoughts. Joan, in her parlour on Tanner's Row, tells the company, with relish, that she has been thinking a
great deal. Neither knows. *Tell her* (what it is costing him: she is mortified, then delighted); *tell him* (he will not
believe it); *take over his fasts* (the company becomes her proxy, and she, told her proxy is changed, thinks harder,
and the company's fast goes on); *feed him* at the Fat Lamb (he breaks a paid fast and owes pains for it); *bring them
both to the Fat Lamb on Fastens' Eve*, masked, where she says it. **Trace:** Joan is Thackray's cousin, and laughs at
him. Married, she makes him laugh at himself.

### S7 The unfinished tower
**Old Joby Thwaite** sets the north tower's last course by day; by morning it is down. Watch the scaffold at night and
see Canon Hamo Pellow's men taking it apart by lantern. *Tell Joby* (he and his masons set it and sit up with it on
Fastens' Eve; on Ash Day morning the tower has a new course, the first in forty years); *confront Pellow* (he will
forgive the Scaifes' arrears to keep it quiet); *tell the Lady Penitentiary* (Pellow is silenced at the trial). The
masons' lodge door opens into the Close: a company working for Joby goes in that way.

### S8 Whose forty nights
Who bought the Cold Vigil, and for what? Found by people: **Raistrick** reports at ten each morning to the man who pays
(follow him down off the fell; or he is seen going into the Wood Exchange yard); **Annis** shows the coin she was paid
in advance, countermarked with the Wood Exchange's billet; **Thackray** himself stands on the fell road at nine on Shrove
Monday, alone, looking at Carth. He bought it for the forty-one who froze in the Row last winter when he held back wood
to raise the price. He is sorry. He has not lowered the price. He sits on the Consistory.

Knowing is a door, not a lever. What moves him: a night of his own at Carth's cold hearth (the company can bring him
up on Shrove Monday); Annis saying it to his face; Joan laughing at him. Moved, he can release the vigil (the Scaifes
paid in full), open the dead-wood right (the Barefoot warm, the strike unnecessary), and, if he has been brought far
enough, bear Hern's sentence himself.

### S9 The Barefoot
**Bess Ackroyd**, Prior of the Barefoot, will call the Row out on Ash Day, the best day's wage of the year, unless the
Wood Exchange opens the dead-wood right in the Bishop's Wood. *Help her* (go round the Row; do a proxy's day for the
Hall's books; carry word to Sowerby's or Dinsdale's proxies); *bring wood* from the Bishop's Wood on a sledge (wolves
at dusk; Thackray's woodmen, who name a price); *get Thackray to open it* (strike off, the Row warm); *break it* (Canon
Pellow pays). Bess and Hern: she thinks his argument a rich man's luxury. If Hern sends to her, or the company has
worked in the Row, she listens. **Trace:** on Ash Day, a struck Row means the rich do their own penance in the streets,
and the trial is held in a city that has just seen Hern's argument walk past the Minster barefoot.

### S10 The rescue
**Hal Metcalfe** will be under the gaol window on Fastens' Eve at eleven with a ladder. **Prudence Garth** has been
offered the informer's fee (her mother's penance is three years behind) and will tell the gaoler at eight. *Help Hal*
(climbing, lockwork, or the serjeants fought); *stop Hal* (Hern will not come if he means to speak); *turn Prudence*
(pay her mother's pains, or talk to her); *tell the gaoler yourself*. Whether Hern goes out of the window depends on
what he has come to want.

### S11 Fastens' Eve
From seven to midnight: licence. Masks in the Middle Town; the Fat King's effigy carried to the Wood Exchange and
burnt at midnight; the Shrove baker's last batch; the Schools' disputation at two having ended in a riot. In the Row,
**the Lady Penitentiary** ladles soup at the Barefoot copper, masked, as she has every Fastens' Eve for thirty years,
and talks as she never does. The company can talk to her there. If it carries a word from Hern (given at the gaol
window), she hears it. At midnight the tenor bell rings the Fast in, and everything said under a mask is unsaid.

### S12 Ash Day
At noon, the Consistory in the Chapter House: Dame Strake presiding, Canon Pellow prosecuting, the Guildhall's man
(Thackray) and two Doctors sitting. The charge is read; Ploughwright's Determination is read; Hern is asked to recant.

What the company may do there depends on what it is:

- **Shriven**, it may be heard as a witness: tell Carth; tell the road (Hern quotes the company's answers back);
  **offer to bear his sentence**.
- **Unshriven**, it stands in the nave with the Row, and may shout, which is a fault.

What happens is the meeting of what has been made true:

- If the Barefoot are out, the city is barefoot, and the Consistory adjourns: **The Fast**.
- If Hern has been moved to live and Hawise gives him the words (or Bess has asked him), he recants: **The
  Recantation**.
- If someone the Lady will accept offers to bear it (the company if shriven; Thackray if moved), and Hawise has been
  moved, she accepts the proxy: **The Bearer**. If she has not been moved, she refuses it, and in refusing says in the
  Minster that a sentence cannot be borne by another, which is the heresy; and puts him out.
- Otherwise: **Put Out**. At dusk he is walked through the Gate of Ash. A company may follow.

If Hern escaped (the fork, or the window): **The Flight**, which closes the book at dusk on Ash Day with the
Consistory sitting to an empty chair.

If Hern has died (of cold at Carth, or the cough in the gaol), the Consistory sits all the same, to try him dead.

## 5. Purely local

- **The Shrove baker.** Mother Tunstall's oven is cold; wood is dear. A bundle of wood from the Bishop's Wood, or an
  hour at her trough. Fastens' Eve she sells out. Ash Day morning a cake is left for whoever helped, which may not be
  eaten, because it is the Fast.
- **The slide.** On the Row ditch the children slide on the ice, and Tib Ackroyd (Bess's grandson) has lost his
  skate-bones, cut by his dead father from a cow's shins. They are under the ice by the culvert grating. The
  children's rhyme, sung while sliding, is Saint Oswy's legend with its second verse: *at the top he set him down: NOW
  WALK, said Oswy.*
- **The dog.** The Scaifes' dog, Fly, follows the company down the fell if it was good to the house, and will not be
  sent back; if Dowsie is alive, Fly goes home the next time the company is at Carth.

## 6. The two principal approaches

Two ways into the city's centre and its trial, which do different things:

- **From above (the Close).** Get shriven (pay, bear, be borne, be remitted), pass the Close Gate, work the
  institutions: the Penitentiary, the Schools, Canon Pellow's tower, Thackray in his yard, a witness's place at the
  Consistory. Reaches: *the Bearer* through Thackray or the company itself; *the Recantation* through Hawise; Pellow
  silenced.
- **From below (the Row).** Stay unshriven or forge it; work the Stand and the Barefoot Hall, the ditch, the Bishop's
  Wood, the gaol's window from the lane, Fastens' Eve. Reaches: *the Fast*; *the Flight*; Hawise met masked; Bess's
  plea that makes Hern recant.

The **Close Gate** is the portal between them, and has five ways through: a clean tally; a forged one (Heb; the
porter may see it); the masons' lodge (work for Joby); over the Close wall at night (climbing); and once, as Hern's
custodian, on the day he is delivered.

## 7. Endings

| | Reached when | Its last morning |
|---|---|---|
| **The Fast** | The Barefoot are out on Ash Day | The rich walked; the Consistory adjourned sine die; the Stand empty for a week |
| **The Recantation** | Hern wants to live, and has the words from Hawise or the plea from Bess | He lives; the Commerce stands; the Row eats |
| **The Bearer** | Someone shriven offers and Hawise, moved, accepts | Someone walks out of the Gate of Ash who did not think the thought; the Commerce has a crack in it |
| **Put Out** | Otherwise | Hern walked into the Levels at dusk, alone or followed |
| **The Flight** | Hern got away | An empty chair; the trial held all the same |

Carth's account beside each: the vigil kept, broken, cheated, or released; the grandmother; the child; the rent.

## 8. Rewards

- **Experience** by milestones for things done, not killed.
- **The Barefoot soles**: a company member who has done a proxy's day and a barefoot mile, and kept Hern company on
  the road, has learned to stand in snow (+1 constitution, once).
- **Hawise's ring** (a remission token): clears the company's shrift in Gaunt, once. Nothing outside it.
- **Joby's mark**: the masons' lodge will open to the company as long as Joby lives.
- **Hern**, as a companion, if he walks out of Gaunt free or put out and the company goes with him; and **Fly**, the
  dog, if Carth has no child to go home to.
- **Coin**: the beadle's fee, proxy wages, Thackray's purse.

## 9. Size

About 45 places (twelve on the road, at Carth and in the wood; thirty in the city), about thirty people, twelve strands
and three local stories, five endings, a dozen fates.
