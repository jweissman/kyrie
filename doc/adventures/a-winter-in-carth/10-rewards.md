# A Winter in Carth: rewards and what they imply

*Gaunt is a city where merit is bought, so its rewards are mostly things that cannot be: what the company did with its
own hands and feet, and who it changed. Source: `growth.kyr`, and the items and coin in the strands.*

## Experience: thirty-seven milestones

Experience comes only from things done (no creature in Gaunt is worth anything dead). In rough bands:

| Worth | For |
|---|---|
| 400 | the three great verdicts: Hern given the words to live by (*Recantation*); a sentence borne by another (*Bearer*); the Consistory adjourned for want of proxies (*Fast*) |
| 300 | moving Thackray to act; moving the Lady Penitentiary; bringing the Barefoot out on Ash Day |
| 250 | keeping the child warm without a fire; keeping the letter of the vigil and the child warm |
| 200 | the vigil released; Joan and Abel at one table; the tower's course standing; witness for Carth; going out of the Gate of Ash with him |
| 150 | the night at Carth got through; who paid for it found out; Annis face to face with him; the Ladder set at itself; Sowerby's own penance; Laverock exposed; the wood brought in; the bars out; a fire lit for the child (which breaks the vigil, and is still worth something) |
| 100 | the road's three questions; Kell's name; the old woman brought in; Ralph laid out; a clean shrift; your own penance; the masked woman; Bess's word carried; Prudence turned; the Doctors set at each other |
| 150 | (second pass) carrying word between a mason and his son |
| 100 | (second pass) finding where the Gate of Ash leads |
| 50 | custody; Tib's skate-bones; Mother Sugden's oven |

What the played journeys earned (from `playtest/`): *from above* 2,900; *the company bears it* 2,150; *the Lady's
words* 1,450; *from below* 1,200; *the window* about 860 (some of it the serjeants); *the letter* 700; *nothing done
at Carth* 200; *left alone* nothing. The spread is the design's: the city pays for attention, not for arrival. The
Close approach earns more than the Row's, which is a fair criticism (12-critique.md).

## Things

- **Dame Hawise's ring** (`gaunt_ring`, worth nothing): shown at the Close Gate it shaves every notch off the
  company's tally. It is good once, and only in Gaunt. Elsewhere it is a plain silver ring with the Penitentiary's keys
  on it, which is to say proof that the Lady Penitentiary once trusted you, and nothing in the world outside will know
  what that means.
- **Tib's skate-bones**, a **sledge of dead wood**, a **faggot of thorn**: carried only to be given away.
- **A clean tally**, which is not an item but a key, and **eighteen pains still notched**, which is a debt "doubled when
  you come back".

## Coin

The beadle's two shillings and eight at the Fell Gate; wages for bearing (Sowerby's mile two, the alderman's son's
night five, Dinsdale's hiring three); Quist gives one back. Out: a proxy at a shilling a pain, Heb's forgery four,
Quist's Determination five, Prudence's mother's six. The ledger is close to even on purpose: a company that does
everything by paying ends poorer than it came, and a company that does everything by its own body ends a little
richer and more hurt.

## Capacities

- **The Barefoot soles**: learned at the Barefoot Hall by one member who has walked with Hern, walked a barefoot mile
  and knelt a night on the Steps: **+1 constitution**, once, for good. It is the only permanent change to a body that
  Gaunt offers, and it is learned from the poorest people in it.

## Companions

- **Ninian Hern**, willing, if the company goes out of the Gate of Ash with him, or is put out itself and he comes
  through the wicket after it. He is a commoner of the third level, ill, barefoot and excellent company; the fate line
  has him at Carth for the last fourteen nights of the vigil.
- **Fly**, the Scaifes' collie, if the company made much of her and the child lived; she goes home the next time the
  company passes Carth.

## What follows the company out

- **Struck from the Register.** A company that bore Hern's sentence can never come back in at the Fell Gate: the way
  is shut, in the world, for good. If Gaunt is placed in a generated world, the Levels are still there, and Carth, and
  the Cross; the city is a wall.
- **A notched tally**, if it forged its way in: the debt waits.
- **Reputation that does not travel**: everything the company did for the Ladder, the Row or the Lady is known only in
  Gaunt. The design means the rewards that matter most to be the ones that cannot be spent anywhere else: a child up
  and about, a tower with a course of stone on it that stayed, an old woman who does not go into the byre at night.
