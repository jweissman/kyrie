# The Book of Faces: the small adventures

*Thirty-eight threads. Each has a person with a motive, a place that matters, something misread, two or more genuinely different approaches, a complication, a consequence, and a clock. R marks one of the Registrar's nine Rounds; L a local story whose stake is personal, comic or practical; C a calling's arc. File in brackets.*

## How they hold together

- *The Burning needs Pell, who is released by Pip's forgiveness (cisterns), a penitent's asking (cleric) or the dead woman's handwriting (rounds).* Three threads and one man.
- *The Lie has five clues* in five places (Rain-Wives, Wall, Peck, the Urn, the Rounds), none of which knows it.
- *Lacke has five ends* from five arcs (Hearing, Lampoon, warrior's fear, graceful resigning, the Key sold).
- *The Swap night* is the lovers' crossing, Slee's squeeze, the Confessional's masks in each other's faces, and Wick leaving the Brothers.
- *The strike, the march, the flooded hall, the Warden loose, the angry room* each lower the Drawing's order; *a Hand played, trust earned, the Hearing held* each raise it.
- *Local stories stay local*: the umbrellas, the eel-race, the dress, the choir, the ladle. Their second faces are about families and faces, never the Drawing.
