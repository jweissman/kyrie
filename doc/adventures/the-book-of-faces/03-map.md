# The Book of Faces: the map

*Ombra as a place: seven Sleeves, a reed-garden, a cistern site; the ways between them and the ways that are conditions; who is where when; the calendar. Built (104 rooms, 114 ways, 103 people).*

## In the generated world

A **province of the first country**, west, on fen and meadow, a road from the nearest town to the end of the delta road (`road_end`). Standalone: the end of the delta road, eight on the first morning, 2 October 1290. Positions on the region map (x east, y south):

 0  Heronry     Quiet Hill     Chapel Island    the Wet Post

The cisterns lie *under the Drip*; the Duelling Stair is a site of Quiet Hill; the Urn Hall is the Chapel's lowest level.

## Ways

*Bridges* join the Gate to the Row and the Post, the Row to the Post, the Chapel and Maskers' Lane, the Post to the Chapel, Maskers' Lane to the Drip; *steps* rise from Maskers' Lane to the Terrace; *ferries* run Row to Drip (Rill's, whose time depends on whether her boat is sound), Chapel to Hill, Drip to the water-gate; a *path* leads Drip to Heronry. The Stair, nine landings, joins the foot of the Hill to the Terrace's head; the water-gate is the way up for those who do not fight.

## Who is where when

By day (08:00–20:00) everybody is masked and answers a *face*; a bare company is given one cold line by nearly everybody. The exceptions are structural and are the book's refuges: the Candid's Reading-room, the Heronry, the Collegium's Master. By night (20:00–08:00) people are home and unmasked, and a bare knock is received. Pell sits in the sorting-hall 23:00–02:00 and on the Chapel steps 02:00–04:00; Peck is lucid only at night; Dov is on the roof 20:00–24:00; Ansel is in the vestry; Vail has the Gatehouse kettle.
