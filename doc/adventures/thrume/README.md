# THRUME — Against the Mountain That Breathes

*A supermodule in six parts. Written as a design first, then in Kyr (`data/adventures/thrume`). Spoilers throughout.*

It begins in the dream-houses of a salt town, among people who pay to sleep. It ends past the edge of every dream,
in front of the thing that eats them. In between are a giants' hold, a mine that turns into a body, a city of
creatures that farm sleep, a tomb built to kill anyone who reaches the bottom of it, and a palace in a sky that has
not existed for a thousand years.

| Part | Place | Model | Danger |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Saltgrave**: the dream-houses, the Salt Office, the night carts | the slaving ring at the start of *GDQ* | a town |
| I | **The Shoulder Hall**: the giants' hold on the ridge | *Against the Giants* | the giants are three times your level |
| II | **The Inward Roads**: the mine, the Veins, the Lungs, the Smiths' Delving | *Descent into the Depths* | the mountain is alive |
| III | **Hush**: the city of the Quiet Court, in the titan's skull | *Vault of the Drow* | a society, which is worse than a monster |
| IV | **The Heart-Tomb**: the Hollow Smiths' killing gauntlet | *Tomb of Horrors* | one wrong touch kills |
| V | **The Palace of Before**: Thrume's dream of the sky it walked under | the sky citadel of *Dark Clouds Gather* | a dream that can drown you |
| VI | **The Between**: under every sleeping god, where eaten dreams fall | the 66th layer at the bottom of *Queen of the Spiders* | the Mouth |

Behind all six run three clocks, none of which waits for the company:
- **Thrume's stirring.** The titan wakes at ten.
- **Thrume's breath.** The Lungs, the Room of Three Breaths and the cloud-stair all follow it, hour by hour.
- **The host of Harle.** The army crosses the Ebb at the start of the tenth day.

---

## 1. Deep time

**The old ones.** Before the hills there were the titans, who were the hills. They walked under a sky that was
nearer then, and they dreamed when they slept, and what they dreamed was solid. Thrume was the youngest of them.

**The Mouth.** Under everything that sleeps there is a place where the dreams that are forgotten go. It is called
the Between, and in it lives something that eats what falls there. For an age it ate only the dregs. Then it
learned how to make dreams fall faster.

**The Hollow Smiths.** A thousand years ago an order of human smiths was paid in starmetal by the young gods to kill
the titans and make room for the world. They killed six. Thrume they could not kill: every blow that should have
killed it woke it. Every waking killed smiths. Their doctrine came out of that: ***what wakes, kills; what kills,
wakes.*** So they did the only thing left. They put Thrume to sleep for ever, sealed its heart in a tomb meant to
kill anyone who reached it, cut their doctrine into every trap, and went into the hill with the heart. The last of
them is still there. He has forgotten why.

**The keepers.** Thrume's own children, the giants, stayed. They keep the body. They cut the trees before roots can
crack the skin. They kill anything that digs. They sing to him, so his sleep stays deep. The song is carved on the
pillars of the Shoulder Hall, and only the Rememberer can still read all of it.

**The Quiet Court.** Three hundred years ago something from the Between found the sleeping titan. It was a seed of
the Mouth: grey, soft, quiet creatures that eat dreams (the catalog's mind destroyers). They grew a city in the hollow
of Thrume's skull, called it Hush, and farmed his sleep. But a titan asleep for ever dreams thin. The Court needs
the dreams *rich*, which means the titan must be stirred, nearly woken, and kept there. For that they needed
someone to cut into the body.

**Saltgrave.** Twelve years ago, prospectors found red salt in the fells: warm, heavy, and worth more than silver
by weight. Idony Vask, Margravine of Ebb, built a town on it. The red salt is Thrume's blood, crystallised. Eaten,
it makes you dream his dreams. The Court's thralls opened dream-houses in Saltgrave. Dreamers whose minds the salt
has opened are carried up to the mine at night in the salt-carts, and down to Hush, where they are livestock.

**Now.** Four months ago the miners of Number Four broke into a wall of red salt that was *warm*. The giants came
down the next week, smashed the mine-heads, burned the overseer and carried off forty miners. The Margravine needs
the salt to pay mercenaries before the Duke of Harle's host crosses the Ebb at the new moon. She has sent her
hardest man, **Overseer Brannoc**, down an old adit with a crew to keep cutting. Every day he cuts, Thrume stirs.

The company comes up the salt road on the morning the Margravine's bounty notice goes up.

## 2. Part 0 — Saltgrave (the town)

A raw boom town of salt-white dust: the Salt Office, three dream-houses (one with a red lantern), the miners' row,
the Saltcellar inn, a shrine to the Hearth-Mother, the carters' yard, and the cart road up to Number Four.

**What the company is told.** The Margravine's envoy, **Sir Odo Varne**, will pay a barony for Thane Mórrach's head.
**What it finds.** **Hesper Dunmore**, a chandler, asks the company to find her son **Col**, who started going to
the Red Lantern after his father died in the mine, and did not come home three nights ago.

**The ring.**
- **Mother Quietly** runs the Red Lantern. She is a thrall of the Court, and her eyes are too still.
- The dreamers in her smoke-room all dream the same thing: a palace in a sky that is too close.
- By night the Hushed load the deepest dreamers into salt-carts in the carters' yard and drive them up to the old
  adit of Number Four.
- **Provost Lisle Hauk** of the Salt Office takes salt-silver from the Lantern and asks nothing. His ledger
  says so.

**Breaking it.** With the cart-ledger or the night carts seen, the company can confront Mother Quietly. When she is
cornered, the Court empties her: she sits down and nothing is behind her eyes. She leaves a *hush-stone*, a grey pebble
that hums at the edge of hearing. The night carts stop. Col is already gone below.

**Ways on.** Up the scree road to the giants (Part I), as hired. Or up the cart road and down the old adit (Part II),
following Col, which at this level is very dangerous.

## 3. Part I — The Shoulder Hall (the giants)

A hold of black timber and grey stone on the titan's shoulder, under a glacier. Twelve rooms and a cellar cut in the ice.

**Factions.**
- **Thane Mórrach** holds the miners as hostages, not food, and does not want a war with the lowlands.
- **Skarn and the young bloods** want vengeance. They mean to kill the miners and burn Saltgrave on the second night.
- **The Rememberer**, the thane's blind mother, keeps the song.
- **Aud Brenning** is the miners' foreman. She hid a lump of the red salt in the midden.
- **Tobias Kell** sold the miners to the giants and will sell anyone else to save himself.

**Approaches.**
- *Guest-right*: strike the pole by day. It binds both sides.
- *The midden chute* and *the blue crevasse*.
- *By night* the hold sleeps, but crossing the long hall without stealth may wake a giant.
- *A frontal assault*: Skarn kills the company, as he should.

**Turning points.**
- Bring the Rememberer the warm salt and she tells you what Thrume is.
- Tell the thane what the Rememberer told you, and swear to close the wound. He gives you his ring, the keepers'
  token, which opens the **Keepers' Stair** down into the body. He lets the miners go with you.
- If the company kills a giant or is caught, **Skarn throws down the thane**: the hold goes to war, and the miners
  die on the second night.

**The thing in the ice.** In the glacier cellar the giants keep the first walking dream they caught: a salt elemental
in a cage of ice. Melt the cage and it is loose. Leave it, and the Rememberer can tell you what it means.

## 4. Part II — The Inward Roads (the body)

Two ways down meet in the **Veins**: the old adit from Saltgrave (Brannoc's workings), and the Keepers' Stair from
the giants' cellar.

- **The Workings.** Overseer Brannoc's deep camp, his cutting face, and miners carving the same blind face over and over.
  Brannoc stops cutting only if shown that the salt is blood (the Smiths' gauge), or ordered by the Margravine's seal,
  or killed. Each morning he cuts, Thrume stirs.
- **The Veins.** Red crystal caverns that brighten with the titan's pulse. A jewel elemental guards the Wound, the face
  where Number Four broke through into living salt.
- **The Lungs.** A cavern the size of a dale that breathes. On the in-breath the wind drags anything loose across the
  floor; on the out-breath the Wind Gate opens for an hour.
- **The Smiths' Last Delving.** The order's abandoned workshop: the forge, the iron library and the Master's cell.
  The **Gauge of Sleep** shows how deep Thrume sleeps. The **Smith-Master's hammer token** is the second key to the
  tomb. The library teaches the doctrine and the three riddles.
- **The Dream-Hollows.** Where Thrume's dreams take shape: the hunger-dream in the cold shaft (a wendigo), and the
  Hushed's way into the skull.

## 5. Part III — Hush (the Quiet Court)

A city grown in the hollow of the titan's skull, where nobody speaks aloud.
- **The Court.** **House Pallor** farms fear-dreams and is militant. **House Lull** farms sweet dreams and wants
  the titan never to wake. The **Abbess of Hush** holds them both, and serves the Mouth.
- **Places.** The Thrall-Market, where the dreamers from Saltgrave are sold and Col is in a salt-niche. The
  Silent Library. The Choir, where the Court feeds together. The Abbess's spire.
- **The third key.** The Abbess keeps the **Eye**, the titan's own closed eye, cut out of the dream and set in grey
  crystal.
- **Getting the Eye.** House Lull will give the company the Eye if it promises to keep Thrume asleep, and means
  to betray it afterwards. It can also be stolen from the spire while the Choir feeds.
- **Freeing Col** is a choice with a cost. The market's master will sell him, and he is not the only one.

## 6. Part IV — The Heart-Tomb (the gauntlet)

The Hollow Smiths built this around the heart to kill whoever reached it. **Every trap is an argument for the
doctrine**, and every room can be read by a company that studied the Smiths in the Delving. A company that did not
will die here in the old way.

1. **The Salt Stair**: steps of salt that dissolve under a weight; the third step is false.
2. **The Gate of Three Hammers**: iron, bronze and salt hammers, and a riddle cut over them. Strike the wrong one
   and it strikes you.
3. **The Hall of Doctrine**: a painted gallery that teaches, panel by panel, how to survive what follows. It
   also contains one lie.
4. **The Sleeper's Face**: a titan's face in the wall with an open mouth (black, silent, and it unmakes whoever
   enters) and closed eyes. *What wakes, kills.* The left lid gives.
5. **The Hall of False Doors**: seven doors. Five are death, one is the Oubliette, one is the way.
6. **The Oubliette**: a pit with a way out for the patient.
7. **The Room of Three Breaths**: fills with salt-dust on Thrume's in-breath. Cross on the out-breath.
8. **The Gearworks**: three great wheels (iron, salt and bone), to be set as the Master's journal sets them.
   Set wrongly, they scald.
9. **The False Heart**: a magnificent chamber with a heart of red crystal the size of a cart. Ardath, the last
   Hollow Smith, now a lich, believes he keeps it. Take the decoy and the ceiling comes down and Ardath rises. Speak
   the doctrine back to him and he lets you pass, and grieves.
10. **The Warder**: a smith-built automaton in the last corridor that kills whatever moves while it is wound.
11. **The Door of Three Locks**: the thane's ring, the Smith-Master's token and the Abbess's Eye.
12. **The Keeper's Chair**: the true heart, beating slowly, and an empty seat beside it.

## 7. Part V — The Palace of Before (the dream)

Sitting in the Keeper's Chair, the company goes into Thrume's dream: a palace in the sky of a thousand years ago,
above a range that was still walking.
- **The cloud-stair** holds only on the out-breath.
- **The Hall of the Feast** is full of remembered titans.
- **The Gallery of the Slaying** shows the Hollow Smiths' war as Thrume remembers it: the smiths were
  *paid*, and they wept.
- **The Grey Orchard** is where the Court feeds, and the dream is rotting from the edges.
- **The Eye-Tower**: the Abbess stands on the bridge to the Between.

## 8. Part VI — The Between (the bottom)

Below every sleeping thing: a grey plain of eaten dreams, the **Archive of Forgotten Things**, the **Shore of
Unremembering**, and **the Mouth**. The Mouth cannot be fought at any level. It can only be refused:
- Give it something it cannot eat, the Rememberer's song (a thing remembered by someone still alive). Its seed in
  Thrume starves. The Court in Hush goes grey and quiet for ever.
- Or wake Thrume from inside, and the dream ends. The Mouth loses its meal, and so does the world.

## 9. Endings

| Ending | Needs | What follows |
|---|---|---|
| **The Song Given** | the song (I), the Chair (IV), reaching the Mouth (VI) | Thrume sleeps clean. The giants keep him; the Court is gone. Ebb fights Harle without heart-salt and loses the river towns, but Saltgrave lives. |
| **The Re-Sealing** | the Chair, and someone who stays | Thrume sleeps; the Court still feeds. The keeper in the Chair is not coming back. |
| **The Heart Cut** | the Margravine's way: Brannoc reaches the heart, or the company cuts it | Ebb wins its war. Within a generation the range is dead stone and the springs fail. |
| **The Waking** | stirring reaches 10, or the company wakes him | The mountains walk. Harle's host is gone in an afternoon, and so is every village on the flanks. |

## 10. Clocks

- *Stirring* starts at 2. +1 every morning Brannoc cuts, +1 for each wrong move in the tomb, +1 if the young bloods
  go down on Saltgrave. −1 when the cutting stops. At 6 the town shakes, and at 10 Thrume wakes.
- *Breath*: Thrume breathes in for one hour and out for one hour, for ever.
- *Harle*: the host crosses the Ebb at the start of the tenth day. What the company did decides who stands on the
  Shoulder road that day: the giants, nobody, or a walking mountain.
