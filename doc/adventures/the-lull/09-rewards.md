## What stays done

**In the valley.** The Working Day settles the Lull for good: the week returns with the law kept on the Lullday
(*The Seventh Day*), or without it (*Monday*), or the Lull goes on. The mended bridge stands across the leat; the
Fold-stone gets AND THEN MONDAY carved on it and the board by the pump changes; Corin keeps a workshop at the bridge
and Tobias a chair; Haskett's men restring the Mill in *Monday*; the Long Table is laid every evening with nothing to
watch. Each person's afterward is told (`endings.kyr`) and some are seen when the company returns (`after.kyr`).

**Institutions.** The Rest-wardens sit down at noon, or break a girl's hand, or are dead; their Chief is softened or
not. The Glossary has a new board, 2,214, that it will be told to paint over. The Open Houses are at peace, swapped,
or widowed. The Club works or disbands. Quick Nan is banned, reformed, or richer. The Old Shift lose a member, or two.

**In the world.** Every night slept inside is a week of the shared world: every other adventure's clock runs. When the
Working Day is over, the country behind the Lull hears it as old news (`placed.kyr`'s `word` goes stale), and its
innkeeper says so to a company come back (`countries.json` `after`). In a generated world the Lull's ending closes its
chapter, not the book: the valley is there to return to (`test/the-lull-placed.test.ts`).
