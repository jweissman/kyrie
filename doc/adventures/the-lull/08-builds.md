**Danger and the lone novice.** The Old Brock is level 5 and territorial; the Overseer level 6; the Hands cannot be
fought (they seize); the wardens are three level-2 warriors and a level-3 Chief Warden. A lone first-level traveller
has a way round every one of them: honey or the drinking place or smoke for the Brock; open hands, the hymn, or the
hoist for the Overseer; open hands or the loom for the Hands; Kezia, Aldith, the Open House's cocoa, Nan's gratitude
or a good tongue for the wardens. The game warns before each (their `Warns` name the ways round).

**What a build changes in the story, not only in a check.** The cleric's Hester is eased without the poppy Tobias
resents, which makes him gentler on the second day; the thief brings Simeon home on the first morning instead of the
afternoon; a bard or anyone who sat with the Old Shift can take the pay-chest without a fight, and with it the notched
shilling that can forgive Tobias himself; a warrior company can settle the wardens, and the Lull will remember it.

**Not built**: a Glosser's ruling the company could argue (deliberately: the survey asks for a rest from hearings); a
mage's spell that interacts with the law (no spell is written against the Ravel).
