# The Lull: the smaller stories

Each is listed by its person, its place, what is unknown, the approaches built, the turn, how it can end, what it
changes later, and how it was checked: **T** a test plays it (`test/the-lull.test.ts`, fixture named), **P** a
transcript of it is in [playtest](playtest), **U** written and reachable but not driven by any test.

### 1. The Bride-Gift (Abel Rooke and Wenna Spurling) — *comic, romantic; touches the climax*
- **Place**: Rooke's yard (the chair under the sack); Spurling's chandlery; the Watching.
- **Unknown**: why Abel's chairs come apart. He thinks he must give up the road to marry into the Lull; she wants the road.
- **Approaches**: ask Abel what he loves (Sixways); ask Wenna what she wants (Lull); tell each the other's secret;
  help with the joints for three coin (paid work, which comes apart with the chair: a wrong turn that teaches).
- **Turn**: the chair is the most settling object there is, made to give himself up. He did not mean it.
- **Ends**: a cart-seat, two wide, that holds; a wedding sung "Go you, go you"; Wenna leaving the Lull, back for the
  Working Day as a Hand who means it. Or the fourth chair in pegs and Abel gone up the Tump road alone.
- **Later**: Wenna's Hand counts on the Working Day; fates. **T** (`wedding`), **P**.

### 2. The Waiting Woman (Dorcas and Piran Trewin) — *domestic, elegiac*
- **Place**: the Waiting Woman's sign; Piran's shelves of heads.
- **Unknown**: she is ill and has not told him; he can see her age and has not told her.
- **Approaches**: tell Piran she is ill (grave: hers to tell); tell Dorcas about the heads.
- **Turn**: each was sparing the other.
- **Ends**: Piran goes out over the Tump that night to keep the bar with her; or Dorcas moves in to be ill a day a week
  instead of seven; or nothing, and "it's the walk". With the week back, he walks out to her every evening.
- **Later**: Piran's Hand on the Working Day if he knows she is ill. **T** (`dorcas`), **P**; Dorcas moving in **U**.

### 3. Hester's Monday — *grave; the company's possible fault*
- **Place**: Hester's room; Ruth Coll's kitchen; the Watching.
- **Unknown**: what Tobias will do; why he will not buy physic.
- **Approaches**: poppy from Ruth (eases her; Tobias minds it was bought); a priest's words for the dying (eases her,
  nothing bought); the phial with the black thread (grave; she drinks it at the Watching); bring Simeon, who sits with
  her at the end; or do nothing, and Tobias does it himself that night.
- **Ends**: Hester eased and carried down to the bridge; Hester's Monday from the company's hand; Hester's Watching,
  the town believing the wasting took her.
- **Later**: decides whether Tobias can come down for love or only in grief. **T** (`seventh`, `monday`, `phial`,
  `rites`), **P**.

### 4. The Open Houses (Lettice Pryce and Mabyn Gale) — *comedy as structure; satire of status by giving*
- **Place**: the Pryces' parlour (two cabinets); the Gift Hall; the Watching.
- **Approaches**: accept Lettice's cloak and wear it to the Watching (it comes apart in public); accept it and leave
  before dusk (privately); warn her first, if you know the law in any form.
- **Turn**: humiliated, she gives Mabyn her house out of pure spite, and spite holds. The Chief Rest-warden sleeps in
  his enemy's spare room and is brought cocoa.
- **Ends**: a receipt for the croup that holds, and Mabyn's mother's Lull-cloth for the company; or the Pryces
  homeless and given the house back; or, with Hobday dead, Mabyn sitting with Lettice every Lullday.
- **Later**: whether Hobday is softened decides whether the wardens come at noon. **T** (`law`, `seventh`), **P**.

### 5. On What Holds (Aldith Mote) — *intellectual; reframes the climax*
- **Place**: the Glossary; the Glossary step; the Gardens; the tarn.
- **Approaches**: stack a cairn for her coin (falls); get Hob Linsey, who loves bees, to set a skep for a coin
  (stands); see a gift to win come apart; see spite hold (Lettice's house or Davy's stone).
- **Turn**: it is not wages, and it is not love: it is meaning it. GLOSS 2,214.
- **Ends**: Aldith paints a board she will be told to paint over; tells Corin; carries stones on the Working Day.
- **Later**: told to Corin, the Working Day stops being about wages (she gives Haskett's purse back) and becomes about
  whether anyone who asked for the Lull means to end it. Told to Simeon, he is delighted to be wrong. **T** (`law`), **P**.

### 6. The Old Brock — *danger, with ways round*
- **Place**: the Hangers, the sett, the Gardens at dusk.
- **Unknown**: why nobody in the Lull will lay him (hired harm does not hold; he was killed for a bounty and got up).
- **Approaches**: fight him (a level-five animal, far beyond a novice); take Hobday's bounty and fight (he gets up at
  sunset, worse, and the bounty goes home); kill him without the bounty (holds); honeycomb at the sett from Hob; a
  ranger's way to his drinking place; smoke him out of the valley with flame.
- **Ends**: Hob feeding a neighbour every Lullday; a badger gone over the hill; a second, savage Brock.
- **Later**: the Gardens at dusk. **T** (`brock`; bounty with a company of three), **P**.

### 7. The Dusk Market (Quick Nan, Jenet Hollis) — *a con with a cruelty in it*
- **Place**: Nan's tray on the Green; the Strike Post; the chapel basin.
- **Unknown**: why newborns' ribbons vanish off the Post at the Watching; the Glosser tells Jenet a ribbon that ravels
  was not meant, and she fears she does not love her child.
- **Approaches**: buy a ribbon and watch the dusk; tell Jenet a sale comes undone whoever loves whom; tell Hobday
  (Nan banned); shame Nan (persuasion); threaten her (intimidation; Gurney fights if it fails); keep her talking past
  four so the sunset catches her in the Lull (deception), and the whole Table sees her day fly home; or go in with her
  and carry the tray for a third.
- **Ends**: an ugly blue ribbon tied in a fierce knot; Nan exposed, banned, reformed, or richer. **T** (`ribbons`),
  **P**; the delay, shaming and partnership **U** (their checks are dice).

### 8. The Sixnight — *horror; optional; the cost of the Lull*
- **Place**: the whole valley, dark; the Mill working; the beam; the bell loft; the Gap.
- **Approaches**: lie down open-handed (Hob's lore) and wake on the next Lullday; be set to the loom by the Hands and
  work the six days, coming out with a bolt you wove; read the strikers' beam and find a name missing; face the
  Overseer: lie down before it, sing it the strikers' hymn (a bard, or anyone the Old Shift have taught), fight it, or
  climb past the loom floor by the hoist-beam; take the Wroughts' shillings and the one notched coin; crawl out by the
  Weaver's Gap into Sixways.
- **Ends**: no revelation. The beam changes how Tobias's telling sounds; the notched shilling can be given back to him.
  **T** (`loom`, `sixnight`, `overseer`), **P**.

### 9. Simeon's Return — *a companion's story*
- **Approaches**: told Hester is dying (or that he was wrong), he will come; the wardens turn him back on the shoulder
  before eleven; after eleven, by the thief's sheep-walk, or through the six days by the Gap.
- **Ends**: home, painting boards; at Hester's end; still in Exiles' Row facing the stone. **T** (`simeon`, `sheep`,
  `phial`), **P**.

### 10. Linnet — *romance across the fold*
- **Beats**: her tunes, her fear; the second day she sees a week on your face and says she will count your weeks;
  "Tell Linnet you came back to see her"; a carving made meaning it (the Gift Hall bench) that holds at sunset and
  becomes the first tune with a next bit; on the Working Day, will you be here tomorrow: stay (a promise kept after
  five of your weeks, or broken on the first Lullday evening you are elsewhere), or ask her out over the Tump. She can
  work on the bridge for you, meaning it.
- **T** (`linnet`, and joining the company), **P**.

### 11. The Club (Corin, Davy, Kezia) and the Working Day — *the principal story's own small stories*
- Corin's clock for her grandfather; Davy who wants a war he would have hated on a Tuesday; Kezia in the Club because
  her mother would hate it (spite holds), who can keep her father off the bank, or be locked in if the company sells
  the Club's names to Hobday (whose payment comes undone at sunset; the names stay told). **T** (`seventh`, `sunday`,
  `wages`), names sold **U**.

### Livelihood and pleasure, outside any story
Carting with Abel for four coin a day (work that keeps); selling Lull-cloth, the Long Shift's cloth and the Wroughts'
shillings to Haskett; courtesies at the Gift Hall; the Long Table, which feeds anyone; carving at Piran's bench;
learning a hymn from eleven old people who disagree about every word; lying in the sun on the Green while a man
teaches a boy to whistle.
