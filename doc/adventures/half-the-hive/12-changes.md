# Half the Hive — what changed, from the first conception to the last revision

## From the five concepts to one (Stage 1)

Chosen: *Half the Hive*. Rejected: *The Tax on Words* (a ledger again, and metered talk would spoil talk), *The Long
Drove* (moving places faked; Thrume's titan), *The Ebb Court* (the canonical Plane of Water is not on main: it would
have been a private plane, the very defect Mission 18 is removing), *The Nightingale Assize* (the city again; romance
as a case). See [00-concepts.md](00-concepts.md).

## From the treatment to the revised design (Stage 2)

| Treatment | Revised | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Three trap sites and one perfect one | Every site costs someone; the best outcomes are made (a road, a truce, a gift) | The principal choice had a right answer |
| Only the Searchers dance | The Sisters' own scouts dance too, for bees' reasons | Persuading a non-human mind, not a vote |
| Duty-versus-art lovers | Each secretly sacrificing for the other; crossed if untold | Stock romance made a comedy of errors in a tragic key |
| A genial squire in debt | A naturalist refused the scent three times; a thief for love | Furniture made a person |
| The old woman remembers the brock-bargain | The truce is cut in dance-marks on the chalk, read by anyone who learned the dance | The survey asked to retire that device |
| Act IV after everyone leaves | The Daughters wake the night of the Leaving; the protagonist cannot be in both places | The fourth act had walked off the page |
| The Teague brothers, bribed scouts | Cut | A plot convenience |
| Duke the drone, a fetch | Tibby is the witness to his grandmother's spoon | A child-witness, not an errand |
| No fire-sale | The Leavers' Market: a fifth of value; the Drewes | The setting's own satire |
| The moth part of a plan | Nobody's plan; light forbidden among the dead | Not every mystery is the conspiracy |

## In implementation (Stage 4)

- **Names.** Every surname but Nell's, Corve's, Penhallow's and the Drewes' collided with another work's person
  (Pollard, Abery, Tench, Garrow, Marl, Bellamy, Loame, Fairweather, Coke...): the treatment's cast was renamed.
  Kestrel Dunn and Hannet Coke kept theirs; Linnet Abery became Linnet Amory, Eben Tench Eben Candlish, Ivo Fairweather
  Ivo Gildersleeve, Sabeth and Pim Loame Elspet and Pim Garnock, Agnes Garrow Agnes Mellor, Ursel Marl Dame Brenna
  Breck, Huw Bellamy Huw Penry, Moss Pollard Moss Ashdown, Dagny Thorle Hild Thorle, Josiah Penhallow Crispin.
- **The crossed lovers at the Calling.** If either declared at the Calling the other would hear it, and the cross
  could never happen. Keepers are not asked (custom), and Eben says nothing while his father declares; the player who
  knows watches him keep his face still.
- **The flask became the player's tool too.** Taken or given, it can be poured away, or uncorked at any of the four
  places: the protagonist can choose for the bees, and be at fault for it (the Gallowtree).
- **Ivo's handprint.** Kestrel's "giant's handprint" became a fresh little handprint she took for a ghost child's; it
  is Ivo's, from when he ran away at nine.
- **The country moved** from east beside forest, marsh and mountain (which reshuffled every other country in every
  world and broke eleven tests on main) to south beside mountain only, where it can never take a first-ring march.
- **Weights.** Brocks and moth from level 3 and 4 to 2; the moth beatable by a fellowship with a light.
- **The Sisters drive you out.** Stung, the company is driven down to the Apron rather than stung to death in place
  while it waits (an ordinary player died that way in the first play).

## After the second reading (Stage 6)

- The Apron's first sentence carries the bees (the glance shows only it).
- The watchers of the other princess-cells are Eben's father and Moss's mother, present in the stinging.
- Return visits: each new comb described; the Brow's empty bed, Tobit's trestle, Eben's bench; twenty after-topics;
  stale topics closed (reports, the Stair, the likeness).
- The dark watch is an act ("Sit down beside Agnes in the dark"), broken off when the thing comes.
- The Searchers' notice is nailed up in Smoke End; Halse tells what he has seen of the squire and his tower.
- The Leaving's keeper looks back at you on the Apron, and the princesses' night is named, so staying is a choice.
- Kestrel makes room for Ivo by the handprints at the founding.
- Fates cut back to the fact where they ended on a consoling custom (the moth, Moss); narrator absence-sentences and
  duplicated descriptions cut.
