# Half the Hive — rewards, progression and what persists

## Experience

Forty-two milestones (`growth.kyr`), none for killing as such. The largest: set the last course of the Lantern
Stair (250), named to the hive (250), the squire's gift (250), Linnet and Eben told (200), the old peace with the
brocks (200), the moth gone (200), walked with the Leaving (200), kept the night of the Daughters (200), Nell named
(200), a cast seen (200). A thorough run (the ranger's Far Brow) reaches about 2,300 for the protagonist.

## Coin and goods

| Source | What |
|---|---|
| Kestrel | A cake of wax per site reported (two for the Far Brow); Martin buys at 2, Penhallow at 1 |
| The honey linden | A slab of wild comb a day (climbing): Martin 3, Penhallow 2 |
| The Drewes | Fine candles, at two coin (cheating them) or six (fair); Penhallow buys at 3 |
| The Deathshead | Its wing (worth 5) |
| Hild | Smoker (3), veil (2), a Sister's sting (2) |
| Nell | Her oil (1): half a day's scent |
| Doll, Martin | Rations; bee-bread |

The economy is meant to pinch: a level-1 traveller with ten coin who buys a scent and a smoker cannot also pay the
Drewes fairly.

## Abilities and qualities

Kestrel teaches *hide* (a Searcher reported, twenty experience); Hild teaches *smoke screen* (a smoker carried); a
night beside the Mother endows one member with +1 wisdom, once, before the Leaving.

## Companions

**Ivo Gildersleeve** (bard, level 2) joins when asked along; stays a companion after the Leaving unless he went with
it; if turned out, he can be asked into the world, and goes with the company out of the Wold. **Moss** (warrior, level
2) joins for the Dell if talked into waiting.

## What persists, and where it is recognised

| Fact | Recognised |
|---|---|
| **Named to the hive** | Never needs scenting again; Elspet's face eases; the account; "you smell of honey" in the country below |
| Where the Mother went | The new comb at that site, its shelters, its people's after-talk; the ending |
| The Lantern Stair lit | A way from the Waxwrights' gallery to the Crown; Eben's after-talk |
| The Deathshead dead or alive | The Gallery's sounds at night; Agnes's stick |
| Nell named or barred | Her shop's mark; the account |
| Corve's gift | The framed deed in the chantry porch, or the board on the gate |
| The Daughters | The new queen on the Mother's bed; a pierced or caged cell |
| Ivo | With you, a founder, kept as a Searcher, or on a milestone at the Wold's edge |
| The Wold's word below | Stale once the Mother goes: the first region's innkeeper is told and says so; the notice comes down |
| Your name on the new comb | The founding night, if you danced for the place and walked with them |

The account and ending close the chapter; in a generated world the world goes on, with every one of these facts in it.
