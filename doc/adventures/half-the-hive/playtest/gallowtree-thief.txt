> >hume__dancing_hall
The Dancing Hall is the strangest room you have ever stood in. Its floor is tilted like a roof, steep enough that you would slide if it were not tacky; and its walls are written all over with marks scored into the wax: lines and loops and figure-eights, thousands of them, overlapping, generations deep. Up at the top of the slope a round window lets in the sun, and the Searchers dance on the slope below it: a straight run, waggling, then a loop back round, then the run again.
