# Half the Hive — the smaller adventures

*Each: who, what is unknown, the ways at it, the turn, what follows, and how it touches the principal story. "Played"
names the test or fixture that walks it.*

| 8 | **The Brocks' Dell** | Moss, Kestrel | The truce in dance-marks on the chalk | Read the marks (needs the dance); the old comb from Eben; fire; fight; or talk Moss into waiting | Nobody has read the marks in sixty years | Peace, blood, or a nightly war; Moss mauled if he goes alone | The Dell becomes a real choice; the Sisters dance for it | `dell`, `left-alone` |
