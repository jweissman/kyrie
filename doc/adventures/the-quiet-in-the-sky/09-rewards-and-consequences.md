# The Quiet in the Sky — rewards, progression and what persists

## Experience

Experience comes from what is made, carried, learned and decided, not from what is killed; killing is cheap up here,
and the one killing that is not cheap is the one the work asks you not to do. Twenty-five milestones in `growth.kyr`,
from 100 (the cairns, standing on the Field alive) to 600 (seeing Halt chosen). A full Halt journey raises a first-level
protagonist to level 2 in four days; the module's budget (`"experience": 1000`) matches the library's.

The cost side is real: Jory's six coin, a sheepskin at four, Larum leaf, rations. A careless player runs out of food on
the third day and cannot rest.

## Capability and knowledge

- **The way through the White** (`white_known`): the Pass becomes yours to come and go by.
- **The law of the living hand**: changes every later conversation it touches. Hild, Torold, Mab and Aulus all have
  something to say to someone who knows.
- **Trust** with Torold, Hild, Cuddy, Edith, Wystan, Mab and Aulus opens deeper topics, and in Hild's and Torold's cases
  the decisive ones.

## What persists and is recognised elsewhere

- **In the region**: the chapter closes on the Morning After and the region goes on. Its places are described as the
  outcome left them (Halt trampled or growing; the ring empty; the clear ice over Halvard). Its people stand where the
  outcome put them: Torold at the Coming-in at the Hour if Halvard is in Larum; Edith in Evenhall; Mab and Ansel in the
  banner lanes or with the Sitters; Ned at the Oven or with the Runners. Coming back gets a chapter ("Halt, Afterwards"
  or "The Field, Afterwards"), and Cleeve has something to say about his new column.
- **In Larum**: the sky is loud or quiet; Bet hangs up her pans or sleeps till ten; Jory goes up every seventh day;
  Bet has something to say about what you tell her.
- **In the world behind it**: once the Morning After has come, the module marks its word stale (`placed.kyr`). Innkeepers
  in the generated country next door then tell the `after` talk ("cannot agree whether the dead up the Pass have started
  again or not"), and say their piece to a company that comes back and tells them. That is the shared mechanism; it
  does not yet distinguish the outcomes (see friction).
- **Played in the world**: proven by `test/quiet-in-the-sky-placed.test.ts`. A wanderer from a marsh country hears the
  rumour in the inn of the country next door and walks up the valley road. The Testing happens without them, the Daily
  comes back, the chapter closes and the world goes on running. Larum hears iron on iron, the word in the country
  behind goes stale, and walking back up the Pass shows the roar of the Daily and, where Halt was, churned snow.
