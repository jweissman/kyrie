Halvard Eld lies in the ring of spears in the middle of the Field under his frost. Torold Hame stands over him. At the Hour, every evening, he watches the face for it, and it does not come. Nobody on the Field knows what killed Halvard Eld, and nor, quite, do you. They believe death has come back to Ysgard, for all of them, and so the horns stay silent, and Halt grows, a tent at a time. It is a gentle peace, made of fear and spoons, and nobody knows how long it will last, and nobody asks.
