# The Quiet in the Sky — the smaller stories

Each story below is listed with what is implemented and how it ties to the main story. Status key: **built** means
in Kyr and reachable; **journeyed** means played through by a test from offered choices
(`test/quiet-in-the-sky.test.ts`); **seen** means watched by me as a reader in a transcript or the browser.

## What is local, and what intersects

Entirely local: Heaven-iron, the plough, the Mending Bell (except as an epilogue line), Cassian's blade, Saltash's horn.
Intersecting: bread, the wedding, the song, the bench, the Sitters coming down (all of them reasons for Hild); the strike
(it stops the Testing); the grave (it stands Torold down); Edith (the truth); Mab's lip (the law, by fault); Jory and
Ned (the song, and the father at the edge of the White who can help carry Halvard down).
