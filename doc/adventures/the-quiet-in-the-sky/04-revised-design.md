# The Quiet in the Sky — revised design and adaptation (Stages 2–3)

*The treatment after the editor's attack, made concrete against what Kyr can say. This is what was built; where the
build departed from it, the final critique (`08-critique.md`) says so.*

## What changed from the treatment

| Treatment | Revised | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Ned Noone, a stranger nobody will sort | **Ned Whitlock**, Jory's son, from Larum, killed on the road below the valley two days before, who comes in at noon | Joins Larum to the Field by a person; makes the first scene in Larum mean something else afterwards; rhymes with Ithel |
| Law learned by investigation | Three routes: **by fault** (a cut the protagonist gives in Morrow's yard that does not close at dusk), by reading Halvard's wound with the Sitters' story, or from Edith | The protagonist learns what they are by what they have done |
| Runners' due as negotiation | **The Field has not been cleared in forty days.** If the Runners will not clear it, no Testing can be fought | Puts a caste's strike in the main plot |
| Edith hidden, her fate a menu | **Edith can face Torold** at the ring | The work does not flinch from Halvard having been a murderer |
| Halt's life a counter for the best ending | **Shown on the morning after**, each thing made told if it was made | Peace is as full as the town is |
| Wystan fetches | **Wystan asks; the protagonist's answers are the song**, seeded by a lullaby he is ashamed of; or the elegy | Making |
| Mab and Ansel misunderstand | **Ansel's courtship is a formal challenge**, and Mab takes it as wanting the war back; the halls forbid a marriage across the line | Complication and reversal |
| Gorm's living mercenaries, the *Breathers' War* | **Cut** | Escalation; a villain's motive in a work about grief |
| Pyre, Ice or Larum funeral | **Ice or Larum** | The pyre was the weakest; the Ice joins the Sitters, Larum joins the bell |

## The shape

**Prologue: Larum** (no clock).
- The silence and the insomnia.
- Bet's ballad of Halvard Eld, which cheerfully tells how he burned a fullers' town.
- Jory waiting for a son three days late from Wendham market.
- Nance's two-hundred-year-old sourdough.
- The chapel bell rung at dusk for a reason nobody knows, with I.S. cut on the frame.
- A collector buying heaven-iron.

**Act I: The Quiet** (the first day in Ysgard). Cleeve ("Hour of death? You're breathing."), Halt, the ring, the
halls, the Clear. The breath that steams and every dead face that turns. Ned comes in at noon. At dusk Torold names
the Testing for the fourth morning.

**Act II: The Breather** (days two and three). The town's lives, and the law learned three ways. Hild's request. The
strike. The funeral. The Sitters. Edith.

**Act III: The Testing** (the fourth day). At seven Even goes down onto the Field to fight itself, unless Torold stands
down or the Field cannot be fought on. At the Hour, dusk, everyone rises, or one does not.

**Act IV: The Morning After** (the fifth day). The horns, or no horns. What the town made. Larum's sky, loud or quiet.

## Who decides what

- **Even follows Torold.** He stands down from the Testing if he has buried Halvard (on the Old Ice or in Larum
  churchyard), or if he knows the truth. He knows it if it is spoken at the ring, or the protagonist tells him with
  the proof (the scar, Edith, or Aulus's word).
- **Morrow follows Hild.** Knowing the law and not knowing it spoken, she asks the living protagonist to kill one man
  at the Testing so the fear holds. Refused, and shown a reason to choose peace unafraid (bread risen, a wedding, a
  song), she can be resolved.
- **The Field is fought on only if cleared.** The Runners always clear it; Cuddy can be brought to strike.

## The outcomes (each from causes; the region goes on after its chapter closes)

| | When | What the morning after is |
|---|---|---|
| **The Kept Fear** | The Testing held, and a living hand killed one of Even in it | Peace on a lie; the protagonist knows |
| **The Daily** | The Testing held; all rose; Hild not resolved | The horns at dawn; Halt trampled; Larum hears the sky again |
| **Half a Field** | The Testing held, all rose, Hild resolved; or no Testing, the truth spoken, and Hild not resolved | One hall fights itself every day; the other keeps Halt |
| **Halt** | No Testing; the truth spoken; Hild resolved | Peace chosen without fear |
| **The Long Quiet** | No Testing; the truth not spoken | Peace on not knowing; gentle if Torold has buried his friend |

Edith's fates: taken home down the pass; confessing at the ring; given up to Even, killed, and coming in out of the snow
at the hour she died, to be sorted among her enemy's comrades forever; or left hidden.

Ned's: if Halt holds, Cleeve opens a new column in his ledger, HALT, for those with no hour, and Ned is its first. If
not, he goes to the Runners.

## Adaptation: what Kyr says directly

- **The plane.** `Place ysgard "Ysgard" is plane`, a region within it, and `law(field_country, plane_ysgard)`, so
  spells are weaker there, as Orsino's canon says. Larum lies in no plane, as the generated countries do, so the road
  from Larum to the Coming-in reads as a crossing between planes, which here it is. (The canonical plane module of
  Mission 18 is not on main; see friction.)
- **The law of the living hand.** Kyrie's ordinary death is exactly Ysgard's true death. A dead warrior the company
  kills in a fight is `dead`, and stays so, and a rule says so at the Hour. The dead killing each other is told, never
  fought, because no fight the company is not in needs simulating.
- **The cold.** A measure of the module's (`chill`), raised each watch the living spend out of doors in Ysgard, lowered
  by the warm rooms (the Oven, the halls' fires, the Scrapes, Cleeve's brazier, Larum); a fur bought in Larum raises
  the threshold; at the threshold the cold strikes as a hazard. The out-and-back loop has a reason.
- **The White.** Hidden until the way is known; Jory takes the protagonist through for a fee the first time; a
  wayfinder can find the cairns; without either, it costs.
- **Making.** Each made thing is a fact the town shows: bread (a crock carried against the skin, `nursed`), the song
  (verses as answers), the marriage (two topics and a meeting), the grave (an action with bearers).
- **People changed by people.** Topics with trust measures; Torold's standing down follows a funeral; Hild's resolution
  follows a refusal and a reason.
- **Danger.** Frost wolves on the Teeth; the Sitters' bear on the ice stair; the Even watch at the ring; the Testing
  itself, which kills a living person who walks into it.
- **Return.** Chapters for coming back; Larum's sky told for each outcome; `countries.json` gives an `after`.

## What it cannot say, or says badly (taken up in `friction.md`)

- No authored way to restore a creature defeated in a fight, so a dead companion in Ysgard cannot be made to rise at
  the Hour. Ned is therefore kept out of fights: he joins as one who follows, not as a fighting member.
- A plane is declared privately, a second Ysgard waiting to happen, until the canon module lands.
- No temperature, so the cold is a module measure and its own rules.
- Sparring is not a fight with stakes short of death. A spar is an action with a check, not a bout.
