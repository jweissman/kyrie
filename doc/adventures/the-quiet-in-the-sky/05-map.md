# The Quiet in the Sky — geography

Two countries joined by a pass: **Larum** in Imbar, and **the Field** in Ysgard. The Larum valley lies beyond the west
march of a frost, mountain or forest country of a generated world (`data/adventures/countries.json`). In every world
the library tests it is placed, and it moves no other country. The valley road comes up from that march into the
hamlet. Above the hamlet the Pass goes up into the White, and out of the White onto the Field.

## The places and why they are where they are

- **Larum** sits where the road gives up, because the Pass is the only way through. It has lived for nine hundred years
  on what comes down it: the noise, which it rocks its children to; the broken iron the Runners trade at the head of
  the Pass; and the ballads it prints about the champions of the Field, which it sells in three countries.
- **The White** is where Ysgard's snow spills over into Imbar. Its cairns, each topped with a ram's skull, are older
  than the guide's family.
- **The Coming-in** is where the dead walk in out of the snow. It is also where the living come out of the White,
  which is why Cleeve keeps a brazier nobody else needs.
- **The Field** is five miles between two heights. Its battle-line ran north and south along the Lathe, and that is
  why the iron lies thickest at the ford and why the Clear, scoured by the wind, shows the most history.
- **Halt** grew where Halvard fell, around the ring the watch planted to keep people off him. It is built out of what
  the Field had lying about.
- **The halls** face each other down the Field, dawn and sunset. Morrow's doors face west to the Field, Even's windows
  east.
- **The Scrapes** are under the Field's southern lip. The overhang keeps the snow off, and the place is closest to the
  Coming-in, where the Runners trade.
- **The Old Ice** is where the First Host sat down seven hundred years ago, as far from the battle-line as the Field
  allows.
- **The Teeth** are where the Field ends and you can look out of Ysgard at the living world, as through glass. It is
  where the dead go to remember, and where Halvard went every week.

## What is known before it is reached

The rumour of Larum is told in the country next to it, in the inn of the town the road leaves from: "Larum can't sleep
for the quiet". It is told further off as "the dead who fought every day in the snow above a mountain hamlet have
stopped". The skyline from the country next door shows "a high pass full of a white cloud that never moves". Within
the region the Sitters' cave, Edith's nook and the cairns through the White are hidden until learned. They are learned
from Cuddy, from a breath seen in the lamplight, and from Jory or a wayfinder's search.
