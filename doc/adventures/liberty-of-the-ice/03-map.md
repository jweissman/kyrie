# Pellam on the Lisk: a spatial account

Pellam is laid out as one region, `lisk`, of seven areas on the region map, and forty-one rooms. The river runs from
west (the Flashes, upstream) to east (the Reach, downstream); the city is on the north bank, Bankside on the south,
and for three days the ice between them is a district of its own.

## The areas

| Area | Kind | Rooms | What it is for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The North Road** | wilderness | North Road | The entry, from the title page or across a generated country's west march; where the Score leaves to, past Harker's man at the North Bar |
| **The Flashes** | wilderness | the Flashes | Frozen water-meadows; the quality skating at dusk; Ursel on skates |
| **Pellam** (the City) | settlement | North Bar, Cheaping, Saint Evenhand's, the Wool Exchange, Gosling's study, Fleece Row, Harker's counting-house and parlour, Swan Lane, the Bridge Foot | The wool city, shut by snow: factors gambling on unshorn fleece, Harker's house and his locked desk, the Exchange where the Perpetual Frost Company's shares are chalked |
| **Pellam Bridge** | site | on the Bridge, the rope-walk house, the cellar in the starling | Houses on the arches; Widow Saffery's rope-walk; a hatch (hidden until the starling is known) to a cellar in the third starling where the newly drowned hide, and a rope ladder down to the ice: the one way onto the ice nobody tolls or watches |
| **The Frost Fair** | settlement (on the ice) | foot of Swan Stairs, under the arches, Freezeland Street by the Glove Post, its lower end, the Hiring Ring, the bear-stake, the ox roast, the Court of the Ice, the printing booth, the Frozen Lamb, the Ice Chapel, foot of Hay Stairs | The Fair itself, under the Liberty; a settlement, so nobody camps in the middle of it. Every one of its rooms closes when the ice goes out |
| **The Reach** | wilderness | head of the Reach, the Black Reach, the deck and hold of the Patience, the sledge road south | Past the last booth: grey ice over the deep channel, the hole that never freezes, a frozen collier-brig where the drowned hide, and the road south over the ice; searchers walk it on the second and third nights |
| **Bankside** | settlement | the Bankside end of the bridge, Bankside, the Spotted Calf, the Watermen's Hall, the Clink, the head of Hay Stairs, Hay Wharf, the bondhouse floor and loft | The other Pellam: inns, idle watermen, the debtors' liberty, and Harker's bondhouse with its back on the ice |

## Ways that change

- **Swan Stairs and Hay Stairs** are barred by the watermen's planks until the company shakes on "a penny a stair",
  pays, or (Swan only) steps over and stares a waterman down; once paid they stay open both banks. They close for good
  when the ice goes out, as do the rope ladder and the bondhouse's river door.
- **The starling hatch** is hidden until Widow Saffery shows it.
- **The bondhouse door** is locked (Kemble and Harker have keys; a thief can pick it, seen by Fowke unless he sleeps,
  from one till four). **The river door** is barred inside, iced in: brawn breaks it, or twenty hired with a kettle.
- **The North Road** out of Pellam is watched, once the company has shaken on the flock, by one of Harker's wharf men,
  until the Landing: a thief slips past behind a hay-sledge, a liar tells him a story.
- **The sledge road south** ends at the watermen's channel if they were left with no work on the second night.
