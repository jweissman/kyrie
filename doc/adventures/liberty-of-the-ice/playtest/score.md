An hour passes. It is a long time since anyone slept. The company is weary, and will fight the worse for it until it sleeps.

*(15 waits in which nothing is told)*

**Wait › An hour**

The North Road goes up out of Pellam into the snow. An hour passes.

*(2 waits in which nothing is told)*

**Wait › An hour**

The North Road, above Pellam, in the thaw: the snow walls slumped and dripping, the road a brown river of slush, and below in the bowl of hills the city, and the bridge, and under it, running high and fast and black, the Lisk. From down the Reach, all at once, a sound like a city falling down: the ice going out. It comes up the river from the sea toward the bridge in the dark, crack after crack, louder, the whole frozen Lisk breaking into plates and rearing up against the starlings and going under, and the black water coming up through it steaming, and people on both banks with torches shouting at it, or crying, or cheering. By midnight there is a river under Pellam Bridge, running, full of ice, where Freezeland Street was.

*(9 waits in which nothing is told)*

**Wait › An hour**

In the morning there is a river under Pellam Bridge, and somewhere at the bottom of it, going out to sea, a bargain for two hundred fell ewes at Hawsfold that was shaken on the ice and never landed. Master Harker is thirty coin the poorer and the Squire of Hawsfold is nowhere. In the taprooms of Bankside they are telling it already, with improvements. Of Harker’s hired, they say nothing: they were landed at the Landing, and went south on Ossett’s agent’s sledges at first light, which is the kind of thing nobody tells.
