# The Long Man (Merrowdale)

*Read in full: `dale.kyr`, `lore.kyr`, `kin.kyr`, `trimmer.kyr`, most of `water.kyr`; `wall.kyr` and `charter.kyr` by their rules and discoveries. Branch `adventure/p2-corpus-c`.*

**Premise.** Three days before an autumn storm, a steward is selling bronze "teeth" out of a hill-wall that is holding a lake back, and calling the leak a ghost. A dale of a dozen people, a clock (steward asleep after supper, the Heron's barge up at noon on day two, storm at 312, wall gives at 396) and a long, quietly lethal ordinary mistake.

**What works.** The best-made small module in the set. Everything that happens has a person who wants it (Hob's spade-cuts, Ysolt's sermon, Gammer Coller's rod, Dunnet's wages), and two or three ways to be answered: shame the steward, buy the foreman, mend the wall, draw the lake. Rooms read as places ("the clock is eleven minutes slow and always has been"). Cross-links are already good: the wax rubbing from a sea-church (`lore.kyr`), the almanack woman, Skerrow's lamp-trimmer (`trimmer.kyr`).

**Shared forms.** Five of the six first meetings opened with "X looks (up) at you" and then a remark (Dunnet, Ysolt, Hob, Gammer Coller, Cass). The people at the well are four Topics, all `when true once`: the village has one thing to say, however long the company stays.

**Weak or flat.** The village did not change its talk as the water rose except in two Observes; `Greets again` existed for one person of eight; Hob Teller had two topics and no life after his confession; nothing pointed *out* of the dale except the Heron's departure.

**Dead ends and connections.** The steward sells to a "factor", but nobody in the dale could say who. The rumour in `countries.json` was the only thread to the first region, and the dale never told the country below when it was over.

**Encounter density and danger.** One band (the Mower; clay figures below), the flood itself is the danger. Few fights by design; the time pressure is the encounter.

**Changed.**
- Five first meetings rewritten without the stock look (`dale.kyr`).
- `Greets again` for the seven people who lacked it.
- New `merrow.kyr`: the well-folk say different things when the low field floods, when Pell is known, when the springs run clear; Hob can read the stroke-and-ring on the rubbing as his grandmother's sheep-count; Martin Pask and Cass Rowell say where the bronze goes (the Founders' Exchange, numbered receipts); Mother Ysolt writes to the registry once the steward is exposed.
- `countries.json`: Merrowdale's `after` was already there; nothing to add.

**Not done.** A second, smaller danger in the low field on day two (a drowned-cart scene) would add a local fight-or-flee beat; left because the module's tests assert the exact first-day rhythm.
