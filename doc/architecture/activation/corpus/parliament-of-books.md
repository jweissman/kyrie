# A Parliament of Books

*Branch adventure/p2-corpus-b. Sampled (about 3,770 lines, 32 files): Greets, repeats, endings, post, placed.*

## Premise
Calamy, a city of paper at a river mouth, where every house keeps a book that knows it and the books have started to talk;
the city burns its old almanacks at the Kindling and this year nobody can say whether it ought.

## What works
- A rule-heavy, idea-dense piece (179 Rules, 245 Topics): books that sleep and wake, the Bound and the Quick Sewn, a
  parliament with standing orders, an almanack that answers questions. Eight endings by which accord was reached.
- The central question (what are the books owed?) is argued by people and by books, and the endings do not agree.

## Shared forms and phrasing
- "a long time" x27, "a great many" x24, "nine hundred years" x20, "its own leave" x20, "says the almanack" x16, "every book in" x12: the
  most repetitive of the batch by voice-repeats. Similes: "as if" x59, the highest in the batch.
- Greets: 34 of 66 open "A [adj] [person]". Mid-scene beat "looks up" x13.

## Weak or flat
- Many stock phrases ("a great many", "a long time") mark the narrator's inability to say how many or how long; the
  numbers could be authored (how many books, how many nights).
