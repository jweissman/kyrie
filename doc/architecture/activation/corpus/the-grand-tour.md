# The Grand Tour (Sennet)

*Read: `clock.kyr` (skim), `endings.kyr` (index), the first meeting and return greeting of everyone, the four elemental sections' openings (`fire`, `earth`, `air`, `lamp`), `collegium.kyr` head. `whelm.kyr`, `cloudgate.kyr`, `remainder.kyr`, `harbour.kyr` scanned. It is 510 KB: this review is the thinnest of the nine and says so.*

**Premise.** A port in three storeys: quays and glass-houses and the House of Doors (whose doors open on the planes); Cloudgate, a hill lifted into the sky; the Whelm, the drowned lower town. The Rector proclaims a Grand Tour: bring her the four petals of the Rose of the Winds and go first through the Door of Doors. A young lord has already fallen off the hill. Eleven endings.

**What works.** The scale and the invention (the Courant that prints thresholds on Saturdays, Murrey's handbook in its sixth edition, a sylph who has heard all about you). 157 topics, 123 of them gated by what the company knows. The most deliberate use of `knows` in the corpus: forty `Observe … as sign / revelation / event`.

**Shared forms.** The measured phrase count: "of the House of Doors" 23, "the Rector of the House of Doors" 16, "the Door of Doors" 13, "at the turn of the tide" 13, "She looks at you" 24. The elemental creatures share a skeleton (a delighted address, a tic of speech, a question: "Oh, it's you!", "You're new", "You're quick") and the human officers a second ("Name. Office. Question.": Threlkeld, Cole, Tolley, Pumphrey, Murrey). All of this is on purpose, as a comedy of officers, but it is the one place in the corpus where one sentence skeleton carries eight characters.

**Weak or flat.** Twenty-five `Greets again`, all under 120 characters and most a bare beat ("Mr. Tolley looks up from his pad and goes pink", "Peg Arklow pulls you a pint"). Zero `Objective`: in a module that is literally a race, the page has no aim to lead with. No temperament anywhere; the danger is the Fall and the Undertow, which are rule-driven.

**Connections.** Strong outward already: `countries.json` has a notice and an `after`, and the Collegium of Measures (which the Tour's Rector heads) turns up in the Dorothea's surveyors and the Long Man's letter.

**Changed.**
- Ten return greetings rewritten to carry a thing the person would now say (Pumphrey's new bylaw, Tolley's forty pages of thresholds, Peg's note from a man in a good coat).

**Not done, and why.** A full pass for stock phrasing across 510 KB, and an Objective set for the four petals, deserve their own pass with the module's six tests run after every edit.
