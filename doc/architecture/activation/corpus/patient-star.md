# The Patient Star: Hold One Season

*Branch adventure/p2-corpus-b. Sampled (about 2,700 lines, 20 files): quell, ennick, lore, Greets coverage, repeats.*

## Premise
The Ghessil Uplands: a star that has held in place for a long time, a plateau of granary vaults in which light slows
whatever drinks it. A cutter's uncle has stood eleven months in an open vault with his hand out, and everyone wants
him brought up, or his jars.

## What works
- The conceit is physical (stillglass, umber felt, night honey, Masons, Remembrancers) and the economy is a debt, a licence
  lottery and a mutual.
- Linked outward through `lore.kyr`: the lens of the lamp shown to Lenna Marsh, and the piece of sky that fell.

## Shared forms and phrasing
- 47 Persons and 1 Greets: the first meeting with Dagny, Ness, Pike, Wenda, Coombe, Kerra, Lenna, Aske, Mance, Coyle and
  Ruan was a generated line from Role and Manner. `voice repeats`: "damage to ally unless" x15 and the hazard skeleton
  throughout `corbel.kyr`/`undersun.kyr`; "seri amar ka" x10 is an invented refrain.

## Weak or flat
- The named people had reasons and no faces. Twelve Greets are now authored, each opening on something the person is doing
  with their hands, none of them "A ..." and several in the middle of an action.

## Dead ends
- None found.

## Connections
- Before: the rumour exists (the light on the glass, the people standing in it). No "after". The hold has no ending kyr
  in the standard form (clock.kyr and keeping.kyr carry state), so no word is asserted. See R50.

## Encounter density and danger
One Temperament line (many hazards); the danger is the light.

## Changed

  Aske, Coyle, Ruan), stairhead.kyr (Mance).
