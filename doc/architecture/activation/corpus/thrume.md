# Thrume

*Read: `clocks.kyr`, `voices.kyr`, the people of `saltgrave.kyr`, `shoulder.kyr` and `inward.kyr`, the Objective list. `tomb.kyr`, `hush.kyr`, `dream.kyr`, `between.kyr` scanned. 160 KB, 121 places.*

**Premise.** A salt town where people pay to sleep; a giants' hold on the ridge; a mountain that is a body; a city that farms dreams; a tomb built to kill. A cutting-quota wakes the mountain a little each day; a duke's host crosses the Ebb on the tenth. Eleven Objectives, four ways to end (woken, sung clean, sealed, cut out).

**What works.** The largest map in the set for its size, and the only one in which the company's own calling speaks (the `voice_*` rules: the ranger reads the giants' tracks, the cleric counts the sleepers' breath, the thief counts the pegs). The strongest danger of the nine: eleven `Temperament` lines, a wolf pair that stands between the company and everything, a warder of grey iron twice a man's height.

**Shared forms.** The company-voice rules share a skeleton ("{?m} does a thing with a hand, says a number or an inference, and stops"), used a dozen times; it is a good one, and it works because it is attached to different callings.

**Weak or flat.** Thirty-eight people, **two** Greets: the Thane, the Rememberer, the Provost, the foreman, the Margravine's envoy, the overseer and the landlord entered the page with no first line, and no `Greets again` at all. `Observe … as sign` zero.

**Connections.** The rumour is the only link out. Quillon's Exchange does not appear; the Duke of Harle's host is a clock only. Nothing leaves the fells when the ending comes.

**Changed.**
- `Greets` and `Greets again` for nine speaking people (Varne, Hauk, Hesper Dunmore, Sister Agathe, Gil Sorrow, the Rememberer, Aud Brenning, Tobias Kell, Overseer Brannoc).
- `after`: a salt-road innkeeper tells the company that the salt did not come for a week and then did.

**Not done.** Greetings for the Court's own (the Abbess, the Weigher, the Lady Lull), which have states I did not want to flatten; the tomb's second danger. See `deem-proposals.md`.
