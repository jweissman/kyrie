# Sallowfield: Three Nights at Sallowfield

*Branch adventure/p2-corpus-b. Read: places, folk, the opening of clock/arcs/parley, battle skimmed; wake read for endings.*

## Premise
A ford, a mill and sixty souls on the Brimble; a coach with a broken axle; an unpaid company of the Margrave's
border-soldiers coming down the east road behind it. Three nights to turn a village into something an unfed army will
decide is not worth the cost, or to buy it off, or to walk away.

## What works
- It is a siege as bookkeeping: `thrust(place)` against `defence(place)`, what Wyre has seen (`sawFord`, `sawBarricade`)
  shaping what he does. The enemy is reasoned with, not beaten: a scout in the cellar, a parley at the stone, a
  lieutenant who can be bought. Fourteen endings, none of them a victory screen.
- The villagers each carry a nerve value and a single small arc (Davy and his sling, Ellen Cragg and the number of
  arrows, Mowbray and the plate); the Greets are broad, funny and exact ("He has dealt with soldiers in nine different
  ways since you came in and each way has been worse than the last").

## Shared forms and phrasing
- "The X does not look up" in Greets (Teasdale, the troopers, the raiders, Orpe in Tallow): reworded for Teasdale and
  Kersey here. Greets that end on a physical beat followed by dialogue are the corpus's default, and they are
  well done here.
- Wayside types: the loud carter (Wadsworth, as Skerrow's Gaff), the frightened clerk (Elland, as Skerrow's Piers),
  the dwarf smith (as Tallow's Usk).
- `voice repeats` is quiet: "the east road" x12 (a place), "the picked men" x6 (a faction). The most self-quoting prose of
  the nine is the battle summary.

## Weak or flat
- Eleven passengers were spoken of in one Greets and never seen: the coach was a prop and its people had no share in the
  three nights.
- Nobody left the valley: no ending sent word anywhere. The Margrave was a name.
- No Temperament anywhere: danger is entirely the clock and the arithmetic, so a company that does not read the
  situation meets no creature at all before the assault.

## Dead ends
- None found. `walked_on` and `coach_goes` both leave cleanly.

## Encounter density and danger
Low encounter count, high stakes concentrated in night three. A careful reader loses no one before then.
