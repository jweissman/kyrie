# Skerrow: The Light on Scarth Holm

*Reviewed on branch adventure/p2-corpus-b. Read: places, scenes, sea, caller, folk, morning, the start of wake; night.kyr and the endings skimmed.*

## Premise
A lighthouse on a rock at the end of a causeway that dries for three hours at noon. A company that crosses after twelve
sleeps on the holm with six strangers, a dog and a keeper, and something comes up out of the cistern with the tide to say
the names of the drowned. One night and a day and a second night; the way out is the same causeway, at noon.

## What works
- The rules are the story and nobody is told them: the lamp's oil is time, the bell sends it back for an hour, the Caller
  goes where the company is, and one frightened answer costs a person. A player infers; the cast is where they learn.
- Every person has a want, a secret and a verbal tic written in the file's header (Seddon short, Hettie food and the
  dead in one breath, Gaff loud, Piers qualifying, Dib asking without waiting). The Seddon and Hettie thread (`love`, the
  clock stopped at four) is the finest small piece of character play in the nine.
- Room texts are specific and unpadded (the glove "taken the shape of the sill", the ring on the hatch "wet").

## Shared forms and phrasing
- Seven Greets, seven opened with "A [adjective] [person] [doing a thing]" (the corpus-wide stock opening; see quittance.md
  for the extreme). Four of the five tea-table Greets reworded here so the first meeting opens on a different element
  (the loaf, the price, the satchel, the boy's questions).
- `voice repeats`: "says a name" x8, "its hands open" x7, "the iron stair" x7. The eight `caller_takes_*` rules are one
  sentence skeleton (it arrives with hands open, says a name, someone goes). It reads as a refrain, but not at eight
  uses; the rules differ in setting only. Left as it is (their texts are keyed by tests); see R51.
- Duplicated NPC type: the rope-seller (Corrie) and the drover (Gaff) are the corpus's wayside-trader pair, as in
  Sallowfield's carter and Tallow's storekeeper.

## Weak or flat
- The kitchen, where the company will spend most of the first night, changed hardly at all between evening and morning.
- Gaff's cheeses, Corrie's rope and Piers's Board were closed jokes: nothing led anywhere off the rock.

## Dead ends
- None structural. The three non-fatal outcomes (Everyone Out, The Light Goes On, Alive and Apart) are all distinct.

## Connections
- Before: the rumour named no one. Now it names Seddon, and the country has an "after" (the coast road stops telling
  people to cross before twelve; a darker version if the keeper or the widow was taken).
- Out: `befell(scarth_kept)` / `befell(scarth_lost)` asserted when the company has crossed; Skeel's Loveday Kitto writes
  them in her night log (a lamp-keeper reading another's light). In: `remainder_kept` is read by Piers (the Board's list).
- Gaff's road goes to "the market in the first town off the main road, where the innkeeper has a contract".
- Possible: Dib's wreck ("last winter") could be Skeel's Constancy; the Tide Board's notice to discontinue the light could
  be the same Board that licenses the Reach.

## Encounter density and danger
One lethal presence on a clock, no fights. High danger per hour, no combat to repeat. Correct for the form.
