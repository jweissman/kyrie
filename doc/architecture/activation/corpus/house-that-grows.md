# The House That Grows (Strewnfield, Cobble Ring)

*Phase Two corpus review, branch `adventure/p2-corpus-a`. Read in full: `cobble-ring`, `attention`, `lowmere`, `keel`, `lore`; `annex` and `slope` from the cast, the Dace/Haddo/watchers sections and the endings.*

**Premise.** A giant's bedroom of glass fell into Haddo's barley; it grows wherever nobody is watching, so a crater town sits up with it on stools. The company is a stare-student in Kezu's kitchen the morning after Kezu shut her eyes on purpose to let a girl's door open. Beyond the barley the house reaches up into the Keel, the underside of a flying city, and a girl called Neme has knocked at its front door for two and a half thousand years.

**What works.** The rule is the story: every scene is a decision about who is looking at what (stools, Ithren lamps, the child's chair, Tobu as a held room). Kezu sleepwalking toward a door she is slowly dreaming is the best clock in the corpus: it asks for attention rather than for time. Quill/Ottie and Sennu Arru's red door is a quiet second spine, and the three endings are different in kind. The Orrel/Sabe link and the Dace to Corran to Low Water line are the finest cross-adventure threads in this set.

**Shared forms.** Almost none: the author wrote a distinct voice (flat, domestic, unexplained). The one stock move is the closing aside in speech ("He wrote it down.", "I told Dace."), used with taste.

**Weak or thin.** Cobble Ring itself was the thin part: five talkers with one to three topics each, no return visits once a thing was said, and the town reacted to the vote only through the reeve. Lowmere was one woman and a loom.

**Danger and density.** Low by design (glass mice, a wind hazard, three riggers). The danger is losing the room's state, which is a different kind of encounter and a good one. A cold-start player meets six topics and a door within a minute.

**Connections.** To the first region: the rumour in `countries.json` is good; there was no consequence leaving. To other adventures: Sabe Orrel to Orrel on the Dorothea; Dace's Exchange to Corran to Low Water's dreg lenses.
