# The Book of Faces (Ombra)

*Corpus review. Sampled only: prose census, tic counts, the persons and endings list, `placed`, `lore` hooks. At 1.8 MB it is a quarter of all the adventure Kyr; not read whole.*

**Premise.** A delta city where it rains nine days in ten and everyone goes masked; the Registrar who kept the book of who is who is dead; unless the Drawing is read on the ninth night, every Office dissolves at midnight. The Watch has hired a stranger.

**What works.** One of the most coherent ideas in the corpus, carried through six callings (`bard`, `cleric`, `thief`, `warrior`, `monk`, `mage`) that each read the city differently, 129 milestones and ten endings. A hundred persons and almost five hundred topics; the silence is characterisation ("Not at noon... I have said nothing about noon").

**Shared forms.** "Does not look up" (45) and "says nothing" (43) are the moves of the book, and they belong to a city where looking up is an offence; this is a voice, not a tic, but at that frequency it needs a pass to find the ones that no longer land. "Looks at the" appears 103 times. Clerks and wardens recur as NPC types (eight clerk roles, six wardens).

**Weak or thin.** Little for the company to *do* with the hands in many scenes; hazards are eight in 1.8 MB.

**Connections.** First region: a notice with a torn bottom edge and a pencilled "Nine days. It is always raining.". Others: the almanack woman at the Gate.

**Done.** Nothing edited: too large to improve responsibly by the sample I could read.

**Left.** The pass on silence; a cull of one Sleeve.
