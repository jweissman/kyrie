# Low Water (the Bowl of Ammerlin)

*Corpus review. Sampled: place and prose census, endings, `lore`, `placed`, the topic count per person; not read whole.*

**Premise.** A crater city's lake has been falling for three years; the Office of Measures needs a stranger to measure it. The answer lies under the lake: a copied city, a count the Founders left, a voice that wants to be received.

**What works.** The densest conversational adventure of the nine: 24 people hold 217 topics. Twenty endings, each phrased as a verdict on a method of counting. The Exchange carter and the bell-teeth are a model for how a first-region scenario reaches in.

**Shared forms.** "A pause." (eleven) and "for a long time" (eleven) lead the counts; "old script" appears seven times, each time a source of a secret. The wheel rooms (the eighth wheel, the makers' room, the planes) repeat one beat of a locked wheel under a sign by design; the repetition is the monument, but the third reads as the first.

**Weak or thin.** No milestones and no module experience, though it is long and branching. Several wheel rooms are the same beat three times. The surface (the Bowl above the water) has fewer people than the depths.

**Danger and density.** Three hazards, two temperamental parties; the danger is moral and arithmetical.

**Connections.** First region: factor/carter scenario (excellent); the rumour is good. Now an `after` by verdict (accord / false count). Others: Dace of Cobble Ring sells dreg to lens-grinders here; the almanack woman.

**Done.** `word`/`after` wiring. Not edited otherwise: I judged it did not need the depth work more than the others and that a milestone budget there is a design decision (see the requests).
