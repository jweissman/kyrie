# The Wrong Boat

*Read: `folk.kyr` head, `scenes.kyr` head, `places.kyr`, `hunt.kyr` (structure), endings list in `wake.kyr`, all first meetings.*

**Premise.** A comic chase on the Aldwater. A company boards the wrong barge, carrying the wrong hamper, and a toll-clerk who has lost her late husband's music box is coming after them. Twenty people, forty-two places, thirty-four endings, the shortest, loudest module in the set.

**What works.** The register is perfect and held (everything shouted, everyone certain, everyone wrong: Jack Parr's thirty-plus verses; Mrs Brabant crying into the stock because it improves it). Real pursuit logic: Bess and the lads go to where the company is, by the ways that are open, and stop when they are there. Thirty-four endings are what a farce should have.

**Shared forms.** Nearly every first meeting is a *shouted opening* followed by an *explanation by a person who is wrong about it*; it is the module's engine and not a tic. The one thing that is a tic: the Parr/Dolly/Cobbold/Shim voices are all upper-case shouters and are hard to tell apart on the page.

**Weak or flat.** `Greets again` for none of fifteen. The hunt file is sixty hand-expanded `Pursuit` lines (30 places × 2 chasers): the same line with the place changed. That is not narration, but it is the one place where this module is not data.

**Connections.** The rumour is the only link out. There is a barge called the Heron, and a Heron and Pipe, in a module whose neighbour's barge is also the Heron; nobody had noticed.

**Encounter density and danger.** The chase is the encounter. The danger is a beating (`beatenUp`), a lock-up, and a hand over hand across the weir.
