# Intimations of a larger world: the threads left in the starting region

*11 November 2026. Phase One's obligation to the campaign that comes later: credible intimations, no revelations, no new canon. Everything here is implemented in `data/generation/town/threads.kyrt` (drawn per settlement, none twice in a country, different in every seed) or in the posting-house (`roadstation.kyrt`), the Hall's muniments and the old stones. Each is something a person has, says or keeps, with two readings; none decides between them.*

## What each leaves open

| Thread (where) | What the player can learn | What it does **not** say | Foreshadows |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The bright coin** (a trader's cord) | an Imperial piece of a regnal year higher than any list allows; two scholars, two verdicts | whether it is forged or the Empire kept striking | the Empire's end is not an end; a late issue |
| **The late coin in the posting-house's fisc-chest** | the same coin, in a chest sealed for centuries | who put it there, or whether the day-book was wrong | the same |
| **The bronze credential** (a traveller's grandmother's) | a courier's pass "by the Office of the Road" from a *Prefecture of the Further Shore* that no clerk can place | where that is | something of the Empire survives elsewhere |
| **The factor's letters** (a merchant's) | a lord over the water "being shown to the guilds as the one the roads are being kept for"; salt falling a third where he is liked | who, or what roads | a new king, a court, an emerging order |
| **Two chronicles** (a shrine's) | one says the Empire's last prefect crowned the kingdom's first king; the older says no prefect existed | which is true | rival historical interpretations; the kingdom's pedigree |
| **The veteran** (a well-side old soldier) | a column under a wheel-and-wing standard that spoke no tongue he knew, over the water | whether they were in earnest or players | the Empire (or its heirs) elsewhere |
| **The Keepers of the Road** (a preacher) | a movement that says the road is a gift to be kept for its givers' return and tells folk to pay the numbered posts | whether it is faith, fashion or policy; who funds it | religious movements pursuing their own objectives |
| **The black plaque** (an innkeeper's doorstop) | columns of marks in no script, sums that come out true however added, older than the Empire | what they were sums *of* | the Mathematical Age, kept apart from the Aenean |
| **The levy** (a notice on a door) | a war levy and a bushel quietly redefined up the line | what the kingdom is running out of | a present kingdom's ambitions and vulnerabilities |
| **The grant of the road** (the Hall's press) | fifty paces either side of the road belong to the road; the clause stops "as far as the Works" | what the Works is | the Mathematical Age; why the Posts bent a road |
| **The painted road's grey blank** (posting-house hall) | a bend, one word: THE WORKS; the stationmaster's complaint about a detour | the cause | an arcology (designed, unbuilt) |
| **The Hall's letter** (steward, after the quarrel) | the family at court "would like the names of whoever read the paper" | what they want them for | a court and a royal family beginning to look for readers |
| **The scored-out word on the dais** (Isaure's paragon) | the Empire named the hall a judgement-seat; the makers' figures hold rods | what is being measured | the Empire as misreader of older things |

## How a player might notice, and is not told

The motifs recur across threads on purpose: a **wheel with a wing** (coin, standard, post, lead seal on the grant), the **Fourth Stone** (day-book, clerk's wedding), **the Works** (map, grant), **the Office of the Road** (credential, tariff). A company that has heard three of them can ask each companion what they make of it (Isaure, Anselm and Wystan each answer once, differently, and none says it is one thing: "that is what one does with a pattern before it is a theory").

## What is deliberately not here

No hidden imperial rump state; no cause of the Empire's disappearance; no named king; no time travel; no cult; no Dark Lord. Each thread has a prosaic explanation that remains available.

## For the campaign's later authors

A thread is the smallest unit of foreshadowing: a person, an object, a fact, two readings. To add one, write a section in `threads.kyrt`; the draw gives it to some countries and not others. To *pay one off*, give the fact's `Entity` a reader in a later adventure (a topic or objective that asks `knows(party, …)` after an authored country's own material has been entered); no thread has a dependent, so none will break if it is not drawn.
