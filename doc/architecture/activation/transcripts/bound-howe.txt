## 1. From the starting town, alone: a first-level warrior

`bin/kyrie play --world kahitude --origin debt --as human/warrior/Mira` and the steps below. The walk from Thornhaven to Brockminster, the rumour, the walk to the mound, the sheep-fold's salt, the watch's toll, the warden's gift of the tally-stick, the camp, the ring. Walked, not set down: every step is a way or an act the page offered. It stops at the ring, where the page says something is lying in wait in the forecourt and shows the two iron-capped shapes on the grass; one first-level warrior should not go on.

## 2. A prepared company of four at level four, in a country whose Kin sealed themselves in (seed `a1`)

`scripts/play-party.ts` over `DrawnWorld.start` and `Journey` (the same page and labels); the company is set down at the camp to skip the walk, then every step is a label the page offered.
