# Mission 13: plan

## Benchmarks (judged by playing, at 1440, 820 and 390 wide)

1. **Arrival.** A first-time player understands within ten seconds where they are, who is there and what they
   could do, and wants to do something.
2. **A conversation.** Speaking to someone changes the screen: their painting is large, their words are theirs, the
   player's replies are replies, and leaving returns to the place.
3. **A journey.** Taking a road shows going somewhere: from where, through what country, how long, arriving. It stops
   only where something happens.
4. **Something strange.** A trap, a wonder or a mechanism is shown as a thing with parts and possibilities.
5. **A fight.** Who is fighting whom, how each stands, what can be done, what just happened, at a glance.
6. **Two settlements in one country are different places** in why they exist, how they are laid out, who lives
   there and what goes on.
7. **Walking for half an hour without a quest finds something worth following.**

## Order of work

1. Art direction: three prototypes of the same states, compared; one chosen.
2. Paintings: what each one shows (sitter, sex, age, station, setting), and where to crop it; chosen by that.
3. The world knows a conversation: talking to someone is a Kyr act, with greetings and farewells from the voice
   tables by bond, manner, hour and memory.
4. The new reader: one composition, five attentions (place, person, road, thing, fight).
5. Settlements from their reason for being.
6. Roads and wild country with life of their own: travellers with errands, traces, consequences.
7. Saves without the compiled country; a faster cold begin.
8. Orsino: riddles, shops, aspects, undead, fighting objects.
9. Kyr: making things in play, who sees whom, observations of variable subjects, fatigue.
10. Prose: registers, memory of places, rhythm on roads.
11. Playthroughs in fresh worlds without privileged knowledge; visual review; reports.
