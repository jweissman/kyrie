# Hidden Reads: 26 November 2026

## Finding

`WorldReads` records calls made through `World`; it has no visibility into `Campaign.readout` or the live `CreatureStore` (`src/lang/world.ts:121-123`, `src/campaign/campaign.ts:197`). The conger reproducer retained `Guarded.keeper(campaign, 'gate__well')` by those world reads, then disagreed with a fresh answer after the party took damage. At walk turns 44 and 45 the held value was `gate__conger`; the fresh value was `undefined`.

The data omitted from the read set is the current creature record, especially hit points. `DangerSense.setup` reads `campaign.readout` and keys a reckoning from each fighter's hp, mana, conditions, equipped inventory and daily uses (`src/presentation/danger-sense.ts:108-120`); `DangerSense.standing` filters on `state.hp > 0` (`:177-178`). `Campaign.readout` returns `CreatureStore.live` (`src/campaign/campaign.ts:197`), while store changes advance `CreatureStore.version` outside `World` (`src/creature-store.ts:24-25,112`). Creature projection records a group's `able` state (`src/campaign/creature-facts.ts:195-196`), but that boolean does not change when a living party member loses hit points. The held keeper therefore remained valid by world stamps while its danger verdict had changed.

`Guarded.felling` also enumerates `campaign.readout.inhabitants` directly (`src/presentation/guarded.ts:37`). The fix is at the owning boundary: `Campaign.readout` calls `WorldReads.all()` before exposing the current state (`src/campaign/campaign.ts:198`). Any enclosing world-read computation is therefore unkeepable whenever it reaches this mutable, non-World data, whether through `Guarded`, `DangerSense` or another caller. The fresh-equals-kept walk passes with this declaration.

## Reproduction

The added assertion in `test/gate-well-conger.test.ts` checks the retained keeper against a fresh computation after each accepted route turn and each `fight:auto` turn. Before the fix it failed at turns 44 and 45 with `{ held: 'gate__conger', fresh: undefined }`. After the fix the targeted run passed (1 pass, 3 filtered; 14.33 seconds). The pre-fix run took 15.12 seconds.

## Recommendation

Choose **(c): computations that read mutable state outside `World` are unkeepable unless that input is explicitly part of their key**, and enforce that rule with a lint. `WorldReads.all()` is the existing opt-out and makes this repair small and fail-closed. It costs reuse for the affected outer computation; `DangerSense` still has its own state-keyed reckoning cache. A lint should inspect callbacks passed to `WorldMemo`, `KeptReading`, `KeptParts` and `KeptGroundings`, including reachable helpers, and reject reads of `campaign.readout`, `campaign.state`, `tickState`, creature stores or history unless they are explicitly represented in the cache key or the computation is marked unkeepable. The history-length cases above show why matching only direct `readout` property access is insufficient.

Option (a), synthetic revisions visible to `WorldReads`, could preserve more reuse, but a correct token must cover all inputs: `CreatureStore.version` alone misses `Campaign.current` fields such as party membership and elapsed time, and a coarse campaign revision would invalidate broadly. Option (b), moving health, conditions, daily use and inventory into world facts, duplicates the currently authoritative creature store and expands projection writes on every change. Neither is as small as the fail-closed rule.

## Verification and Limits

Measured: the focused walk diverged at turns 44 and 45 before the fix and passed afterward (23.16 seconds, within the requested 30-second timeout); the folder-cycle, deep-import and register-placement checks pass after moving the guard to `Campaign.readout` and restoring the feature map. `typecheck:fast` and `lint:fast` passed. `check:fast` ran 2,438 tests with 2 transitional failures: the temporary deep imports and OR-1219's missing feature mapping; both were corrected and their focused checks pass. `test:small` ran 1,176 tests with 5 failures: the same temporary folder-cycle issue and feature-map edit, both corrected, plus three terminal/company behavior specs (headless wording, a fallen-company ending and terminal colour). Those three were not rerun after the final repairs. The full lanes were not rerun after the focused fixes.

Not checked: a full CPU/quality profile, browser rendering, the same-length history-amendment case, or the independent `DangerSense.foreseen` key concern. Those remain explicit follow-up evidence, not acceptance claims for this fix.
