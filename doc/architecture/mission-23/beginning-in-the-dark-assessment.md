# Beginning in the dark: a design assessment

The lead, 18 November 2026, answering the owner's note (beginning-in-the-dark.md). A hypothesis weighed, not a plan;
nothing here is commissioned or built.

## What already supports it

- **Darkness and light are real state.** A place without light says "It is pitch dark. Without a light you can see
  nothing here, and do nothing that needs seeing"; lights are conditions with a duration and a line when they run out
  (a candle, a torch, a lantern, the Light spell); `Need: Dark, a light to hand` is said; a light running out is told
  mid-act and now mid-journey (TR-01). Lighting a candle is already a first act with a perceptible result.
- **Perception already gates what is offered.** The glance's lenses (here, people, things, ways) are built from what
  the company perceives; observations carry kinds (feature, fixture, sign, near) and teach facts; a thing is focused
  and its acts are its rows (the riddle at the speaking door is an engine focus as of today). The rail's marks are made
  only of rows a lens draws (LensRows), and the page may make no control of its own (OR-94). Nothing needs tutorial
  logic for a lens to be empty in the dark and full in the light.
- **Authored enclosures exist and persist.** Generated deep sites (the barrow, the undertown under Porthalen: a lettered
  flag, a sealed vault, a keeper), the iscoed gate-well (dark, rising water, the pawl in the silt, the sawn pin), the
  Silent Lord's oubliette with its alleged royal rider. What is done in them is world state and is saved.
- **Choices already arrive as engine steps.** Creation is engine-owned (CreationFlow: each step the engine's question,
  committed by a long press), including today's first-spell steps; the origins are Kyr modules bound into the world
  (data/origins: the drove, the weir), and the sky's gift is a perk chosen once.

## The gaps it would expose

1. **Senses other than sight.** Darkness now abolishes everything that "needs seeing", and observations do not say by
   which sense they are had. The note's own condition (hearing, touch, memory, darkvision) needs observations typed by
   sense and a projection that asks the body which senses it has. This is the real architectural work, and it is
   general: it would also mend the cold plays' "aim knew what I had not seen" (CL-56).
2. **A character begun incomplete.** A campaign needs ancestry, calling and level before its first watch (hit points,
   spells and checks are computed from them). Creation during play means a character whose calling, background or
   specialization is a later commitment: a fact written once, at the moment it becomes relevant, with the SRD's numbers
   applied then. The safe first form is to defer only what does not change the numbers (background, relationships, a
   perk, a specialization within a calling); deferring the calling itself is a much larger change. A fully specified
   start stays as it is.
3. **The enclosure placed in the world.** An origin binds today to a town and its rooms. An enclosure under a hall, in
   a ravine or an arcology wants the placement vocabulary the maturity track's step 8 (MT-08) would give: near a road,
   under a named site, reachable to a town.
4. **Shared shape without sameness.** The structure (isolation, perception, investigation, danger, discovery,
   emergence) is the critique's warning in another form: a fixed sequence is a tutorial. The Story/Incident claim of
   the adventure-structure brief (ST-08) is the right size: each opening authors its own incident and beats in Kyr,
   Deem varies the mechanism, the danger's nature and who else is there, and no introduction framework is built.

## What is promising, and what is the risk

- **Promising:** the first act is meaningful and physical; the interface is learned as perception rather than taught;
  the character becomes particular through what they do there (a background confessed when a guard asks, a perk taken
  when a gift is needed); and the opening is part of the world, returned to later.
- **The risk is the conventional tutorial dungeon:** a lever, a rat, a key, a door, the amnesiac hero. The critique of
  18 November says exactly why it fails: a situation with no human claim. The opening needs someone who put the
  character there, or wants them kept there, for a reason the world goes on acting on; a danger with an alternative to
  killing; and an exit straight into a social situation, not a corridor to the map.

## The smallest credible slice

The iscoed gate-well at night, for the weir life, because every piece of it exists: the company falls or is put down
the well (the predicament told in two sentences); the first act lights the stub of a candle; the light shows the
chain, the silt and the pawl; the pawl's sawn pin is the investigation and the turn; the eels and the rising tide are
the danger, met by waiting for the ebb as well as by a blade; the smugglers' rope on the ledge is the way out and the
human claim (whoever sawed the pin knows someone is down there); the hamlet above, and Nest, are the wider world and
the first relationship. One background fact (how the character came to know eels, or tides) is asked when it first
matters. Measured as the route is now: the beat map, one cold play, the harness for what moved.
