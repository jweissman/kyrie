# The first expedition into Obscurusport, as played

Written for: anyone judging whether the mine is worth going into. This is a record of one ordinary play of the
generated world `wren` (Hillfells) by the words on the page, made with the text harness that the browser's labels
mirror; `test/long-expedition.test.ts` plays the same steps and checks that the record replays and that a game
saved halfway resumes to the same end. Nothing was set up by hand: the company begins on the road into Gothgiba,
learns of the mine at the inn, and does everything by choices the page offers. Each bold line is a choice taken;
under it, where the company is, the clock, how hurt they are, and their coin; then what the page said. A scene is
given the first time it is read, not again.

What the record shows: the rumour, the way and the story heard in town; a companion hired; light, a bar and a
ration bought; the walk out over the fell; the dark adit and a torch; two riddle caskets opened; three goblins
fought (Thoradin falls); an arcane glyph read for what it is and then made safe with the pole; a search that the
light gave out on, broken off and made again; the air shaft found by its draft, with an earlier party's camp and
notebook; a wonder; ore and the Delvers' valuables carried up the iron rungs, a second way out; all of it sold to
the smith (393 coin, and the same in experience); Thoradin raised at the shrine for 300; a glossary of the old
script bought, for the words on the wall further down; a night at the inn.
