# The first hour: the evidence of cold plays 4 to 9 (20 November)

This is the evidence base for the lead's first-hour design. It holds facts and quotes, not recommendations. The
sources are the cold plays' reports and logs in doc/architecture/mission-22/cold/. Quotes are the players' own words
unless marked as the game's.

**How turns become minutes.** Each turn is taken as about 25 seconds of a player's time, the middle of the 20–30 s
estimate. That gives these windows:

| Window | Turns |
|---|---|
| The first 30 seconds | T0–T1 |
| The first 5 minutes | T0–T12 |
| The first 15 minutes | T0–T36 |
| The hour | T0–T144 |

The minutes given below are T × 25 s, rounded.

No play reached an hour at that rate. The longest, cold play 9, ran 114 turns, about 48 minutes; cold play 4 ran 115.
Two plays ended early in death: cold play 6 at T39 (about 16 min) and cold play 7 at T25 (about 10 min). In the tables,
"the hour" is therefore what the play reached.

- All the plays are in iscoed. Five began on the drove road at Porthalen (4, 6, 7, 8, 9); one began at the weir at
  Gatesend (5).
- Cold play 8 was an earlier local play with no report and no score.
- Cold play 9 was played on the deployed page. From about T86 its driver could not work the page's controls, and that
  stretch measures the driver, not the game (cold-9-triage.md, GR-24).
- Cold plays 2 and 3 (6/10 each) are before this window. Cold play 4 itself gives cold play 3's score as 6.

## Across the plays

### What reliably delights
- **The drove opening's evidence**, in all five drove plays (4, 6, 7, 8, 9): the tally-stick, Owain's count and the
  drowned four. "Clear, satisfying" (6); "consistent, contradicting evidence that you can test" (7); "Nice physical
  evidence" (8).
- **The first small success comes early, about 5–9 minutes in.** Bran is freed at T11 (4), T12 (6), T15 (8) and T22
  (9); in 7, about T14. The weir's gate shuts at T23 (5). The quickest is ~5 min (4); 9's is the latest, ~9 min.
- **Mardle's moral grey and the reeve's ruling:** "Third drove this year" (4, 6); "I have three children" (8, 9).
- **A mechanism solved by looking and preparing:** the pawl and sawn pin (5), the sea-gate (4), the lettered flag (6),
  the pole beating the runes (4).
- **Light as a clock:** "light is a real clock. Good tension" (8); "real light pressure, I like it" (9); in 6, "Light as
  a resource works".
- **A way through without a fight:** the black-candle man talked down (6) or crept past (9).
- **People with their own voice:** Wystan Hale and Orla Pennick (5), Leif naming the dead (9).
- **Rewards with character:** "go with a light that won't blow out" (5, 9).
- **Small ordinary acts:** watering the drove (8, 9); Patch the dog (9).

### What reliably bores or stalls
- **The opening wall of rows:** about 50–60 rows at T0 in 4, 5, 6, 8 and 9.
- **Acts and aims that name what the player has not met:** the factor or Guldríce agent (4, 6, 8); Gek and Tegwen (5);
  Mardle (4, 9); aims "from nowhere" (4, 6, 8).
- **Threads that drop after the first success:**
  - Owain and the drove: "What do we do?" with nothing to answer (4); "Talk to your cousin printed nothing" (6); "Owain
    turns to you." (8); "no tell Owain/Bran option" (9).
  - The sawn pin (4, 5).
  - Joss Hatherly (6).
  - Caer Brynach, with no aim and no route (6, 8, 9).
- **"Nests" that are two weak beasts:** 5, 8, 9.
- **People who do not answer what was done:** Nest (5); Owain (4, 8).
- **Horizons named and unreachable:** Gatesend (7 died on the road; 9 could not leave); Brynach (6, 8, 9); the travel
  to Gatesend stopping every hex (4).
- **Sudden deaths:** "Press on to the end" or Attack resolving a lost fight in one move (6, 7).

### Which opening produced the best first 5 minutes

**The weir (cold play 5, one play).** By T10, about 4 minutes in, the player had:
- a dead aunt's weir and a deadline ("Spring tide's in six days");
- "two smokes on the ridge";
- a gate-well in the dark, a pawl found by foot;
- a pin "sawn through, not worn", which the player called "Sabotage! Now I really care".

The report: "The first twenty minutes are the best opening I've had in a text game for a while." Against it, its 30
seconds were the weakest: "Who is Tegwen?", a lease picked up by reading a notice, and "~60 entries".

**The drove road (cold plays 4, 6, 7, 8, 9).** Its 30 seconds are the stronger: "Strong hook" (6), Owain's "We heard
them in the water" (6), "Strong opening prose" (8). Its first 5 minutes are consistently praised as clear and
satisfying, but none of them has a strange or wondrous event. Its first such moments come at 10–27 minutes:
- Joss's body (6, ~10 min);
- SANCTUSREX (4, ~11 min);
- the grave rings (8, ~19 min);
- the zombies (9, ~23 min).

Cold play 6's report: "The opening is the best part ... The first 37 turns were a 7."

Scores of the plays that reported: the weir 6.5 (one play); the drove road 6.5, 5, 6 and 5 (mean 5.6, cold play 8
unscored).
