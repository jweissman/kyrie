“Unique/run” is the mean number of distinct offered action identities bearing the facet. “Offered/100 turns” counts all exposures; an action with several facets contributes to each. “Realized/selected” means the chosen action produced fresh prose, so it is promise follow-through rather than task completion. Coverage is the share of runs that saw at least one opportunity of the facet.

## Experimental cautions

- Ancestry and calling are not fully independent: the game permits only legal combinations. Human spans all seven callings; dwarf only warrior and cleric. Read ancestry differences descriptively, not as clean causal estimates.
- Promotion constrains the roulette to the led choices. Temperament therefore measures what a player can select from the interface, not latent authored content.
- Fresh prose is a deliberately weak realization proxy. It can over-credit atmospheric text and under-credit silent state changes.
- 3 rotated cells per ancestry/calling pair gives broad factor coverage, not uncertainty intervals. A fully crossed run (`--full`) or seed replication is the next useful expansion if these baselines become acceptance thresholds.
