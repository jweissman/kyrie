lane". One lane for the work that makes the fiction better rather than the machinery truer, beside the sprints and the
maturity track, its rows in the register (ST-01.., WB-01..). Its sources:

- **The structural critique** (doc/architecture/reviews/scenario-structural-literary-critique-2026-11-18.md): Kyrie

- **The adventure-structure brief** (mission-22/adventure-structure-brief.md): the journey review (Phase A landed) and

## How the two reviews meet

The critique asks each story for a moving actor, an opposing claim, a collision, a choice with a cost and an aftermath.
The report asks the world for range: appetite, terror, splendour, comedy, nonhuman agency, a present history, and
civilizations that do not think in obligations. A story can pass the critique and still be another audit; the golden
route's Iscoed sequence (a held drove, a tally written over, a toll, a sea-gate's keeping) is the Account in its
purest form, made better as drama on 18 November (ST-02..ST-04: Mardle's fear, Ifan's family on the cut) but not wider.

So the lane keeps both measures: the critique's for every story, the report's for the portfolio as a whole.

## The editorial rules, kept

The report's rules for rejecting a pitch (its central noun a ledger, tally, oath, debt, office, measure, register,
charter, missing name, old machine or inherited duty; a mystery resolved to an old procedure; a climax that is a hearing,
accounting, reading, delivery or restoration) apply to new works in this lane. The critique's seven sentences apply to
every story, new or rebuilt.
