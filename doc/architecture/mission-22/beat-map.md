## What the map says

1. **The mage's first 24 turns are the best first hour the game has**: desire, a step, preparation that changes the odds,
   danger met by help rather than strength, a discovery, a consequence chosen. The warrior's drove story is quick (12
   turns) and then the hour becomes the barrow, which is good danger with no tie to why the company came.
2. **The return beat is what both lack.** Nothing at the landing (warrior) or at Gatesend and Porthalen (mage) answers
   what was done. This is S2-5, and the warrior's is the larger gap: Bran freed should change the landing, the drove
   should be sold or lost, and Bran's pointing west to Brynach (the route's beat 8) should be the next desire.
3. **The warrior's preparation does not matter.** Carlin takes all the coin and nothing shows her changing the barrow;
   food never moves (two rations from first to last in both plays). S2-4's target: one act at Porthalen that changes
   the barrow (light, rope, a word from the factor or Gwil about the king) and is seen to.
4. **Aims glut is real on the mage's route** (S2-3): seven held from T60, four new from one notice at T1; on the
   warrior's, Owain's answer adds two aims he never spoke of (CL-45, with the burn-down agent).
5. **The alternate character is viable** (S2-6) on this route, by help: a 4-hp mage came through two rat fights with
   Gesk. CB-03's measure stands for other routes; this one is not blocked.
