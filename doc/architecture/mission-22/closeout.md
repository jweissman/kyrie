# Mission 22: closeout (21 November 2026)

## Closure statement

**M22 is closed as an alpha, a proving phase. It is not accepted as a first-hour product.** It proved that the
engine can own the player's offer for every client, answer every input at once, hold an engagement as a state of the
frame, and stop the gold looping on barred and guarded ways. It did not show the first fifteen minutes holding for a
player: the owner's playtests of 20–21 November found focus, opportunity and undertaking continuity failing in
ordinary play (alpha-acceptance.md). What follows is beta's (M22-beta), whose theme is the trust contracts, not new
content.

## The ruling on responsiveness (OR-91's remainders: OR-170, OR-127)

**Target revised, and made beta's first foundation epic.** The 2 ms engine and 20 ms main-thread figures are kept as
diagnostics, not as the target, because a player perceives acknowledgement and settling, not engine time. Beta's
responsiveness contract replaces them with a perceptual budget, measured in the browser (the CI browser lane, at a phone's and a laptop's size; the owner, 23 November: no per-device
timing):

1. **Acknowledged** within 100 ms of every press: the choice drawn taken, and the page closed to a second command
   (already held in the browser; OR-145's half to finish).
2. **Settled** (the answer readable and the page open again) within 300 ms at p95 for an ordinary act.
3. **Long work** (a new world, an install, a journey) acknowledged at once and shown progressing, never a still page;
   a new world settled within 3 s (AX-04).

It belongs to beta's Authority contract: the command transaction and its receipt (MT-24) are where acknowledgement and
settling are defined. OR-127 (the browser half: creation waiting on the worker's world read, the page's per-turn cost)
and OR-170 are its first rows. It is not an entry gate.

## Beta's entry conditions

Beta begins when these hold, none of them new work in alpha:

1. **Its four contracts named, in order** (the owner, 21 November): *Authority* (one player frame, one executable
   opportunity identity, one command transaction and receipt; clients render, never recover semantics), *Integrity*
   (knowledge, perception, causality, travel, time and restoration coherent across a command and across save and
   resume), *Separation* (the platform supplies simulation, projection, receipts, provenance and authoring primitives;
   Westernesse supplies doctrine, patterns, content and presentation), *Trust* (every signal warranted, every
   commitment acknowledged, every important deed answered, failures explained, saved worlds resumed whole).
2. **The trust contracts carried as beta commitments**, not investigation notes (trust-contracts.md's table):
   knowledge integrity (its negative test unwritten: a lens or the map revealing what the company does not know),
   restoration (not begun), travel continuity (partial: three gaps). With them the three continuity invariants of
   alpha-acceptance.md: focus (OR-177), opportunity (OR-178), undertaking (OR-179).
3. **Register zero replaced by two bounded measures**: *supported-slice debt* (blockers to the declared 30–45 minute
   first hour) and *platform-contract debt* (violations of the four contracts). Everything else stays recorded and
   does not compete for the active count.
4. **The owner's awaiting-judgment portfolio set apart** (below), so that no engineering orbits it.

The three demonstrations the retrospective recommends before committing beta's plan: the continuity invariants as
walk-wide specs (failing is fine), a transition-summary spike over Iscoed's first hour, and one recorded unfamiliar
player for fifteen minutes on the page.
