# Cold play 8 — log

T0: Wenna, human warrior, drover. Bran taken at the customs for the count; cousin Owain beside me. Strong opening prose. ~60 options on the green — a wall of shops, people, roads. Aims offered. Asked Owain.
T1: "Thirty-six... four went in at the Black Rhyne." Good, concrete. New aim: search the Rhyne behind the pens. Also two unrelated town aims piled in (fuel boat, sea-gate). A "Talk to the Guldríce agent — ask about the king-barrow" is a *leading* P option although I never met or heard of a Guldríce agent. Odd.
T2-3: Took and read the tally-stick: "thirty-six notches... four more cut through at the Black Rhyne." Nice physical evidence.
T4-8: Customs-house. Bran: "It is not the Rì's [tenth]." Mardle shows the book: "Forty head at the march-stone." Asked who he counts for: toll-farmer at Dun Rìerdan docks him a crown per short beast; "I have three children." Good moral shading. Odd: the option "Ask Mardle how many droves he has found four short" vanished after I asked the count.
T8: Follow showed [led] travel to pens but [going] said the ink-look step. Mixed signal.
T10: Watered the drove (an hour). "the boy says something that might be thanks." Lovely, if inconsequential. Talking to Owain afterwards gave just "Owain turns to you." — nothing to say.
T13: Searched the Rhyne: four branded bullocks under the water. Aim updates to tell the clerk, "at his counter or before the reeve on the green". Chose counter (spare Mardle public shame).
T15: Mardle: "Beasts in a ditch prove nothing" but the customs-man checks; Bran freed. +150 xp. Bran invites me up the pass to Caer Brynach — "something has been at the cattle on the high pasture". But no aim recorded for it! Only the salt-boat and sea-gate aims remain.
T16-20: Mardle: "'You again,' flatly." Bran gone, no goodbye. Guldríce agent pitch: crown of the first king from Holyking, 380 coin; got a 30-coin advance just by asking. Felt too easy, but it's a nice hook (signed a ledger — I wonder if it binds me). Notice: rats, catacombs, spiders — the classic three.
T21-26: Holgar: talk inspect showed "Holgar:" then blank, then "Holgar, salter". Got rats key; he also wants fuel. Bought torch + goodberry.
T27-30: Corn-store -> cellar (pitch dark, "Need: Dark, a light to hand"; I already had 2 torches + candle — didn't know). Lit torch. Rat-runs: "They will not have you here for long."
T31-36: Fight vs 2 rats (1 hp each). Cleave and Sweep both missed and then vanished from the menu without saying they were used up. Rat 2 bit twice in one round. "Wenna bleeds, for 1. Wenna's wound festers." — from what? After killing a rat: "does not look pleased." Odd tone for rat-killing. New aim: the old buck rat in the nest.
T37-42: Old buck rat: described with the very same sentence as an ordinary rat. Four misses running, then one hit for 10. "Wenna swears under their breath" — pronoun wobble. Aim → tell Holgar; but "find where they get in" seems quietly dropped.
T43-45: Search in the nest: torch ran out mid-search ("Your light gives out"), search lost. A torch burns maybe an hour, and every search is 20 min — light is a real clock. Good tension, harsh. Relit; found "a cord of grave rings" (+3 xp). Grave rings under a corn-store — intriguing.
T47-52: Old drain: a great centipede. Took venom 3. Won at 7/14. Torch nearly out; undercroft beyond. Retreating.
