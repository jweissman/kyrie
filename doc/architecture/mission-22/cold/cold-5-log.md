# Cold play 5 — Ilse, elf mage (scholar), iscoed

T0 arrive at gate-yard of Gatesend. Rainy morning. One aim: why the sea-gate won't shut, before the spring tide. Only 3 "primary" acts shown, not four. Rest list is enormous (~60 entries: twelve "Set out for", five "Have Ilse prepare X in place of Y", four haggles, set-down items). Overwhelming; "Ask Gek what the old workings are worth" is primary but Gek isn't introduced in the prose (only Wat is).
Want: get oriented. The notice board is the obvious first read.
T1 read notice: three jobs (rats 10c, catacombs 15c, spiders 12c). Good, clear. BUT the same outcome says "You take Tegwen's lease." — I did not take anything; reading a notice made me pick up someone's lease. Who is Tegwen? Never introduced. (defect)
  Three new aims appeared, each a long run-on: "Ask Gek, herb-wife, selling from the house door at the gate-yard of Gatesend, about the rats..." Fine but wordy.
  Marks: aspects knowledge/exploration on almost everything; "marks" always empty so far.
T2 read lease: "The weir at Gatesend is yours on the reeve's lease. Five coin owed... a month off." Confused: am I Tegwen? Only on following the aim (still T2, "inspect") does Nest explain "You'll be Tegwen's... since the burying" — so I inherited a dead woman's weir. That framing should have been on the arrival screen; T0 shows none of it. The full look also gives a SECOND notice by the well (clerk's tally of iron and gold) — two notices at one well, plus the lease line repeated verbatim from T2.
  Nice: two smokes on the ridge (black from digging, white from the old woman's fire) — a real hook. Nest: "gate's not shut since the new moon. Spring tide's in six days." Clear stakes. First self-chosen goal: **save my inherited weir by finding why the sea-gate won't shut.**
  Follow lit "To the sea-bank" with an 'undertaking' aspect. Clear.
T3-5 sea-bank -> gatehouse -> search. Lovely, concrete: windlass, bronze ratchet, empty socket with bright pin-holes; the pawl fell down the gate-well. Aim narrows to "find the lost pawl". This is the game at its best: a mechanical mystery I can reason about. Enjoying it.
  Oddity: on the bank a primary act was "Ilse: prepare spells" and "Wait" — filler in the top four. And "To the guest-hall of Gatesend" as a travel:11 min sits beside "To the gate-yard" — guest-hall ≠ brothers' house? Names of the same building drift (brothers' house / guest-hall / taproom id).
T6 gate-well: pitch dark. "Need: Dark" — odd label; what I need is light. Offered candle (undertaking mark) or Light spell. Cast Light (save the candle) — a mage getting to be a mage. Good.
T8 search: foot finds the pawl under silt, "the wardens' wave cut in its face". Lovely.
T9 take: undertaking mark had NOT been on "Collect the pawl" (it was on nothing; first primary was the gallery). The mark failed to point at the act the aim named.
T10 back in gatehouse: the pin stub is "bright and square: sawn through, not worn." Sabotage! Now I really care: who cut it? Winding is "unlikely" (60 min). I'm a frail elf. As a player I want help: someone strong (Wat the collier?) before I try. Testing whether preparation/help is a route.
T11-13 back to gate-yard looking for a strong arm. Wat: "If it's trouble, I have enough of my own." — only topic "his work". Nest, who greeted me warmly as Tegwen's heir and told me about the gate, now says "Let me guess. You want something." and has NO topics at all — not the gate, not the weir, not the sawn pin. I hold a sawn-through pawl (evidence of sabotage!) and there is no one to show it to. Expected: tell Nest / the reeve / the wardens. Nothing. (defect: the discovery has no social outlet; help is not a route)
  New primary "Read the royal proclamation under the notices" — a third notice at the well now.
T14-15 Aldous Fenn: good scene — chain across the old road, his father put out, "find the paper. There is one. There is always one." A scholar's job! New aim: "...the parchment lies in the Hall's muniment press, behind a door the rats have made difficult." The aim knows where the paper is and about rats; Fenn said neither (aim says more than the speaker — not earned). Second goal: find the paper for Fenn (fits my scholar).
  Nobody to help with the windlass. I'll try it alone.
T16-17 tried the windlass alone: "It wants more weight than yours, or a fresh try." Good failure — tells me what to do (more weight). After failing, the undertaking mark vanished from the wind act (moved to the rest list); first primaries became "To the guest-hall/chapel" with exploration only.
T18 guest-hall: Dreg volunteers "Gesk there is looking for work". Very good timing: the game handed me the more-weight answer.
T19-20 Gesk, lizardfolk warrior, 6 coin. I paid 6 of my 7 (I owe 5 on the lease in a month — a real trade-off, I liked weighing it). Preparation: recruiting to solve the windlass.
T21 back at the windlass with Gesk: the wind act now says "an even chance" (was "unlikely") — preparation visibly changed the odds. Good. BUT it is buried in the rest list with no undertaking mark; primaries are "To the chapel", "To the churchyard", well, Wait. The aim says wind the gate, and I'm standing at the windlass.
T22 failed again with IDENTICAL text: "You hang on the bar until your arms shake... It wants more weight than yours" — Gesk is right there, the prose ignores him; and "more weight" is now untrue advice (I had it). (said twice / not so)
T23 success: "The pawl drops into its socket with a sound like a bell... something enormous seats itself in stone, and the mill-race sound stops." Best moment so far. 200 xp each.
  But: the sawn pin — the most interesting thing I learned — goes nowhere. No aim "find who cut it". Next aim: tell the reeve at Porthalen — a 831-minute journey (overnight; 'night' mark). Also mild contradiction: lease said I owe the reeve of Porthalen; now I'm reporting to him.
T24 gate-yard after: "Every weir below the sea-bank has a trap set in it, and the water through them runs the right way: out." Lovely payoff line in the world.
T25 Nest: "Still here. I suppose that's something." — no word about the gate I just shut for her and the whole town. The world changed one sentence; the people didn't notice. Deflating. (defect)
  Now: 1 coin. Porthalen is 14 hours away. I want money and I want Fenn's paper; the rats job (10 coin + lantern) and the "door the rats have made difficult" look linked. Going for rats — as a frail mage, with a lizardfolk warrior now.
T26 Gek gives the job and the key: "Kill them, and find where they're getting in... Or lay ratsbane where they come up, if you'd sooner not be bitten. It wants a night." YES — a non-fight route for a frail mage. But no ratsbane is for sale from Gek (only herbs, bandages; poultice/antidote/dandelion/heather to haggle over). Where does ratsbane come from? Not said.
T27 Talk to Gek: "I don't know you," says Gek, and waits to be told why she should. — she handed me her key one turn ago. (contradiction) Also "Take your leave of Nest" still listed while I talk to Gek; "Wrench the padlock's hasp out" still offered though I hold the key; "To the old corn-store" dropped out of the primaries.
T29 corn-store: on arrival the undertaking mark pointed OUT ("To the gate-yard") because the followed aim was still Porthalen; I had to follow the rats aim by hand. Fine once I understood, but the mark followed the stale aim, not what I'd just taken on.
T30 search the store: "Nothing is put away or hidden here." no ratsbane.
T31 prepared Siphon Life instead of Second Sight (an hour). Clear and good: I chose to prepare for a fight.
T32 cellar dark again; my Light from T7 had gone out without a word. Gesk lit his torch. Fine.
T34 rat-runs: "The rats under the corn-store are here. / The rats under the corn-store stand their ground and watch you." — clunky double naming. 'danger'+'undertaking' on Attack with 'fight' mark: clear promise. No ratsbane route available here (never found any to buy). So it's a fight after all.
T35-37 fight: 2 rats, 1 hp each. "a whole nest of them, and bold" = two rats that die to one blow. Siphon Life killed one (3 dmg). Gesk "sneers: 'Pathetic.'" Second Sight still offered in the fight though I swapped it out at T31.
