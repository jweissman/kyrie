# Cold play 6 — Wenna, human warrior, report

A blind agent, no code or register, `bin/kyrie run --world iscoed --as human/warrior/Wenna` at c7fa2382 (Sprint 2, S2-1: the representative character after the 18 November landings). Log: cold-6-log.md; lines: cold-6-lines.txt. Its defects are registered as CL-51..CL-56.

**Score: 5 / 10.** The first 37 turns were a 7: a strong hook, a quick payoff, and a good small puzzle. Then one click ended the game, and I was told nothing of the fight that killed me.

## Why
The opening is the best part. A drover is arrested, a frightened cousin stands by, a tally-stick is thrown at my feet, and four bullocks lie drowned in a ditch. That gave me a goal at once, and I settled it in about 12 turns with real prose and a satisfying turn of events: Bran freed, and Mardle's skimming reported to the reeve. Leif's catacomb job led into a chain of rooms with lights that run out, letters cut into stone, a flag that slides aside and a hidden vault. It felt like a proper small dungeon. Then "Attack the keeper of the vault" played all four rounds in one move. I saw only the last blow: "Wenna takes 6 piercing damage (0 HP left)… The fight is lost." I made no choices in the fight, could not retreat, and got no warning beyond a 'danger' tag. That ended a 14-HP warrior on the first morning. Losing a fight is fine; losing one I could not see or steer makes me not want to start again. Around that core there was a lot of noise:
- about 50 rows on the opening screen;
- conversations that open with a line saying nothing;
- prompts that don't fit the moment;
- aims that appear from nowhere;
- a book I couldn't read.

## What I wanted
1. **Find out how many head came down, and free Bran.** Done (T1–T12). Owain's count, the drowned four in the Black Rhyne, Bran's hint, and telling Mardle all worked cleanly. It was very satisfying.
2. **Expose Mardle.** Mardle himself said "tell the reeve nothing she need be troubled with", so I wanted to. Done (T15): "Third drove this year… I will have the book off him by the quarter-day." Great.
3. **Go up the pass to Caer Brynach with Bran and Owain** (paid sword work, "something has been at the cattle"). Not attempted. No aim was made for it, and I couldn't find a drove road or Brynach among the 20-odd routes.
4. **Lay the dead in the catacombs for Leif (15 coin).** Done at T31, by talking the man with the black candle down. I never collected the money because I died first.
5. **Find out who killed Joss Hatherly** (the bruise, the river-silt, the hobnails "of a pattern the Hall's keeper does not wear"). This was my own goal, and the game never made an aim for it. Not pursued.
6. **Follow the old ways under Porthalen to the undercroft and what lies below.** I opened the sealed vault (T37) and died to its keeper (T39).
7. **Earn coin for the sea-gate job (30 crowns) and the fuel boat.** Not reached.

## What worked
1. The opening fiction is excellent. "He went without a word, only throwing his tally-stick down at your feet", Owain's "We heard them in the water", and the bullocks "bawling to be watered" all give instant motive.
2. The drove problem has a clean chain: ask, search, find, tell, payoff. Bran's hint about Mardle added a second layer that the reeve then closes.
3. The reeve's reply about Mardle felt like the world reacting to me.
4. The well-post notice turned three jobs into three clear aims, each naming who to ask and what is locked.
5. Leif hands over the key at once, with no runaround.
6. Light as a resource works. "It is pitch dark…", a torch with "40 minutes left", and the torch guttering out made the dungeon tense.
7. The lettered-flag puzzle is a fair, discoverable puzzle with a satisfying "click somewhere under the floor". It was the best moment.
8. Talking the man with the black candle down was a good non-violent way through. The image of the dead folding "into the bone-heaps like coats let fall" is lovely.
9. The ending summary at least said what happened: "It began when you attacked… struck the last blow with its bite, for 6… 4 rounds."
