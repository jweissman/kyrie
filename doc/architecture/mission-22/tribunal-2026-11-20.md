## Patterns

1. **No `partly` row was wholly met.** Every row that names a remainder still has one. The proved part is usually
   real and pinned (38 splits), and the remainder is honest but has nowhere to live. Banning `partly` mostly turns
   one row into two: a closed row and an open one. That is the owner's ruling working as intended.
2. **Remainders fall into four kinds.** Browser or measurement work (NM-02, T24-05, OR-37, OR-69, OR-96, FM-01,
   FM-02, OR-129); an owner's ruling or reading (PB-02, OR-71, OR-75, OR-116, FM-03, TR-02); generated countries
   other than Iscoed (WD-05, WD-08, PB-24, BO-04); and a feature not yet built (CB-05, OR-72, OR-74, US-07). Only the
   last is engine work. That is why most of the new rows went to P2.
3. **"Held in code" or "checked in code" is the commonest weak claim.** OR-112 (BoardKnowing), OR-37 (SiteBuild.mark),
   OR-71, OR-11, OB-16, FM-01, AP-02's repeatable acts and OR-72's no-prefix were all fixed by a code read with
   nothing pinning them. The closing rule already counts these as `partly`. Each now has its pin written into the
   remainder.
4. **Replays and censuses stand in for specs.** CL-01's terminal replay, CL-10's antechamber, US-09's cold plays, and
   the census scripts behind CB-01, SI-07, DN-03, BO-01 and PB-24 are all evidence, but nothing fails if the defect
   comes back. A census that is used as evidence should have a bound pinned in a spec, as clause-load does, and per
   file where the claim is per file.
5. **One remainder often has several homes.** The start's cost appears in SP-01, OR-08, OR-23, OR-120 and OR-121.
   The page's journey walk appears in TR-01, OR-112, OR-113 and TR-20. The telling's phone box appears in T24-05 and
   OR-69. The tribunal folded each into one new row (OR-137, TR-21, OR-131) and marked the others DUPLICATE.
6. **Played chains do hold the headline claims.** stories-apart, notice-spent, notice-leads, sea-gate-claim,
   barrow-forewarned and hex-only-by-travel are real played chains on the shipped worlds, and they passed. The
   weakness is breadth: Iscoed and a1 only, where the claims speak of every country.
