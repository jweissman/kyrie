# Boredom as a first-class defect (the owner, 17 November 2026, evening)

Verbatim:

> I want to say we're getting closer but there are still a lot of problems. Even though we are beginning to get past
> the opening interface, what we're finding is that ordinary play is still too repetitive, thin, and unrewarding. We
> need to treat boredom as a first-class defect. Use our opportunity and behavior measurements to diagnose it, but do
> not optimize raw content counts. Improve the diversity of situations, the depth of explorable places, the
> discoverability of wonder, and the economy of new capabilities. Remove the abstract origin-quest selection in favor
> of diegetic starting circumstances. Demonstrate the result through an independently reviewed first hour in a
> representative generated starting region. I don't think we can call even the first phase of m22 done until an agent
> gives several positive cold play reports in a row

## What this changes

- **M22's first phase is not done** until several cold play reports in a row are positive. A cold player is an agent
  with no access to the code or the register, playing the deployed (or locally built) game through its own page or
  terminal, from a representative generated starting region, for a first hour, and reporting what it did, what it
  wanted, where it was bored and why, without being told what we changed.
- **Boredom is a defect with causes**, each measured before and after: a situation met again with nothing new; a
  place with nothing beneath its first look; a wonder nobody can find; an hour with no new thing the company can do.
  Raw counts (more items, more rooms, more topics) are not the measure.
- **The origin pick goes.** "Why have you come?" as a menu of quests is replaced by a starting circumstance the world
  gives: where the life begins, in the middle of something, told by the opening and met in play.

## The work, by lane

1. **Diagnosis (headless):** a boredom census over the first hour in a1, kite and thorn and two more generated
   regions: repetition (situations and lines met again), depth (places whose second look gives nothing), wonder
   (marvels placed against marvels found by an unguided player), capability (new verbs gained per hour). Each
   number a baseline the later reports are read against. (The register agent.)
2. **Diversity and depth (content and engine):** the worst causes the census names, fixed at their cause; FH-03's
   dense start; BP-07, CO-03; places that repay a second look (RI-01..03); wonder discoverable by attention, not by
   luck. (The SRD agent, now on variety and depth.)
3. **Diegetic starts (the lead):** the origin step removed from creation; the start chosen by the world and the life
   (seeded, varied), told by the opening and present in the first scene.
4. **Cold play loop (the lead commissions):** after each landed batch, a fresh cold player plays the first hour and
   reports; findings go to the register as BO- rows; the loop repeats until several reports in a row are positive.
