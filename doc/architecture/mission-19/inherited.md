brief's §XIII asks and given its disposition: **Fixed** (with the commit), **Partial** (what was done and what
remains), **Not reproduced**, or **Deferred** (beyond Mission 19, with why). Nothing is left an open wish. Classes:
**A** correctness or action integrity, **U** UX or gameplay, **C** content or authoring, **R** architecture, **D** a
larger ambition deliberately deferred. Mission 19's own findings are in [register.md](register.md).
