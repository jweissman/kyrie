# Mission 19 register

Findings with stable ids, as [brief.md](brief.md) §XIII asks: evidence, source, priority, resolution, verification.
Mission 18's 138 carried rows (138 distinct ids, checked against the wishlist on 6 November; their closures classed
by kind in [inherited.md](inherited.md)) are in [../mission-18/wishlist.md](../mission-18/wishlist.md) under their own ids; a
row of this register that answers one names it. Classes, as the brief distinguishes them:

- **A** blocking correctness or action-integrity defect
- **U** systemic UX or gameplay deficiency
- **C** content or authoring integration gap
- **R** architectural weakness needing bounded remediation
- **D** larger ambition deliberately deferred beyond Mission 19

Status is the bold word at the head of its cell: **Fixed** (with the commit and how it was verified), **Partial**,
**Open** (an unresolved defect or obligation: open risk), **Deferred** (D rows: an ambition, never a defect),
**Superseded**. After the process audit of 4 November ([../m18-m19-process-audit.md](../m18-m19-process-audit.md)),
"Fixed" claims the evidence it names and no more: **implemented** (a commit), **reachable** (a spec or scripted
journey), **usable** (done through the page by the builder), never **accepted**, which only the owner or an
independent player can give; what was played, how, and with what result is in [evidence.md](evidence.md).
