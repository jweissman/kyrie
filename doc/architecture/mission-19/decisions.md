# Mission 19: the owner's rulings

What the builder had gathered for the owner, and the owner's rulings of 7 November, which are design guidance for
the builder to carry out by its own judgment, not gates ("treat these as design guidance, not invitations to reopen
owner approval gates"). Above all: a normal expedition enjoyed without fighting the interface or the engine.

| # | Decision | Rows | What waited on it | The ruling, and where it is being done |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | How many companions a shipped campaign begins with | M19-105 | Most shipped campaigns begin with one, which shuts the calling-unique beats the Imbar author wrote; a fuller company changes every campaign | Each authored campaign begins with the company its story establishes, ordinarily two or three; lone starts, recruiting and travelling alone kept; no padding to unlock beats |
| 2 | What a company can read of a foe in the dark | ARV-H5, COR-12 | A foe's card shows its exact hit points whatever the light | A foe's health in words always (unhurt, hurt, badly hurt, near death), never its hit points even in good light; in the dark only whether it stands; particular powers may reveal more. **Done** (plants/recipes branch: the fight's cards and chronicle) |
| 3 | The old-school rules still open: uses per day, level drain, the shapes of an area | M19-81 | The baseline is Orsino's own (THAC0, d20 saves by category); these three are not run | Faithful to Orsino, simplified where needed: uses per day; level drain a consequential, recoverable supernatural affliction; areas keep real places, targeting and friendly fire under a consistent abstract positioning, never a magical "all foes". In hand (rules/osr) |
