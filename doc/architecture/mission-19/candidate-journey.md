the ordinary flow: town, talk, buy, travel, interruption, explore, danger or retreat, return, reload. This is the
builder's walk of it, on the build of 6 November, through the page (the play server, `scripts/play-server.ts`, at
1440×900): a route for the independent players of the acceptance protocol (evidence.md, PD-14), not acceptance.
