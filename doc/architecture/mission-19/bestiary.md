# The bestiary in generated play (M19-76)

What of the bestiary ordinary generated countries meet, made by `bin/kyrie bestiary report` (6 November) over eight seeds: every creature their generated text places, counted by the way it came there (den, deep site room, the party `beside` it, the open country, a meeting, other generated people's animals), with the variations laid on it; and beside that each kind's reachability decided from its record alone (`BestiaryReport.reach`), so that a rare kind missing from the sample is told apart from one nothing can reach. Challenge is the measured challenge at the first level (`data/catalog/challenge.json`). The celestials and fiends are called by summons and belong to the planes, not to a country's dens; the tame are placed with their people. See [creatures.md](creatures.md) for how composition works, and [bestiary-inventory.md](bestiary-inventory.md) for what became of each Orsino entry.

Seeds: a1, b2, kite, ash, wren, mere, thorn, cov2. 73 of 128 kinds met by generation in the sample; 29 more eligible; 5 unreachable. Variations known: 55.
