  the old quarry pit · Day 1, 8:05 a.m.
  A bowl of grey stone cut out of the hill long ago, ivy over its faces. At the bottom a square adit goes back into the rock behind a barred gate. A board on the gate says KEEP OUT in three hands.
  Runs worn to bare earth go in under the ivy at the foot of the east face. The smell of fox is on them.
  No food.

--- Work along the faces of the pit, ledge by ledge (outcome)
  Halfway up the west face, a ledge runs along under the ivy. It was the quarrymen’s, with a niche cut in its back for their dinners. In it, a purse rotted to a rag, and two coins green with age.

--- Search here (outcome)
  You search the old quarry pit with care, a little at a time.
  You turn the spoil over, a stone at a time. Under the last of the broken face, a flint with a hole worn through it comes up into your hand. It is somebody’s luck, dropped and never found.
