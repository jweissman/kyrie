How to read the classes. They are read off each lost turn by what it shows, in this order: an obstacle said
(a barred way's need, the dark, a grave choice at the aim's place), then away from the aim's site, then a room come
back to, then a place with nothing but ways, then the rest. Most lost turns come in runs inside a walk: the first
turn of a run is the cause, and the rest are how a blind walker spends the run (back and forth, the same rooms).
Read per walk, the generated tier has four causes:

- **Barred, and nothing leads to what opens it** (unreachable aim content, 85). Ironkeep's portcullis (needs
  brawn) and breach (climbing or rope), Tinhold's fall (digging or a shovel) and lift-cage (brawn), Princeport's
  strongroom (lockwork), Eaglelow's broken stair (climbing or rope), Mistlake's riddle (a grave choice). Each is now
  said as a need line (Threshold.barred), but nothing points to the rope, the shovel or a strong companion in town.
- **Out of the site with the aim unmet, and nothing back** (navigational confusion, 81). Leaving a work it had
  not finished, the company is not led back. The likely cause, to be confirmed by a trace before any change: a place
  left having done nothing there is idle (CompanyRecall), so neither its way nor its journey is the aim's again. The
  stones' garden was one case of it (b50a7c4f).
- **Inside, the unexplored ways not told from the way back** (repeated rooms 35, and most of the 35 "acts,
  none toward the aim"). After the portcullis is heaved up, the bailey's way is one way among the way back out and
  a wait. Gold rightly stays off it: this is EX-02's frontier.
- **A lookout with nothing to look into** (missing investigation acts, 5): "look out over the country" leaves a
  wild hex near the site with no act of its own. Few, and not the aim's.
