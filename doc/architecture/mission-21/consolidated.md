# Mission 21, consolidated (14 November, late)

The owner: fewer coordinated efforts brought to experiential completion, rather than more initiatives reporting
partial progress; make Westernesse progressively cheaper to improve and unmistakably better to play. This page
supersedes the earlier plans' sequencing; their register rows stand.

## Three priorities

1. **A radically cheaper inner loop.**
   - Measure the runtime surgically on tiny Kyr worlds of rising size (empty, 100, 1,000, 10,000 facts), with the
     production compiler and runtime, holding a five-object room constant while unrelated facts grow.
   - The invariant: **an operation pays for its relevant working set, not the whole possible universe.**
   - Then repair the highest-leverage costs, and keep moving behavioural assertions to tiny fixtures and situations
     alone.
   - Owned by the lead. The independent architecture review informs it.
2. **One interaction grammar.**
   - Across the title, creation, exploration, dialogue, combat, lenses, Backstage and special interactions, one model
     of: what is in focus, which choices are legal, how focus moves, commits, inspects further and returns, what the
     engine owns, and no accidental dead ends.
   - A controller is the design constraint.
   - Four promoted is an editorial default, not a quota.
   - Each screen keeps its own dramatic composition and feels like the same game. Browser and terminal run one
     workflow.
   - Owned by the aesthetic line.
3. **One small, complete, repeatedly playable experience.**
   - A compact, real, Kyr-authored scenario on the production engine, built mostly from existing material.
   - Cheap to start and replay, with a recognizable sequence: beginning, forming an intention, exploring, resistance,
     acting, a persistent consequence, continuing or returning.
   - The integration and editorial laboratory, through terminal walks and browser review.
   - Owned by the playability line.
