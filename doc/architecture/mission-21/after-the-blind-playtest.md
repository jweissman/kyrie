# After the blind playtest: what changes, and who does it (14 November, night)

The owner: convert the groundwork into a substantially better game; the decisive evidence is sitting down to play. The
first genuinely blind playtest of the deployed build (2ea49419;
[history/playtests/blind-2026-11-13](../../history/playtests/blind-2026-11-13/README.md)) would not play again
tomorrow. "I would want to return to Westernesse's world, but not yet to the act of playing Westernesse." This page
says what the lead has decided from it and where each part went. Register rows: BP-01 to BP-09 (§33).

## Its findings, and the decisions taken

1. **Combat converts certainty into tedium.** Six rounds of a fight the game called "easily won". **Decided: a foregone
   fight is one act.** A fight reckoned easily won is offered as one consequential choice ("Drive them off") that
   resolves it whole and is told in a sentence or two: who fell, what it cost. Round by round stays for a fight of
   real uncertainty, or when the player asks for it. A fight's telling says each outcome once: "for 9" and then
   "takes 1" is one blow, which killed the rat.
2. **The fiction and the simulation contradict each other.** Examples:
   - "1 on the die, needing 11", with no meaning attached;
   - enemies who "will not wait" but let the company walk past;
   - "Enter Shaynof" returning to the road outside it;
   - "No light" beside "light a candle".

   **Decided: numbers behind the prose.** Odds are words on the choice, and the outcome is prose; the dice are shown
   only on a deliberate look (Details). A warning the world gives, the world keeps: a band that will not wait acts if
   the company lingers or passes.
3. **The starting premise is not pursuable.** Greyhold is on the map, but nothing routes to it. **Every starting
   premise is pursuable from its first map interaction:** its place selectable, routed and set out for, and its next
   step said.
4. **Abilities are acknowledged, not dramatized.** The thief's lock "hangs open on its hasp". **A distinctive act is
   told as an act:** a sentence of texture, and a consequence where one is due (noise, suspicion, time), from
   authorable templates, not TypeScript.
5. **The underworld is stocked, not inhabited.** Rats, beetles, a centipede and a spider in a row, most trivial or
   passed safely. **Fewer, more credible dangers** in the first town's undertown, each told and each meaning it.
6. **Classificatory labels** ("to learn", "to gain", "worth a look", "for you", "by the rules") **leave the visible
   page.** They stay where colour cannot be seen, for screen readers and the terminal without colour. "In play, by the
   rules" becomes words of the world.
7. **Press-and-hold to choose a card made the game seem to ignore the player. Decided: a tap chooses.** This reverses
   the owner's earlier direction that creation cards need a long press. Hold stays only for sustained acts (search,
   wait, camp), shown by a visible ring cue, where its weight means something.
8. **Repetitive emotional garnish** ("Haze looks away solemnly" after a rat) is cut, or tied to the character and the
   moment.

Kept and protected: the archaeological layering (roads over roads, rooms cut into forgotten rooms); the road into
Shaynof; the map's look; the second start's people (Adrik, Brother Anselm); the tablet layout.

## Who does it

- **Lead:**
  - 1, the engine: the foregone fight offered as one act, from the reckoning, in both clients, and its telling;
  - 2, the engine: the check's odds said and its dice behind a look;
  - the cold playtest programme;
  - the independent architecture review;
  - the burn-down.
- **Playability line:** the two gamebreakers first (PL-14, T24-18), then 3 (the premise pursuable: Greyhold, "Enter
  Shaynof"), 2's "no light" contradiction, and 4 (the lock's texture), with the runner and walks to prove them.
- **Aesthetic line:** look-5a (the browser reds), then:
  - 7, the tap;
  - 6, labels off the visible page;
  - 1's fight page (one act, one sentence, stable geometry);
  - the heading words;
  - then the title passage.
- **Rules line:** within its proof slices, 2's warnings kept (a band that will not wait acts) and 5's fewer, credible
  dangers in the first undertown, measured by the placement tools.

## Cold playtests from here on

Independent reviewers approach the deployed site without the doctrine, the register or any brief, as this one did.
They are asked what breaks immersion, where it stops feeling like a game, what is empty or tedious, and what excites
curiosity. One runs after each landing that changes the first hour. Their findings set the next priorities, ahead of
the register.
