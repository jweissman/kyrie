# Mission 21: a guide to playing the first hour

For the owner. FH-01, "no person has accepted the first hour", is the one open P0, and only you can close it. This page
says where to play, what M21 changed that you should notice, what is known to be unfinished, and what to tell us.

## Where

- **The page:** https://jweissman.github.io/kyrie/. It serves current main, and is rebuilt on every push.
- **On an iPad,** in portrait. That is the size M21's page work was checked at (820 by 1180).
- **`?guide=quiet`** plays the game as a player would meet it. The next step of the aim you follow is not shown in
  gold unless your company has a cleric.

## Three starts worth trying

| Start | As | Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| The debt (a1's default world) | a fighter | go straight at what is owed |
| A wanderer on the road in | a thief | avoid fights, take on a companion at the inn |
| A world of your choosing | a caster | explore before following anything |

## What M21 changed that you should notice

- **A place is told in three sentences.** The rest is under "More of the place". A clue is never held back.
- **Gold appears only after you choose Follow this.** It goes into a site only as far as what the aim waits on is
  known to lie; otherwise rooms are yours to explore.
- **When an aim is seen through, the game offers one next thing.** It is the deed's own consequence (the yoke back
  to its hearth), or the strongest lead you hold. It is offered, never followed for you.
- **Fights at the first places are less trivial.** Lone beasts in dens are now pairs. The deepest room under the first
  town has a real keeper, and every deadly group is told at its door, with time to leave.
- **Healing between fights costs a rest.** A healing cantrip mends each of the company once until it rests; it had
  been cast again and again while you waited (R10-12).
- **Small things that used to be wrong.** An antidote given for the spiders' bite can be used on the companion it
  was for. A lantern given you is filled once. An opened chest says what is in it. A band down to its last is "the
  last of the cave spiders". A way off a road names its town once.

## Known to be unfinished: don't spend your time finding these

- **Names.** The first places you set out for still have old-tongue names (Neblalac, Ísencepan). Decided: in the
  starting country they will be called first by their Common meaning (T24-20). Being built.

- **A larger adventure after the town's jobs.** The offer after an aim covers the deed and your leads. The pull
  toward a larger place (the mound, the green-lit tower) is not built (T24-18).
- **The map.** It has no road junctions (NM-01), and the route preview before setting out is unconfirmed in the
  page (OPR-03).

## What to tell us

For each start, a few lines:
- **Fifteen minutes:** did it hold together, yes or no? If not, where did it first break?
- **The first time you didn't know what to do,** and what the page showed you then.
- **The first time something was told that wasn't so** (a light that wasn't lit, a person who wasn't there).
- **One moment you enjoyed,** and one that bored you.
- **Screenshots** of anything wrong, with the time shown in the place's heading.

A gamebreaker you can reproduce outweighs any number of green tests. Report it as such, and we treat it as a P0.
