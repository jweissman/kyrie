# The first minute as a microscope (the owner, 14 November)

An addition to the interaction-grammar line (consolidated.md, priority 2). It is not a redesign, and not a fatigue
economy or a controller system. The aesthetic work already underway keeps landing.

**Look at the continuous journey:** studio stab → title → creation → first scene → its first two or three actions.
Treat the primary action control as the game's fundamental instrument. It has six phases: focus, selection, optional
preview, commitment, response and continuation.

**Questions:**
- Does it feel like operating a game, or like navigating between web pages?
- Is focus always obvious and stable?
- Can the interaction be read as controller inputs (move focus, confirm, back, inspect), even before controller support
  exists?
- Do the title, creation and ordinary scene choices share one design language?
- What do timing, transitions, typography, highlighted states, motion and sound add or take away?
- Can an act with a meaningful Energy cost later use a brief hold-to-commit without a separate interaction system? The
  sustained-act hold of BP-09, the ring that fills while held, is the candidate instrument.
- What is the smallest intervention that makes the whole opening substantially better?

**Deliverables:**
- A review of the deployed opening, with the implementation read where useful.
- One or two concrete improvements or prototypes, made within the aesthetic work.
- An unedited recording of the opening interaction.
- A candid critique of what still feels awkward, ordinary or unconvincing.

The goal is a common interaction grammar established now, so that M22 can pursue extraordinary polish without undoing
M21's work.
