## What the owner can now reliably do that they could not

Open the title and find four rows exactly aligned, with the saved game said beneath; start a game; follow an aim as any
act, and see the next step gold on the scene and on the map, or no gold and still travel; choose a road at a crossroads
from the menu and arrive in a scene (not a map card); focus a speaking door and see only its words; haggle with a seller;
search the story; see deeds; play the same game in the terminal and be offered the same acts by the same names.
