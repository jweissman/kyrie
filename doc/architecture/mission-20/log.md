# Mission 20: the experience log

One short, prioritized list of what stands between a newcomer and a good first hour. Each row: what happens, how to
reproduce it, its state. M19 register ids are cross-references, not obligations. States: **open**, **fixed** (commit;
technically verified), **agent-played** (a clean-context agent met the fix), **accepted** (a person did).

Reproduce headlessly with `bun /var/tmp/kyrie/m20/tools/leads.ts <origin> <race/calling/Name> "<label>" … "map:<place>" …`
(prints what the page leads with at each step) or `bin/kyrie play --world kahitude --origin debt --as human/warrior/Ralf …`.

## Amendments from the owner

1. **10 November — the opening design.** Adds: design A of [opening-design.md](opening-design.md) (a town with
   trouble: one in front, threads tied, the decisive choice made in exchange, the road meetings kept). Supersedes, for
   the moment after the prologue only, M19's "start on the one full-screen map": the first screen is the place the
   origin puts the company in; the map stays the way to travel. Excludes B and C.
2. **10 November, evening — working expectation and priorities.** Adds: no pausing for approval at ordinary
   checkpoints (pause only for a consequential design choice outside the commission, a scope or resource conflict, an
   external action, or a blocker); agent-play builds immutable while played. Priorities, in order: (1) CI on `5a6618a0`
   green and a verified candidate; (2) fewer early aims, a clearer hierarchy, the golden path kept; (3) keys that name
   their locks, consequences told when they happen (the firebomb), creation glitches, repeated paintings; (4) an
   engaging opening: difficulty, preparation, danger, negotiation, retreat, consequence — fair, not lethal or padded;
   (5) the title, creation, introduction and key pages as one designed whole; (6) a third cold round on a frozen
   candidate, testing unfamiliar intentions, boredom, tactics and wish to go on. Then the other phases. Invite the
   owner's play when the candidate is worth it, and keep working meanwhile.
3. **10 November, evening — one app on main.** Supersedes the `m20` branch and any candidate workflow: Westernesse is
   one application, developed on `main` and deployed by GitHub Pages. Small verified increments go to main often;
   short-lived branches and worktrees only for isolated work. Restore Pages first and verify the deployed app loads and
   plays; show the deployed commit. Agents test a locally served build pinned to a commit (a fixture, not a product);
   the owner plays the Pages app. The owner keeps committing art to main; no freeze. If main breaks, restoring it comes
   first.
4. **10 November, late — environment pass.** Adds, bounded and subordinate to the first hour's acceptance: place
   existing paintings in existing rooms where the match is strong, batched rather than per arriving file; two modest
   explorable places near the starting town, a stately home and an ancient ruin, reusing structure before adding it,
   each with coherent ways, fitting scenery, a few details, a plausible tie to the world, no elaborate quest. Excludes
   rooms made only to hold a painting, and anything the opening journey depends on.

5. **10 November, night — the owner's play ([user-notes.md](user-notes.md)).** Adds, in order: (1) trust in quest and
   interaction guidance: a visible mark must answer to an action or an explained state; active aims, clues, rumours,
   actions and rewards told apart; related aims made legible (the Albustagn sister and springs; a green-marked person
   with nothing in their talk; gold and green meaning different things in different places); (2) a subtraction and
   composition pass on the title (embers, no blurry painting), the introduction (text centred, not at the foot), and a
   scene-led layout (painting to the viewport, controls in restrained overlays), compared in screenshots at desktop and
   phone sizes; the top rail, buttons and link-like controls; (3) travel as a journey: slower and legible, what is
   passed through seen without its secrets told, ETA and rations and rest before setting out, consulting the map apart
   from setting out, the rail's Map reconsidered; (4) first-hour combat challenge, with a small diagnostic, not
   monster-by-monster inflation; (5) gendered professions and titles from one meaning (no male abbess), creation's
   glitches and order, the Encyclopedia inside the game; (6) the larger ideas recorded as design opportunities, not
   built. Excludes new openings, adventures and world-generation redesign in this pass.
6. **11 November — an illustrated, responsive RPG ([amendment-6.md](amendment-6.md)).** Supersedes the overlapping UX
   notes of amendments 2 (5), 5 (2) and 5 (3) with one direction: (A) the scene is the primary surface, the painting to
   the viewport and narration, talk, portraits and actions in restrained overlays; the top rail removed or reduced to a
   compact toolbar; Character and Book one temporary dossier; the map a distinct spatial tool; (B) two to four salient,
   genuinely different opportunities, each a legal visible action or an explained approach, never filler or a
   heuristic invention; (C) a semantic atlas: gold the followed undertaking's next step, silver promising exploration,
   green payoff, blue knowledge, red danger, violet wonder, neutral controls, with non-colour cues and no secret told by
   styling; (D) a playable thread whether or not an aim is followed; (E) combat a mode of the scene, with real pressure
   in the first hour; (F) map, scene and travel one geography; (G) the interface an honest integration test; (H) a
   silver-path walker over headless sessions. In two phases: A, composition and information architecture with the
   action semantics kept; B, the opportunity foreground, the colours and the encounter flow. Excludes new adventures, a
   narrative grammar, world-generation refactors and an unbounded rewrite.
   *Addendum, same day:* opportunities are perceived, not commands ranked: meaning (aspects), perception (what a
   character knows and can notice) and presentation (colour) kept apart; see [opportunities.md](opportunities.md).
7. **11 November, later — continue through closure.** The recent feedback clarifies the experience, it is not a run of
   new commissions: carry on without checkpoints. (1) Finish the game-like experience: scene first, a foreground of two
   to four opportunities with the rest reachable through the world, the semantic vocabulary, meaning, perception and
   presentation kept apart, combat part of the same experience with real danger in ordinary exploration; never
   beautiful but unplayable, nor functional but lifeless. (2) The character concept: make creation's skills,
   background and spell choices clear and useful, distinctions that matter early, staged choices reused where they
   help, a clean path to Kyr-authored character-defining moments; no feat/flaw/alignment/advancement redesign; the
   setting's orientation constructive and heroic. (3) Test real journeys: honest starts, independent agents, a cheap
   public-action gauntlet; keep implementation, automated checks, agent experience and human acceptance apart. (4) Then
   consolidate by player impact, coherence and risk: inconsistencies, obsolete UI paths and heuristics, overlapping
   tests, the verification loop measured, stale documents; not the whole register. (5) A bounded, current authoring
   trail for Kyr and Deem: entry points, exemplary examples, the path to play, who owns what, superseded guides marked,
   the scene and opportunity conventions as built. The Kyr/Deem shells review is M21 input; the independent authoring
   thread is not a dependency. (6) One app on main; no competing preview. (7) An honest handoff: what the deployed build
   offers, rendered and independent evidence, remaining defects, register resolved or deferred, authoritative docs, a
   short M21 list grounded in what was observed.
8. **11 November, night — sustained progress.** Adds: land the scene-first and opportunity work on main and verify
   the deployment, fixing any real failure; round 6 of cold play, attentive to the first fifteen minutes, scene
   continuity, discoverability, guidance, talk, the pack, travel and combat, and the redesign not accepted on tests
   alone; the silver walker's near-absence of danger investigated (scarcity, placement, inaccessible sites, cues, or the
   walker's policy), encounter frequency told apart from combat balance, and bounded first-hour danger added where
   right (safe places safe, no indiscriminate random encounters, but not a danger-free exploration); the walkers kept
   as a recurring diagnostic, recording places, sites, interactions, discoveries, rewards, encounters, stalls, repeats
   and false promises, over a few characters and seeds; the whole first hour, not only the resting scene; then the
   register reconciled against the implementation, high-value obligations resolved, the rest planned or deferred with
   a reason; then cleanup and a small authoritative authoring path. Handoff distinguishes implemented, automated,
   agent-experienced and human-accepted.
