# World-quality censuses (12 November, early)

The owner asked, at Mission 20's cutoff, for the content-placement and density censuses to be run again, and the
major gaps in reachability, distribution and meaningful opportunity noted. Two were run: the navigation census on main
(e792a581's tree) and the content-activation census on the adventure branch's review merge (`review/first-steps`),
where it lives. Both outputs are below, unedited; this summary is what they say.

## What they say

- **Reachability is whole.** In kahitude every one of 565 rooms is reached from the start by the ways, and in a1 all
  531.
- **The first green is crowded.** Thornhaven's landing has 37 ways and Lowmouth's 31: the flat Go list players keep
  naming (round 7, L; round 8, N "about 18 places"). Twelve places in kahitude and 23 in a1 have more ways than the
  census thinks readable.
- **Dead ends.** 35 rooms in kahitude and 36 in a1 are entered and never left by an offered way. They are mostly the
  works' inner rooms: the gate-passage, the strongroom, the foot of the stair, the sump gallery. These are places
  where the way back is a try or is hidden. 122 and 121 rooms have only the way back.
- **Shared names.** 30 and 33 names are shared by two or more places ("the bailey", "the great hall", "the undercroft",
  "the ossuary"). Go lists and aims can then name two places alike.
- **Paintings.**
  - 222 of 223 are imported. The one not yet imported is `city-overlook`, the owner's resend, now in main's assets;
    the branch's importer has not run on it.
  - 18 imported paintings map to nothing, and 90 map to kinds no sampled place names. Most of the latter are town
    trades whose shops the sample's towns do not keep.
- **Creatures.**
  - 146 species: 48 met in the sample, 41 eligible but not met, 35 only in authored scenes, 17 only summoned.
  - 5 have no path into a world, all intended: the golem by its material, and hens, pigs, sheep and goats kept by
    people.
- **Scenarios.**
  - Several stand in every country by design: the arcology, the circus, the fellowship, the Walk, the Silent Lord and
    the Bound Howe, placed in 4 of 4 worlds.
  - Some fit every world but are placed in few: oldcoin, disputation and quarrel in 1 of 4; fever in 2 of 4.
  - So an origin that needs one is not offered everywhere: herbs needs the fever, and in a1 (surveyed) the herbs
    origin starts with no errand held.
- **Abilities.** 26 records; 2 named nowhere (detonation, hornet_swarm).

## Rerun, 12 November, about 4:30 a.m. (daa96430)

- **Navigation census** (`bun scripts/navigation-census.ts world:kahitude world:a1 --top 8`): the same as above, figure
  for figure.
  - Reachability: 565/565 and 531/531.
  - Crowded places: 12 and 23.
  - Entered and never left: 35 and 36.
  - Only the way back: 122 and 121.
  - Shared names: 30 and 33.

  Nothing done since moved content. Output: /var/tmp/kyrie/m20/nav-census-0412.txt.
- **Signal census** (new, `/var/tmp/kyrie/m20/tools/signals.ts`, after E183–E185). It walks the first town's places
  (seven or eight each) in kahitude, a1, b2 and thorn, by the debt, order and kin origins. For every act led with that
  opens something, it checks each aspect the act bears beyond exploration, danger and the aim:
  - Is there an act inside that the aspect is for?
  - Does the talk draw that act in the same colour?
  - Is it above the talk's fold?

  Results:
  - 59 acts were led with, 49 of them people.
  - 37 bore blue (something to learn) and 3 rose (a companion).
  - Every one has its act, drawn in its colour and in view.
  - The three flags were the friendly dog's "Make much of", which is an act itself rather than an opener; its colour
    is on it.

  The spec `test/signal-inside.test.ts` holds the same property for kahitude.

second is "walking out step by step" (round 8, M). The shared names bear on aims. The rest is distribution, for
