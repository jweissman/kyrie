# Cold play, round 4, player E

Build: `397022c2` (main), served locally from a pinned copy, with the real opening. Screenshots
`/var/tmp/kyrie/m20/cold4/e/shots`, notes alongside. About 150 actions. Character: Wynn, human bard, once a herald
(Charisma 21), *The Sealed Letter*; hired Xyn; Joan joined. Intention given: a peaceable talker; avoid fights; notice
reactions and boredom.

## 1. Summary
Hauled the wagoner's cart out; at Thornhaven paid the stranded players' reckoning (8 coin), freeing their wagon; hired
Xyn. On the road to Elmham talked a deserter sergeant out of his toll; delivered the letter to Naillil (origin seen
through). At Fearnmaere bought herbs and sat up all night with a fevered child; the mother's friend Joan joined for
nothing. Night travel back; walked away from walking dead at Elmham; sang for a bed. Day 2: paid the toll, found the
circus, bargained down a merchant for its cook; looked into a dispute over moved boundary stones. Three errands done
(+100/+200/+300 xp), **no fight at all**. Stopped Day 2, 3:30 p.m., at Tuck's, 23 coin, 600/1000 xp.

## 2. The first five minutes
Clear and attractive: each creation card's Details says what it does in play; the opening's prose (a scroll-case,
"into no other hands") and the wagoner give who, where and why at once. The landing the first truly interesting
screen (24): a painted quay, eleven people, a sleeping bear with a clown for a pillow; Gosse ("I do not ask for money.
I never ask for money. I mention that it exists.") hooks at once.

## 3. What I pursued, and why
The letter (the game's: the map opened with Elmham selected). The players' bill (found myself, item 4 of 14 under
"Something else…"). The sick child, the circus larder, the stones dispute (found by asking). "I always felt I had a
choice", but the game's own nudges (the notice, the castellan, the herb-wife, the widow) all pointed at dungeons or
killing; the peaceful work was there but buried in topic lists.

## 6. Conversation, fights, inventory, map, notes
**Conversation:** the best writing (Gosse, Hepzibah, Jem Pudding, Honoria, Billie Ostrander); people remember ("The
very one!", "Again,"); but many generic "Ask about his work" townsfolk and database-like innkeeper lists; no bargaining
for a hire; no introducing oneself to the castellan's "I don't know you." **Fights:** none; two dangers flagged on the
map in advance with getaway odds; the toll talked down or paid; walked away from the dead (only Attack offered).
**Inventory:** clear; the inn's Services (bed, work, sing for your supper) a lovely touch. **Map:** beautiful;
crowded with fogged "way into X" labels; two zoom-outs to find Thornhaven; clicking labels hit the wrong ones.
**Notes:** everything, who told what and when; but it switched the current aim to the Nocstumul dungeon on its own,
never recorded Gosse's offer or the circus errand, no next step for the stones. The Story page nice.

## 7. Writing
Memorable: Gosse's tragedy "brought about by a flaw in the hero, who failed to pay his chain-man for four months";
"Nobody refuses a bear"; Honoria and Mumchance, "the most married thing you have ever seen"; the circus bills, "you'll
know they're near when the cow says COMING"; the fever night, "her hand goes cool in yours"; the sergeant, "nobody is
coming to hang you either"; "Properly asleep, not that other thing." Repetitive: both taprooms' identical toll
sentence; the sergeant's same speech the second day; "Hears you come, and finishes what he was doing before he looks
round" again and again. Unknowable: Joan's "You went when nobody else would" (I walked to a shop); the wagoner "talks
while you eat", nothing shown.

## 8. Danger
Always seen coming (the map stops with a red "!", a warning, getaway odds); pay, persuade, go on or walk away. Night
travel warned of only as "after dark". The walking dead felt like scenery.

## 9. Want to keep playing?
Moderately: the circus, Honoria, Gosse's company at Pentrice. But the stones dispute dead-ended, and all the open
errands were dungeons; with no talking way forward, interest faded.

## 10. Boredom and interest
Bored: generic townsfolk; long innkeeper lists; the same taproom sentence; hunting the map for Thornhaven; the stones
dispute. Pulled back: paying the players' bill (Gosse takes off his hat; on return "inspecting its wheels as if he had
just bought it"); the circus. Most interesting: sitting with the fevered child: eight hours, the risk of missing Gosse,
the best prose, a friend who joined for free.

## 11. Decisions
Paying the players' 8 coin (changed the landing, opened Gosse's offer, xp). Sitting with the child instead of hurrying
back (missed Gosse's departure, which happened without a word on screen; gained Joan). Persuading or paying the
sergeant (both worked). Hiring Xyn (never used).

   wagoner's opener; the players leaving without a line).
4. Navigation friction (round trips in Go…; the one-way garden; the circus gone from the map; "way into" labels;
   Escape in talk).
5. Text and pictures disagree, or text is clipped (danger and "You know now" boxes; scene text past the panel; a
   temple for a slab; day paintings at night; Naillil a "merchant").
