# Cold play, round 2, player B

Build: the `m20` site at `78c9b45c`, `?world=kahitude&opening` (the real opening); rebuilt once under the agent
mid-play by the builder's mistake (its loaded page kept its scripts). Screenshots `/var/tmp/kyrie/m20/cold2/b/shots`,
notes alongside. About 200 requests. Character: Mialee, elf woman thief, once a trader; *What is owed*; hired Xyn.
**The materially different character and approach.**

## 1. Summary
Asked Tuck for five days more (debt 55). Day 1: laid ratsbane for the corn-store rats instead of fighting; hired Xyn;
at Seolforfyrd slipped past the hob guards, freed the captive boy, learned from him that hobs flee a bell, found the
stolen bell and rang it — the whole hold ran; Xyn killed the Gaffer, who ambushed them on the hillside. Home: sold the
loot, hung the bell back over the well, slept at the inn. Day 2: burned out the quarry spiders and their eggs; crept up
on the necromancer in the ossuary and pinched out his black candle. **Paid Tuck the full 55 on the morning of Day 2:
"What is owed" seen through, level 2.** One reload; the save kept.

## 2. The first five minutes
Creation attractive (dark cards over a painting, clear icons, good detail panels, a tidy summary line). Clicks passed
through the detail overlays (skipped the ancestry screen unmeant, 03–08). The weapon screen confused: the shortsword
marked "A THIEF'S OWN" uses Strength while the dart gets +5 from Dexterity; a click back to the dart didn't register.
"The opening was very good": the painting, the debt, a ten-day clock, straight to Tuck's door. Within two minutes
Tuck's questions gave four or five aims; the notice and the landing four more: "interesting, but an avalanche".

## 3. What I pursued, and why
The debt (my own goal; five more days "a real choice with a cost"). The rats (Tuck's ratsbane suited a thief; I had to
guess the chandlery sold it). Seolforfyrd (nudged as "nearest"; chosen because the bell and the princes' strongroom
"sounded like thief work"). The quarry and the crypt, for coin (the crypt paid exactly the 15 I was short). "I always
felt I had a choice, sometimes too much of one."

## 5. Misleading or useless controls and text
Tuck sells thieves' tools (made me think I had none; I had a set). Backstab "1 in 6" with no Dexterity bonus; the dart
"suits you best" though the sling averages more. Examine on the heap and chest gave no text. Listening always "nothing
to hear". Headers repeat themselves ("The corn-store door to the old corn-store"). The MAP key glowing gold for no
reason. A stray "<" over "Go on" after a fight. The "Seen through" card clipped. Aiden "will not take your money
tonight", then charged 3 coin for the bed.

## 6. Conversation, fights, inventory, map, notes
**Conversation** the best system: voices (Gamel, Dannel Hobb, Tuck's late smile). Tuck has an acorn glyph for a
portrait. **Nobody reacts to my being an elf**, not even Erevan, the other elf. **Fights** clean and readable; over in two
or three rounds, mostly by Xyn; only Dirty Trick felt like a thief; a natural 20 for 3 damage deflating; "Mialee is under
hidden". **Inventory:** handsome; what I picked up went to Xyn's pack. **Map:** beautiful; far too many labels; couldn't
find known towns. **Notes:** useful; long; finished jobs quietly disappear.

## 7. Writing
Memorable: the bells "with its clapper torn out"; "The Gaffer screams like a kettle and goes out of the back way with
his mitre over his ears"; "He hears nothing until the flame is a wisp between your fingers"; the dead "fold down into the
bone-heaps like coats let fall"; Indira saying the names of the three in the opened graves; the bell rope. Stale or
contradictory: **after the bell was hung back the landing still said "The bell is gone… Nobody has gone after it"
(130)**; another shepherd's boy still telling of Silkholme beside the rescued one; the Gaffer "comes off his fleeces"
after he was dead (123); Tuck's "nobody from here has been inside it" after I had been (194b); a tied boy slaps a table;
"the best thing all morning" in the evening; "The spiders in the old workings is here."; paintings against the text.

## 8. Danger
Usually seen coming (map card warnings with escape odds, red danger boxes, reckonings); countdowns ("They will not
wait") added real tension; preparing and avoiding were possible (ratsbane, firebomb, antidote, a hired sword; slip past,
ring the bell, pinch out the candle). Stealing the rings brought the Gaffer's ambush on an unmarked hillside: fair but
unseen. Mialee took no damage all session.

## 9. Want to keep playing?
"Yes, more than I expected." Best moments: winning by a clue rather than a blade (the bell, the candle). Dampened by:
the world not noticing what I did; thief tricks hidden in Look…; the sellsword doing the killing; ancestry changing
nothing; deeds talked about as if they never happened.

## 10. Top five problems
1. The world doesn't react (the landing, Tuck, the rumours contradict what was just done).
2. Thief options buried in Look…; the firebomb silently a fight; keys never used.
3. Being an elf thief barely registers.
4. Too many aims too fast; their places missing from Go… until followed; Elmham not findable.
5. Paintings and geography against the text; small faults (creation overlays, empty Examine, the antidote, the bed).
