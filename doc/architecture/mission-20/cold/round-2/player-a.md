# Cold play, round 2, player A

Build: the `m20` site at `78c9b45c` (place first after the opening, the hold's fixes, the debt's selling loop), with
`?world=kahitude&opening` (the real opening). Rebuilt once under the agent mid-play by the builder's mistake (talk-page
and Notes changes; the agent's loaded page kept its scripts). Screenshots `/var/tmp/kyrie/m20/cold2/a/shots` (00–153).
About 150 actions. Character: Baldric, human warrior, once a trader; *What is owed*; hired Xyn.

## 1. Summary
Talked to Tuck and Aiden, read the notice, hired Xyn, cleared the rats and their nest (Tuck paid 10 and a lantern; sold
him a ring). First real expedition: three hours on the map to Seolforfyrd; talked past the hobs at the gate, found
their cave, took back the stolen bell and the Gaffer's rings, freed the shepherd's boy; the hobs attacked twice on the
way out and lost. Back in Thornhaven: **hung the bell back, sold the loot to Tuck (68 coin), paid the debt — "Seen
through", +300 xp, Day 1, 8:50 p.m.** A reload kept everything. Stopped at ~150 actions with 7 aims open.

## 2. The first five minutes
"Very clear and attractive." The reason-for-coming cards are the best thing in the opening; the painted interstitial and
"YOUR FIRST GOAL: 50 coin by market day" told me who I was and what I wanted; Tuck's yard (painting left, prose right)
calm and handsome. Then within ~3 minutes of talking to Tuck about 7 aims (bronze factor, Arborturris, Birds Cave, the
crown, rats, star-iron, the old workings), then 2 more on the landing and 2 on the notice: "exciting for a minute, then
a blur". A woodwright as the fountain of all the land's lore felt odd. Creation: screen order inconsistent, BACK from
ancestry jumped to the reason screen, the summary once said "An elf thief" after WARRIOR was clicked; "What did you do
before this?" a wall of 14 tiles and 26 chips.

## 3. What I pursued and why
The debt (the hook; "FOLLOWING" kept it in view). The rats, chosen as a safe first job (key, posted pay, close). Seolforfyrd,
pushed there by Aiden's "THE AIM NOW" and the Go panel, but the clapperless bells made me curious. Inside the cave —
the bell, the rings, the boy — "that was when I felt I had real choices."

- 33: Go… had no corn-store though I had its key and aim.
- 60: the guide pointed at Seolforfyrd while I did the rats.

- 138: clicking Elmham on the map to take the boy home did nothing.
- 150: after paying, Tuck says nobody has been inside Seolforfyrd "in living memory". I had just come back from it.

## 5. Misleading or useless controls and text
Faint line-glyph portraits for Tuck (an acorn) and the Gaffer; Baldric and Xyn share a portrait; paintings against the
prose (glade with deer for the toll-house, a Gothic cathedral for the toll-hall, one cellar image three times, the
cave-mouth image for the smoky cavern, a barred prison cell for a muck pit); a painting's credit caption on the scene; a
crowbar "for sale" in Look with no price or buy; "Ring the bell" listed in Look among objects; the MAP key glowing gold
for no clear reason; NEW AIM blocks clipped in the talk panel.

## 6. Conversation, fights, inventory, map, notes
**Conversation:** numbered options clear; "Pay what you owe: costs 50, you have 21" good; but each answer unlocking the
next topic makes people quest dispensers. **Fights:** clean layout, forecasts accurate, T convenient; damage reads oddly,
Xyn acted twice in a row, "The raiding party: Fall on You" leaked into prose; every fight trivial. **Inventory:** clear;
all loot went to Xyn unasked; selling in Trade worked well. **Map:** the route panel excellent; the fogged map cluttered.
**Notes:** "genuinely useful"; but "you saw it" for things only told, and the bell and rats not listed as seen through.

## 7. Writing
Memorable: the bear asleep "with a thin man for a pillow"; the Gaffer "in a bishop's mitre and somebody's spectacles";
"Gaffer says come in and wipe your feet. Don't touch nothing."; hanging the bell, "somebody gives the rope a pull before
you have your hands off it. Doors open all round the well." Mechanical: "The rats under the corn-store" five times; "The
south-western cave … they speak of is called Birds Cave"; "The way down to the old dead under Thornhaven is under
Thornhaven…"; "The strongroom door to the strongroom"; "It has not reached you yet." Contradictions: the boy "trying not
to cry" then "Well? Out with it!"; the landing still says a shepherd's boy is telling everyone about Silkholme while the
rescued boy stands beside me; a single ring described as plural.

## 8. Danger
Nothing threatened me; no fight cost a hit point. Forecasts and parley let me see and avoid trouble. Stealing the rings
had a consequence ("THIEVES!", two ambushes on the way out), which I liked, but those fights were trivial. The ten-day
deadline meaningless (paid on Day 1). Never short of light, food or health.

## 9. Want to keep playing?
"Mildly, yes" — for the undercroft, the locked strongroom (I'd buy the crowbar now). Charm and good painted moments; but
the main goal resolved in a day with no tension, people mostly hand out aims, and mismatched pictures broke the spell.
"I'd continue out of curiosity, not need."

## 10. Top five problems
1. No challenge or stakes.
2. Aim overload; the guide points away from the job being done.
3. Paintings and portraits contradicting the text.
4. Spatial confusion at the site (the hold a separate cave; light state; Elmham unpickable).
5. Creation flow glitches; Look/Take toggles and shifting buttons; no Give to the Gaffer, no asking about the bell.
