## Summary
T gave Eogan the letter, travelled about 7 hours to Neblalac, solved the riddle door ("Cloud"), quieted a trap and lost
to a cave hound alone. T fled, hired Krex at Mossminster (7 coin), camped, and came back. Together they killed the
hound, and T read the pillar of water-marks, which was the writing. T took it to Eogan: **"Seen through: the brothers'
errand"**, with 300 xp and 40 coin. Five other aims were waiting.

## Danger
The cave hound came with a Danger box, an honest odds line and a timer. Fleeing was a true 1 in 2. "With no one to get
you up" pointed T toward hiring help, which worked. The second fight said "should be won", but the hound bit for 6
before T could act.

## Keep playing?
"Mildly yes": the crypt (T holds its key) and the crown. But the errand ended flatly, and six new aims felt like a list
rather than a pull.

## Most interesting
