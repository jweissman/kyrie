# Mission 18: the interface (Gate A), as built for the first review

This is the design system the reconstruction follows, what it has rebuilt so far, what it has not, and what it asks of
you. The [brief](brief.md) keeps the requirements (A1–A10) and their status; the [register](register.md) the inherited
findings this work answers (M17-25, M17-27, M17-29, M17-31, M17-71, PRE-04, M17-69 and others).

## The lantern

One bounded stage, lit, inside a dark room.

- **The ground** is near-black. Behind the stage, the painting of where you are (the place's own scenery, or the
  country's before you enter it) lies far back, dimmed and out of focus, tinted by the hour: warm in the morning, gold
  in the afternoon, ember at evening, blue at night. Indoors and in the dark there is no painting, only the dark.
- **The stage** is the game. On a desktop about a fifth of the width is kept as margin (a tenth each side) and the
  stage is capped near 1320 by 940; on a tablet the margin narrows; on a phone the stage is the screen inside its safe
  areas. Its bounds are marked by four gold corners rather than boxed. Nothing reaches the screen's edges, the page
  itself never scrolls, and only the regions that must (the telling, a conversation's words, a book) scroll inside it.
- **The title** is the one page that breaks out of the stage: the whole screen, dark.

## Colour says something

| Colour | Means |
|---|---|
| Gold | Where your attention is: the choice under your finger or keys, your aim, whose turn it is, where you are and where you are bound on the map |
| Ember | Harm, danger, a foe, what cannot be undone, a risk |
| Verdigris | Something learned or found |
| Azure | Water; what is seen from somewhere high |
| Violet | Magic and wonder; a level gained; what has changed since you were last somewhere |
| Rose | Kin and affection: family, whoever travels with you, whoever would join you |

Never colour alone: each has its words beside it.

## Type

Newsreader tells and speaks (ivory on the dark, about 19–20px). Jost, a geometric face, names and does: place names
large and light, labels in tracked capitals, every control. The narrator's voice and the characters' are the serif;
the game's own voice is the geometric sans.

## Controls

Every choice is a control that is pressed, never a word underlined. At rest, a quiet key with a diamond before it.
Pointed at, or reached by the keys, it lights: the diamond turns and fills gold, light sweeps in from its leading edge,
a gold bar marks the keys' place. Pressed, it sinks a pixel. Chosen, it holds its light while the world answers.
Disabled, it is drawn dashed and says why. A risk's diamond is ember and says "a risk"; what cannot be undone says so
before it is done. Nothing moves under the finger but light and colour: no layout shifts. Replies in a conversation
are numbered keys; the primary action (Continue, Set out, Go on) is solid gold. Less motion, when asked for, turns the
motion off.

## The compositions rebuilt

- **The opening.** Once a session: a studio mark (seven towers rising in light from a horizon, a star over the
  tallest), then black, then the country's painting coming up behind the name, a gold horizon drawn under it, and the
  ways in appearing one by one, centred: Continue (with where and when), Begin, The encyclopedia (for now the
  handbooks), Options. A gold diamond slides to the way the pointer or keys are on. Any key or touch skips; "Open a
  save file" moved into Options.
- **Creation, the choice of a life.** One question a page, answered from a few large cards in a row (more a swipe or
  an arrow away): *Why have you come to Meósfaenn?* first, then your people, your calling, its way (a god, a school,
  a discipline), what you were before (when your reason leaves it open: first the kind of life, then the trade),
  *What do you carry into a fight?* (the family of weapon, then the weapon, each card led by its die and what it
  averages at your scores), *What are you made of?* (six tiles, swapped by choosing one then another), and last your
  name with the life said back. The rules for the chosen card are beneath the cards, not on every card. Your life so
  far is a sentence at the foot. No likeness of you anywhere. "Set out now" is on every page.
- **A place.** Its painting across the head of the stage with its name, where and when set on it (a typographic head
  where there is no fitting painting); the telling under it, read first; beside it (or under it on an upright screen)
  who is here and the ways on, whoever you have a stake in first and larger, ringed in rose for kin or gold for an
  aim; at the foot, quietly, your aim and yourself and what you can do of yourself. A busy place on an upright screen
  shows its nearest three ways as tiles and every way a touch away on a sheet.
- **A conversation.** Kept as you rated it: the painting as large as the stage allows with the name set on it, the
  words newest last, the replies numbered, taking your leave always in view; now lit, with what you learn in
  verdigris and what you said in gold.
- **A fight.** The company and what it faces as lit cards with their health, the acting fighter ringed in gold, foes
  in ember with whom they mean to strike and what they resist or fear; what has just passed between them; the turn
  order as a strip; the moves as a hand of cards at the foot.
- **The map.** One terrain-first map: the country as hexes of what each is (forest with its trees, fell, heath,
  fen, peak, water, open land), rivers glowing, roads cased ochre on black so they read as roads, towns as roofs, sites
  as broken arches, you in gold and where you are bound ringed in gold. The land you have seen is wider now (a couple
  of hexes either side of a road walked); what you only know of is a faint grid. Choosing a place gives its way leg
  by leg and setting out. The square route diagram is gone.

## Not yet rebuilt

The road between places and one thing looked at closely still use the old compositions in the new colours; commerce
and the pack, advancement, and public events (rituals, hearings, performances) have no composition of their own yet;
the books (character, notes, story) are restyled, not recomposed. The portrait audit (A10) has not begun. These come
next, after your look at what is here.

## What I ask of you

1. Play a fresh game on the iPad (title, creation, the first town, a conversation, a fight, the map) and say whether
   this direction is right before I carry it through the rest.
2. The studio ident carries no name. What should it say, if anything?
3. The title reads KYRIE (your commission's name for the game); the old page said "Kyr". Which?
4. The margins: a tenth each side on a desktop. Wider, or narrower?
