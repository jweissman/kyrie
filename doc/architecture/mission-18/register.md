# Mission 18: the register

Mission 18 inherits every finding [Mission 17's register](../mission-17/register.md) left open or partial: 11 open, 61
partial and one not reproduced, 73 rows in all (52 inherited by Mission 17 from Mission 16, 16 found in Mission 17,
5 from the adventures' authors). Each keeps its identifier and its source; each is assigned here to the gate of this
mission whose construction answers it, with a priority, what remains, the repair or the redesign that will supersede
it, and how it will be verified. Mission 17's evidence for each is carried verbatim in the appendix. The 151 rows
Mission 17 fixed stay its record and are not re-proved unless a change threatens them. Findings new to this mission
are added at the end under `M18-`.

**Dispositions**: Fixed (commit and test), Partial (what is done, what remains), Open, Superseded (by a named
redesign, verified against that redesign: removing the old code is not evidence), Not reproduced (versions, seeds,
scenarios), and Unresolved (only with the owner's acknowledgement). **Priorities**: P0, it blocks a P0 gate's
acceptance or the pleasure of ordinary play; P1, a real player-visible or correctness cost, or a dependency of P0
work; P2, moderate, or chiefly authoring; P3, small.

The mission does not work these in their old order: each is closed inside the construction it affects, small
independent ones batched.

### The IF review: holistic remedies (doc/authoring/if-fitness-and-affordances.md, 2026-10-30)

A review of Kyr as an interactive-fiction language proposes ten affordances, each meant to absorb a family of the
authors' frictions. Each is checked here against Kyr as it now stands (what exists, what would conflict with the
direction of fewer, stronger concepts) and given the rows it would hold; those rows stay open as its evidence and
acceptance tests until it lands. None is taken as a commitment to the review's syntax: each lowers into the facts,
rules and operators Kyr already has, or it is not done. The review's order (trace first, then typed effects, one
viewer projection, telling order, rule phases) is kept.

| ID | Remedy | Checked against Kyr now | Holds | Gate | Pri | Now | First step |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| IFR-01 | `why` and a causal trace: why an action is not offered, down to an effect that would be refused; what each watch ran and why | `kyrie inspect` already names a withheld action's failing conditions; there is no effect simulation, rule or watch trace, and LUL-03 showed how silent the tick can be | AUT-23, HTH-10, GTR-X11, GTR-X16, LUL-03's kind | D | P1 | Open | Effect admissibility (a measure's floor) in `inspect`; then `kyrie trace` over a save by watch, rule or fact |
| IFR-02 | Typed transformations: move, place, pay (strict), raise/lower (clamped), give or spend a particular item, create from stock | `Give`/`Spend` exist by item or kind but not both; no clamp, no ceiling; payment is a strict decrease.
