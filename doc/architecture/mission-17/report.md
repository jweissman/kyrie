# Mission 17 report

Mission 17 was to make a fresh campaign enjoyable for thirty minutes, feeling like a fantasy role-playing game and not
a website, and to make authoring pleasant; to close the 105 findings Mission 16 left open or partial; to rebuild the
interface; and to bring the adventures together in one dense region. Your interim report reordered it: what a player
meets first, then the interface on a tablet, then a satisfying local adventure, then the engine and authoring repairs
those need, and the studio last. This report says where that stands. The running record is the
[register](register.md); the plays it rests on are in [play](play/).

Four plays rest under this report: I played it as you would (play 8, a bard with a debt, at tablet size by touch, on
0a19de2); an evaluator who had read nothing of the game played it for about 125 taps on bdc87ba
([its report](play/cold-evaluation.md)); and I played two fresh games on the final builds, a cleric with feverwort
([play 9](play/play-9.md)) and a mage coming home ([play 10](play/play-10.md)). What each found was fixed where it was
the game's fault, and is in the register under its name.

## 1. What thirty minutes is like now

Play 9 began with no world named, as you would begin: Hild, a dwarf cleric of the storm, come into Roseweald, forest
country, with feverwort for a sick child. The village green was the richest page I have seen the game draw: a shrine, a
notice offering forty crowns for the Mower of the Long Man, Gosse's playbill, the toll grumbled over, a cat at my
ankles. The fever was seen through in two taps. The woman who speaks for the village had six things to ask after; one
was a cave four hours off where something takes a ewe a night, and the journey there went in one choice, told leg by
leg.

At the cave the shepherd wanted the spiders burned out; at the silk-frames above the sink, the woman who gathers their
silk said the brood-mother was only starving. I took the dead ewe down, hacked through the webs rather than burn them,
and left it slowly. Nothing was fought: the brood ate and went quiet, and the silk-gatherer heard them spinning again.
That stretch, two people and a choice with a quiet way through it, is what this game is for. The night after, two
philosophers were disputing on Tatharimlad's market-place, and Hild's insight saw where one of them looked at each
"nothing stays". A bed at the inn gave her the second level; in the night, four brothers had gone up to the old mine
with a bell, and I found their mark at its turning the next afternoon, after hiring a bard at the inn. The mine had
spiders waiting in ambush, fought on the new field at tablet size and won; then a hidden switch's blade took Hild from
23 hit points to 12, and would have killed the bard. That is where I stopped.

What lost me was small and frequent, and all fixed after the play: the portcullis's tries at the lone tower drawn and
doing nothing at a touch; a stale aim at the head of the ways after I had told both people it named; the child's
mother and sister of one name, the mother painted cutting a fish; "I've lost 1 to them", "the webs is in the way",
"spiders's bite", "Night, sunny"; a way down "by the light you carry" with nothing lit. What is not fixed: the tower
wanted rope, climbing, lockwork or brawn, and a trap in the mine had no answer for this company but a pole it did not
have.

Play 10 began again from nothing: a human mage coming home to Merinael, where her father, a drover, had gone out with
the cart and not come back. Her brother on a rail with their father's spear joined at once, and told her the left wheel
had been out of true all spring; at the ford he stopped where the ruts went off the road: "That is the left wheel.
That is his." The cart was in the ditch and their father under it. I buried him by the road and took his knife from
the cairn; at home their mother said "I knew. The dog knew", and of the knife, "Keep it. He would want it used, not
hung on a wall." That is the game at its best, and every beat answered what I had done. What broke it was the family
itself: the brother of another people than his sister, an old man in a window and then a young shepherd; the mother
younger than her daughter, greeted in the brother's words, and called by her full name in the telling; "went out on
the ford"; and once the thread was done, nothing to do, though the brother had the next one in him if asked. Nearly
all of it is fixed (the brother now says it unasked); the choices still call her by her name.

### What pulls a player on

The cold evaluator, who had never seen the game, put it plainly: "Yes, in patches, and more than I expected from pages
of text. The writing carries it." What pulled them on was the writing in people's mouths (a priest reading a letter in
a dead brother's hand; a widow counting coins into their palm and going inside), hooks with weight ("Nobody goes in
now. They say something lives in the water"), the dice shown honestly and biting (a trap that put them on the floor
and a night not knowing whether they would wake), a world that moved while they were elsewhere (graves opening one a
night, a spider cave burned out by someone else), and the level gained ("every choice shows exactly what it changes").
In play 8 the same: the unquiet tower was a real small adventure, found by asking at the inn, entered by the crypt
with a companion's torch, fought, and ended by reading a daybook and smashing a lantern; and the town said so when I
came back.

### What still loses a player

- **A place read as a page of rows.** "Every place is a long scroll of bordered lists." Places, travel and fights read
  as well-made web pages (M17-71). A place and a fight were recomposed after it (section 2), but who is here is still a
  list of names and roles, a place has no picture of its own, and travel is still told as a log: this is the largest
  thing still to do, and it is a matter of composition, which your interim report asked for and this mission did only
  part of.
- **The first ruin is lethal.** A careful level-one company that reaches the first ruin it is told of falls there a
  third of the time, nearly always set upon (the audit below). That is the old school's danger, but it is felt as a
  wall by a lone mage or bard.
- **Sameness.** The same lines from town to town are fewer than they were (four ways each calling is seen at work,
  six cats, news in each teller's words, greetings by manner), but a returning player still hears the toll and the
  raiders' paragraph again.
- **Wrong faces.** A third to two thirds of the people met have a painting; the rest are met in type. A painting is
  sometimes wrong for the person (a mourner is now painted in mourning; one painting can still serve two villages).

## 2. What changed for the player

**A place and a fight, recomposed** (a942a22, after the cold evaluation's "a Fighting Fantasy book crossed with a
wiki"). A place is a scene above, scrolling, and its ways on apart beneath it: on a tablet held upright or a phone at
the foot of the screen, as a conversation stands under its painting, never a scroll away; set as a book's index, each
way where it goes, a dotted leader, how long, the road it goes by under it. A fight is a field: each fighter a line
with a bar (a foe's in red, from the right, and at whom it strikes first: "→ Ama · strike with the javelin"), the order
of the round a strip of names with whoever acts marked, and the moves a hand of cards at the foot.

**The page is a game's, not a web application's** (5f4f623, after your interim report). There is no bar across the
top: where and when are said once under the heading of what is before you, and the books (yourself, the notes, the
pack, the map, the story) open from one control in the corner, or by their keys. Nothing is folded into a tree: every
way on is a control that says where it goes and how long, the nearest places you know are journeys to set out on at
once, and a journey set out on is held on every page until you come there or give it up, going on by the known ways
without a choice at each junction. On a tablet held upright, or a phone, the page is one leaf inside the screen's safe
areas, with controls a finger can take, and is held to the screen at 1440, 820 and 390 by the browser specs.

**Creation is five pages of cards**: you and your people, your calling, your qualities (each said by the rules at your
score, and placed where your calling wants them until you change them over), your weapon from every ordinary weapon
your calling is trained to, and why you have come. You are imagined, not painted: no likeness is chosen for you.

**Arriving and setting out.** Your own errand is your aim, said at the head of the ways on; an aim taken up is said
when it is, and a place an aim or a rumour names is somewhere to set out for, however far. Learning where something is
says how far it is. A journey that would end after dark says so before it is begun; a journey toward a place in wild
country whose way in you do not know goes to the wild's edge and asks there. One who comes alone is told, where they
begin, who at the first inn would come along, and what each hire would bring is a card.

**The first fight.** What falls on the company gives it a moment (8999666): seen coming, it acts first; sprung on it,
its turn after the first blows is its moment, in which getting back the way it came is certain, and the page says how
the company reckons the fight from there. Running is surer each time it is tried. What the company carries to use up
(alchemist's fire, a draught) is a choice in the fight (6225833); a creature taunted shows the intent its taunt has
given it; who is still to act leaves out the fallen; a wound bleeds and festers or stays clean as the wound's.

**Talking.** A conversation is a painting with the person's name on it and the conversation at its foot, the replies
numbered under what was said, a seller's wares as controls on the counter, and taking your leave always in view
(d8c2774, then the cold evaluation's room for the words). Someone met again is greeted as met again (543ac0e), news is
told in each teller's own words (269d1a3), and a person's words can be said only while something holds. The unquiet
tower can be talked through: its necromancer says, while he works, that it is one of the dead he wants; the shrine's
priest who wrote his sister's name gives it back; called by it she asks to be let go, and told so he puts out the
flame himself.

**The town's work for one who would not fight** (24f695a): an evening's work at the inn for the bed and supper, a song
for the supper from one who can hold a room, a toll talked down as well as paid or run.

**Seeing from high places** (59abfb3, 71a4c70): a tower's top, a crag or a gate-tower shows the country round the
compass, each way said by its bearing and how far it looks, a ruin known for what it is once a calling makes it out,
somewhere to set out for from the view itself; with a spyglass, further.

**The country goes on** (1bdc9e6): what a country's towns did not have going on at the start begins later, one on the
third morning and another every other morning, and the inns talk of it; a place come back to says what has changed
since; what a company did not do, someone else may (a spider cave burned out while the cold evaluator was away).

**The dark, and being down.** Everyone sets out with a candle, and whoever keeps travellers sells torches and candles;
what bars a way out of the dark is heard there, not seen; one who lies senseless is said to be so on the page, with
what follows, and what they carry is under one choice.

## 3. The ten demonstrations

1. **Thirty minutes in Imbar.** Begin with nothing named, on a tablet: play 9 above is one such thirty minutes, the cold
   evaluation another. Seen; the verdict is in section 1.
2. **A dense shared region, a city interleaving adventures.** Not met. Every completed adventure is installed and
   reachable in one continuing world (twelve countries, each with its skyline seen from a height over its march), but
   as separate countries, not interleaved in one valley or city. It is the first thing in section 6.
3. **A vantage-point discovery.** Climb a lone tower, a crag or a gate-tower in any generated country (59abfb3): the
   country round the compass, each way by its bearing and how far it looks; a ruin known for what it is once a
   calling makes it out; with a spyglass, further (71a4c70); and set out for what was seen from the view itself.
4. **A substantial conversation with initiative.** The unquiet tower talked through (316133e): the necromancer says
   what he wants while he works, the priest gives back the name he wrote, called by it she asks to be let go. In the
   Banns at Hessel, Ottery asks you, after the footpads, whether you have ever lied to somebody for their own good,
   and what you answer is held to.
5. **A major character through the public tools.** `bin/kyrie new character mara --major --in <adventure>`, `bin/kyrie
   play character mara --context …`, and a play kept as a test with `--keep` (69cffd9). Built before your interim
   report, and not extended after it, as it asked.
6. **Module extensibility.** A module's own kinds of thing and species (`Kind … like …`, `Species … like …`), its own
   tables and generators through Deem's `select()` and scoped `gen()` (0948983), and this session's `Give`, `Spend`,
   `within`, `Remarks at`, "or to nothing" and a campaign's own night, each with a probe module that uses it.
7. **Progression that opens a possibility.** Play 9: a night under a roof gave Hild her second level, the development
   page said what each choice changes, and Persuasion opened singing for her supper and talking down a toll.
8. **A persistent world.** The renewal floor (1bdc9e6): what a country's towns did not draw at the start begins later
   and the inns talk of it; a place come back to says what has changed since; in play 9, four brothers went up to the
   old mine in the night and their mark was at its turning the next afternoon; in the cold evaluation, a spider cave
   burned out by someone else while the player was away.
9. **Distinct presentations.** A chapter's arrival (a plate of the country, the chapter, the telling), a conversation
   (the painting with the words at its foot), a fight (the field), a public disputation on a market-place, a song for
   your supper. A physical encounter on a way (a portcullis, a tripwire) is the way's own rows, now each working.
10. **A verified register and a credible verdict.** Section 4, and the three plays above.

## 5. Measurements

- **The first ruin** (the play-structure investigation's careful player, from the debt origin to the first ruin it is
  told of, twenty seeds a company, 28 October): it is reached 85% of the time; a lone mage falls there 41% of the time,
  a mage with the town's traveller 35%, a warrior 29%, nearly all of them set upon by a band; 2 of 17 lone mages died to
  a trap (the investigation had found 9 of 14). Every calling now has an answer to some traps (PT-20).
- **A lone caller in the big ruin** (the class viability rerun, every band twenty times, four countries): median wins
  alone, warrior 0.3 to 0.9, cleric 0.05 to 0.55, thief and mage none; with the first town's traveller, warrior 0.85 to
  1, cleric 0.5 to 1, thief 0.15 to 0.8, mage 0.05 to 0.8 (PT-11).
- **First fights** (the laboratory's careful players, four countries, five callings, 150 steps): fights lost that came
  to the company, five before the moment of 8999666 and one after (CMB-01).
- **Ruins** (fifty, six countries): none without something to meet, look at, change, take or find out, each with two
  or more (PT-40).
- **News** (eight countries): twenty lines alike but for their names before news was told in each teller's words, none
  after (NAR-04).
- **Renewal** (a week at the first inn of six countries): five now have something new talked of by the third or fifth
  morning (PT-29).
- **Faces** (six countries' towns, every room by day): 36% to 69% of the people met have a painting (M17-23).
- **Speed**: a command once a second country is drawn, about 152 ms (it had been a second: COR-22); a long journey of
  What Tessary Owes, 25 ms a command (it had been 40: M17-01); a city of many rooms, 31 (85: M17-02).
- **The gates**: 1,480 tests at 49a7d3a, 54 of which run only when asked for (the 26 browser specs, the long descents,
  the pinned Orsino registry); the suite about fourteen minutes on sixteen workers, the browser specs two and a half.
