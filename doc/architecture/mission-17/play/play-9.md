# Play 9: a fresh game on the final build, at tablet size

Played on 28 October on the build of a942a22 (the place's ways at the foot of the screen, the fight as a field), at
820 by 1180 by touch, with no world named: a fresh game as the owner would begin one, by the controls a player sees.
About forty taps, a day and a half of the game's time. The findings that were the game's fault are fixed in the
commit after this one, and are in the register as M17-74 to M17-88.

## The game

Hild, a dwarf cleric of the storm (Wisdom 20, Dexterity 6), come to Roseweald, forest country, with feverwort for a
sick child at Lassëael.

1. **The opening.** A plate of the country, the chapter's name, the telling, and the one way on at the foot. The
   herb-wife's words ("Don't dawdle.") were the first thing I wanted to act on.
2. **Lassëael green.** A rich village page: a shrine, a notice from Merrowdale (forty crowns for the Mower of the Long
   Man), Gosse's playbill, the toll grumbled over, a cat that winds round your ankles. The child's mother and her elder
   sister were both called Hadarai, and the mother was painted as the smiling woman cutting a fish.
3. **The fever.** Given the feverwort, "Hadarai takes the herbs inside without a word. By evening the fever has
   broken." Seen through, 300 experience. Quick, but it pays. Asked how the child is, a few minutes later in the same
   morning: "She sat up this morning and asked for bread."
4. **Lia, who speaks for the village.** Wares on the counter, a hayloft bed ("until 4:10 p.m."), work for keep, and six
   things to ask after. The ewes taken near a cave south of Tatharimlad: four hours off, set out for at once.
5. **The cave, and Garth.** One journey, told leg by leg. Garth, counting sheep that are not there: spiders from
   Silkholme sink take a ewe a night; burn them out for eight coin, though Odile keeps them for their silk. "I've lost
   1 to them so far," he said, and he leaned on "his crook" but did not like what "they" saw.
6. **The sink.** The shaft wants rope; the frame terrace has Odile, pacing, who greeted me cheerfully ("Now then! A new
   face.") before telling me the brood-mother is starving and only needs feeding. Garth gave the dead ewe, spitting.
   Down by the seep crawl "by the light you carry", with nothing lit; "the webs is in the way"; a torch lit, the webs
   hacked through, the ewe left slowly for the brood-mother. Seen through without a blow, 750 experience. The best
   stretch of the play: two people, a choice, a quiet way through, and Odile's "Listen… They're spinning again."
7. **Tatharimlad at night.** The philosophers disputing on the market-place, and Hild's insight seeing where Jassin's
   eyes go at each "nothing stays": lovely. The running head said "Night, sunny". The brood's aim, "Garth and Odile will
   want to know", stayed after both were told, and headed the ways in place of the tower's new aim.
8. **The White Chalice.** "Take a bed and supper · until morning". Every other place in the town said again that it
   was "by the market-place of Tatharimlad". A bed: level 2, +12 hit points, and the world had moved in the night: four
   brothers gone up to Nigermons at first light with lanterns and a bell. Naivara, a pedlar and an elf bard, hired for
   three coin; at the tower she said "People pay for a story about a place like that. More if you come back out of
   it."
9. **Foliumspira.** Every way in wanted rope, climbing, lockwork or brawn. The portcullis's tries ("Push and pull at
   it") were drawn and did nothing at a touch.
10. **Nigermons.** The brothers' mark at the turning, with a candle-end under it. Spiders in ambush, seen first; the
    wait said before the warning, and the Attack marked as something they "will not wait for". The fight, as a field
    at tablet size: the turn strip, the bars, "→ Naivara · bite", the hand of moves at the foot; won in five rounds,
    "Hild looks away solemnly" as a spider fell; "the second of the agile cave spiders's bite". After it, the dead
    spiders were still under "Danger", and nothing on the page offered to tend Naivara: the herbs were in the pack
    book, her own cure on her sheet. "1 coin, all told" from a row of coins in a crack.
11. **The crossing.** "Hild goes first / Naivara goes first" before a trapped way; a scything blade took Hild from 23
    to 12, and would have killed Naivara. The only other answer was a ten-foot pole. The page told "The hidden switch
    has gone off, and is spent." before "A scything blade swings through the air."

## What pulled, and what lost

**Pulled:** the brood's two sides and the quiet way through it; the philosophers; the world going on without me (the
brothers at Nigermons, their mark at the turning); a hook at every inn; the fight's field.

**Lost:** a control that did nothing (the portcullis's tries); a stale aim at the head of the ways; small faults that
break the voice (numerals in speech, a pronoun that changes, "spiders's", "the webs is"); a trap with no answer for
this company but a pole it did not have.

## Fixed after this play

The barred way's tries; the mother and sister with one name ("the younger"); a worried mother painted at home, of her
years; the child asleep the same day, up and asking for bread the next; Garth's, Odile's and the brood-mother's
pronouns, and a test that every person the stories call "he" or "she" says so; small counts told in words; a way under
the ground told by the light carried, or with none; "the webs are in the way"; the brood's aim done when both are
told, and Garth's quiet night an aim of its own; a sunny sky at night said as clear; the town's shared way said once;
the wait told after the warning; nothing done toward the warner marked as too long for them; whose a blow is, for one
of a band; the dead not under "Danger"; a blow taken on the way in told before the place; the town index's times.

**Left:** tending a hurt companion from the place itself (it is in the book); a trap with an answer for every company
(M17-56); the greeting's manner against the scene (Odile cheerful while frightened); an aim re-told as "A new aim" when
only its text grew; the ring that seemed to stay after it was taken (not reproduced: likely two rings).
