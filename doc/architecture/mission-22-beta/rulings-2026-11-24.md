  product is the feature map.
  - Its sections are thought out encyclopedically: the World has institutions (religion, learning, guilds, law,
    orders) beneath it.
  - Its items are named as feature titles and specific capabilities, not as scenarios.
  - Its experience pins are in it beside its feature pins.
  - Built as doc/architecture/feature-map.json, `scripts/feature-map.ts` and doc/architecture/feature-map.md. The
    capabilities pinned that no request asked for were filed as requests under their titles (OR-340 to OR-364);
    those neither built nor asked for (news and rumour, learning) as directions (OR-365, OR-366).
- **Common blockers** (the owner: "are there common blockers across the features? What does it suggest we should
  prioritize?"): recorded in register-kinds.json's blockedBy, and shown first in the map's negative space:
  - TB-09 (workflows the engine's) blocks ten P0/P1 defects;
  - AP-03 (facets inferred) eight;
  - AX-04 (generating to the AST) four;
  - OR-310 (a varied starting repertoire) three;
  - AX-05 (the screen built incrementally) two.

  The owner likes TB-09 "for architectural and hygiene reasons (and just to know we're at full parity across
  clients)", but not to interrupt existing work: research it, and organise the effort.
- **The quantity gate.** "For symmetry, a script/quantity which asserts similar conditions but over the world":
  combat density, the economy (treasure against what markets sell), Atlas density on walked runs, and more. Built as

  because the world is right, never by widening them.
- **Ratcheting quality toward its targets** (stepping a baseline down on a schedule toward Beta's 15 ms decision, 30 ms
  turn and 5 ms leg) was proposed. It awaits the owner.

## Architecture

- **Facets are inferred, never declared.** "We should infer that something can be learned, taught or discovered by
  what Kyr code actually does: a story-node or conversation in that direction with an action that produces a new
  piece of knowledge for the player, or teaches them an ability." Authors are not burdened with indicators. AP-03 is
  reworded so, its author-side `Facet` form withdrawn.
- **Packages:** `kyr-lang`, `kyrie-runtime` and `kyrie-player`, for platform–product separation.
  - "Ideally everything in `web` becomes just `packages/kyrie-player` too, but that's a longer-term transformation."
  - The external reviewer's consolidation plan is checked in, and mapped onto the packages.
  - See [platform-separation.md](platform-separation.md) and
    [orthogonal-extraction-consolidation-plan.md](orthogonal-extraction-consolidation-plan.md).
- **Medallion layers** for world assembly: raw data as bronze; organisation, cleanup and checking into a silver model
  internal consumers use; a gold fact-and-dimension warehouse of the whole. Filed as OR-408, the vocabulary of the
  platform note.
- **Horizons for evaluation**, the simpler and more pressing: "just testing a tiny rule doesn't pull in the
  universe". Filed as OR-407, blocked by BX-02.
- **Housekeeping.** The top-level `modules/` folder is gone, its note beside its situation and its generators kept
  as history.
- **The data folder's direction:** "two subfolders, settings (campaign-level metadata and tables) and scenarios
  (specific adventures and stories)", with global rule modules beside them, so that humans can latch onto authoring.
  Also review whether the Deem tables and templates are used to their best, and begin to scope gamma: completing the
  SRD, and migrating the remaining Orsino ideas. All three are researched before anything moves.

## The starting world

- **OR-311, the first dungeon:** "significant and deep: at least two levels of serious resistance, each with a boss
  and a treasure cache; ideally a companion at the dungeon, and searchable gear near the secure entry." The quantity
  gate finds the first dungeon by that definition.
- **The first town simplified:** "initial quests folded behind investigation checks rather than just given out
  constantly".

- **Danger, and where combat lives** (OR-321): "I'm fine with roads having a little more danger (the world is starved
  for genuine danger as it is), but the key has to be more dense combat in specific, easy-to-find locations which
  quests return you to periodically and which reward multiple expeditions." The quantity gate's combat measure reads
  so: fights at combat places and on the roads apart, and the places a quest returns to, rewarding each expedition.
- **Design calls.** "Use your best judgment as creative director on design decisions." Ask the owner only for
  direction-level or artistic choices.

## The page

- **Nothing flashes or blinks an Atlas colour, especially on the world map.** Held by test/small/no-flash.
- **Every screen is an engine page.** "Ideally every screen in the app is just the engine telling the presentation
  layer what to do within a fixed, simple layout with different hints (use this instrument or show a background like
  this; for astronomy activities, show the stars if you can). The title is ideally just a normal page from the engine;
  it gets hints to show the embers but otherwise it should be the same as anything else (fine to have a hint to center
  things, but also fine to experiment with a completely unified UI where even the title page conforms)." Asked of the
  title at a desk's size (OR-382), which is reworded so and given to the Game stream.

## Integration

- **Mending dead ends is part of integration.** "Fixing simple dead-ends like the pointless talk would be reasonably
  part of integration, if it's simple enough to resolve by adding gifts, taught things or simple consequences." The
  Weather Drove's nineteen word-only topics were mended so (OR-406), and its softlock given an ending (OR-400).
- **Speed for the builders.** "Use any bandwidth you have to cheapen tests, improve inner-loop performance and so on."
