# The one who follows the gold

Whenever a control has a gold mark, press it; take up what the game offers you to pursue, and follow its lead.
When nothing is gold, say so in your log and choose as you think the game means you to. Record each time the gold
led you somewhere that made sense, nowhere, round in a circle, or into danger unwarned, and whether a task you
followed came to an end you could tell. Stop after about 150 presses, or sooner if you would stop playing.

(Then follow common.md.)
