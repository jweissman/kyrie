# Prose comparison: the first hour, before and after (17 November 2026)

For the owner to read aloud ([plain-prose.md](plain-prose.md), point 7). Every sentence below was told in play: the
first hour of four generated countries (a1, kite, thorn and Iscoed), every origin each offers, the road meeting's
every answer, and two of the laboratory's unguided players (one that talks and looks, one that wanders), 59 walks and
1,751 pages, recorded by `bun scripts/prose-census.ts` before and after the edit. "Walks" is how many of the 59 met
the sentence. The names (Moll, Bess, Beatrix, Brightbridge) are the worlds' own.

The journey reader's route through Iscoed's drove (the same opening in the order one player met it) is
[the reader's compare.md](../architecture/mission-22/journey-reader/iscoed-drove/compare.md), captured against replayed.

The horse's head ("The horse throws its head twice and then leans into the collar. With the wagoner roaring at the
wheel, the cart comes up out of the ditch in a slither of meal.") is the lead's, now "You take the horse by the bridle
and lead it forward while the wagoner pushes on the wheel. The cart comes up out of the ditch and back onto the
road."; met by every player who takes the horse's head.

Also edited, met fewer than twice in the census but in the same tables and of the same kind (the full list, with
each reason, is the commit's): the rest of the animals' greetings and petting ("purrs like a kettle", "It is not a
dignified dog", "tail goes round like a cartwheel"), two farewells, four greetings' hedges, the journey's remaining
figures (the march where "the land changes its mind", the rain that "sets out with you", "a low lid of cloud"),
the poacher's boy and the pedlar's answers ("fights like a cat", "as if he had always been there", "Nobody,
afterwards, will be able to say you did anything at all"), a carrier's wheel ("on the fourth three ... with a noise
like a boot coming out of a bog"), and singing for coin ("The room goes quiet for you"). 47 passages in all, and the
naming sentence.

## Left as written

- **Remarkable moments:** the wonders ("a lamp seen through a hand"), the works' great set pieces, the circus's acts,
  Gosse's players ("a thin man with a face like a folded napkin"). These are chosen or rare, and their figures do
  work a plain word cannot.
- **Plain already:** "You put an ear to the corn-store padlock, and hold your breath. There is nothing to hear from
  it."; "The sky greys, then lightens."; the land's tracts ("Birch and rowan on a slope"). A plain line met often is
  not changed for variety.
- **Another's:** Iscoed's own origins and goings-on (the world agent's), and the authored situations' lines met in
  the first town (the arcology's well, "the women who draw it do so in silence, to save breath", 30 walks): listed in
  the register (PP-06), not edited.
