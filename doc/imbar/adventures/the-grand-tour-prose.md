# The Grand Tour (with the Slack: hours.kyr, remainder.kyr): prose quality pass

Editor's pass on `data/adventures/the-grand-tour/*.kyr`. Text only; no ids, conditions or mechanics changed. Read: the openings and speeches in `collegium`, `rector`, `rivals`, `whelm`, `sexton`, `undertow`, `cloudgate`, `harbour`, `courant`, `handbook`, all of `hours.kyr` and `remainder.kyr`.

## What I found

The Tour is the strongest of the four: Florian and Miss Sone, the Rector, No Admittance, Evander Cole, Mercy and Tolley each have an audible way of speaking, scenes have a place, a face and a turn, and the jokes (the Bursar, "NO ADMITTANCE" read as "Keep out!", the ember that does sparks when Florian talks to it) land. The 16-critical-review fixes to the Remainder were already applied (Tamsin on the lamp is now short). The weaknesses were a narrow set: Tamsin and Sabe sounding like one speaker, the "harbour" speech still doing exposition, house phrases ("a long time", "looks at you") used as stage business for every pause, and Fabian's repeated notebook.

## Before / after

1. **Tamsin asks for a surety (`remainder.kyr`, tamsin_harbour).** *Before:* "The Aeneans must have meant it for us; they built the stair... In every language, in every age: someone to say, these are ours to keep." (also contradicted the bible ruling that the Mathematical Age built it). *After:* she taps the ceiling with a rod, twice, "as you knock on a neighbour's wall"; "I won't take them into a dark I haven't seen"; "I'd thought: whoever comes down the stair. And here you are, with wet boots." Contractions, speed, the Aenean claim dropped.
2. **Sabe on the two rolls and on what the Watch wants.** *Before:* he spoke as Tamsin did, in sentences. *After:* Sabe counts. "Two rolls. One. Two." (a finger for each), "Nothing. One. We keep the hours. Two. We keep the remainders. Three." and looks for a third. The Watch's captain now has a habit; the Reckoner has contractions and swerves. They no longer swap lines.
3. **Tamsin's kept-in-the-Slack scene.** *Before:* "'It will last my time,' she says, to nobody. 'It will not last theirs.'" *After:* she holds the pin in her fist, does not turn round: "It'll see me out. It won't see out the children."
4. **The winter chest.** A stray name from another draft: "and Mab-someone-has put a knee on it". Now "somebody has put a knee on it".
5. **Murrey's portrait.** *Before:* "he looks at the portrait for a long time." *After:* "until the lamp needs trimming." Ten of 31 uses of "a long time" in the Tour were varied the same way: the man reading F. MURREY's name in a child's hand ("He reads it three times, with his lips"), the Rector's helm entrance ("the water runs off her chin onto the boards, and she lets it"), No Admittance ("breathes out a thread of bubbles that goes straight up and does not break"), the North Wind ("reads it twice, the second time with a finger under the line"), Lady Halyard ("sat on until the fire was out").
6. **Fabian's notebook (`handbook.kyr`).** Nine Observes began "Fabian writes in his notebook, and reads it you". Now eight different frames: the pencil smoking and shouting it over the roar of the Fire; the notebook held against his chest against the wind of the Gallery; the page that runs in the Whelm; "murmurs as he writes". Only the Guildhall one (chalk on a wet page) was already different.
7. **The jeweller (`lamp.kyr`).** *Before:* "She holds the real petal up to the furnace light for a long time, turning it, the way a jeweller looks at a stone." *After:* "turns it, once, twice, the way a jeweller turns a stone she wants to be wrong about."
8. **The Assembly's word (`earth.kyr`).** "You listen for a long time before you understand..." became "You listen through two breaths of the whole Assembly before you understand..."; the long pause is measured by the thing that is long.

## Speaking habits already distinct

Rector (keys chime, "my dear", steals from everybody), Florian (stammering enthusiasm; "One doesn't"), Miss Sone (flat, short, the umbrella), No Admittance (bubbles, "Keeping is the whole of it"), Mercy (direct, says "I was mercy on him"), Tolley (pink; footnotes), Cole (dusting), Threlkeld ("Name, licence, and are you sure"), Brother Amis (laying the place), Sabe (counts), Tamsin (contractions).

## Still weak

- "two hundred years" occurs 24 times and "the third step" 27; the first is a world-wide habit (see doc 16 section 4). Some of the 24 should be a different number or no number.
- "as if / as though" about 67 times.
- The Slack's three 'speak the sentence' / 'ring the twelfth' / 'face on the quartermaster' resolutions of the Divider all end with a ceremonial hush. The twelfth stroke is the best; the other two are echoes.
- The Rector's confession ("I was second through every door of my life") is good, but the Rector's last speeches before the ledge each close on a polished line; she is the one character allowed it.

## What a structural fix would need

A rule that a Divider is met only once; the Remainder's hold has three jars (a bend of the Lune, a winter, a tern) and each could have a scene where the item does something rather than being looked at; the 'Slack opened' event is read by only two modules.
