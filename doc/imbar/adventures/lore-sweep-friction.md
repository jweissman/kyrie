# Lore sweep: frictions

1. **A file I meant to create already existed (three times).** `skeel/lore.kyr`, `the-long-man/lore.kyr` and `quittance/lore.kyr` were not all mine to name: Quittance's was a real, older file and my heredoc overwrote it before I noticed in `git diff` (-106 lines). Restored from git; my Quittance content went in `links.kyr`. Cost: a cleanup. Smallest repair: `Write` refuses to overwrite an unread file; `cat >` does not. Check `ls` before a heredoc.
2. **A coordinator commit landed mid-work.** `git checkout` of a file then restored my own earlier version (without a later append), and a copy I deleted had been the only one with the append (Skeel's letter). Re-written from memory. Smallest repair: do not delete scratch copies until the manifest check passes.
3. **Names are global, ids are not.** Display names may repeat (three Dorcases, two Jorys) but I could not reuse a person for the pedlar or the surveyor; both are therefore talk-only itinerants (a `knows` Discovery per place) with no Person, which means the player cannot meet them. Smallest repair: an engine feature for a person who is "elsewhere but talked of". Blocked, worked round.
4. **`befell` reads warn in lint** ("action ... has no possible target in this world") for Halm's topic, because no rule in the standalone module asserts the event. Same as Wrayford's `pell_south`. Benign.
5. **The Lull cannot be talked to by lamplight**: sleepers are not offered. Tests for the Lull's item hook talk in the dark; the event Observe needs a lit candle. Not a defect of the hooks.
6. **Persons walk.** Factor Sault is at the tidewall at watch 214 in a generated world, so the entry-point test puts her in her office. A real player must find her.
7. **Accuracy limits of the grep matrix.** Surname matching is noisy (Reach, Watch, Carth, Hands are common nouns); the matrix in 15 lists only terms I checked by hand. The `Remainder` count for Low Water (69) is the lowercase noun, not the ship.
8. **Not done / honest shortfalls.** Tessary, Patient Star, LOR, Quittance, Grand Tour event reads and the Hessel, Hive, Patient Star, LOR, Wrayford item hooks are linted and loaded but not played in a test. No lamp-trimmer itinerant. No Liberty-of-the-Ice mariner's wife. The Dorothea's own copy of the fish-and-flame explanation was left alone.
9. **Calendar.** The sweep adds characters who disagree about the year (Cattermole, Pask) but no scene where two adventures meet and argue in person; the surety note's journey from Tessary (1144) to Sennet (1290) remains silent about the gap.
