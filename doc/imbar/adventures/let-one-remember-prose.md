# Let One Remember: prose quality pass

Editor's pass over `data/adventures/let-one-remember/*.kyr` (campaign `let-one-remember`, "The Road the Star Came Down"; about 330 distinct passages, all read). Roughly 35 rewritten. This is the most fluent of the three at the sentence level and the one with the most distinct speakers (Agnes, Tobin, the Assessor, Lene, Ruth, Esk, Jory, Saska, Bram, the Finder). The faults found were duplications, a stray quote, the "dry closing line", and rooms that were lists.

## Before and after

**1. Companion voices (seven rules, `witness.kyr`).** Cleric: touches the sprigs, names the form. Ranger: tracks, short. Warrior: "That is how I would drop a wall, if I had been told not to." Thief: the chair and the desk, the wage, "Nicely done, whoever did it. I would have liked to meet him." Mage: "I would not call it a hole... and I have no instrument for that."

**2. The same joke twice.** Esk of Harrow: "It is a free country; I am told so daily." The scouts, two scenes later: "It's a free country. They tell us so every day." Now the scouts: "'Have a look. She waves back, sometimes.'" (tying to Wil's "I wave").

**3. A stray quote.** The Finder's bell speech ended "He frowns at the drawer. ''". Fixed. His embarrassment was "for the first time"; now "slightly embarrassed, which in him is a visible event."

**4. A conclusion stated twice.** "The lake is the mountain's place." closed both `cassen_and_drag` and `lens_and_chart`. The second now ends on the observation: "the same broken rib on the eastern side. The mountain was cut from that bowl."

**5. A biography read aloud.** Iniry Calde's introduction was four dates in a row. Now she says her name "as to a clerk who has spelt it wrong before", looks at the fog "as though it owed her rent", and ends "I know the old rites better than anyone living or forgotten, and I am in a temper." Aron's confession on the roster: "He laughs, once, without much breath" and a paragraph, to "I had the speech written. I was going to tell them all, the day the lake came under us." He finds a pencil.

**6. Rooms that were lists, now with one thing the reader could not guess.** The cookhouse: benches "bolted to a pair of runners so that it can be dragged, and the oldest are worn bright underneath". The pavilion: "The newest [map] is pinned lowest, as though nobody wanted it at eye height." The Lusk station: brass instruments "left behind, as no surveyor ever leaves brass". The eighth bend: "The parapet is polished along the top where carters sit to put their boots back on." The guild: "the smallest [dipper] worn brightest".

**7. "As if" similes.** 35 to 29. Examples: the reeve's coat "still warm, as if he had just gone out" became "still warm, and on the table a bowl of porridge with the spoon standing upright in it". The Unfolded "walk through the wagons as if they were not there" became "over a hen coop and across a cook's table, to the horn".

**8. "A long time" gestures.** Eight of the fourteen replaced with a concrete act: the prioress "turns her ring once round her finger"; Mother Saska "lays her hands flat on the chair-arms"; Lene "counts the three of you with her eyes, one, two, three."

## Pattern counts

- "a long time / a long while": 14 to 6. "as if / as though": 35 to 29. "for the first time": 7 to 5 (the remaining ones are payoffs: Teshen in the grey, Hightown not moving in the wind).
- "two thousand six hundred years" appears seven times; left, because it is the Unasked's whole argument, but a candidate for a quiet cut.

## Speaking habits

Agnes (does a chore while she speaks), Tobin (qualifies, drinks), the Assessor (CAPITALS, numbers as notes), Mattie (flustered, repeats her aunt), Lene (sums on the hoist book), Ruth (quotes her grandfather), Esk (courtesy that prices you), Jory ("it's my gatepost"), Saska (kept, let, laid: the grammar of how she knows), Linnet (questions), Bede (his age), the Finder (receipts), Iniry (temper).

## Still weak

- Several dense paragraphs of exposition remain in Observe/Result text (`finale_*`, `ballast_thought`): they are rewarded moments, but they tell.
- Most topics have a verbatim "again" twin; the second hearing adds nothing.
- The Nail's speech and Wenna's are both in the same cadence (ellipsis, repetition).
- The "dry closing line" survives in about a third of the Says; I cut the ones that were jokes about the speaker's own speech, not the in-character ones.

## What a structural fix would need

A calling-keyed voice that can attach to a Person, not only to a place; and a way to give an Ask-again a second line. Neither is prose.
