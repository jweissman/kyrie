# Frictions: the Dorothea archipelago

| # | Friction | Status | Smallest repair |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | **No terrain kinds beyond `region/settlement/wilderness/site/building/room`.** Volcano, jungle and salt flat are prose only. Ashkeld is a `wilderness` with hot springs and a rim hazard written by hand. **Mangrow (jungle) is done the same way and the limit is exact:** the forest is a `wilderness` and the landing a `settlement`; the heat is a `swelter` measure on the party raised by a `Rule` that lists the four green rooms in a `Query` (`inGreen`), halved by a `netted` flag and stopped by a `barked` flag; the fever is a `Hazard` struck by a `Rule`. Nothing on the chart, in travel time, in rest, in the encounter or sound tables knows the place is hot or wet; a `terrain` tag would have let one rule say "in jungle, a sting an hour" for every jungle place, and the same for ash and salt, instead of a hand-kept list of rooms. | **blocked** | A `terrain` tag on a Place that other rules and the chart can read. |
| A2 | **No distance on the world map for a module-local archipelago.** Distance is `progress` thresholds per call in a hull Thing; the chart knows nothing of the sea (as 07-voyage §7.3). Ashkeld reads as three days only by the clock. | **blocked** | A `sea` passage kind with a duration, and `countries.json` placement for non-adjacent provinces. |
| A3 | A passage `opening when` shuts at *both* ends: a failed lock-lift that closed the counting-house door trapped the company inside. Worked round by moving the company out in the failure branch. | tested | Optional "shuts behind" side. |
| A4 | `Decrease … by …` below zero hides the action: a clerk with no gold made `Take the clerk's twelve coin` vanish. Needed `Initially Set quantity(latch, gold)`. | tested | Clamp by default (friction 4 above). |
| A5 | Waiting by `=N` can overshoot a rule's warning window (the raiders land, say "a few minutes more", and the fight fires). Tests use `=until at(raiders, ness_quay)`. | tested | None needed; document `=until`. |
| A6 | A rule that changes `at(party, …)` is not needed: an action can `Retract at(actor, site). Assert at(actor, other).` to move the company (the climb, the lighter). Worked. | tested | -- |
| A7 | Terrain-free tide: `Set phase(tide) to watch(clock) - watch(clock) / 72 * 72` in a `Rule … when true`, read in a passage's `opening when`. Worked, no repair. | tested | -- |
| A8 | Reset for a second voyage is a long list of `Retract`s; a `Reset <group of relations>` would be shorter and safer. | tested | `Reset` construct. |
| A9 | `Person` surnames are global: Rudd and Thane (taken), Meg, Penhale, Cobb, Dray, Tansy, Lanyon. Renamed on lint. | tested | Module-qualified names. |
| A10 | Orsett, "the Wednesday boat", a real counting-house: not a place we can seed without a province (re-seeds every world). Carried as a seat, a board and a seal. | **blocked** | Agent route to a not-yet-installed region (§11.4). |
| A11 | Two `Give <kind> to actor.` in one effect list give **one** item (the second asserts the same fact). Two bales from the Mangrow strong-room needed two actions (`take_bark`, `take_second_bark`). A test that gives twice with `j.give` does hold two. | tested | `Give <n> <kind>`, or make a repeated Give additive. |
| A12 | Module `Kind` ids are bare (`dorothea_bark`, `dorothea_netting`) while relations, measures and places are module-prefixed (`dorothea__swelter`); `j.give` and `holding` want the bare form, `j.measure` the prefixed one. Cost one failed test run. | tested | Document it in the Journey helper's header. |
| A13 | A rule can move the company with a variable place: `Rule … when at(party, ?w) and inGreen(party) and swelter(party) >= 8 { Retract at(party, ?w). Assert at(party, mangrow_pier). }` carries it out of whichever green room it is in. Worked. | tested | -- |
| A14 | A long leg cannot be made longer in *events*: the sea table draws at most two a passage (`seaDrawn < 2`), so Mangrow's five days have the same two draws as Port Ness's afternoon. Distance reads as days through the clock, a fare and four day-marker Observes (`deck_day_two`..`five`), not through weather. | tested | Make the draw cap a measure per call (`seaCap(hull)`). |
| A15 | Combat difficulty is set by calling, level and `Numbers` only; the preset company is a random kit with 10, 6 and 17 hit points. Measured on eight seeds: three level-2 warriors, a company wipe on every seed tried; two level-2 warriors, three seeds in four lost; two level-1 warriors, four in six lost; the lugger's boarders as three level-2 thieves, **the company wins six seeds in eight** (`test/dorothea-entries.test.ts` plays `s1`, a win, and `d`, a wipe). The Kestrel's hands (three level-2 thieves) lose to the company five seeds in six. No surrender or morale for a lugger's crew. | tested | A `breaks at half` line for authored bands: `test/bout.test.ts` shows core has `routed`, but the pier does not use it. |
| A16 | Queries cannot be shared by name between the island's own rules and the hull's strand rules without repeating the room list (`onNess`, `onAntlers`, `onMangrow`, `inGreen` all hand-kept). | read | A `within <place>` test in a condition. |
