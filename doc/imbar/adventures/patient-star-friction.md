# The Patient Star: friction

1. **A company of one has no companions to key.** The preset party is one thief; calling voices only fire for her. Hirelings (Mirel, Garret, Saro) are persons, not members. Smallest repair: `hasCalling` over `travelsWith`.
2. **`capable(actor, light)` is a lit source** (a candle, not an unlit horn lantern). The lantern needs a flask.
3. **A person moved from a place in a rule is not moved back.** Ilis is moved to the holder's house when the word is said, and stays. Intended; noted.
4. **`Greets` on a held person would print on arrival;** I removed it from Doran.
5. **Tideline post:** was untestable cheaply (entering the Keeping needs Ithmar and the Way); removed in the review pass.
6. **Fixture reuse:** none of the patient fixtures lists sources by path, so adding `kept_season.kyr` to `star.json` and `placed.json` was enough.
7. **Review pass.** Shore post, eleven-stroke tally-board and eleven-cleat line cut; the Venn clerk and the twelve-word prayer are the one motif. The shipped thief is not locked out of any flagship beat; `test/patient-star-shipped.test.ts` plays her from Quell to the word said.
