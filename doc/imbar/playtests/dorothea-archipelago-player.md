# The Dorothea archipelago, played as a player

*Played with `bin/kyrie play data/campaigns/the-dorothea.campaign.json --seed <s> --choices "<step>" ...` (the labels the
page offers; `=N` clock jumps for the long waits). Seeds s1 (Skerrow), n4 (Port Ness and the Holm), a1 (the Antlers), e1
(Tolleth entries). Ashkeld and Mangrow were reached by setting the company down on the beach/pier (a Journey `put`) because a
three- and five-day passage replayed from step 0 for every look is slow; the voyage itself was played to the Combs and the race.
Findings first, then the log by session, then what was changed.*

## Top findings

1. **The storm night had no signposts.** On the second night of Skerrow Wen is lost in the weather and the whole island
   depends on a company that is awake, with four answers (beacon on the hill, light the ship, row, or the lamp itself). The page
   said only "a woman is shouting a boy's name". Left to wait, the company at the foot of the tower is offered nothing and
   Wen is lost at +200. The four answers sat in four places and none was named where the company stood.
2. **The deadline was invisible.** Jory signs at +132, the men come at +138, the plate goes at +150, the storm at +152. Nothing
   on the island said "the second evening"; Wen, who is the whole point, was introduced as a boy who asks about the ship's gun
   and was given no topic.
3. **The Wrack Road lied.** The strand said "The causeway is dry now" while the passage was shut and "Wait and watch" returned
   "Nothing comes, and nothing changes" 25 watches later. No page told the player what the tide was doing.
4. **The Holm is a dead end on a first visit.** Marit can be persuaded to let the stump be lit, and then there is nothing to
   light it with. Nothing at the Ness or on the Holm said where a clean oil is kept (Skerrow's kegs, the Dorothea's PHAROS tier).
5. **Same shape on every voyage.** Fog in the Sound fires on every passage (a fixed rule, not a table roll); "Reef. Now."
   or a raft is nearly always the second beat; the station Observe and the arrival rule print together ("Open water. The land is
   gone ... The headlands of the Sound fall away").
6. **Contradictions.** Marit says the bottle went in the water "eleven years" ago on the night the Rosa Lind went; the board on
   the Tolleth quay and Dunstan Auld both put the Rosa Lind this year. The Skerrow jetty page on arrival said only that the ship
   was moored (no island). A failed look at Bascom's ledgers said "you are on the deck again" while the company was still in the cabin.
7. **Dead ends and flat spots.** The hold had a hint (Rune looks at the after hatch) and nothing in it. Nipper's Talk is a bare
   greeting; Wen's likewise. Ashkeld was a three-step errand (Ansel's tip, bell, crate) with no feeling of the mountain itself.
   Port Ness's pans and Skerrow's green are places the company stands with nothing to do but read a reeve's one speech.
8. **What works.** The Tolleth quay notice-board and the five entry scenes (Latch's crate and bond, Dilys's letter, the
   bottle, Pip in Garrick's) all hand the next step to the player; the Antlers (hut, foot, top; Joss and the letter) is the
   best-signposted island; Mangrow has a wholly different rhythm (the heat clock, the ferry, the strong-room, the tree) and
   reads as another country; the Pendry scene is a real scene with five exits.

## Log

### 1. Skerrow by the plain fare (seed s1)

- Quay: the board is the model signpost. Bascom's "I don't discuss the other thing" is a good unexplained hook. Pay six ->
  gangway. Waist: Rune, Nipper, Quint. Rune's "sixty crowns ... looks at the after hatch" lands. Nipper's Talk: a hostile boy,
  no follow-up (**flat**). Mab (forecastle): the best talker aboard; two long answers, then "again" topics.
- Hold: the sea-sound line is lovely and there is nothing in it. **Dead end**. Search: "nothing is hidden here".
- Cabin: Read the ledgers (likely) -> the failure line claims we are back on deck. **Wrong**.
- Voyage: fog (first), a salt-fleet with a good line, a calm, Pendry's cutter. "Watch what passes" with Hale: the Nipper-weeping
  detail is the best moment of the passage. Rune aside -> "Tell me what to do". Intimidation fails -> the hands over the rail
  with an 8-minute fuse: this is an actual dilemma, **delighted**. Pendry's "eight coin to make the nothing mutual" is clear.
- The Combs: "lie off and wait / cut cargo" is signposted. The wait is five empty hours; the cost is only the Orsett man's
  patience, never shown. A flat choice with a stake that is not on the page.
- Roads: "a boat putting out" and nothing to do for ~14 watches. Waiting is the only move, and it works, but nothing says so.
- Jetty on arrival: the page told me about the ship and nothing about the island (**fixed**).
- Green: Jory, the people ("I have enough of my own"), Jory's house with Wen (**Wen: nothing**). The Gull: Quillon's offer is
  the first real thing. The framed paper is a good small mystery and the follow-up needs Quint with the company.
- Pharos: arch, device, lamp room, Marren. The rubbing, the lens (needs a tally the player does not have yet): both fine.
- Left alone (clock jumped) Jory signs at the Gull, the men come at dusk and the plate goes, then the storm -> at the foot of the
  tower **no action, no hint** (**fixed**).

### 2. Port Ness, the Factor and the House (seed n4)

- Fare four. A fog and a squall in the way. "Bear away" had a cost not shown in the label (a leak and the boy's fever).
- Pier: the stencil "newer than the sacks" is a good small wrongness. Salt Row: Stannard on the step, the brass plate, the
  pin on the sill by SKERROW. Counting-house: Mirel's strongbox aside (calling voice) is exactly right.
- Pans: Auld's two topics are good, then nothing to do (**no stake**). Strand: causeway said dry, was not (**fixed**).
- Choices in the counting-room name Kit Skail and her bond before the company has met her; not wrong, a little ahead of the page.
- Light, bond, Pym's day-book: five routes to the same result; the shape is "ask, pay or pick or persuade". Reads as the
  most puzzle-like island, and the cleanest.

### 3. The Antlers (seed a1)

- Pelham: "Resting my judgement. There is a difference, and it is eleven pints." Walk him up and down: a three-line scene
  that pays off at the race. Dilys: "I am asking." The letter arrives in the hand with no ceremony; good.
- At sea a raft with a lit lantern (HOLM) -> Fish it off: this ties the Antlers voyage to the Holm. Race with the pilot is quiet.
- Ledge, hut (Deane in his hammock, Wick with a pencil behind each ear), foot (Hob, Joss and "I'm only not going up that").
  Everything is offered; each place has a person and a verb. **Best signposted island.**
- Complaint: hut, foot and top are the same three-beat "agent says no, expert says yes, pay or prove" as Ness. Not fixed.

### 4. Wrack Holm and Ashkeld

- Holm: Marit persuaded (just: 13 against 13), then **nothing to burn** (**fixed**: she now says it wants a clean oil from the
  Dorothea; Kit Skail says Mab says Skerrow burns white). The gull, the boat's ribs, "everything is wet and everything is
  arranged" read very well. Quint's device line is a strong out-link.
- Ashkeld (placed on the beach): beach, springs, Ansel ("You are standing in my light"), works, rim. Ansel's tip *is* the puzzle;
  Merrow's three ways to the crate are three prices. There was no sense of the mountain's own trouble (**added Ansel's count**).

### 5. Mangrow (placed on the pier)

- Sula Weir ("Whatever is on the log, it is a log"), the ferry, Hale's voice on the white underleaf, Vail's hide, the heat clock.
  This is the only island with a continuous pressure on the company's body; nothing in Skerrow/Ness/Antlers does that. Keep it.

### 6. The five entries (seed e1)

- (a) Latch's crate: the taproom, "I am told the House is a great deal of money. I find it is mostly waiting." Clear, fare in hand.
- (b) The mariner's wife: delivered in the hand, answered at the stack foot.
- (c) The lugger: Latch's bond (thirty crowns, owed), or warn Auld, or parley. A clock (+150) the page names as "before the new moon".
- (d) The bottle: Dacey Hoe ("has plainly not read it eleven times") -> Marit. The "eleven years" contradiction (**fixed**).
- (e) Pip in Garrick's: "Not a word. Please." and a five-crown betrayal on offer from Latch. The strongest of the five.

## What was changed

- `isle.kyr`: the jetty has a landing page when the ship is in.
- `skerrow.kyr`: Quillon names the second evening and who holds the pen.
- `night.kyr`: Wen's topic about his pots and the glass; the pot-boat gone from the shingle when the storm rises; three
  Observes (hill path, jetty, the helm) that say what is possible from where the company stands.
- `ness.kyr`: five tide Observes on the strand (flood, high, ebbing, dry, turning); the false "dry now" line removed; Kit Skail
  says where a clean oil is said to be.
- `holm.kyr`: Marit says what the iron needs; `entries.kyr`: the bottle went in at Lammas, not eleven years ago.
- `ship.kyr`: the hold has the PHAROS tier with the rubbed tally; `crew.kyr`: the ledger failure no longer lies about where we
  are, and Nipper has the gun topic (so Wen's question about the gun and the night of the lanterns agree).
- `ashkeld.kyr`: Ansel's count of the breaths (the interval is lengthening; the women have left the top pool).
- `voyage.kyr`: the Sound-to-open-sea line no longer contradicts the Observe it prints beside.

## Still weak

- Fog in the Sound fires on every passage and the first leg is the same each time; changing it moves the timelines of about
  fourteen tests (they assume it). Needs a seed-dependent fog and a test pass.
- The Combs choice has a cost the page never shows; the Ness pans and the Skerrow green give the company nothing to do.
- Ashkeld is still an errand; the rim has no noon check (the tip is flavour, not mechanics); Hut/foot/top at the Antlers and the
  counting-room at Ness share one shape.
- No ledger-style hint tells the player that a fought Pendry costs nothing but an 8-minute fuse.
