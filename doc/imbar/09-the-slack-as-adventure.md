# 9. The Slack as an adventure: eligibility, pathways, failure

*The meta-adventure, as a play, not as lore. For the history and the ship, see `08-treatment-the-slack.md`. For
the tests, `test/the-slack.test.ts`; for a played journey starting in an ordinary world,
`playtests/ordinary-world-slack.md`.*

## 9.1 Where it begins

It does not begin with a prompt. A company in a generated world meets a sealed black square in the floor of the Nexus
of the House of Doors at Sennet if it goes there for any reason (the Grand Tour, a letter to the Rector, curiosity).
The square hums under the sole. It is cut with a line in Aenean capitals and a longer row of marks that are not. That
is all a company with nothing sees, and it can walk away. **Whether the square becomes a question depends on what
the company has already learned elsewhere, and on whom it has brought.**

The earlier adventures supply it *unevenly*. A company that mended the Long Man's wall has a reeve's rod (a measure)
and may have kept a copy of the Prefect's writ (a standing). One that destroyed the Mower for the bounty has a grey
face (a different standing, bought with a different thing). One that sat the third ebb at Skeel has a rubbing of a
bench; one that sailed to Skerrow, a rubbing of a table: together a reading. One that went to Ammerlin has a
glossary and a letter. One that went to the Delta has the Valuer's note, which is not a key but a term. A company
with *none* of it reaches the square with no more than a purse and a day: it can pay the Collegium of Measures'
scribes thirty coin to copy it, and pace the notches itself with a cold that bites; it cannot find a standing, and the
square will not open. **A missed adventure is therefore a closed door or a costly one, never a locked quest.**

## 9.2 What it is a story about

Not lore. A ship with twenty-two people who are afraid, a Captain who believes nothing is required of him but that the
count be kept, a Reckoner who has found that a pin has moved a hair, a hold of things that did not divide, and a
stranger who comes down a stair and is asked to be a surety. The decisions are the company's:

- **Stand surety**, in the Prefect's formula (a keeping, not an ownership) or the Collegium's name (a collector's
  enthusiasm), **or decline.** Each is recorded in the world (`remainder_harboured` / `remainder_kept`); the first
  has a cost, a boy at nine each morning with the Watch's bread, unless the company went to the Delta and asked a
  clerk what a surety owes.
- **Give a face to the Quartermaster**, if the company has one, and so learn what the Mower's destruction cost.
- **Go to Mechanus**, once the Captain trusts the company with the Slack's secret; twelve watches at the berth and a
  twelve-stroke bell, and a ship that comes again in forty-eight for whoever waits.
- **Open the chest that holds a winter**, or leave it.
- **Listen to the tern**, and write down a sentence of a dead language.

## 9.3 Failure and retreat

- The square can be left shut. Nothing happens.
- The pacing route can hurt: a failed measure strikes the whole company with a cold.
- The Slack costs: the quay and the deck are safe; the winter in the chest is not; a blind surety charges.
- A company that goes ashore on Mechanus and misses the ship is on the threshold of a plane for forty-eight watches
  with a single way off. (They can wait, and the ship comes.)
- A company that declines surety and goes home has left twenty-two people with a pin and a bell. They said so.

## 9.4 What it is not

It is not a supermodule. There are two Kyr files of about 230 lines each (`hours.kyr`, `remainder.kyr`) inside the
Grand Tour, one new scenario of the same size in the Dorothea, one in the Exchange; the rest is gifts in existing
adventures. It is not a campaign with a chain of flags, and nothing reads three flags to unlock a fourth. And the
transition is made in a House that was already there, by a company that was already there, with a stair they had
only to be able to read.
