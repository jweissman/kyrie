# Cold play 3 (cautious planner) — Hild Loderr, dwarf cleric of Life, report

**Expeditions made: 4.** **Would I willingly make a fifth expedition, knowing what happened on the first four? Maybe. The deep place kept opening up: crypt, undercroft, a vault, then AQUAVIGILIA's huge drowned hall and a barrow under the town. The ghoul fight was the first fight that felt earned, and I want to see the far water and whatever is sharpening steel in the recess. Against that, my loot (torcs, rings, a moonstone, grave-plates) buys nothing anywhere, the leads that are left all say "needs lore", and I can't see what a fifth trip would pay for.**

**Score: 7 / 10**

## My path

T13–20: I took the crypt job from Immeral at the shrine, shopped (bandages, herbs, candle, poultice) and brewed a potion. **Expedition 1** (T21–49): crypt bats, then the raiser in the ossuary, whom I laid with the rite of rest. I went back up to cash in. **Expedition 3** (T108–158): I paid the vault's keeper 10 coin to sit down, then went on to AQUAVIGILIA: the ledge, the forecourt, the robbers' camp and the long passage with its riddle casket. I set off two traps with the pole, freed the counterweight, and reached the chalk halls. I saw ghouls and left. **Expedition 4** (T159–196): with Bless and Pure Beam prepared, and Leshanna hired, I killed the three ghouls. I found no way further and walked out.

## What I understood
It's a text RPG played from menus. Under a small town there is one connected dungeon. Its entrances (the churchyard crypt, the quarry, the drain and corn-store) all meet in the undercroft. A puzzle (the letter ring in the quarry opens the flag in the undercroft) leads to a vault, then to a far older deep hall and barrow, then to chalk halls. Light is a resource that runs out: a torch lasts about an hour, a candle about two. Traps can be set off from a distance with a ten-foot pole. Many obstacles have a skill-gated answer ("needs lore", "needs woodcraft", "needs climbing") and a brute or cautious one. Town gives jobs (from notices, which you then ask about), hirelings for coin, shops, healing at the shrine and a rented loft with a chest. Sleeping there restores HP and spell slots. The trip back up is a single "Out to the churchyard" move from anywhere. The trip down is room by room.

## What I wanted
1. **Lay the dead in the crypt.** Done in two rooms with the rite of rest. It was a satisfying cleric moment, if quick.
2. **Never let anyone drop.** Kept. I came close twice (3/11 to a centipede, 5/11 to a ghoul). Taunt, guard and potions held.
3. **Open the lettered flag.** Done, by finding the matching ring in a different branch. This was the best moment of design in the game.
4. **Burn the spiders without fighting the mother.** Done with one firebomb through a crack. The planning paid off directly.
5. **Have a lantern so I'd stop running out of torches.** Failed. I bought one for 8 coin and found another with "oil still in it", and I could never light either (see below).
6. **Sell the grave goods and treasure to pay for better gear.** Failed. I found no way to sell anything anywhere.
7. **Beat the ghouls.** Done on expedition 4 with Pure Beam, taunt, guard and Hugh's Gust.
8. **Find out what's past the ghouls / reach the water.** Not done. The mourners' hall was a dead end, and the leads I had left need lore.
9. **Level up.** Unclear. I earned roughly 900 xp each and never saw a level-up or a character sheet.
10. **Look for my father.** I never pursued this. It was always the top row, but I was busy below ground.

## What worked
1. The deep place's structure. Three town notices (rats, catacombs, spiders) turn out to be three ways into one undercroft ("The corn-store, the church and the quarry were all dug down into it from three sides"). A clue in one branch (the letter ring) opens a door in another.
2. AQUAVIGILIA: "a hall that could hold the whole of Nochlëa … Those who dug the cellars of Nochlëa did not know it was here." A real wow moment, and it made me want to keep going down.
3. Preparation paid off. The firebomb through the crack burned the brood with no risk, the ten-foot pole set off two traps "spent on nothing", and Pure Beam against ghouls was decisive. Each item I bought had a use, except the lantern.
4. The cleric's non-violent answers. The rite of rest laid the dead. Ten coin and "the rite of the door" sat the vault-keeper down "the way an old man sits".
5. The company's writing. Grimnar binds my wound "in Father's voice, and then stops, surprised at himself", and says "Father would have liked Hugh Lamb … then given Hugh Lamb the worst ox." Immeral reads out the names of the three dead.
6. The world remembers. The churchyard's three graves are re-dug and turfed. The brass token from the crypt turns out to be Quarion's back-stair token, and the raiser was his lodger.
7. The ghoul fight, with real danger and real tools: taunt, guard, Gust stuns, Second Wind, potion timing.
8. The loft with its own chest and bed felt like a home base, and sleeping fully reset the company.
9. A single move from the depths back to the churchyard made retreating cheap, which suits a cautious player.

## Surprise
How deep it went. After the crypt job finished in two rooms I expected a small cellar. Instead there was a hidden vault under the undercroft, then an ancient drowned hall that "could hold the whole of Nochlëa", then a barrow and chalk halls. Also: the "boss" keeping the vault was paid off rather than fought, and the hardest fight was three ghouls in a mourners' hall with no exit.
