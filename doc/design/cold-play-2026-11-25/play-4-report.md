# Cold play 4 (newcomer) — Brenna, dwarf warrior (former quarrier), report
**Expeditions made: 2** (the second stopped by the move cap while I was still underground). **Would I willingly make a fifth expedition, knowing what happened on the first two? Maybe, leaning yes.** The mine keeps paying out: loot that sells for real money, small puzzles that hang together (a notebook tells you a bar frees the wheel, a pump-room for a flooded stair), and on the second trip things moved in behind me ("whoever lives here came after you went"). What makes me hesitate is that most fights were trivial, and a lot of my moves went on fighting with light and pickup rows instead of on decisions.
**Score: 7 / 10**

## My path
T0–24: made Brenna. Scared off two robbers and was given a lantern; the pedlar later joined me for free. In Pardatîr the elders, the castellan (Nightking's crown, 20 coin advance), Dayereth and Uriah gave me five aims. Hired Erevan. Uriah's "old workings" told me about the Delvers' mine under the old woods. T25–31: searched my way through the woods (charcoal-burners' clearing, then fallen stones) and found the stair. T32–143, expedition 1: hired Soveliss on the stair and took a cache. Through the adit, a trap set off with the pole, a blasting charge, goblins talked round, the stope with a secret air shaft, the shaft head, down to the shaft bottom (cave hounds, a stamp-lock strong-box), the haulage way, the Delvers' hall with a boarded store (amulet), more hounds, the long haulage, a riddle casket ("Shirt"), a wall broken down onto a flooded stair, and the pump-room's bucket-chain set. I walked home in the dark because my light ran out, and found a ring under a fallen oak. T144–175: rested at the shrine (everyone reached level 2), sold loot (50 → about 210 coin), bought a crowbar, torches and oil. T176–~225, expedition 2: lanterns lit with oil, the barricade dragged open, bats at the winze head, a bear and spiders at the foot, three ghouls. I stopped there at the cap.

## What I understood
It is a text RPG: you choose from lists of actions. You build a small company (hirelings, a free pedlar), talk to townsfolk who hand you "aims", and go into a dungeon (the Delvers' mine) that has levels: the adit, the haulage level, and a flooded stair down to a third. In the mine you search rooms, pick up coins, gems and ore, deal with traps (look closely, then knock them over with a pole), fight (one turn per company member, or "fight:auto"), and solve small puzzles. Light is the real clock: torches last a few rooms. You come back to town to rest at the shrine (which levels you up), sell loot to the store, buy supplies and go down again. I only half understood the numbers ("15 of 15" was health, I guessed) and the "raise Dexterity" and "take up Woodcraft" rows.

## What I wanted
1. Find my sister in Moonlow. Not pursued. It is far off and the mine was right there. It stayed in my head as the reason to keep going.
2. Find the "deep place" from the box. Done: the Delvers' mine, found by asking Uriah about "the old workings" and then searching twice in the woods.
3. Get down past the first level. Done, by the shaft and later the winze. The third level (the drowned stair) was still flooded. I set the pump but never went back to check it.
4. Make money. Done: the loot sold for about 160 coin. That was the most satisfying moment.
5. Open the barricade the goblins sat by. Done on expedition 2 with a crowbar. It only led back down to level 2.
6. Open the paymaster's strong-box. Failed. I couldn't work out the stamp order, forced it, and "what was inside is broken".
7. Find the raiders' mound (elders' 25 crowns) and Nightking's crown. Never got to either. I'm not sure whether the mound in the woods was the elders' mound.

## Confused, bored or stuck
1. T0: "Talk to the pedlar — pay the robbers three coin": talking to one person pays someone else?
2. Creation: "Draftswoman" and "Draughtswoman" are both listed as separate jobs.
3. T6–7: I couldn't find out what I was carrying. The "things" lens showed the shop's goods. I bought leather armor for 12, and only then did the sell list show I already had scale mail, a battleaxe, a shovel and more. I sold it back and lost 5 coin.
4. T12: Dayereth: "There is trouble under the town too, if you have the stomach for it." No row followed it up, so I had to dig the mine out of Uriah myself.
5. T17–18 and after: Uriah's lead talk row became "Sell bandages to Uriah", tagged knowledge/exploration (later payoff).
6. T19: "The Delvers' mine at the old dead under the old woods": "at the old dead" reads like a garbled place name.
7. T31: "Out to the stair under the fallen stones" for going down into the dark. It was tagged companionship.
8. T32–33: "It is pitch dark… do nothing that needs seeing", yet the same text describes a vast pillared hall "your light goes out into" while nothing was lit. After I lit a torch, the text talks about spoil heaps on a hillside and heather. Was I underground or outside?
9. T32 onward: the pedlar's lantern, "a burner's old lamp" "with oil still in it" and "a hooded lantern, oil still in it" were never offered as lights. Provisions said "a lamp, and no oil for it." They only lit after I bought oil (T177). Light is what drove me out of expedition 1, so this mattered.
10. T37: taking the hooded lantern printed only "30 Haysel, afternoon". Picking up the silver lamp (T41) only printed experience. Did I get them? (They showed up later, so yes, but I couldn't tell.)
11. T43: "scattered coins" was a lead row, but choosing it showed "scattered coins:" and nothing else. Only after looking closely at something else did "Gather up the coins" appear. Some places offered both rows from the start, some didn't.
12. T51: "The goblins are here." The game itself flagged "nothing can be done toward them". The lead row was "Attack a blasting charge". At T57, talking to the goblins: "they wait to see what you will do", and the only follow-up was "Attack the goblins", on the Talk row.
13. T53: listening at the barricade set off "A blasting charge comes at you", a bomb with hit points and armour. Afterwards Soveliss says "Got 'em!" Odd. Calling `more` during the fight showed a non-fight screen.
14. T63 and T77: when the torch guttered out, the rows offered no way to light another. "light a torch/candle" appeared only after I used look.
15. T64: "The crack between the flagstones is only a crack now." I had never seen a crack.
16. T74 and T125: I held my hands to the candle, then held the crystal, and the effect went to someone else each time ("Soveliss Holimion is warded against the light", "The pedlar grows sly"). I didn't know what either effect does.
17. T75: the notebook says the cage's wheel is seized and needs a bar, but "Down to the shaft bottom" just worked without one. The clue felt pointless. At T144 Uriah greeted me with "Down the cage?" when I hadn't used the cage.
18. T81 and later: "Cave hound 1 goes down" while it was still 8/9. "Goes down" seems to mean knocked over, but it reads as dead.
19. T88–94: the strong-box stamps first clicked and clanged. Then three more presses printed nothing at all, so I couldn't tell whether the puzzle still worked. I found no clue to the order.
20. T95: "Prise the strong-box open by main force" said "It takes an hour of the crowbar". I had no crowbar (the barricade had just told me so). Then "what was inside is broken."
21. T103–111: "something large, that is not in any hurry" turned out to be two 8–9 HP hounds, the same as before. An anticlimax.
22. T124–125: the riddle text appeared only under look, not when I chose the casket, which showed only "the stone casket:" and four word buttons.
23. T136: "Prod it with the pole" did nothing ("Nothing gives"), but "Knock it over with the pole" set the trap off safely. The difference wasn't clear.
24. T143: "A lamp burns over the door of the Green Mask." What is the Green Mask? No row leads there.
25. T144–146: I couldn't find a rest at Uriah's house, where someone offers "something on the house". I had to walk to the shrine to sleep.
26. T147: on level-up Brenna got +16 HP but Soveliss only +1.
27. T150s: the store lists "Have Brenna raise Dexterity…" and "take up Woodcraft". I didn't know what they were or why they were in a shop. Raising Constitution just happened, with no cost or explanation.
28. T160–165: torches can only be bought one at a time, and the next one appears under a new id only after you buy the last. My batch of three purchases failed twice.
29. T171–175: there is no "go to the mine" travel. Each trip is four legs of walking through the woods.
30. T206: "The burst strongbox is empty, and so is the rust around it" was printed at the winze foot. I broke the strong-box at the shaft bottom.
31. In general: rows are long and repetitive. Every trap lists "Risk it — X goes first" for each of four people, every shop lists Buy and Haggle per item, and "Ask the pedlar to stay here" or "Wait until dark" sits in every room. It's hard to see the few rows that matter.

## What worked
1. The opening: my sister's letter, robbers on the road, standing up to them, and the pedlar handing me a lantern and later joining me "because you stopped for a man in a ditch".
2. Searching pays off. A carving in the burners' hut pointed to the stones, searching there found the stair, and a draft in the stope led to a secret air shaft. It felt like real exploring.
3. The notebook clue ("a bar under the axle would free it… ladders in the winze are sound"), the gate-beam ("NONE BELOW THE CROSSING") and the tally-board made the mine feel like a place with a history.
4. Traps that you inspect and then defuse with the pole. Simple, and I understood them as a newcomer.
5. Selling loot was a clear, big reward (a topaz for 60, an ingot for 30), and gear, light and hirelings gave me obvious things to spend it on.
6. Fights were readable. "fight:auto" kept them quick, and little touches came through: the pedlar's fists stun, Erevan guards Soveliss, Second Wind.
7. The world reacted: the fallen oak with a ring on the walk home, Uriah noticing I'd been down, and especially the ghouls and "whoever lives here came after you went" on the second trip.
8. Resting at the shrine and levelling up felt like a proper return to town.

## Surprise
The fallen oak with a ring in its roots on the dark walk home. And on the second trip the cleared level wasn't empty: a cold "like a breath let out, and out, and never drawn in", then three silent ghouls, then the line that the bodies I'd left had been dragged off and picked over. That made me want to go back. I also didn't expect the wall I broke to open onto a stair flooded to the roof, or that a pump-room upstairs might drain it.
