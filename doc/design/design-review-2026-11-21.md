# Design review: the portrait tablet, surfaces, and the fight (21 November)

Seven questions raised in a design review of the contact sheets (820×1180 portrait tablet and the phone), kept for the
Game workstream's aesthetics and experience track. They are questions to decide, not tickets; the fight's (5) is the
one the owner finds irritating in play now, and is in the register as OR-181.

1. **Layout at a portrait tablet.** Creation, options and the fight are split or corner-anchored over full-bleed art,
   composed for a landscape screen. At 820×1180, should they stay so, or become a centred single column with a
   lower-third composition over the painting?
2. **A visual anchor for each step of creation.** Sign has the zodiac on the left. Will Calling, Specialty, Have,
   Before, Trade, Name and Ready each get one, or is the open left canvas meant for the painting behind?
3. **One rule for surfaces.** The Encyclopaedia is a bright light surface; the three handbooks are dark. One theme, or a
   glare-controlled parchment shared by every book?
4. **Unreachable and sparse views.** A route from Options that is missed in a handbook, and a near-empty Story: hidden,
   visibly disabled with a reason, or a designed empty state?
5. **The fight's reach (OR-181).** The actions are at the bottom left and every one asks for a target, whose cards are
   at the top right; the target menu that folds out does not align with the action menu (its helper text and eyebrow
   push it off the rows). Wanted: the action rows aligned in one column throughout the composition, and a press on an
   enemy's card itself a plain attack on them, so the commonest act needs no trip across the screen.
6. **Reading measure.** The maximum line width (in ch), the least body and table font size, and how tables reflow
   (stacked or scrolled) so that Talk's replies, the Store, the Encyclopaedia and the handbooks read comfortably at
   820 px.
7. **Touch targets.** Whether the bottom navigation's icons and labels meet a tablet's touch-target size and spacing
   on every in-game and reference screen (with OR-180, the buttons' three states and their 44 px hit area).
