# The owner's playtest, 25 November 2026

The owner's raw notes from a play of the deployed build on 25 November, kept here as they wrote them. The register's
rows OR-675 to OR-679 come from them, with AX-04 seen again. Each finding is filed by its kind: a defect is an
observed violation of a claimed contract; a requirement is a commissioned outcome not yet met; a direction is a wanted
future kept whole and not commissioned.

## The notes, as written

> Okay, just some raw playtesting notes but -- I'm pretty sure I was able to travel multiple hexes without using the
> world map; things still feel very chaotic and overburdened. I wonder if we shouldn't _forbid_ using the old masters
> painting for backgrounds; only commissioned pieces. Some of them are nice for set pieces but -- I sort of think they
> should only be for tarot cards and ofc the portraits. It still takes way too long to start up a new world; and once
> you do it's all very sweaty and unclear how to get to the dungeon, if there is one; even if you get to an adventure
> site with a party, it's over in seconds and it was only one combat anyway, you don't really get treasure but then
> again there's nothing to spend it on and no meaningful constraints or upgrades or new verbs so what's the point
> anyway? Just some thought

## Carried into

- **Every new life in the reference game** (World): OR-677. The notes call play chaotic and overburdened. It is unclear
  how to reach the dungeon, if there is one. A site is over in seconds, with only one fight in it. With OR-640 and
  OR-642.
- **World start time** (Maturity): AX-04, seen again. A new world still takes too long to start.
- **Old masters for portraits and tarot only:** OR-675, closed by the ruling's own change. No scene or room background
  resolves to an old master (test/small/backgrounds-commissioned). The places with no commissioned painting are a
  commission list for the owner: OR-676.
- **Progression and economy** (the tabletop deliverable, to be commissioned): OR-678. Little treasure, nothing to spend
  it on, and no meaningful constraints, upgrades or new verbs. With SI-07 and OR-610.
- **Multi-hex travel without the map** (awaiting the owner's answer whether it was one act): OR-679.
