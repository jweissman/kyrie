# The ten-star version (the owner, 17 November 2026, night)

Shared by the owner "just to help us expand the frame": not a commission. Some items are convincing or close to ideas
already held; the bracketed notes are the owner's own and carry concrete asks, registered as rows (TS-01..03). The
owner's text is kept in their message of 17 November; its headings, for reference:

- The ten-star version is "anticipatory, authored, and invisible": systems with taste, memory, restraint and
  philosophical seriousness, not larger systems (prose, choice, character, conflict, world, interface).
- The Palimpsest: a motif ledger; moral memory, not morality points; a philosophical companion that asks rather than
  grades; reading lenses (Stoic, existential, tragic, virtue-ethical); essay mode at chapter breaks; an annotation
  layer; an unreliable codex of contending accounts.
- Narrative and prose: dream-slow text; canon-locked generative prose; a narrator with a secret; motif weather;
  revision-aware text.
- Character and interiority: an NPC interiority engine (routines, gossip, a private theory of you); a consequence
  tapestry; counterfactual memory as marked dreams; palimpsest replay.
- World and exploration: a living codex with scholarly disputes; the map as an artifact with errors and propaganda,
  correcting it a narrative act; an awe scheduler; sensory theatre.
- Philosophical and meta: a philosophical sparring partner; a thematic concordance; consentful co-authorship;
  anti-optimization mode; ecstatic failure.

## The owner's bracketed asks

- **Philosophical schools** (an undelivered ask of an earlier mission): recognizable schools (atomists, fluxists,
  monists…) that state a reasonable, interrogable version of their arguments in good faith; that teach, walking you
  through dialectic, perhaps for a fee, perhaps improving your speech and reasoning; and that argue with one another in
  public, each trying to win over the crowd. (TS-01)
- **Persuading a crowd** as a contested roll against the crowd, not a described mass-persuasion: a common ask of the
  authoring friction reports; a disputation with a philosopher is narrated as one and should resolve as one. (TS-02)
- **Ecstatic failure, and the party's end:** a total party kill should not be a reload; continue as a relative (your
  brother) with the world exactly as it was. (TS-03)
