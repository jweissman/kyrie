# The golden thread: a life, not a level (20 November)

The owner's picture, verbatim (20 November, late):

> A player starts in one of several meaningful circumstances. They belong to a family or community, have some
> recognizable abilities, and can see possible lives ahead of them. They can explore a substantial region, visit several
> culturally different settlements, undertake authored and generated adventures, make friends and enemies, learn skills,
> acquire a home, and pursue a profession.
>
> They can set out on dangerous expeditions, retreat, return, prepare again, and become more capable through the things
> they have learned. Some NPCs act independently and remember important interactions. A relationship can deepen or
> sour. A local situation can develop without the player. The world changes for reasons other than the protagonist.
> There are a few extraordinary places that really justify the fantasy: a terrifying dungeon, a magnificent city, an
> impossible landscape, perhaps an encounter with another plane. Not hundreds of thin attractions. And crucially, a
> different player character or a different generated world gives rise to genuinely different relationships,
> circumstances, opportunities, and discoveries.

The first hour (first-hour-beats.md) is the thread's opening. This page is the rest of it: twelve checkpoints, each a
thing a player can do and can be shown to have done. Each says what stands today, the gap, and the stream that owns it.

## Three streams

| Stream | Owns | Who |
|---|---|---|
| **The Game** | The experience: compelling play, presentation, performance, emotional pacing, human acceptance | the lead, with the sprint work |
| **The World** | Content and creative growth: exceptional authorship, reusable situations, cultures, professions, generated variation, relationships between adventures | a continuous stream: Fun Fridays, adventure integration, the drove's answers, the pleasures |
| **The Instruments** | Kyr, Deem, authoring, inspection, testing and the structural foundations that make the other two more productive | the maturity track |

Every register row belongs to one stream. The planning views (planning-views.md) are read by stream as well as by
slice: each stream keeps a short active list. The Game's list is the first hour's blocker queue.

## How it is proved

- **A long acceptance trace** (the defect-centres review, Centre 7), extended from the first hour: one life played to a
  home and a profession, at least two expeditions with a retreat between them, and one extraordinary place. It is driven
  through the page and kept with frames and screenshots at each checkpoint.
- **A difference census** for checkpoint 12. Two characters in one world, and one character in two worlds: who they meet,
  what they are offered, what they find. It is a count, like the pleasures' census.
- **Human play judges.** None of the above stands for a person playing it and wanting to go on (planning-views.md,
  ruling 5).
