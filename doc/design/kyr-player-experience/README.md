# Moved: the Kyr player-experience specification of 20 October 2026

This specification (vision, product specification, combat model, player journeys, interface specification,
capability gaps, roadmap) was a design commission for review, written on 20 October 2026 against the Caught Below
build. It is **historical**: its central recommendation of three active companions created with presets (PX05) and its
zone-tactics experiment (R10) were replaced by later owner decisions (one situated protagonist, companions by
relationship: the [Living Design Atlas](../ATLAS.md), Missions 16 and 19), and its white-ground interface by the dark
stage of Mission 18. What of it remains unbuilt is traced in
[../../architecture/findings/briefs.md](../../architecture/findings/briefs.md) (BRF-94 to BRF-103).

It now lives, unchanged, in [../../archive/2026-10-20-kyr-player-experience/](../../archive/2026-10-20-kyr-player-experience/README.md)
(moved 6 November 2026 by the documentation reconciliation).
