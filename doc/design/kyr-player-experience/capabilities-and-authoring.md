# E. Capability gaps and authoring requirements

Status applies to the **current working tree and playable content**, not the registry's number of definitions. Supported may mean a narrow working mechanism; partial identifies the boundary. Source links are inspected evidence, not additional play. Desired behavior is defined in the [product contract](product-specification.md) and [combat model](combat-model.md).

## 2. What authors must express

These are semantic obligations, not a requirement to invent a new language wholesale. Reuse current Place/Passage/Thing/Person/Topic/Action/Event/Pursuit/Objective/Discovery and catalog effects where they suffice. Add expressive capabilities only where meaningful product behavior cannot be represented or validated reliably.

| Player-facing behavior | Author must provide | Existing coverage | New semantics / validation needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oriented arrival | Place identity/containment; entry and obvious exits; short spatial observation; salient present inhabitants/features; state variants. | Places, passages, observations, people supported. | Check descriptions agree with state; avoid unconditional “empty bell-cote” after return. Builder cannot infer layout from display node coordinates. |
| Readable guarded passage | Visible obstruction, anchor/side it is perceived from, guard role/location/state, approach boundary, permitted methods, clearing/avoidance and consequences. | Guards, blocking states, changes and topics exist. | Threshold perception/interaction semantics from both sides; distinguish knowing guard from being able to address/attack it. |
| Tool/skill physical method | Target/state, requirements, bound provider if individual, duration, consumed/deployed tool, result and failure transition. | State changes, skill/item predicates, actions and reports supported. | Explicit provider evidence and deploy/recover semantics; validate no invented piton/line reuse if item state contradicts it. |
| Treasure recovery/handover | Specific item identity/properties, owner/custody/location, recipient, accepted return terms, transfer and outcome. | Item take/drop/transfer and arbitrary rules exist. | First-class handover intent and exact custody transition. Bell ground-location trigger is expressible but harmful as the only affordance. |
| Social offer/negotiation | Speaker, terms/consideration, eligibility, available response, acceptance/refusal/counteroffer and world consequences. | Topics and authored actions/tribute support bounded examples. | Consistent offer/result/commitment contract; no generic charisma button to invent terms. |
| Promise retained in Notes | Who promised whom, explicit consent event, content, deadline estimate/source, fulfillment predicate, consequences known/hidden, cancellation/amendment. | Can encode bespoke relations/actions. | Durable typed commitment semantics, provenance, lifecycle and knowledge access. Frontend cannot parse a sentence and decide a legal obligation. |
| Independent NPC activity | Motivation as goal, applicability/priority, resources/knowledge, allowed actions, durations, observable current activity and result. | Pursuit/planner/rules/pending actions exist. | Bound knowledge and institutional delegation; combat/social integration where needed. Do not expose hidden plans as current prose. |
| Developing situation | Participants/roles, pressure, clocks, possible developments, conditions/resources, interruption/reversal, observable evidence and outcomes. | Timed rules/events and generated measures support closures/routines. | Cross-situation commitments/resources and reports; classify public estimate vs hidden due time. |
| Institution response | Members/representatives, authority, property/resources, access, rules for witnessed/reported acts, obligations and restitution. | Custom relations/people/services can express fragments. | Stable institution/permission/obligation semantics; witness scope and reports. No global omniscient dislike meter. |
| Rumor/discovery | Claim, speaker/source, confidence/evidence, access conditions, what establishes/refutes it and what routes it reveals. | Discovery/source/knows and observations exist. | Heard reports and stale knowledge alongside formal facts; contradictions/versioned knowledge rather than overwriting truth. |
| Multi-enemy encounter | Participants/factions/initial extent, hostility/aid/join policy, approach triggers and intent/objectives, retreat/defeat dispositions. | Battle participant arrays; campaign resolves one opponent group. | Encounter composition authoring and boundary checks; no ambient all-room aggro assumption. |
| Tactical zone | Canonical feature/area, connections, reach/visibility, thresholds/cover, initial occupancy and transitions to place exits. | Place graph can supply anchors, not combat geometry. | New zone rules/data and validators; must not guess zones from text or SVG. |
| Environmental intervention | Object state/anchor, actor/tool/reach, action cost, eligibility, effects/risks, immediate authoritative transition and lasting aftermath. | Campaign thing changes/hazards; post-combat elemental exposure. | Legal in-encounter feature action, source/clock/target integration and rollback. |
| Non-kill objective / surrender | Goal predicate/progress, parties' aims, legitimate settlement terms or exit condition, outcome/disposition/rewards. | Arbitrary world goals and topics; battle endings limited. | Encounter outcome conditions and accepted terms integrated with hostility/custody/time. |
| Incapacity/death/recovery | Species life-state rule, explicit death/destruction event, defeat disposition, treatment/providers/costs, body/gear location and rescue exposure. | HP/death/repair/revive effects partially exist. | Coherent life-state/custody/location model; no narrator inferring dead from zero HP. |
| Growth and continuing expedition | Achievement IDs, rewards/eligibility, catalog progression curve, abilities/unlocks, mentors/training, next independent opportunities and prior-world effects. | XP/training/reward actions partial. | Complete progression and campaign composition, content coverage and repeatable non-farmable reward semantics. |

## 3. Logical records the client needs

Prefer extending existing canonical concepts over a second frontend-specific world. The client needs projections of:

- **KnownPlace/Passage:** identity, containment/threshold, observable state, route duration/requirements, visibility source, last observed and actual traversal history.
- **Interaction/Intention:** stable action identity, target, bound actor/provider, eligibility/reason visibility, time/cost/risk/effect summary. A menu tree is not the authority for these semantics.
- **ObservedEvent:** immutable event-time identity/sequence/source/subjects/text or reconstructable reference, related effects/state changes and visibility.
- **KnownMatter:** opportunity/report/request/commitment/discovery with source, status, known places/people/deadline and evidence. Its derived summary is not world truth.
- **Encounter:** extent/participants, world-anchored occupancy, queue/intents, objective and outcome/disposition. Combat projection does not own duplicate locations.
- **Companion/Item:** life/condition, abilities and providers, owner/location/readiness/load, progression and clocks, preserving authoritative instances.

These are design contracts, not prescribed class names or a new persistence format. Existing IDs, world state and event log should be reused. New structures must have one owner; Notes and interface state are derived/cacheable. Do not solve missing facts with prose interpolation.

## 4. Authoring quality gates

Before marking a campaign playable:

1. **Affordance audit:** every salient obstacle/mechanism/request has a perceivable reason and at least one coherent supported approach or a truthful limitation. All offered actions have predictable intention/cost; legal but useless choices are not presented as equally meaningful.
2. **State audit:** each material transition updates current observations, access, custody and relevant responses. A glad bell-ringer cannot coexist with unconditional current prose that says the bell-cote is empty unless explicitly explained.
3. **Knowledge audit:** sources/visibility cover important requests, deadlines, reports and consequences. A UI cannot learn hidden facts by scanning the authoritative snapshot.
4. **Clock audit:** physical travel/treatment and NPC pending actions use declared duration. Conditions identify clock and expiry; authored estimates do not silently become exact remote truth.
5. **Encounter audit:** participants, approach, space, intention, objective, surrender/withdrawal and loss disposition are explicit. A one-zone fallback is accepted; unspecified positions are not invented.
6. **Continuation audit:** completing each immediate matter leaves intelligible resolved state and known unresolved interests, or explicitly completes the bounded adventure. Campaign-capable content includes another expedition and an independent development.
7. **Play audit:** a fresh player can explain decisions and consequences. Compiler validity/replay parity protects correctness but does not establish this result.
