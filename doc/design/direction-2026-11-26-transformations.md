# Transformations: of resources, of forms, of spells, of professions (the owner, 26 November)

The owner's thought, kept whole, as a direction, not a commission:

> the economic transformations behind a tiny rock-paper-scissors system of resources at one level is one thing. The idea
> would be take from simulationist inspirations the generalized transformations of things. The polymorph spell would
> be a particularly obvious example of distinguishing the form from the 'true' identity of a thing. But duplicates,
> clones, holograms; all should be modellable from within Kyr using basic ideas and SRD should plugin to the underlying
> plumbing. We don't have to commit the language as such to an ontology; hopefully that is something that plugs in. But
> more generally the idea should be -- economic resources transform into one another, but so material resources and
> spells -- maybe you can unlock other spells just by knowing similar prereqs that share DNA. More generally the world
> should be like this -- we should take the huge profession list we have and think about ways to make it a
> 'civilizational-industrial algebra' where the different professions shape the productivity of different areas, but
> also combine to unlock new professions … and because some professions only unlock due to the scale of resources in
> one area, can shape overall feel and distribution of the kinds of social and technical development are available. …
> all I mean by 2-paradox tournament is a 5-node rock-paper-scissors graph where each resource 'beats' two other
> resources and is 'beaten by' the remaining ones. Maybe 'machines' and 'junk' are two resources; you can refine junk
> to make machines, but you destroy most of the junk searching through it for the machines. You can investigate
> machines for clues (we need some kind of object to represent a focus on investigation). They might have some
> specific instrument on them; maybe there's an instrument manual you can find which explains how to solve the puzzles

## 1. Five resources, each beating two and beaten by two

The graph is a regular tournament on five nodes: each node points at two and is pointed at by two. The Atlas has five
colours. The lead's suggestion: let the five resources align with the five tones, so the economy's cycle and the
opportunity's colour are one grammar.
- **Red:** meat, hides, what is hunted and fought for.
- **Green:** coin, wares, what is traded.
- **Blue:** knowledge, books, glass, what is learned.
- **Violet:** wonders' stuff, what is uncanny.
- **Gold:** whatever the aim needs, the resource the story asks for.

Which beats which is a design choice for the tabletop (OR-678's economy hypothesis), proved there before it is built.
Junk and machines show the shape of one transformation: junk refined into machines, most of the junk destroyed in the
searching. Each exchange is charged to the day's fatigue (OR-1200, OR-1204).

## 2. Form and identity

Polymorph, disguise, a duplicate, a clone, a hologram: each separates what a thing **is** (its identity, its history,
its relations) from what it **looks like** or **is made as** (its form: its kind, its appearance, its capacities).

| Transformation | Identity | Form |
|---|---|---|
| A polymorph | kept | changed |
| A disguise | kept | seemingly changed (a belief, held by those who see it) |
| A duplicate or a clone | new | copied |
| A hologram | none (not a thing) | seen |

The kernel needs only this distinction, identity and form, held apart, with effects that change one, copy the other,
or show a form without substance. It must not hold an ontology of what forms there are: the SRD's forms (a polymorph's
creatures, an illusion's limits) plug into the plumbing as installed data. That is the typed vocabulary's work (the
compilation review's slice 6), and it should be designed with this case in hand.

## 3. Spells that share DNA

Abilities written as effect sentences (OR-1097, OR-1105's round trip) are made of clauses: a target kind, an effect
kind, a condition, a cost. Two spells sharing clauses share "DNA". So knowing a spell's components can unlock a
neighbour: a mage who knows *light* and *reveal* finds *detect* within reach. This is computable once every ability is
a sentence, and it makes learning a graph the world can teach, not a list.

## 4. A civilizational-industrial algebra of professions

The 598 backgrounds and professions (`bin/kyrie list professions`) become an algebra:
- a profession raises the productivity of the resources of the area it works;
- professions combine to unlock others: a smith and a miner, at scale, make a foundry-master;
- some unlock only when an area's resources reach a scale, so a region's resources shape which social and technical
  developments it can have, and so its feel.

This belongs with the civilizational atlas (doc/design/direction-2026-11-24-civilization.md, gamma's commission). The
algebra is the generator that atlas's civilisations could be drawn from.

## 5. A focus of investigation, and instruments with manuals

Investigation wants an object: a focus that the company turns its attention to (a machine, a ledger, a corpse, a
seal), whose clues are read by checks and by what the company knows. A focus may carry an instrument (a dial, a lock,
a star-glass), and a manual found elsewhere explains how to work it. A found manual is a book as a perception
instrument (a direction kept in AGENTS.md), and an instrument is a wonder's form (OR-1139).

## How they connect

Each of the five is a transformation in Kyr's controlled English over the world's things (OR-1097):
- resources into resources;
- form apart from identity;
- spells into spells by their shared clauses;
- professions into professions by scale;
- objects into knowledge by investigation.

That is why the abilities' world writes come first. Every one of these is an effect sentence once effects reach the
world.
