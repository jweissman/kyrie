The Meóswaeter road, under the oaks

The road goes in under the oaks and the ruts fill with leaf. A hundred paces in, a holloway leaves it on the left, sunk between banks of root: the woodmen’s way to the charcoal clearings.

Ahead the road bends east. At the far end of it the light is the open light of the fen.

Cart-tracks in the soft ground, a week old at least, and beside them the prints of a dog.
