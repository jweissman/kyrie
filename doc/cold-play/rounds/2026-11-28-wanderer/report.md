## Why
The first hour felt like an invitation: one clear errand, a thin town, and woods that rewarded searching. Paying Thal for woodcraft paid back later at the tripwire. Reading the post-stone and copying it turned curiosity into a finished errand: Tomas gave 300 xp each and 40 coin. The wolf, rat and bomb fights matched their "should be won" labels. Most of my friction was interface: the trade screen trap, and one call per actor in combat.

## What worked
1. Aims that update as you act (an "errand home" aim appeared once I copied the writing).
2. "Search here" revealing finds one at a time.
3. Trap rows with several approaches, and perception noticing from learned skills.
4. Talk that gives concrete aims, and goblins who accept "the same thing".
5. The stair's description of the hall that could hold Windworth.
