# Cold play A1 — Odile, human warrior, report
**Clarity: 7 / 10.** The aims and the way were clear. Item pickups and a few offers weren't.
**Wanting to go on: 7 / 10.** I would go back for three things:
- Get past the ghouls at the foot of the stair.
- Hire Hawise once I have 6 coin.
- Carry the copied writing home to K'nar and the brothers.

The crown of the first king is also a standing aim.
**Moves played: about 73. Time in the game: still the morning of 30 Haysel, Day 1.**

## Scores, 0 to 4 each
- **Beginning: 4.** T1–8: the first goal, K'nar's letter, the rope and the stair aim were all given, and the next step was always visible.
- **Agency: 3.** T9–25: I bought a torch and hired Reed. T50–54: there were five ways past the trap, and the pole worked. T55: the side glyph was optional. Most fights came down to "Let X fight".
- **Trust: 2.**
  - T59: "Easily won: no match for you" sat beside text saying five rats "could bring Odile down".
  - Several look and collect rows timed out (T30, T51, T55, T70–72).
- **Discovery: 3.**
  - T36–38: reading the kerb-stones gave the makers' lore ("water remembers").
  - T16: Soveliss told the barrow tale.
  - Searching found the glyph.
  - Loot was visible but unreachable: jet button, ring, coins, notebook, alchemist's fire.
- **Continuation: 3.** The ghouls, the crown, Hawise and the deeper storeys all pull me back.

## Why
The opening is clear and connected. A letter, a named priestess, a way in, a companion and a dungeon all point forward. The prose is restrained and specific. The barrow's traps give real choices, and the lore pays off the errand. Trust broke where the interface did: many examine and collect rows fail with TimeoutError, so loot and lore were unreachable. I stopped at the foot of the stair, facing a hard fight in the dark with Odile at 10 of 15 HP.

## What I wanted
1. Deliver the letter and learn the way. Done (T3).
2. Get light and a companion. I bought a torch and hired Reed (T14, T24).
3. Read and copy the old writing. Done (T38–40), but not yet carried home.
4. Hire Hawise. Failed, one coin short (T42). I couldn't reach the jet button or coins to afford her.
5. Take the crown from the king's bier. Not reached.

## Defects
1. T30: "Put an ear to the humming stone" gave `TimeoutError: click`.
2. T51, T55, T70–72: the jet button, the passage words, the coins and the candle all time out.
3. T4: asking K'nar what the matter is repeats the whole earlier letter-giving text.
4. T31: "Out to the market-place of Millford" is offered as a plain exit from four storeys down.
5. T59: "Easily won" contradicts "could bring Odile down". The rat fight also took about ten "auto" rounds.
6. T14: after buying a torch, the Trade list printed dozens of near-identical Haggle rows.
7. T41: the forecourt zombies attacked on entry, and the offer gave no warning.

## What worked
1. The letter, aims and dungeon thread.
2. The pole trap, with several ways to try it (T50–54).
3. Reading the marks gave real lore ("letters you have met before in older books").
4. Reed was cheap to hire, and his stats and abilities were legible.
5. The dark and light handling.

## Surprise
A trap you can set off harmlessly with a ten-foot pole. The barrow's makers also cast bronze sluice-gates and held that still water shows what is coming. That is water-priest necromancy rather than the usual tombs.
