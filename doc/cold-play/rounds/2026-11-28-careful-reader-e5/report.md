# Cold play E5 — Edwy (human warrior), report
**Clarity: 8 / 10**
**Wanting to go on: 7 / 10.** I would go back for "the noon the paymaster kept in a jar", the new aim Kaldor gave me. I would also try the adit's glyph and smithy traps (unsay the glyph, pole the enchanted object), now with 50 coin to equip Edwy.
**Moves played: about 70; time in the game: Day 1, early afternoon, 30 Haysel**

## Scores
- **Beginning: 3.** The first goal and a letter to deliver gave me a clear start, and Kaldor gave the way at once (T0-6).
- **Agency: 3.** I weighed hiring Gareth against buying kit (T17-30), and the adit traps stated their risk and offered several solutions (T50).
- **Trust: 3.** Offers matched their results, and "set it down" was a real act (T47). The defects below cost it a point.
- **Discovery: 3.** Granger's "old workings" hook (T11), the humming stone (T37) and the post-stone's lore (T45) all repaid attention.
- **Continuation: 3.** The new aim (T67), coin to spend and unsolved traps give me reasons to return.

## Why
It is a small, tight loop: a letter, a stair, writing to read and copy, then a payoff with a deeper hook. Rumours passed from person to person (Kaldor, Jessica, Granger). The dungeon dressing told me honestly what lay ahead. Most of the friction was menu wording and a few acts that were easy to miss.

## What I wanted
1. Deliver the letter: done (T4).
2. Find light for the stair: I already held torches and candles (T40).
3. Get a companion: I hired Gareth, a cleric, for 6 coin (T24).
4. Read and copy the writing, then report it: done, for +40 coin and 300 XP each (T45-69).
5. Push past the glyph in the adit: I held back at 13 HP.

## Defects
1. T9-40: the aim said "Take light", but no shop sold any. I only learned I held torches at T40 and nearly hunted for a lamp.
2. T39: the way to the stair reads "Out to the stair under the fallen stones" though I was going in. "Stair", "adit" and "four storeys down" on a hillside are confusing.
3. T46: "Copy the old writing into your book" sat among the routine rests and was easy to miss. The aim text "go there, and set it down" did not point at it.
4. T47: the torch guttered out within a few turns of lighting (T41 to T47), leaving me in pitch dark again.
5. T5-6: each talk screen reprints all the earlier lines, which buries the new reply.
6. T64: `follow` on an aim errored ("No choice follow:origin__errand_home") although the aim was still listed.
7. T52-56: Cleave needs a second step to pick a target, and Gareth's turn also asks me ("Let Gareth fight this turn"). The bats attacked only Gareth, so Edwy felt idle.

## What worked
1. A first goal with a letter handed to someone who tells you the way (T0-4).
2. Question rows labelled with where you learned them, such as "Kaldor told you" (T11, T17).
3. Hazard text at the adit with a stated risk ("about 7 harm") and several skill-based ways through (T50).
4. Seeing Gareth's full stat line before paying to hire her (T24).
5. A payoff that came with a new, flavourful aim (T67-69).

## Surprise
A haunted mine: a scorched ring round the adit where nothing nests, "the makers kept day in a jar", and a glyph that is a word you can unsay. It was more eerie and less dungeon-crawl than I expected.
