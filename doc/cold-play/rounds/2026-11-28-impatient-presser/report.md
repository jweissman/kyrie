# Cold play D4 — Mindartis, elf warrior, report
**Clarity: 8 / 10** (The aims and rows said what to do. I had to guess the "Go on" id and the loot rows.)
**Wanting to go on: 6 / 10** (I want to win the banshee fight, then read the old writing the brothers asked for. I also want to hire Mialee with the 22 coin I found, and see what lies in the low chamber behind the levered stones.)
**Moves played: about 87 turns; time in the game: still the morning of 30 Haysel, day 1, with the torch about to give out.**

## Scores, 0 to 4 each
- **Beginning: 4.** T0–2: I gave Thamior the letter, got the rope and a new aim to the stair, and the first goal was clear.
- **Agency: 3.** T8 I hired Tigger. T42 I read the carvings and made the hidden switch safe. T49 I talked past the grave-robbers on an even chance.
- **Trust: 3.** Offers matched results. "Fire or radiant would serve better" (T55, T71) was true, and the difficulty labels fit the fights.
- **Discovery: 3.** The loot, the coin niches, the trap and the levered wall were worth pursuing. The scratched mark was unreadable.
- **Continuation: 3.** There is an unread inscription, unspent coin and an unfinished fight.

## Why
The start was fast and plain. Shrine, market, woods and stair all went by pressing the first promising row. The barrow then gave a good mix of easy fights, a trap solved by reading, a parley and coin. The middle held up. The banshee fight, three foes resisting physical damage, turned into about 16 rounds of misses on both sides. I stopped there and never reached the writing.

## What I wanted
1. Read the old writing for the brothers. Not reached.
2. Recruit Mialee. At T24 I had 4 coin against her 6. I now have 22, but she waits back up the stair.
3. Get through the barrow quickly. This mostly worked until the banshees.

## Defects
1. T21: the stair is "pitch dark" and I had bought a single torch. Nothing before the stair said how much light I would need, beyond "take light, and more than you think you need".
2. T30: after a fight the continue row is `onward`, not a `fight:` id. My guess failed. The error did list the valid ids.
3. T66–71: levering the small stones released three banshees. My weapons barely hurt them, and I had no fire or radiant. The oil firebomb was in the shop and I never bought it. Nothing warned me this would matter.
4. T72–87: the banshee fight is dull. Misses on both sides and resistance made progress very slow, and the fight was still going at T87.
5. T66: the "one try" read of the mark failed, and the only way on was the fight. The failure and the fight were not linked in the offer.
6. T13: the shop is one very long list of about 45 rows.
7. Every status line shows "purse None". The coin count only appears in the text.

## What worked
1. The aim row on the first screen, with a clear first goal.
2. Hiring a companion in one press, with their stats shown beforehand.
3. The hidden-switch trap read through the carvings (T42), and the "you know this level now" crossing note (T49).
4. Auto-fight and "Press on to the end" kept the easy fights quick.
5. The parley with the grave-robbers on an even chance.
6. The fight foresight lines ("fire or radiant would serve better") were honest.

## Surprise
A modest errand to a shrine led within about 20 moves to a five-storey tomb under a mound. A catfolk charcoal-burner turned out to be a hireable ranger, and a trap could be defused by reading its carvings rather than fighting. I did not expect those from a game starting at a spring shrine.
