# Gosse's Company (Pentrice)

*Corpus review. Sampled: prose census, places, the endings list, `placed`, the persons census; not read whole (863 KB).*

**Premise.** Pentrice licenses every word said on a stage; three companies of players give three nights at the Wheel and a bench of four critics gives one of them the Patent. Old Absalom Gosse is back after forty years' banishment, and a ghost is on the alderman's fair copy.

**What works.** An astonishing density: 91 people, 144 places, nineteen endings (more than any other in the set) and a rep-company structure (`rep_*`, `rier_*`, `act1` to `act5` by file name) that appears to let the company perform differently each night. The licensing office as villain is funny and humane.

**Shared forms.** "Gosse/Hepzibah/Mistress X sits/takes/looks" is the staging beat; "'Ah,' says the..." opens a few too many speeches; the scene census contains several tiltyard-bench inventories ("a rack of blunted foils and a bench where three idle captains drink").

**Weak or thin.** The sheer volume (`night.kyr` is 87 KB) hides which scenes are doing the work; no cull was attempted. Few danger beats.

**Connections.** First region: the herald scenario (players on the green) and the Westgate bill. Other adventures: none inside the module's own text.

**Done.** Nothing beyond this review: depth went elsewhere.
