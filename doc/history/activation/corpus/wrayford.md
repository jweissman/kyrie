# Wrayford (The Third Night)

*Read: `lore.kyr`, `clock.kyr`, the opening of `city.kyr`; every first meeting and its `Role`; the Discovery list and the endings; the hanging-rail and Jennet Hobday sections of `bridge.kyr`; Kit and the carters in `pardoes.kyr`/`northgate.kyr`. The market, liberty and wade sections were scanned, not read through.*

**Premise.** A bridge-city three days after the lord's riders hanged three carters on the gatehouse rail. The reeve keeps them there until the captain comes at dusk on the third day; a widow means to bury her husband before the third night; a toll-clerk has been skimming; a boy who drove the cart is hiding under the first arch. Eleven endings, none of them clean.

**What works.** The best clocked, best scheduled city in the set: people are at the yards by day, the taproom in the evening, under the arches after dark. The moral weight is carried by small domestic things (the ice, the loaves, the farthing toll). Spare, dry voice that suits the place.

**Shared forms.** Twenty-one of thirty-three first meetings had a look in them ("Name looks up/at you") plus one remark, five of them under twelve words ("The young carters look at you, and at each other"; "Kit looks up, and looks away again"). `Greets again` was absent for all thirty-three. No `Objective` at all, although eleven endings hang on what the company learns.

**Weak or flat.** The market quarter (Quoin, Pratt, Tull, Kemp) is excellent in detail but the least tied to the rail. Nothing in the whole city is dangerous: no temperament, no `Warns` (riders on the gate are `Peaceable`). The danger is political and arrives at nine on the fourth morning.

**Connections.** `lore.kyr` points out (Tolleth's Dorothea, Cattermole to Skeel, a lamplighters' seal). The country below had a rumour and no consequence; it now says when the captain has given her verdict.

**Changed.**
- Fourteen first meetings rewritten to show what the person is doing and want (Pinch, Gault, the riders, the carters, Kit, Corbin, the Abbess, Clout, Dorcas, Kemp, Pratt, the reeve, Hal, Mistress Gidley).
- `Greets again` for thirty-two people, each one particular.
- Five `Objective`s (bury the dead before the third night; Kit at midnight; the captain's question; the letter's hand; ice for the boy).
- `placed.kyr`/`placed.json`/`countries.json`: `after` (the country says the verdict has been given, and the innkeeper hears the company out).

**Not done.** A patrol of the lord's riders on the drove road on days one and two ("show your business") would give the approach some friction. Written up in `deem-proposals.md`, not authored, because a `Warns` band that does not fight is R40.
