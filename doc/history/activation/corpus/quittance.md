# Quittance

*Branch adventure/p2-corpus-b. Sampled (about 6,160 lines, 34 files, the largest of the nine): Greets, repeats, links, endings, placed. Not read in full.*

## Premise
A river-port of eighty thousand, burned in a grandmother's time and built again on credit; every house wears a plate saying
which Office will put it out. This year's Receipt is blank, the thing under the Exchange has not been paid, and the
company takes adjusting work and sits in a dozen offices, bakeries, barges and lamp-houses that are each a small
adventure (`q_*.kyr`, twelve files).

## What works
- The most thickly authored place in the corpus: 92 Persons, 283 Topics, 358 Observes, 269 Rules, a clock to Reckoning
  (watch 1248), seven endings that read a tally (`wardsLit`, `motionCarried`, whether Prewitt turned).
- The conceit holds at every scale: debt, receipt, plate, the Honest Bell, the Roll, the Hall of the Five.

## Shared forms and phrasing
- Greets: 75 of 86 open "A / An [adj] [person] ..." (the corpus stock opening, at its densest). "looks at the {" x16 and "She looks at
  the {" x19; "does not say anything" x9; "a very good" x14; "a gilt bird" x12 (the bird is a motif, the count is a tic).
- Same skeleton in the quest wings: a clerk, a ledger, a worry that the company will take a long time.

## Weak or flat
- By count, the q_ wings run 24-67 K each and are individually thick; the connective tissue between them (what one wing's
  outcome does in another) is thinner than the wings.
- Opening Greets share a head ("A tall man in a ...") so strongly that several offices feel like one.

## Connections
- Before: the rumour and after exist. In: `slack_opened`, `remainder_harboured`, `remainder_kept` (Mr Prest's Roll).
- Added: `calx_opened` and `tallow_names`: Mr Prest has a column for roads with no duty and a sheet of fourteen wages
  marked PAID, "sixty years late, by a village" (Quittance is the one adventure that would care).

## Encounter density and danger
Six Temperament lines; danger is the fire, the clock, and what the Offices will do. Structured by the clock.

## Changed
- `links.kyr`: two Observes. Manifests list two events. Everything else untouched.
