# Skeel: Fair Weather

*Branch adventure/p2-corpus-b. Sampled (about 1,840 lines): lore, endings, Greets, repeats; the Court and the dog not read in full.*

## Premise
Skeel on the Gannet Reach lives off wrecks and prays for them. A big ship has gone onto the sands in fair weather; the
Wreck Court sits in two days and if nothing came ashore alive she is the town's. Something did come ashore, and there is a dog.

## What works
- A town with a living economy and a conscience at once: the Admiral, the charter board (NEITHER MAN NOR DOG NOR CAT), the
  salt company, the factor's packet, and the sums that make the Wreck Court a vote.
- Endings of great specificity (Wreck, The Pilots, The Light in the Pulpit, Wreck While You Were Away), each a paragraph of
  town consequence.
- Already the best connected: lore.kyr reads `remainder_kept` and `slack_opened`, hands the company to Loveday Kitto's
  light, and the almanack woman who walks to the Bowl and Merrowdale.

## Shared forms and phrasing
- 15 of 16 Greets open "A [adj] [person] ... who looks at you". "looks at the {" x10 and "looks at you for a long" x6 against 18
  Persons: the highest rate of the glance beat in the batch. `voice repeats`: "a long time" x19, "the black dog" x13, "the night of the
  wreck" x11. The refrain "neither man nor dog nor cat" is intended; "a long time" is not.

## Weak or flat
- After the Court the Reach mostly goes quiet; `after.kyr` and `growth.kyr` exist, but the town is not different on a return
  visit (not read in full).

## Dead ends
- None found.

## Connections

One tempered band (Temperament appears once); the Court and the tide are the danger.
