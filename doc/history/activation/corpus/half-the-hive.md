# Half the Hive (the Wold of Hume)

*Corpus review, branch `adventure/p2-corpus-a`. Read in full: `country`, `smoke`; structure, topic lists and the leaving/after/growth rules of the rest.*

**Premise.** The old queen of a town that lives inside a honeycomb under a cliff is about to swarm and half the town will walk out behind her. The company is hired as a Searcher to find where; every person has a side and a reason, and the week ends in a Leaving that the company can shape.

**What works.** The best-organised module in the set: a single clock, a decision per person (who goes, who stays, settled by named rules in `leaving.kyr`), and an `after.kyr` that answers nearly every question put earlier. The prose is concrete and sensory (the honey vaults, the Capped Gallery, the Dancing Hall). Nell Quarrie's forged scent and Polglaze's filed beam are both moral problems with a real price.

**Shared forms.** "As big as hounds/ponies/a cradle" is the setting's scale idiom (ten uses) and is fine; "She looks at you." as a closing beat (eight) and an inside-speech "A pause." (five) are the corpus-wide tics, here lightly used. I varied six.

**Weak or thin.** Smoke End, the outsiders' street, had nothing to say after the Leaving: Hild, Nell, Crispin and the Drewes were dumb. The dell, the moor and the crag are scenery with a fight, not a place with a person in it.

**Danger and density.** Brocks in the dell (the only physical risk besides stings and the moth); otherwise social and moral.

**Connections.** To the first region: the candles and wax go down the south road, and Crispin's factor now says so ("in your inns by the first frost"). To other adventures: only the Cattermole line and `lore.kyr`'s ship's people; the Chandlers' Company could meet the Founders' Exchange (Low Water) as a rival buyer.

**Done.** Six after-the-Leaving topics in `smoke.kyr` (Hild; Nell in three states: scented, barred, still forging; Crispin and the lowland trade; the Drewes at the new comb), each reachable only in the state it describes. Three beats rewritten.

**Left.** A person on the heather moor; a use for the shepherds' drove beyond the shortcut; Martin's talk could change by how many Searchers are already out.
