# A Winter in Carth

*Read: `clock.kyr`, `endings.kyr` by structure, every first meeting and every `Greets again`, `Description` lines throughout, the Discovery list. `country.kyr`, `carth.kyr`, `trial.kyr` and `tower.kyr` were scanned, not read through.*

**Premise.** Gaunt, a walled cathedral city on a white plain three days before the Fast, where every fault has its price in penance and any penance may be borne by another. A barefoot man walks to his trial; a farm keeps a vigil of no fire for wages; a wood-reeve is buying winter. A moral economy built as an economy: the Stand where proxies are hired, the Row where they sleep, the Commerce of Merit.

**What works.** The conceit is thorough: the Last Inch (candles bought back at a halfpenny), the chestnut man who sells indulgences, the thirty-second loop on a ring of keys. The cold is paid for in rules. `Greets again` is present for twenty-nine of thirty-two, which no other long module manages. Six endings, by verdict.

**Shared forms.** The thin `Greets again`: of twenty-nine, twenty-eight were under 120 characters, and a third were "X looks up / nods / beams at you" ("Raistrick looks up from his book and waits", "Canon Laverock beams at you"). The pair is exactly where a person should change on return, and here they mostly did not. Twelve of the thirty-two first meetings also open on a look ("X looks you over / up from").

**Weak or flat.** No `Objective`: the page cannot lead with any of the six strands. Danger is three bands that fight (wolves in the Row's clearing, the Fabric's men with crowbars, one more) and four that warn; in sixty-one places that is low, and the cold is dramatized but never a pressure.

**Dead ends and connections.** The Hessel module already names Carth; the first region knows Gaunt only by a rumour. Nothing the company does in Gaunt reaches the country below.

**Changed.**
- All twenty-nine return greetings rewritten so that each says something the person would say now (Raistrick has not opened the book; Cuthbert Thwaite counts forty bells; Hal Metcalfe winks with his whole face; Alderman Thackray stops talking about wood).
- Six first meetings rewritten without the stock look.
- Four `Objective`s: who paid for Carth; the window at eleven on Fastens' Eve; the Consistory on Ash Day; the unbuilding of the north tower.
- `after`: the fell road's news of Gaunt once the book is closed (the beadle who came back down the march road).

**Not done.** One road encounter between the cross-road and the gate; see `deem-proposals.md` (the bought vigil).
