# The Banns at Hessel

*Corpus review. Sampled: `carth`, `city`, `cherretts`, `endings`, `lore`, the scene census; not read whole.*

**Premise.** A spa city in the last four days of its Season: a rich old woman who cooked for navvies is buying husbands for her family, and a house in the best street has kept its shutters closed nine years with the fires lit.

**What works.** Austen-and-Dickens comic social machinery: 21 events on a four-day calendar (172 clock rules) and endings that depend on whether Mrs Cherrett woke. The St Ebba tablet ("HE CAME TOO LATE"), Dr Hartopp's tipping chair and the vestry's thin register of banns are the house voice at its best.

**Shared forms.** "Mopsa looks at you" is the dominant speaker beat (six); otherwise the voice is distinct. Only one hazard, no species: it should not need more.

**Weak or thin.** Some small scenes were bare inventories ("a desk, a gun-case, a fire"); I rewrote the study, the coal cellar, the card room and the shepherd's hut. The Orm road is the one place that is not a drawing room, and it is thin.

**Danger and density.** Close to none; the ankle-bound Mrs Cherrett is the sole physical stake.

**Connections.** First region: rumour; now an `after` that says how the Season ended (gone / danced). Others: the Dorothea and Tolleth (lore), Jessamy Cattermole.

**Left.** A reason to return to Hessel after the Season: nothing changes in it for a second visit.
