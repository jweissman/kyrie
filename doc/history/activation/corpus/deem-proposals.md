# Where Deem generation, or a Deem template that calls Kyr, would add real variation

*Proposals only. Nothing here is built. "Template" means a `.kyrt` section as in `data/generation/town/troubles.kyrt`; "table" means a weighted JSON as in `data/generation/tables/`. Engine gaps are R47 onward in `../kyr-deem-requests.md`.*

Each proposal says what is the same in every seed today, what would vary, and what would be authored once.

1. **The same rumour in every inn.** `data/adventures/countries.json` gives each country one `rumour` string; `src/world/region-borders.ts` substitutes only `{keeper}`. Every innkeeper in every town tells Wrayford's hanged carters in the same words. *Vary:* a table keyed by the innkeeper's `Manner` that frames the same facts (`heard` stays fixed): a cheerful keeper leans on the bar, a grim one tells it to the cup. *Author once:* six frames and the `{fact}` hole. (R49.)
2. **Return greetings from manner.** The 100 persons who gained `Greets again` here were written by hand because `Greets again` is a literal. For generated persons (every first-town neighbour) a table of three return skeletons per `Manner`, with a hole for what the person was doing, would give each a change on return. (R50.)
3. **The bought vigil (Carth to the first town).** A Kyrt in `town/` makes one farm in the first region keep a vigil for wages, bought by a named first-town person (merchant, priest or reeve), paid in coin countermarked with that person's mark. Winter in Carth already has the other end: the Scaifes' shillings are countermarked with the Wood-reeve's cut log (`billet_coin`). Share it as a `canon` fact (as the Long Man shares `slack_opened`): the template asserts `billet_marked` and Carth's Thackray reads `befell(billet_marked)`. The company that has seen the first town's shilling arrives in Gaunt already knowing what the log means.
4. **A steward who sells the estate's metal.** Merrowdale is fixed; the shape is not. A template for the wild: a steward selling one structure's metal (sluice-gate, bell, weir-teeth, roof-lead) to the world's Exchange factor (herald-bound, as in `factors`), with a consequence drawn per seed (flood, silence, collapse). Merrowdale stays the long version.
5. **A numbered receipt.** In `merrow.kyr` the Exchange's receipts are numbered and Cass is the thirty-first. A draw per world would give the Exchange a number per seller that Quillon's green ledger (Dorothea) and Cass agree on, so that two adventures name the same count.
6. **Why the carters rose.** Wrayford's cause is the bridge toll. A draw of four (toll, bread price, a fence across the commons, the press-gang) with the matching rumour, notice and the 'what the board says' Discovery, would let the town be a *kind* of town; the hanging, the third night and the captain stay.
7. **A sleeper's house in the first town's troubles.** `troubles.kyrt`'s missing-person group has two options; add `lantern`: a boy who pays to sleep at a lamp-house in the undertown and has not come home in three nights (Thrume's Col Dunmore, Saltgrave's Red Lantern). A corpus-wide rhyme (Thrume, Book of Faces' Dream-houses) rather than a one-off.
8. **Tessary's slate.** The thief's chalk-cant on the ebb-runners' quay reads "who may pass". Draw the six marks per seed so that a thief who has learned them in one world cannot walk past a seal in the next.
9. **The hamper (Wrongboat).** The farce depends on what is in the hamper and what the label says. A module-level draw (R48) of contents, label hand and recipient would make three plays differ in the first minute; today only the order of events differs.
10. **A patrol on the drove road.** A Kyrt `wild/` meeting that places a lord's rider pair on a road in a country with a hanging nearby (Wrayford's neighbour): "show your business". Three answers: a pass, a coin, a name from the hanged. Needs R40 fixed so that a `Peaceable` band that warns does not fight on arrival.
