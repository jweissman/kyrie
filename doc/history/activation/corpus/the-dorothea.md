# The Dorothea

*Read: `skerrow.kyr` (people and the lamp), `entries.kyr` (Tolleth), `return.kyr` (rules), `ring.kyr` (head), every first meeting. `weather.kyr`, `night.kyr`, `mangrow.kyr`, `lamp.kyr` and `combs.kyr` scanned.*

**Premise.** A ketch under a captain in debt carries a company out of Tolleth to Skerrow, an island kept by a lighthouse nobody can afford: the House of Marchbank leases the kept lights of the Reach and does not light them, because wreck-rights are worth more. Six landings, a reef with four ways through, a storm, a lamp and a bronze table.

**What works.** The sea is a place with clocks (the Combs, the squall, the stay at each island), and every island has a different problem for the same company. Quillon, the House's agent, is a pleasure: he is the only person in the module who rises for you. The Prefect's table and the keepers' bench give the module a real mystery that other adventures can rhyme with.

**Shared forms.** The first meeting is nearly always a costume plus a sentence, then "Surname. Given name. Trade." ("Mate. Tobias Rune." "Auld. Reeve." "Ketteridge. Joss." "Tarn. Oswin. Measures.") across seven people; three more open by *not* looking up. Fifty-three of eighty-three Topics are `when true once`: the first time they are good, and then there is nothing more to say. `Greets again` was absent for all thirty-three.

**Weak or flat.** Zero `Objective`: nothing leads from Tolleth quay to the Combs in the page's own foreground. No bands except four; the sea is the only danger, and it only arrives with `weather.kyr`.

**Connections.** The ring and its Principal (the Founders' Exchange in the country behind) are bound in from `factors`, which is an excellent seam. Quillon buys old metal, and nothing yet said so to the Long Man's steward, whose Heron sells him bell-bronze.
