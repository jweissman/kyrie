# Muster: The Long Muster's Road

*Branch adventure/p2-corpus-b. Sampled, not read whole (about 1,950 lines): Greets, chapters, endings, the repeats report.*

## Premise
Three days of broken Aenean road over the Hesper Fells, from Kell Ford to the valley of the Last Cohort, a garrison that
has kept the hours of a vanished empire for eleven hundred years. A company carries a dispatch north and a decision
south: what to tell the forum, and what to bring home.

## What works
- A long road with real chapters (the Lower Country, the Heights and the Viaduct, the Giant's Pass, the Cohort), real
  weather (late season closes the passes on the seventh day), and a set of endings that read what the company carried
  and who came back (eighteen endings, including the boy who follows you south).
- The Cohort is a society, not a dungeon: a forum, a tribunal, a mill-reeve with a form for every sack. Companions
  (the largest single file) carry their own wants.
- Wit: "The standard does not rest, and neither does the man who bears it ... The lamp can hear."

## Shared forms and phrasing
- Greets: 20 of 24 open "A [adj] [person] [doing]". `voice repeats`: "eleven hundred years" x18, "the fifth arch" x13, "lamp in the
  window" x8. The span-of-years boast ("nine hundred" in Quiet in the Sky and Parliament, "eleven hundred" here) recurs
  across the batch as the stock way of saying a place is old.
- Duplicated NPC types: the garrulous veteran on a bench (Gaius, "thirty-one springs"), the scholar among scrolls, the
  pilgrim from the sea; each has a twin in a neighbour's Greets.

## Weak or flat
- The road itself (road.kyr) has eight Observes for three days of walking. The Cohort has sixteen and the Pass most of
  the rest; a company that dawdles in the lower country finds little for the time it spends.
- Danger is five Temperament lines for 39 people: the road is menaced by weather and lateness more than by creatures.

## Dead ends
- None found in the endings; `ending_turned` and `ending_snowed` are honest failures.

## Connections
- Before: the rumour names the reeve (Greta Hennick, who is in the endings) and now has an "after": a keeper who has
  never asked where the road goes, and lights a lamp.
- Out: nothing asserted. The standard, the Hearth and the Prefect are not read by any neighbour.
- Possible: the Aenean wheel-and-number milestones are also Tallow's Calx Way; the road station every first country holds
  (data/generation/works/roadstation.kyrt) is the same Empire's. Muster should say so once, from a grandmother's mouth
  in the first town (see kyr-deem-requests R49).

## Encounter density and danger
Danger arrives as obstacles and choices; beasts.kyr and gauntlet.kyr carry the fights. The late-season clock does more work
than any monster.
