# The Quiet in the Sky: Larum and the Pass

*Branch adventure/p2-corpus-b. Sampled (about 1,890 lines): lore, after, placed, Greets, repeats, endings headings.*

## Premise
For nine hundred years, on a still day, you could hear the dead fighting up on the Pass in the snow. Forty days ago it
stopped. Larum cannot sleep for the quiet; the company climbs to find why and finds the dead making bread.

## What works
- A single, strong idea carried with warmth: the dead are not enemies and not tragic, they are tired, and the
  living town has built its income on their noise.
- Variation by path: the "after" has a `loud` variant (the sky loud again), the only adventure in the batch that already
  tells different towns different news depending on which way it went.
- Large cast of dead with their own manners (the dwarf in a coat of rings, the herald "Name, and hour of death").

## Shared forms and phrasing
- 15 of 23 Greets open "A [adj] [person] ... looks up". "nine hundred years" x36 (!); "the third horn" x18; "ring of spears" x15;
  "a living hand" x15. The title phrase "the quiet in the sky" is used 27 times: a refrain, but at that rate a tic.
- "She sees your breath" Greets x4 (the dead notice the living by their breath): a good device, repeated as the same
  beat each time.

## Weak or flat
- "nine hundred years" is the standard unit of weight for every statement of age or grievance in the file; the number stops
  doing work by the thirtieth use.

## Connections
- Before: rumour + after (loud/quiet) already exist. In: `cloudgate_fell` is read in the street. Out: nothing asserted.
- Possible: the Collegium surveyor (Pym) and the Teeth link to the Lull and the Grand Tour already.

## Encounter density and danger
Three Temperament lines; danger is the cold, the Pass, and the third horn.
