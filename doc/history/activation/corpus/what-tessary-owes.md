# What Tessary Owes

*Read: first meetings and Objectives throughout, `clock.kyr`, `lore.kyr`, the endings in `feast.kyr`, `people.kyr` head. `plot.kyr` (50 KB), `strongrooms.kyr`, `holding.kyr`, `lowtide.kyr`, `city.kyr` scanned.*

**Premise.** Tessary on the Ossel, a city of salt and paper built on piles over a drowned Aenean city, in the fortnight before the Feast of Settlement. The Long Muster has come home from eleven years of war and some of its soldiers walk into the sea, barefoot, eyes open, in step. They are not dead: they are collateral. Seven endings by who holds the note.

**What works.** A mystery with a genuine answer, delivered by ten Objectives in order (find Davy, follow him, why held, what owed, pay it). The setting does the work: seals and queues, the slate on the ebb-runners' quay, the Ninth Valuer whose skin is faintly ruled. The strongest ratio of "a person and a document" in the set.

**Shared forms.** Twelve of seventeen first meetings opened on a look ("X looks up from the book / a splice / the trap / the ledger"): the hiring clerk, the Notary, Davy, Pitch, Nan Teazle, Rackham, Durrance, Sault, the Treasurer, Amaury, the Ninth Valuer, the Comptroller. In a city whose subject is attention, the identical glance diminished it. Forty-seven of 119 topics are `when true once`. No `Greets again` for any of the seventeen.

**Weak or flat.** Several named Court officers speak only in their one topic. The Muffled Drum (the soldiers' tavern) has an innkeeper and nothing for the company's second visit.

**Connections.** `lore.kyr` links to a Collegium surveyor, the Valuer shown a writ, a Tolleth packet. The rumour (soldiers walking into the sea; an eel-wife's notice) is strong. Nothing came back to the country below.

**Changed.**
- Twelve first meetings rewritten (all but the Notary's) to give the person an action and a want (the clerk's pen held a half-inch off the page until he has decided you are not a soldier; the Treasurer's chain on backwards).
- Seventeen `Greets again`, each referring to what the person is stuck on (Davy: "don't leave me alone with the tide").
- `after`: the east road's news once the note's holder is known.

**Not done.** The one-shot topic problem (see `the-dorothea.md`).
