# Play 11: the brothers' errand, as a thief, on a phone

Played on 28 October on the build of 49a7d3a (play 10's fixes), at 390 by 844 by touch: a phone, with no world named,
a third origin and a fourth calling after plays 8 to 10. About twenty-five taps, a day of the game's time. The
findings that were the game's fault are fixed in the commit after this one, and are in the register as M17-102 to
M17-109.

## The game

Tobin, a human thief (Dexterity 18, Intelligence 17, Wisdom 4, a dart), sent by the brothers of his order to
Karashilgab in Gebaglorkatzeh, high country, with a letter for the priest and an errand: to copy the old writing at
Achilaspir, which no one has ever copied.

1. **Creation** at phone size: five pages of cards, one column. The weapon page said "what thiefs carry by custom".
2. **The shrine.** The plate, the chapter, the statue of the goddess rubbed bright at the knees. On a phone the ways
   on take the lower two fifths of the screen; the ways about the place stood two to a row, and "To the market-place
   of Karashilgab" wrapped over three lines in half the width.
3. **The priest.** "You've come a long way to find somebody. Is it me?" Given the letter: "Brother Anselm's hand. I
   did not know he was still living." A bed in the back, and the way to Achilaspir: "Go in daylight."
4. **The market-place and the inn.** A cheese the size of a cartwheel, a widow in black on a cold step. Achilaspir,
   now the way was known, was about fifteen and a half hours off. Taklinn, a drover with blisters and nothing else to
   show for a drove, came along for three coin.
5. **The walk.** The journey went by the road, the foot of Selagiba and the tarn, larks going up out of the grass; and
   stopped at a shieling at half past five, because Taklinn remarked on the summer huts ("My grandmother had one like
   it"), giving as its reason "Summer huts,’ says Taklinn." The card holding the journey said "Next, by a wall-side
   track to region__z1_heart: about 5½ hours. About about 6½ hours in all." Going on, it stopped again at a sheepfold
   for another remark.
6. **Achilaspir**, near midnight: a roofless tower on a height, the old script round its door so large it can be read
   from the ground, "AQUILASPIRA: in the old tongue, “eagles spire”." The writing the brothers want is inside, and the
   door is high in the wall: "You would need climbing or a coil of rope." Neither the thief nor the drover had either,
   and nothing had said so before the sixteen hours' walk. I made camp; in the morning the camp offered to last "until
   3:00 p.m. on day 2".

## What pulled, and what lost

**Pulled:** the letter and the priest's welcome; the long walk, told well, with a companion who has things to say of
the fells; the tower on its height with its name cut over the door.

**Lost:** a journey that stopped for a companion's small talk, twice; ids on the card that holds the journey; an
errand whose end wanted a rope no one had mentioned, sixteen hours from town; small slips in the words.

## Fixed after this play

A companion's word does not stop a journey (it is told on the way), and a stop said in someone's words keeps the words
spoken; the card holding a journey names where each leg comes, and says how long in all once; the brothers' priest
sends the shrine's rope with the way ("the old places were not built to be walked into"); "eagle spire"; "thieves";
a camp or a bed by day says only its hour, or "tomorrow"; and on a phone a long way about a place has its row to
itself.

**Left:** the errand's work fifteen hours from the town it is told of, which the priest's "Go in daylight" cannot
mean; carved letters read at midnight without a light.
