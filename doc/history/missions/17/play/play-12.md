# Play 12: over the hills, as a monk, on a desktop

Played on 28 October on the build of 17af588 (play 11's fixes), at 1440 by 900 with a mouse: the last origin and
calling not yet played this mission (a monk, come over the hills with a scout's trade), on the screen size the earlier
plays had not used. About twenty clicks, an afternoon of the game's time. What was the game's fault is fixed in the
commit after this one, and is in the register as M17-110.

## The game

Ines, a human monk (Dexterity 17, Wisdom 19, bare hands), over the hills into Hillfells, high country, to see what
there is for someone who will work.

1. **Creation.** A hundred and fifty-six trades to bring, by kind; a scout's. ("Badger" stands among the wild
   country's trades, as Orsino has it: the old word for a dealer, read as the animal.)
2. **The road down into Muzgnekuda**, a fort town under spoil heaps. On a desktop the right page gave the ways on its
   lower 44% however few they were: here one way, and the telling above it cut short at its third paragraph.
3. **The bailey and the inn.** A soldier mending a boot "with the air of a man who has been told to do something
   else"; the innkeeper's seven things to ask after, among them the Delvers' mine at Stanumons: "Go in daylight, and
   take more light than you think." Two torches for two coin.
4. **Stanumons.** Four hours by the known ways. Spoil heaps the heather has never taken, ravens only at noon. Bats at
   the adit mouth let her by; the adit was pitch dark until a torch was lit. A riddle casket ("The more of this there
   is, the less you see"), answered "Darkness", gave a flask of oil. At the crossing, goblins reckoned beyond a lone
   monk, who could be talked to, asked to let her by, or warned off; a glyph on the way to the old stope, the risk
   marked "not looked at closely", and the scrape of a whetstone from the spring: "whoever is in there is in no hurry".

## What pulled, and what lost

**Pulled:** the mine as a place, with its signs read before it is entered; a riddle that answers the place; a
dangerous room that offers talk before a fight. **Lost:** the desktop's ways pane cutting the telling.

## Fixed after this play

On a desktop the ways stand at the foot of the right page, as tall as they need up to half of it, and what is here
takes the rest, as on a tablet; the rule between the pages runs their whole height. And from play 11: the brothers'
errand sends the company to the nearest of the makers' works.
