## Evidence limits

Images capture particular states, not every frame or possible interaction. Some captures contain prior response text beside a newly selected object; that coexistence is itself relevant to focus, but does not prove the text describes that object. No portrait's biographical identity was inferred from appearance alone. No cold import was performed on the earlier builds, so the final failure cannot be dated to the newest deployment. No ordinary formerly conversational NPC died during these journeys; dead hounds are not a sufficient generalization test for dead-person dialogue.
