## Encouraging changes not promoted into play verification

The inspected people rules add safeguards around incapacitated conversation and hostility. The works inscriptions separate maker information from a second source about the workers' fate; historical synthesis uses conditional language. These are relevant hypotheses for the dead-speaker and automatic-history findings, but neither exact regression was reached in these sessions. Their matrix status remains **not verifiable**.

The newest engine includes additional combat-aftermath handling. The final cold-save failure prevented a comparable fight on that deployment. The report therefore credits only the finishing information actually observed on 4ecc0e82, not source-only improvements in aa06ad01.

## Diagnosis boundaries

This was targeted inspection, not an architectural audit. Combat condition text was contradictory on screen, but the damage and condition pipelines were not traced far enough to pronounce the simulation's arithmetic wrong. Portrait mismatches are an artistic and identity assessment, not proof of a specific assignment algorithm defect. The philosophical conversation's abruptness is a content and dramaturgy problem even if its rules execute exactly as authored. Source sophistication cannot repair these judgments retroactively.
