MerscfaennEvening, late summer

Coming home

The causeway runs on its piles across the last of the fen, and at the end of it is Murkstede: salt-houses black with tar, boats drawn up on the landing, one lit window in the inn.

You have been three years on the other side of the hills, hiring out with the droves. Your mother’s letter took a week to find you. Your father went out on the Meóswaeter road with the cart, it said, and did not come back.

A boy is sitting on the causeway rail with a spear across his knees. It takes you a moment to know him. Amrod is taller by a head, and he has your father’s old spear.
