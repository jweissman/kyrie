# The money of a country

**Mission 19 (4 November, night).** Measured again after Orsino's goods were brought within reach: each kind of shop
drawing its town's shelves from its trade's whole range by what the town can afford (300 coin a thing, 800 in a market
town; two of what is cheap, one of what is dear), and each village merchant dealing in three things of their trade
besides their own stock. Against Mission 18's measure ([economy.md](../18/economy.md)), the median country's
haul is 1.90 times all it sells and teaches (was 1.93); the two whose market towns keep rare goods (g7, kite) cost a
little more to buy out entirely (0.92 and 0.85, were 1.03 and 0.93), which is the point: rings and wands to want,
not everything at once. Paid outright is 732 (was 660), from work done since that this note has not traced; it counts no sale of a made
remedy (a herb-wife pays 8 for a minor potion of healing, a chandler 6).

Written by `bun scripts/economy-measure.ts`.
