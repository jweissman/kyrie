# What became of Orsino's bestiary (M19-60, M19-76)

Each creature-like entry of the Orsino corpus at the revision `data/sources/orsino/registry.json` pins, and what it is in Westernesse now: a canonical base (its own record in `animals.json` or `monsters.json`), a variation (`variations.json`, one axis), a device (`devices.json`, not a creature), or not carried, with how ordinary generation reaches it (`den`, `open` country, `deep` sites' composed parties, `authored` content, `summoned`). Made by `/var/tmp/kyrie/m19/inventory.ts` (6 November) against the records and `BestiaryReport.reach`. A changed classification is not a lost entry: the composed kinds (the fire elemental, the iron golem, the red dragon) stand under their variation. Not yet in it: Orsino's peoples and NPC types (its parties' members), which Kyr makes as people of an ancestry and calling. What generated countries actually met: [bestiary.md](bestiary.md).

**Counts.** canonical base: 82; variation: 42; device: 5; not carried: 8; composition: 1.
