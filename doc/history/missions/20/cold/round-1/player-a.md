Cleared the rats under the corn-store and their buck rat; hurt by a centipede in the old drain; slept at the Stormy
Horn; hired Xyn. From the undercroft levered up a lettered flag and killed a ghoul ("the keeper of the vault"); the stair

Understood the situation at once: Tuck's "Fifty coin, by market day" and "patient until market day … and not after".
The first screen of the world (09) was confusing: a 3D hex island with my portrait, dozens of italic labels in fog
("way into the vale of the Arle", "windfall") — couldn't tell known, reachable, or decoration. After Enter, the
wood-yard scene was immediately inviting: painting, good prose, one person. Tuck's topics opened four hooks in a minute

"Thirty coin a lump, and no questions"); the undercroft was behind the drain I had cleared; following the ways down felt
like my own choice and paid off. Seolforfyrd: the game kept pushing it (the "Following" aim in every Go panel, Aiden,
Tuck's farewell); it never noticed I carried 90 coin of star-iron. Too many aims: ~10 by evening of day 1.
