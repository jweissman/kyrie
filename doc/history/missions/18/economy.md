# The money of a country

Written by `bun scripts/economy-measure.ts` on 3 November 2026, for PT-02 and the closure of Mission 18 (Gate C5).
For each of twelve generated countries: the valuables its places hold that a company can carry home (lying or kept in
them, not for sale), what they are worth and what a dealer pays for them (half: `dealerPays`, 08dbfc49); the coin its
own jobs, finds and rewards pay outright; against every ware its towns sell, by its price, and every lesson its
teachers give, by its fee, once each. The last column is what a company could bring home in coin over all there is to
spend it on.

**Reading.** Mission 16 measured a median ruin paying 8.75 times every shop's stock; PT-02 asked for 1.5 to 3. With a
treasure fetching half its worth, the median country's whole haul is 1.93 times all it sells and teaches (0.93 to
3.08): enough to buy what one wants and not everything, and a company that empties every site of a country still has
coin left to save. Two things remain thin: only the first town teaches (two teachers in every country: its drill-yard
and its scrivener), and the measure counts coin, not the experience a find also gives (a quarter of its worth).
