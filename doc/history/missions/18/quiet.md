# Mission 18: the quiet interface

The owner's feedback of 2026-10-29 (OWN-14 to OWN-21): the title has improved and the map is becoming spatial, but
ordinary play is still far too busy, "a complex website rather than an immersive RPG". The aim is not smaller text or
narrower panels: it is to stop showing what the moment does not need. This is the design the work follows, phase by
phase, each judged on the rendered screens at a desk's width (1440 by 900) and a tablet's (1024 by 1366).

## The rule

Each moment shows what is perceived now and what can be done now. What is known, what can be inspected and what is
reference are a deliberate touch away: in a sheet over the page, the map, the character, the notes, the encyclopedia.
Each activity has its own composition in one artistic world (the title's: dark ground, ivory, the lantern's gold, Jost
and Newsreader), not one framed application with different text in its panels.

## Creation: the cards and almost nothing else (OWN-14, OWN-15, OWN-16)

- No window frame, header bar, step dots, country pitch or summary footer. The ground is the title's: dark, the
  country's painting faint behind, a vignette.
- One heading, centred: "Choose your ancestry", "Choose your calling", "Choose your school", "Why have you come?".
- The cards centred in the viewport, several together, of one proportion (about 3:4), each an emblem, a short name,
  and one identifying line of a few words. Chosen: a gold edge, lifted, the others quieter.
- Under the cards: the chosen card's one sentence, a quiet "Details" that opens the mechanics in a sheet, and one
  Continue. Back is a quiet word at the top left; "Set out now" a quiet word at the top right (the automation's way).
- Fewer screens: why, ancestry, calling, school or way (where the calling has one), name. The weapon, the qualities
  and a wanderer's trade are taken at sensible defaults, each changeable from "Details" on the name screen; larger
  sets (52 weapons, 211 trades) open in an inspectable view that scrolls down, grouped, never a horizontal wall.
- Spell schools: each card the school's emblem, its practical identity in a line, and, for the chosen school, its
  starting spells as a strip of rows, each a function icon, the spell's name and what it does in a line. What the
  preview shows is what the protagonist gets: the choice fixes the spells.
- The name: a default drawn for the ancestry and its people's names (no longer "Wenna" for everyone), large and
  centred, with "Another name" and editing in place; drawn again if the ancestry changes while it is untouched.
- Icons: one modest stroke set at 48 px, after the existing glyphs: the eight schools, the twelve domains, the ways,
  and a dozen spell functions (harm, ward, heal, light, sight, charm, fear, sleep, move, summon, curse, craft).

## The opening: a quiet stage (OWN-17)

After "Set out", before the first place: the approach's painting over the whole screen, no HUD, no Book. A small gold
kicker (the origin's chapter: "Coming home"), the first beat as one centred paragraph (the letter, the errand, what is
owed), a touch or Enter, the second beat (the arrival, naming the first town), and one "Go on". Drawn from the origin
lines the engine already writes for the first moment; the first place then opens on one observation. What the first
screen used to say of companions for hire goes to the notes and the innkeeper.

## A place: a glance, and more on request (OWN-18, OWN-19)

- A first arrival: one distinctive observation (the place's own lead line, at most two sentences), one sign of life
  now (a person present and what they are doing or saying, a goings-on), and one thing to do (the place's own act, the
  person behind the life, a thing to look at, the aimed way). Three short lines and one key action.
- Under them: "Look about", which opens the full telling, the people, the things, the services and the lore in a sheet;
  and the ways as the nearest few, with "Every way" for the rest.
- A return: shorter still, the place's "again" line, or what has changed.
- The engine decides it (a glance on the leaf: sight, life, doing, first or return), so that the page draws and does not
  guess; the full telling stays as it is behind "Look about", so nothing written is lost.

## The play view: quiet (OWN-19, OWN-20)

The present scene and the actions that matter: the painting, the place's name, the glance, a few actions. No persistent
footer of status and acts; the company's health only when it is hurt; wait, search and camp among the place's acts or
behind "More"; the Book, the map and the character as quiet icons. Conversation stays the speaker and their words (the
large-portrait page is the one the owner rates right). The map stays the whole screen. The sheet, the pack, the notes
and the encyclopedia may be dense, because they are opened on purpose.

## Names of places (OWN-16)

Settlements of the human heartland in a plain Common tradition of translated compounds after their lie (Whitefall,
Thornford, Greenfield, Duskvale), in place of Westron's Old-English forms; other peoples' settlements and old names
where history puts them. Settlement and land names are not facts (no fact or digest moves); region names seed draws and
are frozen as keys first. Staged: the protagonist's name; settlements; land; regions.

## Phases

1. Creation, schools and spells, the protagonist's name, the icon set.
2. The opening stage.
3. The glance, "Look about", and the quiet play view.
4. Common names for places, staged.
5. The demonstration of the whole flow at desk and tablet sizes (OWN-21), published for the owner.
