# Down the salt road to Brack

*A journey beyond the region, and an urban adventure. Company: delvers; seed val-brack.*

**Take the salt road to the salt pans** — the salt pans

> Salt pans, square and shining, going out to the sea on both sides of a raised causeway. Brack is at the end of it: a wall, a gate, and a harbour full of masts.

**Take the causeway across the pans to the Landgate** — the Landgate

> The Landgate: a stone arch with the Watch's lantern over it, and a board of tolls and permissions as long as a sermon.

**Pay Sergeant Morrow two coin for a stranger's pass** — the Landgate

Morrow writes your names in his book slowly, spelling them wrong, and hands you a wooden tally with the Watch's lantern burned into it.

**Take the Landgate street to the Saltquays** — the Saltquays

> The Saltquays: ships tied up three deep, barrows of salt, gulls, and the Salt Exchange's front of grey stone with its doors open onto the water.

> The Exchange's doors are barred for the night.

**Wait until first light** — the Saltquays

The sky greys, then lightens. It is morning.

> The Exchange's doors stand open onto the quay.

**Take the Exchange's doors to the Exchange floor** — the Exchange floor

> The Exchange floor: a hall of slates chalked with prices, factors shouting, and a great balance in the middle with a clerk on a stool beside it.

> The slate for Skerry silver reads high and rising, and the one for Hollin grain reads lower than anyone here remembers.

**Take the counting-house door to the counting-house** — the counting-house

> The counting-house: desks, ledgers on chains, and a door bound in iron at the back.

> The strongroom door is shut and locked: iron, with a lock the size of a book.

**Ask Pim what is wrong** — the counting-house

Pim looks at the strongroom door and then at you. 'Nothing,' he says. 'Only some ledgers aren't kept on the chains, and some are kept in black, and I'd not like to be the one who shows anyone which.'

**Pay Pim ten coin to leave the strongroom key on his desk** — the counting-house

Pim pockets the coins without looking at them, and goes out for some air, and the key is on his desk.

> The strongroom door stands open.

**Take the strongroom door to the strongroom** — the strongroom

> The strongroom: chests, a strong-box for every factor, and a shelf of ledgers bound in black.

**Have Ivo use Light on Ivo** — the strongroom

Ivo uses Light on Ivo.

**Collect a ledger bound in black for Ivo** — the strongroom

Ivo takes a ledger bound in black.

> The black ledger is in a clerk's hand, and it is plain enough: forty crowns a month, out of the strongroom, to 'O.N., at the Gap'. The signature under every entry is Corvin Hale's.

> Corvin Hale, the Salt Exchange's factor for silver, has paid Ninefingers every month since spring to hold the Gap, so that Skerry's silver comes to Brack cheap through his agents and to nobody else.

**Take the strongroom door to the counting-house** — the counting-house


**Take the counting-house door to the Exchange floor** — the Exchange floor


**Tell Dame Marrick what her factor has been paying for** — the Exchange floor

Dame Marrick reads the black ledger to the last line without a word, closes it, and sends for the Watch. 'The Exchange buys,' she says. 'It does not hire cut-throats to make the price. Hale is done. And you are owed: name the Exchange your friend, and it will be one.'

> A clerk of the Exchange finds you with a purse of eighty crowns, and Dame Marrick's compliments.
