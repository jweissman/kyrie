Reedwake · The Serewater March

Barges nose into reed-fringed piles. The Serewater is high and brown. Upriver, the derelict foundry on Censer Hill stands above the willows. The Oar's sign creaks over one door, Nera's herbs hang in another, the shrine porch faces the water, and a salvage company's chalked board leans by the office.

A shepherd's boy is telling anyone who will listen that something comes up out of Silkholme sink at night and takes a ewe off Chalk ridge, and that Garth means to burn it out.
