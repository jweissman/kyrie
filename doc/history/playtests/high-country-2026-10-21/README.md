# The high country, played by its builder (2026-10-21)

This is the builder's own playtest, not an external one. It was played by the author of the content, through the
browser client (`site/mono.html?campaign=data/campaigns/varrow.campaign.json`), with the Old hands company, at
1440×900 and 390×844. The screenshots are numbered in the order they were taken; each comes at both sizes.

## The route

From Hollin to the Varrow road, the ford and the Gap. The company pays the Toll and goes on through the Lanthorn's
gate passage to Skerry. Captain Hest lowers it in the deep cage, and it crosses the Brimming and the still lake by
Spill's boat. Searching the far shore finds the drowned stair (02), which leads into the Unlit (03).

At the Ash Mere the thing under the dust is spotted and warns (04). The company waits, and it rises (05). The fight
is fought under the Unlit's law (06), and the company wins (07). On the ash road the wraith riders fall on it at
once (08). At Lampfall the lamp-bearers bar the gate, and the Hollow Warden names his price (09). Spell
preparation for a twelfth-level mage is shown at Hollin (10).

## What it found, and what was done

| Found | Done |
|---|---|
| The shared rules modules were installed once per module, so each inhabitants rule ran nine times a watch. One copy warned, a second spent the creature's patience and a third attacked, all in the same watch. | Shared rules are now joined into the world once. |
| A warning and its patience both ran out in the watches after a long move arrived. | A warning now stops the company where it stands (the core's `halts`). |
| A halted company's response repeated the warning that was also told as an event. | The response says who stopped the company; the warning is told once. |
| Two choices to prepare a spell were identical when the spell was held in two slots. | The label names the slot, and circles now run to the ninth-level slot. |
| The Unlit's law was worn as a red chip by every combatant. | Laws are told once, under the fight's heading. |
| An event's time was drawn over its first line. | It sits above the words when nothing was done. |
| The Warden told a company that came by the lake that it came "by the Lanthorn". | His line names the doors, not the company's way. |
| The Warden's regalia could be taken from under his pen. | Taking it ends whatever was agreed. |
| A passage read backwards ("the slope down into the cinders to the ash road"). | Renamed. |
