The upper works · Censer Hill foundry

A split pipe hisses over the boiler arch. Fresh scorch marks stripe the boiler arch; turning its seized valve needs a crowbar. A fire ward can blunt a scald, but the jet must still be crossed.

The stores door stands open; its padlock hangs from the hasp.

Half-cast bells lean against the walls. Doorways lead to the stores and the vestibule; steps go down to the casting gallery, and the culvert mouth gapes in the floor.

The rising river has closed the drain culvert.
