# Kyr cold player assessment — 19 October 2026

**Verdict:** Caught Below contains a small, genuinely legible rescue adventure. Kyr does not yet present it as a consistently understandable RPG. The worst failures concern the meaning of a click, continuity of consequences, and the relationship between a fictional situation and the actions the engine happens to permit. Combat exposes rules without explaining decisions. A new visual skin would leave these problems intact.
