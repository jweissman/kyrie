**Take the Varrow road to Two Barrows** — Two Barrows

> Two grassy barrows stand by the road like a pair of sleeping cattle. The nearer has a dark slot low on its side, half hidden by brambles. Beyond them the valley narrows toward the ford.

**Take the river road to the alder bend** — the alder bend

> The road bends with the river under alders. Someone has built a fishing platform out over the water, and a boat is pulled up beneath it.

**Take the ford lane to Crake's Ford** — Crake's Ford

> A hamlet of slate and turf at the ford of the Varrow: a dozen houses, the Heron and Eel, a smithy, and sheep-pens standing empty.

> The Varrow runs knee-deep and clear over the ford stones.

> A woman in black sits on the step of a cold house with her hands in her lap, watching the road.

**Take the ford to the far bank of the ford** — the far bank of the ford

> The far bank of the ford, where the Gap road leaves the river and starts to climb.

**Take the Gap road to the switchbacks** — the switchbacks

> The road climbs in switchbacks above the river. A cairn of white stones stands by a gully where the road turns for the third time.

**Take the road past the cairn to the wayside cairn** — the wayside cairn

> A wayside cairn of offerings: pins, buttons, a doll's shoe. Above, the road makes its last pull to the Gap, and the Lanthorn stands over it, black against the sky.

**Take the last pull to the Toll gate** — the Toll gate

> The road runs straight into a gatehouse built across the Gap. A board nailed to the gate reads TOLL: TWO CROWNS A WHEEL, TWO A HEAD, BY ORDER, and someone has scratched a ninth finger on the drawn hand.

> Two of Ninefingers' men lean on the inside of the gate with crossbows, waiting for someone to tax.

> The outer gate is shut and barred on the inside; there is a grille in it at head height.

**Pay the wardens the Toll, six coin** — the Toll gate

Coins go through the grille. A bar scrapes, and the gate swings open.

> The outer gate stands open.

**Take the outer gate to the gate passage** — the gate passage

> A vaulted tunnel through the gatehouse, wide enough for a cart. The vault is pierced with square holes, and the far end is closed by an iron portcullis.

> The far portcullis is wound up into its slot.

> The guardroom door is locked.

**Take the far portcullis to the far side of the Gap** — the far side of the Gap

> Behind the Lanthorn the Gap opens onto the heights: stone, heather, and far off the smoke of Skerry.

> On the Skerry side the gatehouse is a blank wall with a portcullis in it. Along the cliff, under the kitchen windows, there is a small iron-bound postern, and below the road the cliff drops into a gorge.

> The far end of the gate passage is shut by an iron portcullis, down to the stones.

> The postern is shut, and from the outside there is nothing to take hold of.

**Take the heights road to the cairn field** — the cairn field

> A field of cairns, hundreds, each one a miner's grave. The road to Skerry runs between them.

**Take the road down into Skerry to Skerry square** — Skerry square

> Skerry square: grey stone houses round a mine head with a great wheel. Everyone is thin. A queue at the Assay waits to sell silver nobody can carry down to Hollin.

**Take the mine yard to the mine head** — the mine head

> The mine head: the wheel, the cage, and a cold breath coming up the shaft. A notice says DEEP GALLERIES CLOSED.

> The deep cage is chained to its post, with a padlock.

**Ask Captain Hest to lower you down the deep shaft** — the mine head

Hest looks at you a long time. 'Find out what took Dunstan and Rafe,' he says, 'and bring me back anything of theirs. I'll lower you, and I'll be at the top when you come up, if you come up.' He takes the key off his belt.

> The deep cage hangs ready at the top of the shaft.

**Take the deep cage to the deep gallery** — the deep gallery

> A gallery deeper than the rest, its timbers newer. A board nailed across a side-drift says KEEP OUT in a hurried hand. The air moves, coming up from somewhere.

**Take the deep gallery to the breakthrough** — the breakthrough

> The gallery ends in a ragged hole where the miners broke into a cave. Two miners' lamps lie on the floor by the hole, cold, their oil still in them.

> By the hole, scratches in the rock at the height of a man's hand, going away into the dark in a line: fingernails.

**Take the Glimmerkin's ladder to the Glimmer Market** — the Glimmer Market

> A cavern roofed with glow-worms like a night sky, and under it stalls of jars that shine: green, gold, white as noon. Small soft-footed people with eyes like a cat's in lamplight move between them, talking in whispers.

**Take the shore path to the shore of the still lake** — the shore of the still lake

> A black lake as still as glass, going off into the dark. A flat boat, and a post with a little bell on it.

> Spill sits in the boat with his pole across his knees, humming.

> The flat boat is tied up, and Spill will not have strangers poling it.

**Pay Spill two coin to pole you across** — the shore of the still lake

Spill counts the coins twice and pushes off. The boat makes no sound at all on the still water.

> Spill has the boat out on the lake for you.

**Take the still lake to the far shore** — the far shore

> The far shore of the lake: stones, and Glimmerkin jars smashed on them, dark.

**Search the far shore** — the far shore

You go over the far shore carefully.

> Under the still lake a stair goes down, and on down, out of the water and into the Unlit: the Lamplighters' back door, which the Glimmerkin's boatmen will not pole over.

**Take the drowned stair to the head of the drowned stair** — the head of the drowned stair

> Steps come up out of the dust here, wet, as if from under water. Above them the dark is a little less dark.
