Then Gaudumport, "a smooth doorway in a crag in Syndrigholm/Lonecrag" (Lyle the drover), about 4.5 hours away. This is the real delve: a riddle door, a trap, a dark round hall with a stone guardian, a hob warren with a captive boy.

## In the player's words

I finished every step: lead, route, travel, an inside, fights, a riddle, a rescue, and home again. It was rarely fun, and I had to use outside persistence to get there. The individual pieces are often good: the opening premise, Aspen's rumour turning into a route and one-click travel, Dunstan spotting the ruts, finding my father, my mother's 'The dog knew', the speaking-door riddle, the hobs' warren and the captive boy.

The systems don't add up because nothing joins them. The rumour I was handed ('a cave', 'something made its den') led to a single screen with no inside. The real dungeon came from a throwaway question to a drover, with no aim attached. Its interior route was gated by skills the default warrior lacks, plus a guardian the game told me I would 'likely beat'; it nearly killed my party. The town's own hook (the stolen bell, hobs) visibly connects to Gaudumport's hold of bells and to the boy's 'hobs can't abide bells', yet it never becomes something I can pursue, carry back or fix. Coming home, the world ignored everything except the scripted father thread: no thanks for the den or the boy, the boy has no home, the innkeeper's rumour and my notes stay stale.

On screen, the thing that matters (danger, countdown, the one sensible next move) is regularly below the fold, under prose, logs and long lists of ways. Interrupted travel produces the most crowded screen in the game, with five identical unlabelled chips. The map is attractive but did not help me understand where anything was: half the screen is fog, and the camera pans away from home when you pick a place.

My diagnosis: content is authored as separate, well-written lumps (place prose, NPC ask-lists, dangers, gated exits), and leads, aims, rewards and reactions do not tie them into one arc. The danger reckoning and the gates are not tuned to the character the game makes by default.

The site was rebuilt twice by another process during my session (renamed places and hero at about 23:54; then a 'quiet' bridge-foot that hides the inn behind 'EVERY WAY FROM HERE 16' at about 00:18). I replayed the identical route on the 23:54 build, and every outcome and line matched. The 00:18 change looks like it would make the first lead even harder to find. My driver and replay files are in /home/jweissman/work/kyrie/.claude/worktrees/diagnosis/p1/ (play.ts, run.sh, nb1-nb8.json).
