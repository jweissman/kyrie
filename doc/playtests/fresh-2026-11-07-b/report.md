# A second fresh session's expedition, 7 November 2026

A second playtester new to the game played through the browser client (build 0b68a180, Chromium 1280×800, driven by
scripts/play-server.ts), about 120 actions: origin Over the Hills, a catfolk cleric of the Light (Sable), Karir in
the Tinfells, Traubon the dwarf warrior hired at the inn. Screenshots were kept outside the repository
(/var/tmp/kyrie/m19/playtest-7nov-2/shots/, by number below). One session's account, not acceptance (PD-14 unmet).

## What was done

Creation (clean and quick; the catfolk portrait admired); the wagoner's cart; the well-post notice and its three
aims; the crypt job taken; a torch, a ration, bandages and a firebomb bought (shops sold); Traubon hired. The rats
under the corn-store, then the drain into the undercroft, a stair found by searching, the ossuary's man with the black
candle beaten by the rite of rest, the crypt job paid. The lone tower (Lapisspira) by the map, eight hours, at night:
two revenants and a skeletal hound fought with Pure Beam and Sunmark; the daybook read, the bone lantern smashed (the
tower seen through), the necromancer sent away, the drowned girl laid to rest. Back to Karir; grave rings sold; both to
the second level at the inn.

## What was enjoyable

The rite of rest and the bone lantern (choices from the calling and the life before); radiant spells against the dead
with a warrior holding the line; the wagoner and Beak's lantern; the flag hidden in the undercroft; the map's route
card ("8 hours, arrive 8:20 p.m.") and the journey; the town plan; the prose throughout.

## Friction, ranked

Blocking or nearly: the crypt not findable from town (the churchyard inside "Waterside", unmarked; 56, 59, 104); the
lone tower's marker invisible on the map and under the fixed card, the Book's "on the map" not going to it (115, 120);
an aim "Kellen will want to know" naming no one met (174).

Confusing: results below the fold (the notice's aims, a rite's aim, levels gained; 29, 94b, 183); a job's report
fifth under Something else (109) and a hireling's offer seventh (51) (fixed since); guidance pointing elsewhere
(47, 147) and a Go there that did nothing (173); number keys by the drawn acts dead (70) (fixed since); keys held
offering no unlock (67, 100); the fight's Defend, Flee and firebomb below the panel (74, 83, 134); waiting until first
light leaving the company worn out and its lights burned dry (171); a journey stopping halfway without a word (122);
"easily won" before a fight and "could fall" within it (83).

Tedious: rats dying to one blow before the cleric acts; the inn's five notices on the first evening; torches out in
about an hour of play, the lantern's message thrice.

Cosmetic: paintings not fitting (a palm grotto for rat-runs, a library for a taproom, a log cabin for a tower, a beagle
for a skeletal hound, a revenant's face for a drowned girl); engine talk in the fight log (THAC0, "resistance -100%",
"death save … against 25"); small slips of text; layout slips on the map's cards; development choices in long capitals;
the Book listing finished jobs under leads.
