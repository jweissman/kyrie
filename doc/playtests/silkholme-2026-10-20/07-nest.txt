The caves below · Silkholme sink

◆ Spiders from Silkholme sink take a ewe from Garth's flock every night. Garth wants them burned out; Odile, who gathers their silk, says the brood-mother only needs feeding.

A high chamber, its roof lost in grey silk. Old husks of sheep and rabbits hang in the threads like washing.

Cave spiders hang in the silk overhead, legs folded, waiting.

The brood-mother crouches at the back of the nest, big as a pony and thin as a rake, turning all her eyes on you at once.

A ragged hole has been hacked through the silk in the web gallery.
