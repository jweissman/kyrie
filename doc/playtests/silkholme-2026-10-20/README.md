# Silkholme: playtest of the integrated expedition

20 October 2026. Played through the built Mono client (`site/mono.html?campaign=data/campaigns/silkholme.campaign.json`)
in Chromium, at 1280×900, with real key presses. The default company: Elen (elf ranger, hunter: rope, woodcraft),
Bran (human warrior, locksmith), Dori (dwarf cleric, medic). I wrote the content, so this is not a blind playtest;
it tests whether the game shows what a player needs to decide and whether consequences are visible and coherent.
Each capture has a `.png` and the screen's exact text in a `.txt` file.

## What the expedition is

A chalk sink on the ridge above Reedwake, where cave spiders nest. Garth, a shepherd, loses a ewe every night and
wants them burned out. Odile gathers their silk and says the brood-mother is starving and only needs feeding.
Getting down needs rope or an hour's crawl; webs close the nest. Left alone, Garth burns it himself after the next
night's raid. Built from general parts:

| Requirement | How |
|---|---|
| A locality whose details cohere | Site [silkholme](../../../data/sites/silkholme/site.kyr) attached to the existing Serewater region; caves are a level (no camping in the nest) |
| Creatures from compositional Deem definitions | `Creature body cave_spider. Numbers 3.` (the brood) and `Creature body cave_spider. Level 3.` (the brood-mother), generated by the public `creature` generator. The cave spider species was itself added as data |
| Differentiated executable capabilities | Venomous bite and ensnare (spiders); sweeping strike, cures, ensnare, hunter's mark (company); rope for the shaft; a torch for fire |
| Environmental challenge | Shaft needing rope (or a one-hour crawl); webs that block until hacked or burned; fire spreads into the nest and up the seep |
| An NPC situation with more than one approach | Feed, burn, fight, or do nothing; two people with opposed interests, each reacting to the outcome |
| Preparation, inspection, consequential choice | Rope, the torch, the dead ewe as goods; every option shows its time and cost before it is chosen |
| A return showing persistent change | Frames, Odile's stock and mood, Garth's flock and pay, what the quay talks about the next day |

Situation source: [brood-hollow](../../../data/situations/brood-hollow/situation.kyr). Engine tests: [silkholme.test.ts](../../../test/silkholme.test.ts) (all four approaches plus night).

## Journey 2: the torch (captures 21–23)

Took Garth's torch (Take › for Bran), went down, put the torch to the webs. Fire runs into the nest, the brood and
mother flee into the chalk, smoke pours up the seep. On the terrace Odile stands among the ashes of her frames and
will not look at us. By midday the quay is talking about the smoke and Odile's frames. Garth pays eight coin.
Burning is quicker and pays slightly more coin; feeding keeps Odile, her silk and the good will of both.

## Journey 3: the fight (earlier captures, not committed)

Attacking the brood is a three-against-three encounter. The combat screen lists three spiders with their own HP and
conditions ("shred · prone"); each ability shows its generated meaning for the acting companion ("One enemy gains
entangled for 3 turns (a reflex save against 13 avoids it). Entangled: −2 to hit, −5 evasion…"). The log groups each
action with what it caused. The company won on automatic tactics with moderate injuries; the brood-mother fled and
Odile was left grieving.

## What changed because of this play

Fixed during or because of play, each as a general change rather than a Silkholme patch:

- Selecting a row never commits; digits pick from the list in focus, and open menus are numbered.
- A way says how far it is ("half an hour away by the ridge track"), not "a few steps".
- Conversation replies stay in the history; the history no longer repeats the topic as its own result; duplicate
  lines are removed; goods such as a carried dead ewe appear in the company strip.
- Campaign manifests carry their own creation pitch (creation always described Censer Hill).
- The Journal leads with matters in hand; an objective only reports what the company has heard.
- The world knows night (`dark()`), so the quay can be dark and gossip waits for day; "Wait until first light".
- Combat log grouped in causal order; saves say which save; species rows no longer read "cave spider undefined".
- Generic engine-guarantee narration removed ("Survivors retain their injuries…").
- Content: no camping in the nest; first silk instead of full frames an hour after feeding; quay news only after a
  quiet night; events that remove their anchor are anchored where the player can perceive them.

## Candid assessment

- **Understanding.** The hook, the two positions and the physical problem were all clear from the screens, and every
  option showed its time before I chose it. The best moment is Garth refusing to believe until a night has passed.
- **Meaningful choice.** Four genuinely different resolutions with different people left better or worse off, and one
  that happens without you. Its stakes are small: a shepherd's ewes and a silk-gatherer's trade.
- **Weak spots.** Hacking the webs is free and safe; it should cost time or risk waking the brood. Odile says to "go
  slowly", but feeding carries no risk. Garth's deadline lives only in his dialogue, not on a clock you can see.
  Lodging is always eight hours. Quantity changes still read like a ledger. A fight is optional and, on automatic
  tactics, not dangerous for a full-health company.
- **Combat** is clearer than before and has variety with three opponents, but it is still abstract: no reach, cover
  or protecting someone, so tactics reduce to choosing a target and an ability.
- **Not proven.** A second linked expedition, advancement between them, a larger site. Silkholme is one day of play.
