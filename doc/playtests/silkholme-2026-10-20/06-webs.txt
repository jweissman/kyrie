The caves below · Silkholme sink

◆ Spiders from Silkholme sink take a ewe from Garth's flock every night. Garth wants them burned out; Odile, who gathers their silk, says the brood-mother only needs feeding.

Water drips from the chalk into shallow pools. Pale threads run along the walls towards a low arch, and every thread trembles a little.

Sheets of grey silk close the web gallery from wall to wall. Things hang in them.
