# A fresh session's ordinary expedition, 7 November 2026

A playtester new to the game played one ordinary expedition through the browser client (build a6eab40f, Chromium
1280×800, driven by scripts/play-server.ts): about 110 actions over a day and a half of the game's time. The origin was
Coming Home, a dwarf warrior come back to Elmford in Lilyweald. Screenshots and page text were kept outside the
repository (/var/tmp/kyrie/m19/playtest-7nov/shots/, by number below). This is one session's account, not acceptance
(PD-14 stays unmet).

## What was done

1. Title, New Game, Coming Home, dwarf warrior, Toughness, herdsman, named Harbek: six clear screens, about a minute;
   Set out about four seconds (01–09).
2. The road into Elmford: the wagoner's cart righted (by Look), two coin (10–15).
3. The market-place: the brother joins with the clue of the wheel out of true; the notice read; the corn-store key
   from Brom; a torch bought; the general store and the clothier sold nothing (16–32).
4. Out of town by the map: the toll bridge, the ruts off the road, the father found and buried (33–51).
5. The barrow Tenebrerecs, two storeys: four traps with the pole, a notebook's hint, two riddle caskets; beetles,
   zombies, a banshee, a slump, the mummy king, a hollow-stalker and a ticking charge; about fifty coin (53–141).
6. Home three hours by the road; the mother told at the inn; a night's sleep, both to the second level (142–158).
7. A cloak-pin sold for fifteen, a cloak bought (159–164).

## What was enjoyable

- The father's story: the clue, the ruts, the body, the burial, the mother's "He could never stand to be under a roof".
- The barrow's prose, the notebook's hint paying off, traps told with their expected harm.
- Fights clear and quick, with their odds and a summing-up; a level's choices saying what they open.
- The map growing with the journey; the interruption on the road finding the story by itself.

## Friction, ranked

Blocking:
1. Shops would not sell and never said why (31, 32, 161–164). Cause found: the brother was drawn with the
   protagonist's own name, so "Buy … for Harbek" was two choices the page could not tell apart. Fixed (kin-names).
2. The brother a copy of the protagonist: same name ("You look at you…", "Harbek and Harbek each gain…"), the same card
   twice in fights, "HARBEK CARRIES" twice; mother and brother painted human in a dwarf family (17, 27, 28, 77, 82,
   133, 153). The name fixed; the likenesses open.
3. Loot unreachable or flickering: riddle caskets' contents never shown (131, 132); a wand, elixir and rope appearing
   and vanishing in the hall of bones (99, 100); rotted packs' "Put in" and "Examine" doing nothing (73, 75); the
   king's bier never offered after he fell, so the crown could not be had (130).

Confusing:
4. Results below the fold in the fixed column: new aims, the king's danger card, levels gained (19, 55, 125, 156).
5. The wagoner asks for a hand, but the help is under Look, not in the talk (11, 13).
6. The map: a marker under the info card, an empty grey image in the route card, the header and the card naming
   different places, "You know no way there yet", zoom barely changing the view (33, 34, 41–44).
7. Threat and climax: a level-8 mummy king "easily won" by two first-levels, and it was; experience comes fast
   (126, 128).
8. Traps named in the Look list before found, all answered by the pole; an electrified floor and a ticking charge in a
   barrow (65).
9. The job come for (the rats) under "Something else" behind three rumours (23, 24).
10. Number keys do not fire the scene's choices, though they do in talk.
11. "Go there → Market" led into Brom's forge (22).

Tedious: long fights with nothing to settle them (ten presses for the banshee); Look in two steps; trap answers
"chosen again"; next-door shops twenty minutes said, two passed.

Cosmetic: paintings not matching places (a mountain range for Elmford's gate, a library for a taproom, one barrow
painting for every room, a file name "A10464.jpg" as a caption); the barrow Tenebrerecs and its lintel TENEBRAEREX;
the rat-runs sentence twice; "swears under their breath" of a man; "Harbek is under insane"; "a falling rocks"; the
toll-reeve card clipped.
