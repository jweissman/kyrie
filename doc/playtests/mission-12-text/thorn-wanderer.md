Seed `thorn`, origin `wanderer`, calling `warrior`. Written by `bun bin/playtext.ts`.

### The causeway into Merscheald — Day 1, 8:00 a.m.

The causeway runs on its piles across the last of the fen, and at the end of it is Merscheald: low roofs against the sky, eel-traps stacked against the walls, boats drawn up on the landing, smoke going straight up from the inn.

You have come over the hills into Faennmeós with your trade and not much else. There is work in any country for someone who will do what others will not, and the place to hear of it is the inn.

**1. Take the causeway to Merscheald green**

### Merscheald green — Day 1, 8:10 a.m.

Merscheald is a town of narrow lanes round a broad green, with eel-traps stacked against every wall. It grew up beside the Sealtbrōc. It looks out over Haseclaene. Next to the Laughing Flame is the shrine of Belenus, so close that the inn’s smoke goes over its roof. Two women are skinning eels on a bench by the water, and the cats are watching.

Nest is driving a gaggle of geese along the edge of the green with a willow switch, and the geese are winning.

Averil is grinding something in a stone mortar, sniffing it now and then.

Emmot is crying wares to nobody in particular: pots mended, knives ground, news for nothing.

Lambert is forking hay to the cows, who watch him do it without any gratitude.

Bran, the inn’s dog, lies across the doorstep of the Laughing Flame with her chin on her paws, and watches who comes.

Clover, Lambert’s cow, is never far from Lambert.

A woman in black sits on the step of a cold house with her hands in her lap, watching the road.
